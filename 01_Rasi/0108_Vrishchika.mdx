## Introduction to Vrishchika Rashi

<AIBlufSummary>
Vrishchika Rashi (Scorpio) is the eighth sign of the Vedic zodiac, a fixed water sign ruled by **[Mars (Mangal)](../03_Planets/0303_Mars.mdx)**. It represents the transformative cycle of life, death, and rebirth, along with deep emotional depth and occult knowledge. Symbolized by the scorpion, Vrishchika natives possess intense determination, strategic brilliance, and an intuitive understanding of hidden truths. This sign governs the natural 8th house (Randhra Bhava), relating to deep psychological transformation, long-term research, and mystical secrets. While profoundly resilient, Vrishchika natives must manage tendencies toward secrecy, jealousy, and emotional obsession.
</AIBlufSummary>

Ruled by **[Mars (Mangal)](../03_Planets/0303_Mars.mdx)** in its mooltrikona sign, Vrishchika natives navigate life through intense passion and profound psychological insight.
