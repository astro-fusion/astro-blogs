Mercury, the planet of intellect, communication, and adaptability, brings unique results depending on its house placement in the birth chart:

* **Mercury in the First House:** The native is likely to be intelligent, learned, and wealthy, with good communication skills. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060400_mercury_in_Houses)
* **Mercury in the Second House:** The person may have a pleasant voice, be financially prosperous, and knowledgeable, but also potentially deceitful. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060402_Mercury_in_2nd_House)
* **Mercury in the Third House:** This placement suggests courage, strength, and potential for success in writing, publishing, or communication-related fields. The native may have many siblings. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060403_Mercury_in_3rd_House)
* **Mercury in the Fourth House:** The person might enjoy domestic happiness, own vehicles and property, and be well-educated, but could also be prone to worry and anxiety. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060404_Mercury_in_4th_House)
* **Mercury in the Fifth House:** This position can indicate intelligence, good children, and success in education and speculation. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060405_Mercury_in_5th_House)
* **Mercury in the Sixth House:** The individual may face health challenges, debts, and enmity with others, but also possess strong analytical and problem-solving skills. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060406_Mercury_in_6th_House)
* **Mercury in the Seventh House:** This placement can suggest a happy marriage, a learned and intelligent spouse, and success in business partnerships. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060407_Mercury_in_7th_House)
* **Mercury in the Eighth House:** The person might have a sharp intellect, an interest in occult subjects, and potential for inheritance, but could also face health issues and a shorter lifespan. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060408_Mercury_in_8th_House)
* **Mercury in the Ninth House:** This position indicates good fortune, religious inclination, higher education, and a supportive father. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060409_Mercury_in_9th_House)
* **Mercury in the Tenth House:** The individual is likely to be successful in their career, particularly in fields related to communication, intellect, or business. They may attain a high social status. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060410_Mercury_in_10th_House)
* **Mercury in the Eleventh House:** This placement suggests financial gains, fulfillment of desires, and a large social circle. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060411_Mercury_in_11th_House)
* **Mercury in the Twelfth House:** The person might experience losses, expenditures, and separation from loved ones. They may also have a keen imagination and interest in spiritual matters. [Read more](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0604_Mercury_in_Houses/060412_Mercury_in_12th_House)

> **Note:** These are general interpretations. The actual effects of Mercury in a specific house will depend on various other factors in the birth chart, such as aspects from other planets, sign placement, and planetary strength.
