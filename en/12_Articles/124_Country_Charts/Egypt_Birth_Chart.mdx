# Egypt Birth Chart: Aries Rising and the 18 June or 19 June Trap

Most astrologers who publish an Egypt chart use the proclamation of the republic on 18 June 1953. The time comes from Campion: 11:30 pm GMT on 18 June, which is 01:30 on 19 June in Cairo. So the same chart carries two dates, and sources quote either one. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole-sign) it has Aries rising and the Moon in Leo, in Uttara Phalguni. Confidence is medium: the time is traditional rather than documented, and almost no Vedic astrologer has published a chart for Egypt.

The 1953 republic chart is the most cited. The 1922 charts rest on one Western source and one unnamed Vedic forum post with no reasoning and no replies. The research rates that Vedic post as low in reliability. Hale quotes an ascendant of 29 degrees of Aries for c1, which matches the tropical figure below.

Two traps matter here.

**The date.** Campion's time is 11:30 pm GMT on 18 June. Cairo was two hours ahead of GMT, so the same instant is 01:30 on 19 June. A page that shows 18 June and a page that shows 19 June can be the same chart. Whether summer time applied in Cairo in June 1953 was not verified in the research, and the engine used +02:00. AstrologyKing's page also mistypes the year as 1956.

**The event.** For 1922, the choices are the Commons decision on 14 March, the accession of King Fuad on 15 March and the unilateral declaration on 28 February. Wikipedia also shows 22 February in one place and 28 February in another. For 1953, the Revolutionary Command Council proclaimed the republic on 18 June and Naguib became the first president.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, and it is worded as tradition.

Aries rising is read as a state that acts first and asks later. The lagna lord is Mars, and Mars is in Gemini in the 3rd house, together with the Sun and Mercury. The 3rd house governs effort, courage, communication and short journeys. The tradition reads a lagna lord there as an identity built through initiative, argument and the written and spoken word. The Sun is in the same sign, close to Mars (within about 6 degrees), which is read as strong will and a leader-led state. That suits a republic proclaimed by a council of officers, as a symbol and no more.

Venus sits in the 1st house in Aries, in Bharani. A benefic on the ascendant is read as a sense of beauty and attachment to the body of the state: the culture, the monuments and the pride in an old civilisation. Jupiter is in the 2nd house in Taurus, in Rohini, in the house of wealth and treasury. That is read as a store of resources, in this case land, grain and the river.

The Moon is in Leo in the 5th house, in Uttara Phalguni, at 28.5 degrees, close to the end of the sign. The 5th is the house of the public mood, creativity and the next generation. It is read as a nation with pride in its own history. The Sun squares the Moon, 5.7 degrees apart, which is read as friction between rulers and the public.

The 10th house is Capricorn, and Rahu sits in it. Saturn, the 10th lord, is in the 6th house in Virgo. The 6th is the house of debt, service, disputes and adversaries. In tradition the 10th lord in the 6th speaks to a government that is busy with service and conflict, and Rahu in the 10th to an unusual or unorthodox path to power. Ketu sits in the 4th in Cancer, which is read as a detached or unsettled relation to the home ground.

None of this is evidence. The chart is traditional and its time is a quoted figure.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses are:

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
- economic crisis: 6, 8, 12, or Saturn
- death of a leader: 8, 10
- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10
- territorial change: 4, 7, 12

Only events dated to the day are scored. The maximum is 20 points for ten events. The facts below follow the research file and carry its sources.

## What this chart cannot tell you

- The time is traditional. It is quoted from Campion in GMT, and the research could not confirm the source record or whether summer time applied in 1953.
- The date appears as 18 June or 19 June depending on the time zone. Check the zone before comparing two pages.
- No Vedic source treats the 1953 chart. The only Vedic source we found is an anonymous post for a 1922 date.
- Egypt has more than one plausible birth: 1922 independence, 1953 republic and, for the union with Syria in 1958, a further start. Each belongs to a different political structure.
- A chart that scores 17 of 20 on ten chosen events does not predict the next ten.
