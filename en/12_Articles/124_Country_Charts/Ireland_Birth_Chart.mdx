# Ireland Birth Chart: Scorpio Rising and the 23:00 or Midnight Doubt

Every astrologer page we opened treats the Republic of Ireland Act coming into force in April 1949 as Ireland's birth, but they do not agree on the time. Jessica Adams quotes Campion's Book of World Horoscopes: 17 April 1949 at 11:00 pm in Dublin. Bill Sheeran's list gives 00:00 on 18 April, Irish summer time. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole-sign) the 23:00 chart has Scorpio rising and the Moon in Sagittarius, in Mula. Confidence is medium: the sources agree on the event, but the 23:00 or 00:00 question is unresolved, and no Vedic source was found.

All four pages opened agree that 1949 is the republic, although Cosmologer prefers the signing of 21 December 1948. Only Adams gives Campion's time and place. Adams also says, in a reply to a reader, that both charts work and that she is familiar with the 1919 chart too. No chart data came with that remark. The research notes other possible births: the 1916 Rising, the 1921 Treaty, the Free State of 6 December 1922 and the 1937 Constitution.

**The 23:00 versus 00:00 question.** The research file describes it this way. The Republic of Ireland Act came into force at midnight, 17 to 18 April 1949. Campion's version is 11:00 pm in Dublin on 17 April. Sheeran's version of the same moment is 00:00 BST on 18 April. Ireland was on summer time, one hour ahead of GMT, so 00:00 BST on the 18th is 23:00 GMT on the 17th. Campion's "11.00pm in Dublin" may therefore be GMT and not the Dublin clock. The blog says only "11.00pm in Dublin". If 23:00 is GMT, c1 and c2 are the same moment, and c2 is the correct form of it.

We could not settle this. The engine computed c1 as 23:00 on the Dublin clock, which is 22:00 UTC, and c2 as 00:00 BST, which is 23:00 UTC. The two are one hour apart. They give different ascendants, as shown below.

Other traps: Ireland used Dublin Mean Time, 25 minutes behind GMT, until October 1916, so the 12:25 GMT of the Rising chart is local noon. Ireland used GMT from October 1916.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each timed chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses are:

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
- economic crisis: 6, 8, 12, or Saturn
- death of a leader: 8, 10
- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10

Only events dated to the day are scored, and only events after the chart date count. For the 1949 charts, that leaves five events. The facts below follow the research file and carry its sources.
