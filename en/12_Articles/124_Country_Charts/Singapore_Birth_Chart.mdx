There is also a time-zone trap. According to Wikipedia's [Time in Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Singapore), Singapore used +07:30 from 12 September 1945 to 31 December 1981, and moved to +08:00 on 1 January 1982. A modern +08:00 chart is half an hour out. We use +07:30. Some astrologers might prefer the 1963 Malaysia merger or the 1959 self-government as the founding, but we found no source for either.
