# China Birth Chart: Capricorn Rising on 1 October 1949, 15:15

The chart astrologers use for the People's Republic of China is the proclamation by Mao Zedong at Tiananmen in Beijing on 1 October 1949. The date is near-unanimous. The clock time is not: 15:15 is the most common, 15:01 comes from ceremony video, and either way Capricorn rises in the sidereal zodiac, at 12.8 degrees for 15:15. We rate confidence as medium. This page covers the PRC only, not the Republic of China of 1912.

The research records that the Western sources are split three to one for 15:15 over 15:01, and that the one Vedic source with a stated time (Astrogle) uses 15:01:36. The research lists 15:15 as the consensus and 15:01:36 as the Vedic preference.

The evidence differs in quality. Murphy says Charles Carter, from Campion's Book of World Horoscopes, gave 15:15 and notes that 15:01 would move the ascendant and Midheaven by about five degrees. Goddard treats 15:15 as the government chart and suspects it may be a recorded time. Partridge says his 15:01:36 comes from video tape and Xinhua accounts as interpreted by Guanghui Zhou, and that he checked it against the chart of China's first nuclear test. That check is his own claim and we did not repeat it. Murphy cites Van Kevin Golay for a ceremony start at 15:00 and the proclamation at 15:01. The research calls 15:01:36 the better documented time. In our engine the two sidereal ascendants differ by 4.4 degrees (Capricorn 12.8 and 8.4). The research note gave about 3.5 degrees; we report the engine's figure.

Goddard alone treats 21 September 1949 as the chart for the country. The CPPCC opening session of that day is where the new state was proclaimed, and Goddard's 10:24 is his own rectification, tested, he says, against the Great Leap Forward and Mao's death. No recorded time exists for it.

Two traps. The first is the time zone. China officially used +08:00 for Beijing, but Wikipedia's [Time in China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_China) page says the national standard in autumn 1949 is itself disputed (27 September to 6 October 1949), and the page calls the adoption date dubious. Beijing's local mean time is +07:46. We used +08:00; 15:15 Beijing is 07:15 UTC. The second is the other "China" charts. The Republic of China of 1 January 1912 and the 1911 Wuchang uprising are not the PRC. The Sanjay Rath [page](https://srath.com/jyoti%e1%b9%a3a/mundane/china/first-republic-of-china/) covers the first Republic only and has no chart data.

## What the chart says about the country

Capricorn rises at 12.8 degrees, with the Moon in the first house at 10.0 in Shravana. The ascendant is read as the nation. Capricorn is read as discipline, hierarchy, endurance and a long view. The Moon on the ascendant is read as a public that is closely bound up with the state's identity. The Moon rules the 7th house, which is read as the country's relations with others, so the national identity and the foreign-relations house are tied together.

Saturn, the lagna lord, is in Leo in the 8th house, in Purva Phalguni. The 8th is read as sudden change, shared resources and transformation. A lagna lord there is classically read as a state that has gone through large reversals, and that rebuilds. Venus, ruler of the 10th and the 5th, is in Libra, its own sign, in the 10th house, in Vishakha. A 10th lord in its own sign and house is the strongest placement for government in this tradition: a state whose organisation, and whose public image, is solid and deliberate.

The 9th house contains three bodies: the Sun at 14.6, Mercury at 20.0 and Ketu at 23.8, all in Virgo. Mercury rules Virgo and is in its own sign. The Sun here is the lord of the 8th, and Mercury is lord of the 6th and 9th. The 9th is read as law, belief, learning and foreign trade. Three planets together there are read as a society in which doctrine, administration and the written word play a large part, with Ketu adding detachment. This is the tradition's reading, not a statement about any people.

Mars is in Cancer in the 7th, the classical weak (debilitated) Mars, in Ashlesha. It rules the 4th and the 11th. The 7th is read as relations with other nations and open conflict. Jupiter is in Sagittarius in the 12th house at 29.4 degrees, at the very end of the sign. The 12th is read as distant lands and hidden matters. Rahu is in Pisces in the 3rd. As always, these are descriptions of emphasis.

K. N. Rao, according to the research, reads the 2008 republic chart of Nepal as parallel to this chart. Both have Capricorn rising in our engine.

## Testing the chart against history

The scoring rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, counted whole-sign from the Vedic ascendant, or if it is the named planet. The houses are: war 6, 7, 8 or Mars; leader death 8, 10; natural disaster 4, 8, 12; pandemic 6, 8, 12. Only events dated to the day, with a rule, are scored. The Great Chinese Famine and the WTO accession are dated only by year, the Deng Xiaoping reforms only by month, and the Cultural Revolution, the Nixon visit and the 1989 crackdown have no rule for their kind of event, so none of these is scored.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The time is not settled. 15:15 and 15:01 differ by 14 minutes and 4.4 degrees of sidereal ascendant. The 15:01 time is supported by video and Xinhua accounts, but the 15:15 figure traces to Carter and Campion and its original source is unknown. Even the time zone for that autumn is disputed. All of this could move the ascendant, the Moon's nakshatra and every dasha date.

China also has a long history that begins long before 1949, and the 1949 chart is the chart of one state. The research found only six pages, and Goddard's 21 September chart is a single author's rectification. The test uses six events and a rule that gives a random chart 6.7 of 12 points. It cannot decide between the candidates.
