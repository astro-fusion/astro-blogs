# Sweden Birth Chart: No Time for 1523, So We Built 1865

The birth chart of Sweden is most often cast for the election of Gustav Vasa as king at Strangnas on 6 June 1523 (Julian calendar), which is Sweden's National Day. That date has no recorded time, so it has no ascendant, and it is older than 1700, so our engine did not compute it, as with every pre-1700 candidate in this series. The chart sections below are therefore built on a candidate: the 1865 constitution chart, 7 December 1865, 15:20 in Stockholm, from Nicholas Campion. It gives Gemini rising in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri). Research confidence for Sweden is low.

Every source is Western. The 1523 date is the consensus because it is the one used by most of them, and because it is the National Day. But the only time anyone gives for it is one rectification, 10:55, by Ivan Wilhelm, and the original time is unknown. Wikipedia confirms that the election took place in Strangnas in June 1523 ([Gustav Vasa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Vasa), [National Day of Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Day_of_Sweden)).

The research found no Vedic source it could read. A Medium post, "Sweden's Horoscope and Transition", returned an error, and a search summary placed its Sun in Sagittarius. We could not verify that, so we do not rely on it. Other foundations were also mentioned in the research without a chart: 970, 1319 and 1809. The 1809 constitution had two possible moments, 6 June and 27 June.

There are traps. The 1523 date is Julian: the Gregorian date is 16 June 1523, and some sites list 6 June without converting. The research also found Sun degrees in Gemini that differ between sites, about 15 against 24 in search summaries, which fits calendar and time differences. Before 1879 Sweden had only local mean time, and Stockholm local mean time is 1 hour 12 minutes ahead of Greenwich, so the 1865 chart uses +01:12. The 1865 time itself is 15:20 in Campion and about 15:30 on the Swedish site.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each computed candidate against dated events. Rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the relevant houses (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. Economic crisis uses houses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. Leader death uses 8 and 10. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Territorial change uses 4, 7 and 12. Only events dated to the day are scored, and events before a chart's date have no period in it. Eight events from 1632 to 1814 come before 1865, and the 1905 and 1932 events come before 1974. That leaves only four events for the 1865 chart and two for the 1974 chart. "Chance" is what a random pair of periods would score on the same events with the same chart.
