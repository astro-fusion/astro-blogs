# Kazakhstan Birth Chart: Cancer Rising, and the 14:00 Question

Kazakhstan's birth chart is cast for 16 December 1991, when the Supreme Council adopted the constitutional law on state independence. The date and the place, Almaty, are agreed. The time is the problem. One source gives 14:00 Universal Time, which is 19:00 in Almaty and gives Cancer rising. Another gives 14:00 with no zone, which, read as local time, gives Aries rising. We use the first as the lead chart, because it is the research consensus, and we rate the confidence as low.

The same "14:00" read as local time gives Aries rising (21.1 degrees). Against seven dated events the UT chart scored 4 out of 14, below the chance figure of 6.7, and the local-time chart scored 13 out of 14, against 8.8. All the sources that name an event use the 16 December 1991 independence law and Almaty. The disagreement is about the clock. The Artley blog says the chart is listed by Russian astrologers at 14:00 UT. GaneshaSpeaks prints 14:00 and does not say whether it means local time or UT. These may be the same figure read two ways. If so, the two charts are five hours apart. Optimesia's author says the date is clearly correct but that no validation of the time was done, and the page does not say which time it uses. We do not know which of the two charts the author used. Future Point's 25 December chart has no event and no reasoning, and it is probably a template.

The time-zone trap is in the conversion. Almaty was on +05:00 on 16 December 1991. Both the [tz database](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eggert/tz/main/asia) (Asia/Almaty is +05:00 from 31 March 1991 to 19 January 1992 at 02:00, then +06:00) and Wikipedia's [Time in Kazakhstan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Kazakhstan) agree, though that page marks Almaty's 1991 entry as inferred. There is no summer time in December. A chart made with the modern +06:00 for Almaty would be an hour off. A second question is Almaty against Astana as the place, and we use Almaty.

We rate the confidence as low. Only four pages opened, only one of them is a substantive Vedic page, and the time is unvalidated by the one author who comments on it.

## What the chart says about the country

Cancer rises, so the Moon is the lagna lord. It sits in Pisces in the 9th house, in Revati, the last nakshatra of the zodiac, at 26.1 degrees near the end of the sign. The tradition reads the Moon as the people and the 9th as law, belief and fortune, and Pisces is the sign of Jupiter, the planet of law. A lagna lord there is read as an identity formed through belief, distance and a long road. Revati is read as the nakshatra of journeys' ends. These are classical readings, offered as description.

Three planets are in their own signs. Mars, which rules the 5th and 10th houses, is in Scorpio in the 5th, in Jyeshtha, with Mercury. Mars rules the 5th and the 10th for Cancer rising, which the tradition treats as a strong placement for government and policy. Venus is in Libra in the 4th, its own sign, and rules the 4th and 11th houses. Saturn is in Capricorn in the 7th, also its own sign, and rules the 7th and 8th. The tradition reads a chart with several planets in their own signs as stable in form.

The 10th house is Aries and is empty, and its lord is Mars in the 5th. That links the country's public standing to policy and intelligence, and to the sign of Mars with Mercury. Jupiter in Leo in the 2nd house rules the 6th and 9th houses and is read as a link between resources, speech and law. The Sun is in the 6th with Rahu, in Sagittarius, in Mula and Purva Ashadha. The 6th is the house of service, debt and disputes, and Rahu there is read as a theme of outside pressures that the country manages through effort. Ketu in the 12th, in Gemini, is read as a theme of detachment from the foreign.

All of this depends on the time. If the true chart is c2, the lagna is Aries, the Moon is in the 12th, and the reading changes.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The time is the whole question. No source records the hour of the vote, and the two sources that give a time may be the same figure read two ways. Until a documented time appears, the ascendant is open between Cancer and Aries, and the house placements with it.

The scoring is too small to decide. Seven events, mostly constitutional, gave a large gap, but so few events leave plenty of room for chance. Almaty and Astana are two possible places, and the capital moved in 1997. We use Almaty because the sources do.
