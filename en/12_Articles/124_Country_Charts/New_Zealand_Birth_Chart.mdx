# New Zealand Birth Chart: Aries Rising at Waitangi, Time Disputed

The birth chart of New Zealand is most often cast for the first signing of the Treaty of Waitangi on 6 February 1840, at 10:52 local mean time at Waitangi. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri) that gives Aries rising, at 1.9 degrees, with the Sun in Capricorn and the Moon in Aquarius. Confidence is low. Only two astrologers' pages could be read, they disagree on the hour (10:52 against 13:00), and the 1907 Dominion chart is a real rival.

The research found no Vedic source for New Zealand, so the sidereal reading is our own computation. Astrotheme has a page for Auckland that appeared only in a search summary, and it is not used. Adams says there are four possible New Zealand charts. Ibell calls the 1907 Dominion chart the most publicised one, but says the Treaty chart is increasingly used by New Zealand astrologers and is the better choice in his view. That is why the Treaty chart is the lead here, with low confidence: it rests on two readable authors.

The date of the Treaty and the place are not in dispute. The time is. Ibell's 10:52 comes from the astrologer Richard Paul, after talks with the historian Claudia Orange and a rectification. Adams uses 13:00 as a convention for "signing began that afternoon". A reader of Adams's post suggested 10:17, which is unsourced and not counted as a candidate. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_Waitangi) says about 45 chiefs were waiting that morning, a ship with officers arrived around noon and the Governor came afterwards. So a signing time after noon is also plausible, and 10:52 is not documented.

The 1907 Dominion chart has its own split. The 11:00 time is the proclamation on the steps of Parliament, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominion_of_New_Zealand) and Ibell. Campion's book is reported to print midnight instead. The two give very different charts, shown below.

There is a clock trap. Before 1868 New Zealand had no standard time, so the 1840 charts use local mean time at Waitangi, +11:36. From 1868 the country used New Zealand Mean Time, +11:30, so we used that for 1907. Ibell's caption labels the 1907 chart "LMT, Wellington", but true local mean time would be +11:39 and make the clock time about 10:51. We read it as a software label and the 11 am as a clock time, which we could not verify. Daylight saving did not exist in either year.

## What the chart says about the country

Aries rising is read as a young, direct, pioneering nation. The ascendant is at 1.9 degrees, so it is near the start of the sign, and an earlier time would put the rising sign in Pisces. The lagna lord, Mars, is in Aquarius in the 11th house, in Shatabhisha, with the Moon and Rahu. The 11th is the house of gains, groups and networks, and the lord of the self sitting there is read as an identity formed through community and shared aims. Mars also rules the 8th, the house of sudden change and shared resources.

The 10th house, Capricorn, holds the Sun and Mercury. The 10th house lord is Saturn, which sits in Scorpio in the 8th house at 27.8 degrees, in Jyeshtha, late in the sign. Saturn also rules the 11th. So government is tied to the house of endurance and change, and to the house of gains. Mercury, which rules the 3rd and 6th, joins the Sun in the 10th. The Sun rules the 5th, and with Mercury in the house of public standing it is read as a government that speaks, writes and administers. We describe the pattern and do not claim it explains events.

The Moon, which stands for the people, is in Aquarius in the 11th house, in Purva Bhadrapada. The Moon rules the 4th house of land and home, so land and home are tied to the house of groups. Venus, lord of the 2nd and 7th, is in Sagittarius in the 9th, the house of law and belief. Jupiter, lord of the 9th and 12th, is in Libra in the 7th, in Vishakha, the house of partners and foreign dealings. Ketu is in Leo in the 5th. All of this is description in the language of the tradition, not a forecast.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each candidate against dated events. Rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the relevant houses (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Natural disaster uses 4, 8 and 12. Only events dated to the day are scored. Four events (the 1935 Labour election, the 1951 ANZUS treaty, the 1985 sinking of the Rainbow Warrior and the 2019 Christchurch shootings) have a kind ("other") with no rule, so they are not scored. "Chance" is what a random pair of periods would score on the same events with the same chart.

With eight events and generous rules, differences this small are not evidence.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The date of the Treaty is agreed, but the hour is not, and Wikipedia's account of the day makes a time after noon plausible, which the lead chart does not use. The ascendant at 1.9 degrees of Aries is close to the start of the sign, so a small change in time moves it. The research found only two readable astrologers for New Zealand, and no Vedic source. The Treaty is also not the only founding: 1907, 1853 and 1769 all have advocates, and the Dominion chart is called the most publicised. Eight events is a short test. A chart cannot say what will happen to a nation or the people in it.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is New Zealand's rising sign?**
It depends on the chart. The 10:52 Treaty chart gives Aries 1.9 in the sidereal zodiac and Aries 23.5 in the tropical. The 13:00 chart gives Aries 28.1 sidereal and Taurus 19.8 tropical. The 11:00 Dominion chart gives Sagittarius 19.1 sidereal. No time is documented, so no rising sign is certain.

**What is New Zealand's zodiac sign?**
On 6 February 1840 the tropical Sun is in Aquarius at 16.3. The sidereal Sun (Lahiri) is in Capricorn at 24.7, in Dhanishta.

**What is New Zealand's Moon sign?**
In the sidereal chart the Moon is in Aquarius in Purva Bhadrapada in both Treaty charts: 24.0 degrees at 10:52 and 25.2 degrees at 13:00. In the tropical chart at 10:52 the Moon is in Pisces at 15.6.

**Which dasha is New Zealand running in 2026?**
On the lead chart it is the Venus mahadasha (12 April 2014 to 12 April 2034) and the Jupiter antardasha (11 June 2024 to 10 February 2027). The other candidates give different periods.

**Which New Zealand chart is best?**
None is proved. The 10:52 Treaty chart is the lead because its author calls it increasingly used, the 13:00 chart scores higher in our test, and the 1907 Dominion chart is the most publicised.
