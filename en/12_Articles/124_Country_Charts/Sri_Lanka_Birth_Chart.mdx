# Sri Lanka Birth Chart: Capricorn Rising, and One Source Behind It All

The Sri Lanka birth chart most astrologers use is cast for independence from Britain on 4 February 1948, at 06:00 in Colombo. It gives a Capricorn ascendant at 12.6 degrees and the Moon in Scorpio in Anuradha. The date is agreed. The time is not recorded anywhere we could find, and the three astrologer pages that use 06:00 all trace to a single database entry. We rate the confidence as low.

The disagreement is about the clock, not the date. The Vedic pages use 06:00 and cite the Astro-Databank entry. Our researcher could not open astro.com, so that entry itself was not read. None of the pages explains the choice of 06:00, and the historical sources we read give no clock time for independence. They say only that bells pealed and the flag was raised in the morning. So 06:00 is a convention, not a documented moment. Campion is reported to read independence as granted at 00:00, "in an eastern form" still used by politicians.

The main finding is that the three Vedic sources are not independent. Three web pages agreeing looks like consensus, but they share one parent. We also found no Sri Lankan astrologer publishing their own national chart, though we searched in English only.

There is a time-zone trap too. Ceylon time in 1948 is believed to have been +05:30, but we did not confirm that from a dated source. Sri Lanka used +06:00 and +06:30 at other times, which would shift the ascendant. We use +05:30 throughout.

## What the chart says about the country

Capricorn rises, so Saturn is the lagna lord. Saturn sits in the 7th house in Cancer, retrograde, in Ashlesha. The tradition reads the 7th as the house of partners, treaties and the outside world. A lagna lord there is read as a country whose identity is shaped through its relations with others. Saturn in Cancer is also in the sign opposite its own, which classical texts treat as a place of strain. That is a description of the tradition's reading, not evidence about any event.

The Sun, in Capricorn at 21 degrees in Shravana, is in the 1st house with the ascendant. This is read as a strong sense of national authority and a leadership that carries the state's image. The Moon, as the significator of the people, is in Scorpio in Anuradha in the 11th, the house of gains and networks. Scorpio is the sign of the Moon's debilitation in classical texts. Jupiter shares the sign at 28.8 degrees in Jyeshtha, a late degree. A Moon and Jupiter pair in the 11th is read as a popular mood tied to hopes and collective ambitions.

The 10th house, the house of government and standing, is Libra, and it holds Ketu in Vishakha. Its lord, Venus, is in Aquarius in the 2nd with Mercury. Classically, Ketu in the 10th is read as detachment from office, with authority exercised without attachment to its outward trappings. Venus and Mercury in the 2nd link the state's finances and trade with its speech and bookkeeping.

Mars, the planet of conflict, is retrograde in Leo in the 8th, the house of sudden change and what is hidden. Rahu is in the 4th, the house of the homeland and domestic peace. Traditional readers take the 8th and the 4th together as a country whose internal stability is the sensitive point. We state this only as what the tradition says about these placements. It is not a claim that the chart caused or foretold anything.

- War: houses 6, 7, 8, or Mars.

## What this chart cannot tell you

There is no recorded time. Everything that depends on the ascendant, which includes every house placement and the dasha dates, rests on a convention that a single database entry has passed on. Moving the time by a few hours changes the rising sign. The time zone for 1948 is itself unconfirmed.

The independence moment is also contested in kind. A ceremony in the morning and a legal effect at midnight are both reasonable readings of "independence". The 1972 republic is a second founding. Sri Lanka has a long constitutional history, and astrologers do not agree on which moment is the country's start.

The scores are weak. Ten events cannot separate charts, and the lead chart scored below chance. Treat this page as a description of one candidate chart, not a diagnosis of the country.
