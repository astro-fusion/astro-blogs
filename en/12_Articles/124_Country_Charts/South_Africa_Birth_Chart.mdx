# South Africa Birth Chart: Gemini Rising for 27 April 1994

The South Africa chart most used is the start of the new constitution and the first multiracial elections on 27 April 1994, at 10:59 am in Pretoria. It is published on the Astrodienst national-chart pages, in a text by Liz Greene from 1999. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole-sign) it has Gemini rising and the Moon in Scorpio, in Anuradha. Confidence is low: only two astrologer pages were opened, they give different times for the same date, and no Vedic astrologer source was found. Only four events after April 1994 could be scored, which is too few to prove anything either way.

The Astrodienst page says "Data used: 27 April 1994, 10.59 am, Pretoria". It does not say where 10:59 comes from. Greene's text notes the Sun in Taurus opposite Jupiter in Scorpio and the Moon with Pluto in Scorpio, which matches the tropical positions below. Javed labels his chart "00:00 EET, Pretoria". The midnight time is a convention and has no recorded basis.

The Astrodienst page also names earlier "births", the 1652 settlement at Table Bay and the Union of 31 May 1910, but gives no chart for either. We found no chart with a time for the 1910 Union or the 1961 Republic. Two other pages we opened, [Astrococktail](https://astrococktail.com/Celestial%20Configurations%20of%20Africa.html) and [Paul Saunders](https://paulsaundersastrology.blogspot.com/search/label/Africa), had no national chart for South Africa.

Two traps matter here.

**The event.** South Africa can be dated at 1652, at the Union of 1910, at the Republic of 31 May 1961 or at the new constitution of 27 April 1994. Only the last has a published chart, and it marks the start of the present constitutional order.

**The clock label.** Javed's page says EET and Astrodienst gives no zone. Pretoria is on South African Standard Time, which is also UTC+02:00, and there was no summer time in April 1994, so the label changes nothing. The real difference between the two charts is the time of day, ten hours and 59 minutes.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, worded as tradition. Because the time is not documented, the ascendant and houses are a working frame.

Gemini rising is read as a state of many voices, languages and trades. The lagna lord is Mercury, in the 11th house in Aries, in Ashwini, with the Sun. The 11th is the house of gains, networks and the fulfilment of hopes. The Sun and Mercury are 3.5 degrees apart, which the tradition reads as a mind closely tied to public authority and to a shared public project. Venus and Ketu sit together in Taurus in the 12th house, the house of loss, retreat and foreign lands.

The Moon is in Scorpio in Anuradha, in the 6th house, with Rahu, 3.9 degrees apart. The 6th house is the house of service, debt, disease and disputes, and the tradition counts it a place where hardship is worked through over time. Moon with Rahu is read as an unsettled public mood, strong feeling and unusual alliances. Anuradha is a nakshatra classically tied to friendship and to unity across differences.

The 10th house is Pisces, and Mars sits in it, in Uttara Bhadrapada. Its lord is Jupiter, retrograde in the 5th house in Libra. The tradition reads a Mars in the 10th as an assertive, sometimes forceful state and a government shaped by struggle. Jupiter in the 5th is read as a hopeful public mood and an emphasis on the next generation and on education. Jupiter stands opposite the Sun, 3.3 degrees apart. Jupiter and Saturn make a trine only 0.3 degrees from exact. Saturn is in the 9th house in Aquarius, in Shatabhisha, the house of law and belief, which is read as a country whose law, constitution and courts are central to its identity.

None of this is evidence. The chart's time is not documented, and only four events could test it.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses used here are:

- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10
- pandemic: 6, 8, 12

Only events dated to the day are scored, and a chart cannot be tested on events before its own date. That rules out the research file's seven earlier events, among them Sharpeville, the 1961 Republic, the Soweto uprising and the 1990 unbanning of the ANC. Four events remain, for a maximum of 8 points.

We cannot call either chart confirmed. Confidence stays low.
