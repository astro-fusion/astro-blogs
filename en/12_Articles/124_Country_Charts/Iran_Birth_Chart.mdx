# Iran Birth Chart: Leo Rising on 1 April 1979, and an Unsettled Time

The birth chart of the Islamic Republic of Iran is most often cast for 1 April 1979, about 15:00 in Tehran, the day the result of the referendum was announced and the Republic proclaimed. That gives Leo rising at 6.0 degrees in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole sign) and the Moon in Taurus in Rohini. Confidence in the date is medium and in the hour is low, because no source we read says where the afternoon time comes from. Other astrologers use 00:01 on the same day, the revolution of 11 February 1979, or the return of Ayatollah Khomeini on 1 February 1979.

The dispute is about which event counts as the birth. Rath argues the revolution happened on 11 February and the Republic is a separate chart. Khomeini landed in Tehran around 9 am on 1 February. The Islamic Republic was proclaimed after the referendum of 30 and 31 March; Wikipedia's [referendum page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1979_Iranian_Islamic_Republic_referendum) says the result was reported on 1 April and the declaration appears in reports dated 2 April, so even that date is not entirely clean.

The research counts six of nine pages by date for 1 April, which is why the date confidence is medium. The two times used for 1 April, about 15:00 and 00:01, come from sources that state no provenance. Astrotheme's AA rating notwithstanding, 00:01 is a start-of-day convention and not a recorded moment. Tempora flags that the lagna is sensitive to plus or minus 60 minutes. One astrologer changed charts: Jessica Adams used the February return in 2018 and 00:01 on 1 April in 2026. The poli-sci blog says "15:00 (IST)", which is not reconciled with Tehran time. Iran Standard Time was +03:30 on all the dates here, according to the time-zone database. The Persian date for 1 April is 12 Farvardin 1358, and every source gives Gregorian dates, so there is no calendar trap.

## What the chart says about the country

Leo rising is read as a nation with a strong sense of authority and dignity, one that wants a visible centre of command. The ascendant lord, the Sun, is in Pisces in the 8th house in Revati, with Mars and Mercury. Classical texts treat the 8th as the house of transformation, hidden matters and sudden change. A stellium of three planets there, and the lord of the lagna among them, is read as a country whose identity has been shaped by upheaval and by what is not said in public. This describes the tradition, not any group of people.

Saturn and Rahu are both in Leo in the 1st house. Saturn is retrograde. Saturn in the ascendant is read as seriousness, restraint, endurance and a long memory, and Rahu in the ascendant as intensity and a focus on being seen as different. The Moon in Taurus in Rohini in the 10th house is a placement tradition treats as strong. Taurus is the sign where the Moon is exalted. In the 10th house it is read as a public that is stable, attached to its land and its customs, and visible in how the country is governed.

The 7th house, which stands for partners and open rivals, is Aquarius, and holds Venus and Ketu. The 7th lord is Saturn, in the 1st. Tradition reads that as a country defined strongly by its relationships with others, close to the way it defines itself. Jupiter is in Cancer in the 12th house, in Pushya. The 12th house is foreign lands, expense and retreat. Jupiter is exalted in Cancer, so Jupiter's themes of faith, law and teaching are read as running through the unseen and the foreign part of national life.

The Western chart gives a different emphasis. The Sun in Aries and the Moon in Gemini describe a different temperament from the Vedic reading, and the Ascendant at Leo 29.5 is close to a sign boundary. These readings are conventions. They are not findings.

## Testing the chart against history

We scored each timed chart with rules fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to the kind of event (whole sign, from the Vedic ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Assassination or death of a leader uses houses 8 and 10. Natural disaster uses houses 4, 8 and 12. Only events dated to the day are scored; here the scorable events are six, so the maximum is 12. "Chance" is the average score of random periods on the same events.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The Iranian state does not have a single birth moment. February 1979 had a revolution and a return, and April 1979 had a referendum and a proclamation. The afternoon time has no documented source. Each chart assigns the Moon and the angles differently, and the angles are what the reading depends on.

A six-event test is too small to rank charts, and the largest score gaps here are about one and a half points. The chart cannot tell you what will happen in a country, and nothing here is a judgement on any government, group or conflict. Some sources we could not open (Beliefnet, Astro-Databank) may hold more data.
