# Poland Birth Chart: Scorpio Rising, 14 November 1918, Low Confidence

The birth chart of Poland is most often cast for 14 November 1918, at 10:30 in Warsaw, the day the Regency Council dissolved and Jozef Pilsudski was asked to run the country provisionally. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri) that gives Scorpio rising, at 26.2 degrees, with the Sun in Libra and the Moon in Pisces. Confidence is low: sources agree that autumn 1918 is the birth of the Second Republic, but they split on the day (10, 11 or 14 November), and none gives a documented time.

The research found no Vedic source, and Polish-language searches found no national chart. So the sidereal reading here is our own computation, not an established practice. Jessica Adams advises running candidate charts against known events such as 3 September 1939 to choose between them ([her Russia chart post](https://www.jessicaadams.com/2022/03/13/blog/the-russia-astrology-chart/)). We did that, with more events, below.

The split on the day is the heart of the dispute. 14 November 1918 is the day power was transferred and the republic named, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic). 11 November is the National Independence Day, when the Regency Council handed Pilsudski command of the Polish forces ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Independence_Day_(Poland))). 10 November is the day Pilsudski returned to Warsaw ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B3zef_Pi%C5%82sudski)) and the date Astro-Databank uses. Others used 7 October and 21 October 1918, or the 5 November 1916 act by which the German and Austro-Hungarian emperors proclaimed a Kingdom of Poland.

There is also a clock trap. Warsaw clock time in 1918 was changing: under German occupation it followed Central European Time, and according to the time-zone database it moved to Eastern European Time (+02:00) from September 1918. We used +02:00 for the 1918 charts on that basis. We could not confirm it from a primary source, and Astrotheme does not state its zone. Warsaw local mean time would be +01:24, and the choice moves the ascendant. The 1916 chart uses +01:00 and the 1989 chart +02:00 for summer time, both from the same database.

## Testing the chart against history

A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the relevant houses (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Leader death uses 8 and 10. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Only events dated to the day are scored. The 1956 Poznan protests and the 1980 Gdansk Agreement are dated but their kind ("other") has no rule, so they are not scored.
