## The time-zone trap

Hanoi was on Japanese time, +09:00, under the occupation. According to the [tz database](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eggert/tz/main/asia), a decree by Vo Nguyen Giap (Official Gazette, 29 September 1945) moved clocks back two hours to +07:00. The main tz file puts the change at 24:00 on 1 September 1945, and its [backzone](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eggert/tz/main/backzone) entry for Hanoi shows it at 00:00 on 2 September. Either way, the whole of 2 September 1945 was at +07:00.

So a chart for noon on 2 September 1945 must use +07:00. A source that carried the old +09:00, or an in-between +08:00, would be one or two hours off. From the files, a two-hour move matters a lot here: noon gives Scorpio 12.1 rising and 14:00 gives Sagittarius 9.0. All four charts above use +07:00.

## What the chart says about the country

Scorpio rises, so Mars is the lagna lord. Mars is in Gemini in the 8th house, in Mrigashira, and it is the first of four planets in that sign. Moon, Saturn and Rahu are there too. The tradition reads the 8th as the house of sudden events, crisis, transformation and what is hidden, and a lagna lord there as an identity formed through upheaval. Four planets in the 8th is the strongest feature of this chart, and it is read as a country whose story is defined by strain and renewal. These are classical readings, offered as description, and they depend on a noon time.

The Moon, which signifies the people, is in Gemini in Punarvasu at 24.7 degrees, in the 8th house with Saturn at 28.2. The Moon rules the 9th house (Cancer), so the people's mood is read as tied to belief and the long view, but placed in the house of crisis. Punarvasu is the nakshatra of return, which the tradition connects with recovery after loss. Saturn rules the 3rd and 4th houses for Scorpio rising (Capricorn and Aquarius).

The Sun is in Leo in the 10th house, in Purva Phalguni. Leo is the Sun's own sign, and the Sun rules the 10th, so the lord of government sits in its own house. The tradition reads that as strong emphasis on state authority and public leadership. Venus and Mercury are together in Cancer in the 9th, and Mercury is at 29.4 degrees at the very end of the sign. Venus rules the 7th and 12th houses and Mercury the 8th and 11th. Jupiter in Virgo in the 11th, in Uttara Phalguni, rules the 2nd and 5th houses and is read as support for gains and networks. Ketu is in Sagittarius in the 2nd, in Mula, read as a theme of detachment from accumulated wealth.

## What this chart cannot tell you

No clock time is recorded for the declaration, so noon is a convention, and the rising sign could be Taurus, Scorpio or Sagittarius depending on the time chosen. The Moon's position at noon fixes the dasha dates, and a different time would move them.

We found no Vedic astrologer's chart and no Campion citation, only two Western pages. The choice of 1945, 1954 or 1976 as the founding is open as well. Several scored events are wars.
