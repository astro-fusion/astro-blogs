South Korea's birth chart is cast for the establishment of the government in Seoul on 15 August 1948, at 11:00 local time. Every astrology source we read agrees on the date. The hour is another matter: one source gives 11:00 and two use a noon placeholder. At 11:00 the sidereal ascendant is Virgo; at noon it is Libra. Confidence is medium, and both timed charts score below chance in our test.

A third item, the date alone, is used by [a Korea Times column on Saju](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/lifestyle/others/20180607/381-saju-reading-of-south-korea). Saju is the East Asian Four Pillars system, not zodiac astrology, and it gives no hour. We list it only because it confirms the date. We found no Vedic source for South Korea at all, so the sidereal reading in this article is our computation and not an established Vedic practice.

Astrotheme lists 11:00 and calls the source timed. A search summary we saw said the 11:00 comes from the presidential archives' record of the ceremony, but the Astrotheme page does not say so and we could not confirm it, so treat that as unverified. Stockhorst uses noon because he found no exact time. myAstro's page does not state its zodiac system.

The main trap is the clock. Korea had daylight saving in the summer of 1948 according to the time-zone database, so 11:00 on 15 August was probably +10:00, not the usual +09:00. We used +10:00 for both charts (UTC 1:00 for the 11:00 chart). Astrotheme does not say which offset it used. Its published tropical figures (ascendant 12 Libra 31, Midheaven 14 Cancer 06, Sun 22 Leo 06, Moon 0 Capricorn 04) agree with our engine at +10:00, which suggests it used the same offset. Other founding dates exist but we found no astrologer using them: liberation on 15 August 1945, the constitution of 17 July 1948 and the 10 May 1948 election. North Korea's founding on 9 September 1948 is a different chart and should not be mixed up with this one.

## Testing the chart against history

The rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Leader death uses houses 8 and 10. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Only events dated to the day are scored, and "chance" is what a random pair of periods would score on the same events with the same chart. ## What this chart cannot tell you

The date is certain and the time is not. The 11:00 time rests on one source of rating B, the noon chart is a placeholder, and neither beats chance in our test. The daylight-time offset adds another doubt: with +09:00 the chart would be different. No Vedic astrologer's chart was found, so the sidereal reading has no tradition behind it for this country. The founding of a state can be dated to several moments, and a chart for one of them does not describe the other. It does not describe the people of the peninsula or predict anything about them.
