## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, worded as tradition, of a chart whose event nobody has identified. Read it as an exercise in method, not as a portrait of Oman.

Virgo rising is read as a state of careful, practical habits, tied to trade and service. The lagna lord is Mercury, and Mercury also rules Gemini, the 10th house of government. One planet ruling both the 1st and the 10th is read as a state whose identity and government are closely tied. Mercury is in the 11th house in Cancer, in Punarvasu, with Venus. The 11th is the house of gains, networks and ambitions, and Mercury there is read as a country that gains through trade and contact. Venus in Pushya is in the same house.

The Sun is in the 10th house in Gemini, in Ardra, which is read as a visible and authoritative government. The Moon is in the 9th house in Taurus, in Rohini, the house of fortune, law and religion. The Moon is square Saturn, 3.3 degrees apart.

The striking feature is the 12th house. Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are all in Leo in the 12th, in Purva Phalguni and Magha. The 12th is the house of loss, foreign lands, isolation and outflow. Three planets there are classically read as a state oriented to the outside world and as one that spends effort abroad. Jupiter and Saturn are together, 6.9 degrees apart, and Mars and Rahu are 1.9 degrees from a trine. Jupiter rules the 4th and 7th houses and Saturn the 5th and 6th, so these are not simple placements.

Rahu is in the 8th house in Aries and Ketu is in the 2nd in Libra. The 8th is the house of crises and hidden matters. The 2nd is the house of wealth and speech.

None of this is evidence. A reader should keep in mind that the date has no known event behind it.
