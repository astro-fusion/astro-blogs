**Which date?** Jordan's founding has several dates. Wikipedia's [Treaty of London (1946)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Treaty_of_London_(1946)) says the treaty was signed in London on 22 March 1946 between Britain and the Emir of Transjordan, recognising independence, and took effect on 17 June 1946. The page on the [Emirate of Transjordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emirate_of_Transjordan) and the page on [Abdullah I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_I_of_Jordan) say parliament ratified the treaty and re-designated Abdullah as King on 25 May 1946, and that he was crowned in Amman. That is Jordan's [Independence Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_(Jordan)). The Emirate page also says the mandate ended on 17 June 1946 on the exchange of ratifications, which conflicts with its own infobox date of 25 May. The research file also lists the 1921 Emirate and the 1949 renaming as other candidates.

**Two traps in the lead chart.** First, the treaty was signed in London, but Astrotheme places the chart in Amman. The coordinates and the +02:00 offset (Eastern European Time, with no daylight saving in Jordan before 1973 according to the time-zone database) are those of Amman. A London chart would be cast with a different place and a different clock. Second, Astrotheme rates the data AA, "timed official source", yet shows 12:00 AM. An official time would not usually be midnight, so we treat 00:00 as a placeholder. Astrotheme's own figures agree with the engine on position: its Sun at 0 degrees 41 minutes of Aries matches the engine's 0.7, and its ascendant of 20 degrees 12 minutes of Sagittarius matches the tropical 20.2.
