# Brazil Birth Chart: Aquarius Rising at Ipiranga, Time Unknown

The birth chart of Brazil is usually cast for the Cry of Ipiranga, when Dom Pedro declared independence on 7 September 1822. Historians record only late afternoon, so astrologers use times from 16:08 to 16:58, and the most common is about 16:30. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri) every one of those times gives Aquarius rising, and the Moon is in Taurus in Rohini. Confidence is low: the date is secure, the time is not, and only a few Brazilian events are dated to the day.

The 1822 chart is the main one for Western astrologers. The one Vedic source we found, Astral Harmony, leads with the 1988 Constitution chart and calls the 1822 times controversial. Personare, a Brazilian portal, says many Brazilian astrologers use 16:08 (ascendant 22 Aquarius) or 16:30 (ascendant 27 Aquarius) and names no one. It also notes that Aquarius rises until 16:42 and Pisces from 16:43, which puts Stockhorst's time on the boundary in the tropical zodiac. Duboc's 16:58 comes from rectification, which Personare calls complex and subjective. The Adams attribution for 16:30 is a reader's comment, not her article.

The 1988 chart is the only one with a recorded time. Adams cites Campion's Book of World Horoscopes, and a comment says Antonio Harries timed it from a television broadcast. The place is disputed: Adams gives Rio de Janeiro, Astral Harmony gives Brasilia. We computed it for Brasilia at -03:00. A marginal 1550 chart for Salvador, Bahia, quoted in a comment, is not computed here. We found no astrologer using the 1889 proclamation of the republic.

The clock is a trap. In 1822 there was no standard time, so the 1822 times are presumably local mean time, about -03:06 at Ipiranga. No source says so. We used -03:06 for all five 1822 charts. Astrotheme's tropical figures for 16:35 (ascendant 28 Aquarius 10, Sun 14 Virgo 34, Moon 7 Gemini 02) agree with our engine at that offset, which suggests the same assumption.

## What the chart says about the country

Aquarius rising is read as a nation of many peoples, a collective, forward-looking state. The lagna lord is Saturn. It sits retrograde in Aries in the 3rd house, in Bharani. The 3rd is the house of effort, skill and communication. The tradition reads a country that builds itself through sustained work. This is a classical reading, not a claim.

The Moon, which stands for the people, is in Taurus in the 4th house, in Rohini, with Jupiter. Taurus is the sign of the Moon's exaltation. The 4th is the house of land, home and the public. The Moon and Jupiter together in a kendra from the lagna are called Gajakesari in the tradition, and are read as a generous, rooted public mood. Rohini is a nakshatra linked with growth and abundance. A land-based identity and a large public are the usual keywords.

The 10th house, government, is Scorpio and holds no planet. Its lord, Mars, is in Libra in the 9th house, in Swati, the house of law, belief and fortune. So the state is read as one whose government is tied to law and to the idea of principle. The Sun is in Leo in the 7th, opposite the lagna, which is read as a country that defines itself through others and through partnership. Mercury is in Virgo in the 8th. Venus is in Cancer in the 6th with Ketu, and Rahu is in Capricorn in the 12th. All of this depends on a time of day nobody recorded.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The hour of the Cry of Ipiranga is not recorded, and five astrologers give five times. The sidereal rising sign survives that doubt; the degree and the houses beyond the 1st may not. All five 1822 times score below chance on ten events, and a few events dated to the day is a thin base for any score. The 1988 chart is timed from a broadcast, but its place is disputed and it describes a constitution, not the nation. A chart cannot judge a government or the people it governs.

**What is Brazil's zodiac sign?**

**What is Brazil's Moon sign?**
In all five 1822 charts the sidereal Moon is in Taurus in Rohini (15.6 degrees in the 16:30 chart). The tropical Moon is in Gemini at 7.0.

**Which dasha is Brazil running in 2026?**
On the lead 1822 chart (16:30) it is the Ketu mahadasha (23 June 2025 to 23 June 2032) and the Venus antardasha (19 November 2025 to 19 January 2027). On the 1988 Constitution chart it is the Sun mahadasha and the Jupiter antardasha (23 March 2026 to 9 January 2027).

**Which Brazil chart is the most reliable?**
