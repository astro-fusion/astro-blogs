# Turkey Birth Chart: Gemini Rising and Four Planets in Libra

Astrologers cast the birth chart of the Republic of Turkey for 29 October 1923, when the Grand National Assembly in Ankara proclaimed the Republic and elected Mustafa Kemal as its president. The hour most often used is 20:30. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole sign) that gives Gemini rising at 15.0 degrees, with the Moon in Gemini in Ardra and the Sun, Mercury, Venus and Saturn all in Libra. Confidence is medium for the date and low for the time. The chart scored 12 of 22 in our test against 13.7 by chance, which is below chance.

The date has the most support. Four of the five chart-giving sources use 29 October 1923 in Ankara, and only the Cosmologer page uses a different event. The two Vedic sources split: Tempora uses the proclamation at 20:30, and Cosmologer uses the Lausanne treaty.

The Lausanne chart was not computed, because it belongs to a different place and its clock offset is uncertain. The Treaty of Lausanne was signed on 24 July 1923 and gave the new Turkey international recognition. Kalawar proposed a chart in April 2017 at 11:30 in Lausanne with Virgo rising near 18 degrees. Cosmologer revised it to 11:15 in Ankara with Virgo rising near 25 degrees, and calls it tentative, saying the true ascendant lies between 18 and 25 Virgo. The blog says Ankara is two hours ahead of Lausanne, but on the time-zone database the gap is one hour, so the offset is uncertain. We discuss it in words only.

The proclamation time is the main trap. The 20:30 comes from a Turkish news piece in [Ekonomist](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/gundem/turkiye-cumhuriyeti-103-yasinda-turkiye-cumhuriyeti-mesut-muvaffak-ve-muzaffer-olacaktir-80363), which says the vote came "when the clock showed 20:30", all 158 deputies present, after a session that opened at 18:00. We did not find it in the parliamentary minutes. Turkey adopted the Gregorian calendar and international clock hours in the 1920s, so a 1923 Ottoman document could use the older Rumi calendar or alaturka hours. The sources give Gregorian dates, but we did not verify which clock Ankara used. The time-zone database gives +02:00 for Istanbul in 1923, and Ankara local mean time is about +02:11. A difference of four minutes moves the ascendant about one degree.

## What the chart says about the country

Gemini rising is read as a nation of communication, trade, translation and two minds: a country that faces two directions. The ascendant lord, Mercury, is in Libra in the 5th house in Chitra, with the Sun, Venus and Saturn. Four planets in the 5th, the house of intelligence, creativity and statecraft, is a striking feature. The tradition reads it as a country that makes policy by thought and by deliberate design, and one that puts weight on reform and on education.

Saturn is in Libra, the sign where it is exalted in classical tradition, right beside Mercury at 1.8 degrees. Saturn with the ascendant lord is read as discipline and a taste for form and order. Venus at 25.4 degrees of Libra is in its own sign, which the tradition treats as supportive for law, art and diplomacy, and the Sun in Libra is read as authority that is shared and balanced and not absolute.

The Moon, the people, is in Gemini in Ardra in the 1st house. Ardra is a nakshatra associated with storms and with change after upheaval. The Moon in the ascendant is read as a public that is mobile, curious and closely identified with the nation itself.

The 10th house is Pisces and holds no planets. Its lord, Jupiter, is in Scorpio in the 6th house in Vishakha. The 6th is the house of service, struggle and competition, so the tradition reads public life as often marked by contest and by the need to defend its position. Mars is in Virgo in the 4th house of homeland, with Rahu in Leo in the 3rd (courage, communication, neighbours) and Ketu in Aquarius in the 9th (law and belief). These are readings from tradition, not statements about any group of people.

In the Western chart Cancer rises, and Pluto in Cancer 12.3 is in the 1st house. The Sun is in Scorpio and the Moon in Gemini. Western astrologers, among them [Rodenbeck](https://oxfordastrologer.com/turkey-a-question-of-identity/), write about the country's identity in terms of the chart, and they disagree with each other.

## Testing the chart against history

An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to the kind of event (whole sign, from the Vedic ascendant) or is the named planet. Constitutional change uses houses 1, 9 and 10. Territorial change uses 4, 7 and 12. Natural disaster uses 4, 8 and 12. War uses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Economic crisis uses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. Death of a leader uses 8 and 10. ## What this chart cannot tell you

The 20:30 time is attested only by Astrotheme (rating C, origin unknown), Tempora and a news article, and the research found no parliamentary minutes that give it. A search summary of a Habertturk report of the minutes says the session met at 18:00, which would fit the news piece's account of a session opening at 18:00, but we did not open it and it is not a source for the vote time.

The Lausanne chart is a different event for a different place and was not computed. A single-chart test has no comparison, and eleven events is a small sample. A result below chance cannot settle which event is the birth of the Republic. The chart cannot say what will happen to a country, and nothing here is a judgement on any government, group or event.
