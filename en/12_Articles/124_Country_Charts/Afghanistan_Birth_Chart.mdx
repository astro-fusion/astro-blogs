# Afghanistan Birth Chart: Five Founding Dates, No Agreed Chart

There is no agreed birth chart for Afghanistan. The sources we found use five different founding moments, from 1747 to 2004, and none uses the independence date of 19 August 1919. This article therefore sets out the candidate charts for Afghanistan and tests them. The one we lead with is the republic proclaimed on 17 July 1973, set at midnight in Kabul, the version a Vedic astrologer uses. It has Aries rising and the Moon in Capricorn, in Shravana. Confidence is low, and in our test this midnight chart scored below chance.

Only two of the six sources that name a country chart use the 1973 republic. Each of the other dates is used by one author. An [AstrologyKing](https://astrologyking.com/afghanistan-horoscope/) page says its Afghanistan chart comes from Astro-Databank, but shows no data, and Astro-Databank could not be read. That is why confidence is low.

The dating traps are many.

- **1973.** The coup began on the morning of 17 July and the proclamation was made on radio at about 7 a.m. A midnight chart is set before the event it represents. The author of the Vedic chart says no time is known and uses midnight. The dawn chart is a time chosen by its author, not a recorded one.
- **1919.** The Treaty of Rawalpindi was signed on 8 August 1919, but independence day is marked on 19 August. We found no astrologer chart for either date.
- **1992.** AstroWow uses 18 April. Wikipedia gives the proclamation on 28 April 1992, after Najibullah resigned on 15 April.
- **1747.** The date comes from the Afghan embassy by way of Jupiter Astrology, and whether it is Julian or Gregorian is not stated. The chart is set in local mean time, which the engine computes as +04:37.
- **Time zone.** Afghanistan used +04:30 from 1957 and +04:00 before that. The javed22 blog writes GMT+5 for the 2001 chart. We used +04:30 for every Kabul chart, which is an assumption.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading of the midnight candidate in the classical manner. It describes tradition and rests on a time no one recorded.

Aries rising is read as a state that comes into being by force of action. The lagna lord is Mars, and Mars is in the 12th house in Pisces, in Revati. The 12th is the house of loss, withdrawal, exile and foreign lands. A lagna lord there is read as a country whose strength is spent outside itself, or an identity that is hard to hold. It is also the chart that the Vedic source reads for the events of 1978 to 1992.

The Moon and Jupiter are together in the 10th house in Capricorn, within a fraction of a degree, both in Shravana. The 10th is the house of government. The Moon is the public, and with Jupiter it is read as a public bound up with law and belief. Jupiter in Capricorn is in the sign of its debilitation, which the tradition reads as wisdom under constraint. The nakshatra Shravana is tied to listening and to learning by hearing.

The Sun, Mercury and Venus are together in Cancer in the 4th house, the house of the homeland, land and the common people. A group of bodies in the 4th is read as a strong attachment to the home ground. The 10th lord is Saturn, which sits in the 3rd house in Gemini with Ketu. The 3rd is the house of courage, effort and neighbours, and Saturn there is read as slow, hard-won effort.

Rahu is in the 9th house in Sagittarius. The 9th is the house of law, religion and fortune, and Rahu there is read as an unorthodox or contested relation to belief and law.

None of this is evidence. A different time of day would change the ascendant and every house, and the dawn chart (below) has a different rising sign.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses are:

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10
- territorial change: 4, 7, 12
- natural disaster: 4, 8, 12

Only events dated to the day are scored, and only those after the chart's own date. The maximum for c1, c2 and c3 is 22 points for eleven events. Vimshottari repeats every 120 years; for charts older than that the later cycles come from the same engine. The facts follow the research file. ## What this chart cannot tell you

- There is no agreed founding moment. Five dates are in use, and the one most often named for the country as a nation, 19 August 1919, has no astrologer chart.
- The lead chart's time is a convention that precedes the event it marks, and it scored below chance.
- The dawn chart's 05:56 was chosen by its author and is not a record.
- The 1747 date has an unstated calendar, its noon is a convention, and its local mean time (+04:37) cannot be checked against a clock. Its score of 13 of 22 is only 0.7 above chance, and its periods come from later Vimshottari cycles of the same engine.
- Each candidate marks a different state: a monarchy, a republic, a mujahideen government, an interim authority and an elected constitution. A chart can only describe the one it marks.
- The scores come from small sets of events and broad rules. A chart that fits the past does not predict the future.
