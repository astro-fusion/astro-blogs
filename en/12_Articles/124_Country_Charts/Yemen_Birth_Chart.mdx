# Yemen Birth Chart: Leo Rising at Noon, Three Founding Dates

The Yemen birth chart in the sources we could open is cast for the unification of North and South Yemen on 22 May 1990, with noon in Aden as the time. That gives Leo rising at 6.6 degrees in the Vedic zodiac, with the Moon in Aries in Ashwini. Noon is a placeholder, not a recorded moment, and only two astrologer pages could be opened for any Yemeni chart. We rate the confidence as low. Two earlier charts, for North Yemen in 1962 and South Yemen in 1967, appear in one Western source that we could see only in a search summary.

The 1962 and 1967 rows are unverified. We saw them only in a search summary, and the page returned nothing to our reader. We list them so the reader knows they exist, and we do not rely on them.

The dates also differ from the record. According to Wikipedia's [North Yemen Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Yemen_Civil_War) and [Yemen Arab Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemen_Arab_Republic) articles, the revolution in Sanaa is dated to 26 September 1962. The army began shelling the palace at 11:45 pm on 25 September, and Sanaa was secure by late morning on 26 September. We found no source for 27 September. For South Yemen, Wikipedia's [South Yemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Yemen) article says the state was born at midnight on 29 to 30 November 1967, eleven hours after the last British troops left, and dates the proclamation to 30 November 1967. The 15:00 time on 29 November in the summary matches neither moment, and we do not know where it comes from.

For unification, Wikipedia's [Yemeni unification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yemeni_unification) article gives no time of day. A caption says the new flag was raised on the presidential palace of former South Yemen in Aden. That is why c1 uses Aden, but Aden against Sanaa as the place is not settled.

The time zone is not in dispute. The [tz database](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eggert/tz/main/asia) has Aden at +03:00 since 14 March 1947 with no summer time, and all three charts use it. Wikipedia's Time in Yemen page printed a wrong offset when we opened it, so we did not use it.

The two Vedic sources do not agree with each other. Astrobabushka gives a Leo ascendant. Sanjay Rath's chart has Aquarius rising with Mars in the lagna, and its time and place are not stated in the text. Noon at Aden gives Leo rising (see below), so Rath's chart must rest on another time or place. That is why the confidence is low: two pages, one date, no shared time.

## What the chart says about the country

Leo rises, so the Sun is the lagna lord. It sits in the 10th house in Taurus, in Krittika, a nakshatra the Sun itself rules. The tradition reads a lagna lord in the 10th as an identity expressed through public office and government. Taurus is a sign of land and resources. These are classical readings, offered as description, and the chart's ascendant rests on a placeholder noon.

The Moon, which signifies the people, is in Aries in the 9th house, in Ashwini, with Mercury close by in the same sign. The 9th is the house of law, belief and fortune. The Moon rules the 12th (Cancer), so the people's mood is read as linked to loss, distance and what lies beyond the border. Mercury rules the 2nd and 11th houses and shares the sign, which is read as a close tie between communication, resources and gains.

Mars, which rules the 4th and 9th houses for Leo rising, is in the 7th house at 29.7 degrees of Aquarius, the very end of the sign. The 7th is the house of partners and open opposition. A planet that late in a sign is read as being on the edge of a change of sign. Venus, the 10th lord, is in the 8th house in Pisces at 27.0 degrees, and Pisces is the sign where the tradition places Venus at its strongest. The 8th is the house of sudden events and shared resources. Venus is also the 3rd lord.

Saturn and Rahu sit together in the 6th house in Capricorn. The 6th is the house of service, debt and disputes. Saturn rules the 6th and 7th houses for Leo, so Saturn is in its own sign in its own house. The tradition reads that as a country with a long relationship to hardship and dispute. Jupiter in the 11th, in Gemini, rules the 5th and 8th houses and is read as a source of gains through networks and communication. Ketu in the 12th in Cancer is read as a theme of detachment.

## What this chart cannot tell you

There is no documented time for any Yemeni founding, so the ascendant, the houses and the dasha dates are all open. The two Vedic pages we opened do not agree on the ascendant. The choice of founding is also open: the 1990 unification, the 1962 North Yemen revolution and the 1967 South Yemen independence each have a claim, and the Western source reportedly charts the two separate states.

The one date we can check is the unification, and it is the chart's own date, so it cannot test the chart. Most of the other events fall before 1990, and c1 has no periods for them. Several of the later events belong to armed conflicts. A chart cannot assign blame to any party in them, and nothing here does.
