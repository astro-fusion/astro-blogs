That is the whole list. The research opened Astrotheme and nothing else on the astrology side. A second chart-site address returned an error, a world-map astrology site has no Lebanon page, and several web searches returned only Astrotheme. No Vedic source was found, so the sidereal chart here is the Western chart recast with the Lahiri ayanamsa.

**Which date?** Wikipedia's [Independence of Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_of_Lebanon) article says the Chamber amended the constitution on 8 November 1943, France then arrested the government, and France released the prisoners from Rashaya on the morning of Monday 22 November 1943. That day is celebrated as Independence Day, and it is the date Astrotheme uses. The research file lists other moments a chart could be built on: the amendment of 8 November 1943, the proclamation by Catroux on 26 November 1941, the complete departure of French troops on 31 December 1946 and the creation of Greater Lebanon in 1920. None of these has an astrologer's chart that we found.

**The time.** Astrotheme shows 12:01 AM. Because the release took place in the morning, 00:01 cannot be the hour of the event. It looks like a convention, a minute after midnight at the start of the day. The chart is therefore a chart for the date, and not for any moment on it.

**The offset.** We used +02:00. The time-zone database treats Beirut as Eastern European Time, with Lebanese daylight-saving rules covering only 1920 to 1923 and from 1957, so none in 1943. The database may not capture wartime changes, and we could not check them. Astrotheme's own figures agree with the engine on position: its ascendant of 9 degrees 38 minutes of Virgo matches the tropical 9.6, and its Sun at 28 degrees 43 minutes of Scorpio matches 28.7.
