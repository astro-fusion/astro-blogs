# Russia Birth Chart: The Candidate Charts, and Why None Wins

There is no agreed birth chart for Russia. The one named most often for the Russian Federation is 25 December 1991, about 19:45 in Moscow (+02:00), the evening Mikhail Gorbachev resigned and the Russian flag replaced the Soviet flag over the Kremlin. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole sign) it has Leo rising at 1.6 degrees and the Moon in Leo in Magha. Research confidence is low, because the evidence is thin and astrologers split between 1991, 1990, 1922 and 1917. This article treats the charts as candidates and tests five of them.

Three more candidates were not computed. The 1917 charts have no reliable clock offset, so we discuss them in words only. [Sanjay Rath](https://srath.com/?p=64) uses the Bolshevik proclamation of Soviet power on 7 November 1917 (25 October in the Julian calendar), at 10:00 in Petrograd. An archive text, [Under the Sign of the Scorpion](https://archive.org/details/UnderTheSignOfTheScorpion), uses 8 November 1917 at 02:04, with the Sun in Scorpio. [Astrotheme](https://www.astrotheme.com/astrology/Russia) lists the traditional founding of the Rus state at Novgorod on 20 September 862 (Julian), at noon, as a convention.

The dispute is about what "Russia" is. The state has several starting points: the Bolshevik state (1917), the formation of the USSR (30 December 1922), the sovereignty declaration of the Russian republic (12 June 1990), the Belovezha Accords (8 December 1991), Gorbachev's resignation and the flag change (25 December 1991), and the Supreme Soviet's dissolution declaration (26 December 1991). The research chose 25 December 1991 because it is the date of the state's succession and the only one named by both a Western and a Vedic astrologer. Two of the three Vedic sources split between 1991 and 1990. Tempora's own page gives "12 June 1991" for the sovereignty declaration, but that declaration was in 1990 (12 June 1991 was a presidential election), so the research did not use it.

The traps are calendar and clock. The October Revolution is 25 October in the Julian calendar and 7 November in the Gregorian. In December 1991 Moscow was on +02:00, not the +03:00 "MSK" that Tempora labels it; in June 1990 Moscow was on summer time, +04:00. The 17:45 GMT time is Balakrishnan's, converted to 19:45 local. No source gives a time for the flag lowering to the second.

## What the chart says about the country

The ascendant is Leo at 1.6 degrees, with the Moon and Jupiter in the same sign. Leo rising is read as a country with a strong sense of rank, ceremony and a central authority. The ascendant lord, the Sun, is in Sagittarius in the 5th house in Mula, close to Rahu. The 5th is the house of intelligence, statecraft and what a nation creates. Sun with Rahu is read as a visible, forceful centre that is also restless about its own legitimacy. This is the tradition's language and does not describe any leader.

The Moon, the people, is in Leo in the 1st house in Magha, a nakshatra tradition links with ancestry and royalty. Jupiter in the same sign, in Purva Phalguni, is read as breadth and a taste for large aims. A Moon-Jupiter pairing in the ascendant is considered a support, and this is the most favourable feature of the chart in classical terms.

Mars and Mercury are together in Scorpio in the 4th house, the house of homeland, territory and security. Mars is at 25.5 degrees, Mercury at 17.7. Classical authors read this as a preoccupation with defence of land and with intelligence, secrecy and strategy. Saturn in Capricorn in the 6th house, in its own sign, is read as stamina in the face of struggle, debt and service. The 6th is the house of competition and open conflict, which is how the tradition links it to disputes.

The 10th house is Taurus, and its lord Venus is in the 3rd, at 29.2 degrees of Libra, the last degree of the sign. Ketu is in Gemini in the 11th, the house of gains. Western astrologers see the Capricorn Sun with Uranus and Neptune in the 5th house as a state that is rebuilding. These are readings, and astrologers differ on all of them.

## Testing the chart against history

A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to the kind of event (whole sign, from the Vedic ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Economic crisis uses houses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. Constitutional change uses houses 1, 9 and 10. Territorial change uses houses 4, 7 and 12. Pandemic uses 6, 8 and 12. Death of a leader uses 8 and 10. ## What this chart cannot tell you

The Russian Federation did not have one birth. The flag-change time is derived from a GMT time in a single source, and the sovereignty-declaration times come from single sources without provenance (14:10 and 01:45, and the second looks implausible for a daytime vote). Two pages the research relied on could not be opened and are cited here only as unverified.

The 1917 and 862 charts could not be computed. The nine-event test is small, and the rules are general. No chart here predicts a war, a leader or an outcome, and nothing in this article is a judgement on any state or government. The conflict events are listed with dates taken from the cited pages and are not interpreted.

## Frequently asked questions

On the most used candidate (25 December 1991, 19:45) the Vedic ascendant is Leo 1.6 and the Western ascendant is Leo 25.3. Ten minutes earlier the Vedic ascendant is Cancer 29.9. No candidate is certain.

**What is Russia's zodiac sign?**
On the 1991 chart the Sun is in Sagittarius in the sidereal zodiac (9.7 degrees) and Capricorn in the tropical (3.4 degrees). Russia is therefore a Capricorn-Sun country in Western terms for 1991. The 1990 charts have the Sun in Taurus (sidereal).

**Which dasha is Russia running in 2026?**
On the 1991 chart it is the Moon mahadasha (4 January 2020 to 4 January 2030) and the Mercury antardasha (3 November 2025 to 5 April 2027). Other charts give different periods.

**When was Russia born?**
There are several answers: 25 December 1991 for the Federation, 12 June 1990 for the sovereignty declaration, 30 December 1922 for the USSR and November 1917 for the Soviet state. Astrologers disagree and the research confidence is low.
