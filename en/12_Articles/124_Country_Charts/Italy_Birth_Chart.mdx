The research opened two astrologer pages for Italy. The astro.com page gives the 1946 chart. It says 10 June 1946 at 4:00 pm GMT in Rome, and it does not name the event. The research reads the date as the day the referendum result was announced by the Court of Cassation. That reading is the researcher's, not the page's. The Elsa Elsa blog shows a Kingdom of Italy chart for 1861 without a time. Its image is captioned 1871, while commenters argue for 1861, and the source it cites returned an error when we tried it. The Astrotheme entry for 18 February 1861 was blocked when we tried to open it. We know it only from a search result, so treat c3 as unverified.

Three traps matter here.

**The event.** Italy has at least two founding moments. The Kingdom followed the law of 17 March 1861 that named Victor Emmanuel II king. The Republic followed the referendum of 2 June 1946. For the Republic, the result was confirmed on 10 June, and other accounts say the republic was proclaimed on 18 June.

**The time zone.** The astro.com page gives GMT. Converting to the Rome clock gives 18:00 only if Italian summer time (+02:00) was in force. The time-zone database says it was, but the research did not confirm that from a printed source. The engine used +02:00.

**The 1861 offset.** Italy adopted a standard time only in 1893. For c3 we used Turin local mean time, +00:31, from the longitude of 7.69 degrees east.

There is no Julian or Gregorian trap for these dates.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each timed chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses are:

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
- economic crisis: 6, 8, 12, or Saturn
- death of a leader: 8, 10
- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10
- territorial change: 4, 7, 12
- pandemic: 6, 8, 12
