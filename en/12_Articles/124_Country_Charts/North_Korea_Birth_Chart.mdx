The disagreement has two layers. The first is the time of day. A rectified 12:39, a plain noon and a midnight are all in circulation. A rectification from photographs of a daytime parade can narrow the hour, but it cannot fix a minute, and the author calls the time an estimate. The second is the date. Hayes reportedly rejects the 10 September chart as an error. Other moments astrologers weigh are the constitution being signed on 5 September (Hayes) or promulgated on 3 September, and the People's Assembly vote of 10 July 1948 (Wikipedia). Hayes also reportedly rejects a 10 August 1945 partition chart. These alternatives have no times in our files, so we computed only the 9 September charts.

There is a time-zone trap. According to the tz database, North Korea used +09:00 in 1948, and +08:30 only in 2015 to 2018. The sources state no offset and we could not check this on a dated page. We use +09:00.

The tradition reads the 12th as the house of loss, seclusion, expenditure and foreign lands. A lagna lord there is read as an identity that works at a distance or out of view. Mars also rules the 6th house of disputes and service. This is the tradition's wording about the placement. It is not a statement about any government.

The Moon sits in the ascendant, in Scorpio in Anuradha at 9.5 degrees. Scorpio is the sign of the Moon's debilitation in classical texts, and the tradition reads that as a popular mood that is intense and guarded. Jupiter is in the same sign, in Jyeshtha at 26.9 degrees, a late degree. Two planets in the 1st house put a strong mark on the national character. Mercury is in Virgo in the 11th in Hasta, the house of gains and networks. Venus is in Cancer in the 9th in Pushya, a house of law, belief and long-held ideas.
