# Switzerland Birth Chart: Libra Rising at 11:12, or Scorpio at 12:50

The birth chart of Switzerland is most often cast for the adoption of the Federal Constitution on 12 September 1848, at 11:12 in Bern. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri) that gives Libra rising, at 29.7 degrees, the last degree of the sign. Confidence is medium: all the sources agree on the date and the place, but they split on the time, 11:12 against about 12:50, and that split changes the rising sign from Libra to Scorpio. The older 1291 oath chart is used by one source only.

The date is not in dispute: the Diet adopted the Federal Constitution on 12 September 1848 and the Swiss federal state was founded, as described on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Federal_Constitution). The time is. The 11:12 figure comes from J.H. Taeger and Nicholas Campion and is the one on astro.com. The 12:50 figure comes from Astrotheme, whose entry is rated A and says it is from a timed, documented news source. The blog quotes 12:55 in one place and describes Ganz's check as "shortly before 1 pm" in another, so the small difference between 12:50 and 12:55 is not resolved. We computed both c1 and c2.

The 1291 chart is a midday chart with no stated place. The Federal Charter of 1291 is dated only to early August, and the blog's own text also mentions the year 1307, so it is a weak candidate. We found one Vedic source, the IndiaDivine forum post of 2010. It uses the 11:12 time.

There are traps. The first is the zone. Switzerland had no standard time until 1894, so a clock time in 1848 is Bern local mean time, 29 minutes 47 seconds ahead of Greenwich. We used +00:30. The forum post labels the same 11:12 as CET, which cannot be right for 1848. The post also writes the date as 9/12/1848, which is ambiguous between 12 September and 9 December. The second trap is the ascendant. The post states "Asc 24 Libra", while our computation with the Lahiri ayanamsa gives Libra 29.7. The post does not state its ayanamsa, and we did not resolve the difference.

## What the chart says about the country

Libra rising is read as a nation of balance, negotiation and arrangement between parties. The ascendant sits at 29.7 degrees, the last degree of the sign, which makes the rising sign fragile: a few minutes either way would change it. The lagna lord, Venus, is in Virgo in the 12th house, in Hasta, together with Mars and Mercury. The 12th is the house of distance, foreign places and what is held at arm's length. Venus also rules the 8th, the house of shared funds and what is hidden. Lagna lord in the 12th with the lords of the 2nd, 7th, 9th and 12th is read as a country whose identity is bound up with money, outside dealings and keeping a distance.

The Moon, which stands for the people, is in Aquarius in the 5th house, in Shatabhisha. The 5th is the house of the mind, learning and public judgement. The Moon rules the 10th house, which is government, so the Moon in the 5th ties the public mood to the way the state is run. Ketu sits with the Moon in the 5th, close to the end of Aquarius. In tradition that adds a detached, withdrawn quality to the house.

Jupiter, in Cancer in the 10th house, is the strongest planet in this picture in classical terms: Cancer is the sign of Jupiter's exaltation, and the 10th is the house of government and public standing. Jupiter rules the 3rd and 6th, the houses of communication and service and of debts and disputes. The Sun and Rahu sit together in Leo in the 11th, the house of gains and networks, both at the very end of the sign. The 11th house lord is the Sun, so the lord of the 11th sits in its own house with the north node.

Saturn, lord of the 4th and 5th, is in Pisces in the 6th house, at 0.3 degrees, retrograde. The 6th is read as the house of service, debt and rivals, and Saturn in it is a classical placement for steady, disciplined work. All of this is description in the language of the tradition, not a forecast.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each timed candidate against dated events. Rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the relevant houses (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Economic crisis uses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Only events dated to the day are scored. The 28 November 1848 choice of Bern as federal city is dated but its kind ("other") has no rule, so it is not scored. "Chance" is what a random pair of periods would score on the same events with the same chart.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The date is solid and the time is not. The ascendant of the lead chart is at 29.7 degrees of Libra, so even a small change of time moves it into Scorpio, and the 12:50 chart does exactly that. The two times come from different published sources, and neither source is a primary document we could read. We found one Vedic source, and its stated ascendant of 24 Libra does not match our 29.7, for a reason we could not check. The 1291 oath was not computed because it is older than 1700, and one blog is the only source for it. Nine scored events is a short test. A chart cannot say what will happen to a nation or the people in it.
