# Myanmar Birth Chart: Scorpio Rising at an Astrologers' Hour

The Myanmar birth chart that astrologers use is cast for independence from Britain on 4 January 1948, at 04:20 in Rangoon (now Yangon). It gives a Scorpio ascendant at 17.5 degrees and the Moon in Virgo in Chitra. This is an unusual national chart because the time is not a guess. Michael O'Reilly, quoting Nicholas Campion, writes that Burmese astrologers chose the hour. We rate the confidence as medium: the hour has a documented story, but we found only two astrologer sources, and both are Western.

The three rows are not three guesses at one moment. Only c1 is a candidate for the founding of the state. c2 and c3 are charts for the military governments of 1962 and 1988, which astrologers cast to read those governments, not the country itself.

The story of c1 is the useful part. The Union of Burma became independent from Britain at a moment that Burmese astrologers picked (see [the Burmese declaration of independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_Declaration_of_Independence)). O'Reilly quotes Campion to that effect, and Astrologicalworldmap credits the timing to a New Internationalist article. That makes 04:20 a deliberate election, not a noon or midnight default. A search summary of a Wikipedia page mentioned a 4:25 am figure, and another summary said the date was moved from 6 to 4 January. Our researcher did not find either claim on a page they opened, so treat both as unverified.

There are two traps. First, 04:20 on 4 January local is 21:50 UT on 3 January, so a calculator set to UT needs the earlier date. Second, Burma time was +06:30, not +06:00. We use +06:30 throughout. We found no Vedic astrologer's page on the founding chart. The one Vedic page seen in search covered the 1988 coup only, and we did not open it.

## What the chart says about the country

Scorpio rises, so Mars is the lagna lord. Mars is in Leo in the 10th house, in Purva Phalguni. The tradition reads the 10th as the house of government, authority and public standing. A lagna lord there is read as a national identity expressed through the state and through action. Mars also rules the 6th house, which classical texts link with disputes and service. We state this as the tradition's reading, not as evidence about any event.

Jupiter sits in the ascendant sign itself, in Jyeshtha at 22.6 degrees. A planet in the 1st is read as a strong mark on the country's character. The Sun and Mercury share Sagittarius in the 2nd house, at 19.3 and 19.5 degrees, a very tight conjunction. The Sun rules the 10th house, so the lord of government sits in the house of wealth, speech and family. Classical readers connect that with a state tied to its resources and its treasury.

The Moon, the significator of the people, is in Virgo in Chitra in the 11th, the house of gains and networks. It is at 24.8 degrees, late in the sign. The Moon rules the 9th house, and Saturn, retrograde in Cancer in Ashlesha, occupies it. The 9th is read as law, belief and long-held tradition, and Saturn there is read as a weight on them. Saturn is at 28.7 degrees, the last degrees of the sign.

Rahu is in the 6th in Aries and Ketu in the 12th in Libra. The 6th and 12th are the houses of struggle and of loss, withdrawal and distant lands. This axis is read as a country that handles friction and separation as recurring themes. Venus, lord of the 7th and 12th, is in Capricorn in the 3rd, which tradition links with communication and effort. These are descriptions of classical meaning only.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each timed candidate against events dated to the day. The rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses linked to that kind of event (counted from the Vedic ascendant), or is the named planet:

- War: houses 6, 7, 8, or Mars.
- Economic crisis: houses 6, 8, 12, or Saturn.
- Leader death or assassination: houses 8, 10.
- Constitutional change: houses 1, 9, 10.
- Territorial change: houses 4, 7, 12.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The time is documented as a choice, but the record is thin. We found two astrologer sources, both Western, and one of them credits a magazine article for the hour. We could not confirm the claim of a 4:25 figure or of a date change from 6 to 4 January. If the exact minute is off, the ascendant and the dasha dates shift.

The chart is also a chart of a moment that was picked for its meaning, not the moment a state came into being in a legal sense. Astrologers disagree about whether a chosen hour carries more weight than a natural one. Myanmar has had several governments, and the 1962 and 1988 charts exist because astrologers treat those as new starts for a regime. Naypyidaw became the administrative capital on 6 November 2005, and Astrologicalworldmap casts that move at 0:00. Which moment is the country's chart depends on what you mean by the country.

Finally, the score is a lead of 4.1 on nine events. It cannot show that the chart works.
