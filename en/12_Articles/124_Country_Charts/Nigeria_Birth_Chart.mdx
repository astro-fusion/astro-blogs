Two traps matter here.

**Which midnight.** Midnight at the start of 1 October is the same instant as midnight at the end of 30 September. Pages that say "30 September" and pages that say "1 October" can mean the same chart. The engine used 00:00 on 1 October, which is 23:00 UTC on 30 September.

**The offset.** Neither page states a time zone. We used +01:00, West Africa Time, which is our understanding for Lagos in 1960. The research did not verify it.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, worded as tradition. The ascendant moves a degree about every four minutes, so with no recorded time, the houses below are one possible frame among many. The signs and nakshatras of the slower planets are more stable than the houses, but the Moon moves about 13 degrees in a day, so even its place is uncertain.

Gemini rising is read as a lively, talkative and many-sided state. The lagna lord is Mercury, in the 5th house in Libra, in Chitra, with Venus, 5.6 degrees apart. The 5th house is the house of the public mood, creativity and the young. Mercury with Venus is read as a culture of music, speech, trade and display. Mars sits in the 1st house in Gemini, in Ardra, and the tradition reads Mars on the ascendant as a forceful, competitive and restless identity.

Mars is in tension with two planets. The Sun squares Mars (2.9 degrees apart) and squares Saturn (4.2 degrees apart), and Mars stands opposite Saturn (7.2 degrees apart). These are symbols, not statements about any group in the country.

The Moon is in Capricorn in the 8th house, in Shravana, at 23.0 degrees. The 8th is the house of crisis, transformation and the unexpected. A Moon there is read as a public mood familiar with sudden change. Shravana is a nakshatra classically tied to listening and learning. The 10th house is Pisces and holds no planet.
