# France Birth Chart: Pisces Rising at the Fifth Republic, 1958

The France birth chart that is easiest to find is the Fifth Republic chart on Astrodienst: 6 October 1958, 18:30, Paris. It has Aries rising in the tropical zodiac and Pisces rising in the sidereal one. France is an old country with several regime foundings, and astrologers do not agree on which one is the birth. No two sources we read agree on both date and time. Confidence is low, and the reasons are set out below.

Six pages name five different charts. The Fifth Republic is the only one on the most visible site and the only one with a rated, timed source, which is why it leads, but we could find no explanation of Greene's date of 6 October or her time of 18:30. The constitution was promulgated on 4 October 1958 and the referendum was on 28 September, according to French law pages seen only as search summaries. We found no Vedic astrologer's France chart.

The traps are the calendar and the clock. Before 1891 Paris kept Paris Mean Time, 9 minutes 21 seconds ahead of Greenwich, and the 1789 and 1792 charts use it. In 1958 Paris was on UTC+1, which the research did not independently verify. The 987 date is in the Julian calendar. In 1792 the 21 and 22 September dates differ: the Convention abolished the monarchy on the 21st and the new year I began on the 22nd, according to a search summary. The Cosmologer author says the 1792 chart replaces an earlier France chart that failed in prediction. The Adams page says that for 987 there is no time, so noon is used.

## What the chart says about the country

This is the traditional reading of the placements, given as description.

Pisces rising is read as a country that is idealistic, cultured and difficult to define in one phrase. The ascendant is at 27.3 degrees, near the end of the sign, with Ketu at 29.3 in Revati on it. Ketu on the ascendant is read as a detached relation to the national self, a country that questions its own identity. The tropical chart has Aries rising, a very different picture, which shows how much rests on the hour.

The lord of the ascendant, Jupiter, is in Libra in the 8th house, in Swati. The 8th house is read as crisis, shared money and transformation. Jupiter there is read as a country that renews itself through upheaval and that has a deep interest in law and principle, since Libra is the sign of balance.

Four points sit in Virgo in the 7th house: the Sun, Mercury, Venus and Rahu. The 7th is the house of partners, treaties, and open rivals. Four in one house are read as a country defined by its relations, with Europe, with allies and with its own citizens, and by detailed argument about rules. Rahu at 29.3 degrees, in Chitra, brings the pull of outside influence into the same house.

The Moon is in Gemini in the 4th house, in Punarvasu at 27.7 degrees. The 4th stands for home and the people, and Gemini is read as a talkative, quick, variable public mood. The 10th house, Sagittarius, holds no planet and its lord Jupiter is in the 8th, so public standing is read as tied to the same renewal themes. Saturn in Scorpio is in the 9th house, in Jyeshtha, read as a serious and guarded relation to law and belief. This reading depends on the 18:30 hour, and the sources do not explain it.

## Testing the chart against history

The rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to that kind of event, or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. A constitutional change uses houses 1, 9 and 10. The death of a leader uses houses 8 and 10. A pandemic uses houses 6, 8 and 12. Only events dated to the day count, and events of the kind "other" (the May 1968 strike and the yellow vests) are not scored. Vimshottari repeats every 120 years; for charts older than that the later cycles come from the same engine.

## What this chart cannot tell you

There is no agreed founding. A reader who prefers the Revolution or the First Republic will cast a different chart, and in our test the 1792 charts scored better than the 1958 ones. For the Fifth Republic, the date alone varies (28 September, 4 October or 6 October), and the 18:00 and 18:30 times are not documented in anything we opened. The Astrotheme time is labelled documented, from news, and Greene's has no source we could find.

Because Pisces is rising at 27.3 degrees, near the sign's end, a small error in the hour could change the rising sign. The test used eight events, and the chance level is high (9.5 to 12.0) because war, constitutional change and pandemic cover many houses. The chart does not describe any one president or government and makes no forecast.
