# Spain Birth Chart: Capricorn Rising in the Juan Carlos Chart

Most astrologers who publish a chart for modern Spain use the swearing-in of King Juan Carlos I before the Cortes on 22 November 1975, two days after Franco's death. The time is 12:45 in Madrid, from Liz Greene on astro.com. In the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole-sign) it has Capricorn rising and the Moon in Gemini, in Ardra. Confidence is medium. Two of the three pages opened name this chart as the one generally used, but nobody explains where 12:45 comes from, and no Vedic astrologer has published a chart for Spain that we could find.

The 1975 chart is the one astro.com describes as generally used for modern Spain, with Aquarius rising in the tropical zodiac. Horoscopes of Europe lists it too, at 12:45 pm CET for Madrid, but prefers the 1479 chart and says that chart "seems to work better". The El Doble Cero blog says most astrologers hold that Spain is Sagittarius-ruled because the constitution was approved on 6 December 1978. It gives no time or place, and we found no chart with a time for that date.

The 1479 chart is dated before 1700 and was not computed for this article. The research flags it as low in reliability, because the blog says Madrid while [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juan_II_of_Aragon) gives Barcelona and 20 January 1479 for the death of Juan II. The blog does not say which calendar it uses, so the nine-day Julian to Gregorian shift is also open.

Two traps matter here.

**The constitution date.** The referendum was on 6 December 1978. According to search summaries, the King promulgated the text on 27 December and it was published on 29 December. A chart for "the constitution" can mean any of the three.

**The time.** For 1975 there is no calendar or summer-time trap, since November used CET. The only open question is the source of the 12:45.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, and it is worded as tradition.

Capricorn rising is read as a state with a serious, formal and duty-bound face. The lagna lord is Saturn, and Saturn is retrograde in Cancer in the 7th house, in Pushya. The 7th is the house of partners, open enemies and treaties. A lagna lord there is read as an identity that is defined by relations with others, whether allies or rivals. For a country whose chart is a royal oath, the 7th-house emphasis fits a crown that stands between factions. That is a symbolic reading only.

The Moon is in Gemini in the 6th house, in Ardra, together with Mars, who is retrograde in the same sign and house. The 6th is the house of service, debt, disputes and opponents. The Moon there is read as a public mood that is restless and quarrelsome, and Mars in the same house as a readiness for conflict and for struggle with adversaries. The tradition holds that Mars in the 6th can also work as a strong rival-beater, so the reading can run either way.

The 10th house is Libra, and Rahu sits in it, in Vishakha. Venus, the 10th lord, is in the 9th house in Virgo, in Hasta. A 10th lord in the 9th is read as a government that draws its authority from law, belief or custom. Rahu in the 10th is read as an unusual route to power, which suits a transition from a dictatorship to a monarchy.

The Sun and Mercury are together in Scorpio in the 11th house, the house of gains and groups. The Sun makes a trine of 3.4 degrees to Saturn in the file. The 11th-house Sun is read as authority that works through assemblies and networks. Ketu is in the 4th, in Aries, which is read as a detached relation to the home ground. Jupiter is retrograde in the 3rd, in Pisces, in Revati.

None of this is evidence. The chart is traditional and its time is a quoted figure.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested the chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before it was cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses are:

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
- economic crisis: 6, 8, 12, or Saturn
- death of a leader: 8, 10
- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10
- territorial change: 4, 7, 12

Only events dated to the day are scored, and only events after the chart date count. The chart dates from 22 November 1975, so the Treaty of Paris, the Republic, the Civil War and the death of Franco on 20 November 1975 are out of scope.
