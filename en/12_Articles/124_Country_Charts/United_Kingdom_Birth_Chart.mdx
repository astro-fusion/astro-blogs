# United Kingdom Birth Chart: Virgo Rising at Midnight, 1 January 1801

The United Kingdom birth chart most astrologers use is the Act of Union chart: 1 January 1801, 00:00, London, the moment the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland began. It has Libra rising in the tropical zodiac and Virgo rising in the sidereal one. The date is shared by every source we found. The hour is a convention, because a law that takes effect on a date takes effect at midnight, so nobody can say it is a recorded time. Confidence is medium.

All five pages we read that name a UK chart use 1 January 1801. Three are Western and two are Vedic. Only the Western pages (Greene and Goddard) give the time of 00:00 Greenwich. The Vedic pages either give no data or, in Tempora's case, an algorithmic chart; Tempora's own later audit flags its dasha work as unverified. Goddard says the 1801 chart is a good fit but that you can take your pick between 1801 and 1922.

The dispute is over which United Kingdom. The 1801 union joined Great Britain and Ireland. After the Irish Free State was established on 6 December 1922 the state became Great Britain and Northern Ireland. Some astrologers date the country from 1707, the union of England and Scotland, and the monarchy from 1066. We did not see 1707 used on any page we read. There are no calendar or time-zone traps worth worrying about: Britain used the Gregorian calendar from 1752, and London's local mean time differs from Greenwich by only about 31 seconds.

## Testing the chart against history

The rules were fixed in advance. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to that kind of event, or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7 and 8 or Mars. An economic crisis uses houses 6, 8 and 12 or Saturn. Territorial change uses houses 4, 7 and 12. A constitutional change uses houses 1, 9 and 10. A leader's death uses houses 8 and 10. Only events dated to the day are scored. "Chance" is the score that a random pair of periods would earn on the same events with the same chart. Vimshottari repeats every 120 years; for charts older than that the later cycles come from the same engine.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The hour is the main limit. Midnight is a rule for when a law takes effect, not a time anyone observed, so the Virgo ascendant and every house are provisional. The Moon's sign and nakshatra are more stable, because the Moon moves about half a degree an hour.

The choice of founding date is the second limit. A chart for 1707, 1801 or 1922 describes a different state, and the Vedic sources we found give little data. The test is the third: eleven events with generous rules cannot tell a good chart from a lucky one. The chart does not describe the monarchy, Parliament or any one government, and it makes no forecast.
