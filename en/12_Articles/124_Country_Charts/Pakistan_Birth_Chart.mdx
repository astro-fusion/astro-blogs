# Pakistan Birth Chart: Aries Rising at Midnight, 14/15 August 1947

Pakistan's birth chart is cast for the midnight between 14 and 15 August 1947 in Karachi, when the Dominion of Pakistan came into existence. Western astrologers agree on this moment almost without exception, and Vedic astrologers are split. Cast for 00:00 on 15 August, it has Aries rising at 24.4 degrees in the sidereal zodiac and the Moon in Cancer, in Pushya. Midnight is a convention, not a recorded time, so we rate confidence as medium.

Western sources are unanimous on midnight. Vedic sources are split between the midnight chart and the Astrogle charts. The research counts five of seven pages on the midnight moment.

Two traps matter. The first is the date. Pakistan celebrates 14 August, while the Indian Independence Act appointed 15 August. These are the same instant, 18:30 UTC on 14 August, and entering the wrong one is a 24-hour error that breaks the chart. The pragyapath blog says that Jinnah's first broadcast and the 1948 stamps gave 15 August as the birthday, which is why that date is preferred. The second is the time zone. Karachi was on +05:30 in August 1947 according to the Wikipedia [Time in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Pakistan) table, not on local mean time, which would be about +04:28, and not on +05:00. Pakistan Standard Time, +05:00, applies to West Pakistan from 1951.

The Astrotheme "AA" rating is unusual for a state. A state has no birth certificate, so we treat that entry as a convention, not a record. The oath chart in c4 is an event inside the founding day, not the legal creation of the dominion. A chart for the 1956 or 1973 constitution is left out because it appears only in one search excerpt that could not be opened.

## What the chart says about the country

The ascendant, Aries at 24.4 degrees, is read as a young state that acts first and asks later. Aries is ruled by Mars, so Mars is the lagna lord, and it sits in Gemini in the 3rd house, in Ardra. The 3rd house is communication, courage, neighbours and the armed effort of a state. A lagna lord there is read as a national character that shows itself through initiative and through its borders. Mars also rules the 8th house, which links the nation's identity to sudden change.

The striking feature is the group of five planets in Cancer in the 4th house: Sun at 28.0, Moon at 4.0, Mercury, Venus and Saturn. The 4th house is read as the land, the people, the home and the mass of the population. The Moon is the lord of the 4th and sits in its own sign there. This is read as a strong bond to land, to family and to a shared identity, and as a public mood that is easily moved. It puts the lords of the 2nd and 7th (Venus), the 3rd and 6th (Mercury), the 5th (Sun) and the 10th and 11th (Saturn) in one place, so the nation's wealth, its relations with other states, its government and its gains are all connected to the home ground.

Saturn, lord of the 10th (government) and 11th (parliament and gains), is placed in the 4th, in Ashlesha. In classical terms a 10th lord in the 4th is read as government tied closely to the land and to its people, with power that depends on public feeling. Saturn is in Venus's company, and Venus is lord of the 7th.

Jupiter is in Libra in the 7th house, in Vishakha. It rules the 9th and the 12th. The 7th is read as relations with other nations, so Jupiter there is read as a state whose foreign dealings are wide and tied to law and belief. Rahu is in Taurus in the 2nd house, the house of wealth and speech, and Ketu is in Scorpio in the 8th. These are readings from the tradition. They describe emphasis, and nothing here is a verdict on any people or government.

A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, counted whole-sign from the Vedic ascendant, or if it is the named planet. ## What this chart cannot tell you

Midnight is not a recorded moment. The Dominion came into being at an appointed day, not at a clock time. The ascendant is also sensitive: c1 and c2 differ by one minute, and their Vedic ascendants differ by 0.3 degrees.

The time zone is a further doubt. The +05:30 offset comes from a time-zone table that covers the whole subcontinent for 1907 to 1951, not from a Karachi record of 1947. The date label, 14 or 15 August, is a matter of convention, and the sources do not agree. And it is open whether a state's legal creation is its "birth". A chart for a later date, such as the 1956 or 1973 constitution, was mentioned in one search excerpt that could not be opened, so none could be checked.
