# Ukraine Birth Chart: Sagittarius Rising on 24 August 1991

Most astrologers cast the birth chart of Ukraine for the vote of the Verkhovna Rada adopting the Act of Declaration of Independence in Kyiv on 24 August 1991, at 18:00 local time. That gives Sagittarius rising at 22.8 degrees in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri, whole sign) and the Moon in Capricorn in Dhanishta. The date is well supported. The hour is split between 18:00 and 17:31, and a time-zone trap matters: Kyiv was on +03:00 summer time that day, not +02:00. Research confidence is medium. Both hour variants scored slightly above chance in our test.

Of the eight astrologer pages that give a chart, six use the 24 August 1991 vote. The research counts the date as high confidence. The time splits: 18:00 has three sources and 17:31 has two, and one Vedic source uses each. A search summary of a Ukrainian outlet, Obozrevatel, which we could not open, gives 17:55 for the vote. We treat that as unverified.

The two traps are the time zone and the calendar. In August 1991 the USSR was on daylight time, and the time-zone database gives +03:00 for Kyiv on 24 August. The standard +02:00 would be an hour wrong. The 17:31 time is 14:31 UT, converted at +03:00, and it matches Tempora's 17:31 EEST, so those two sources agree. Astrotheme's "birth record" label is generic, and 18:00 looks like a rounded session time and not a timestamp.

For 1918, the Fourth Universal was issued on 9 January in the Julian (old style) calendar, which is 22 January in the Gregorian calendar. No clock time is published for the 1918 chart or for the referendum, and the clock offset in Kyiv in 1918 is uncertain, so these two charts are not computed.

## What the chart says about the country

Sagittarius rising is read as a nation with a sense of mission, a taste for independence and a strong bond with ideas of freedom and belief. The ascendant lord, Jupiter, is in Leo in the 9th house, in Magha. The 9th house is the house of fortune, law, belief and long journeys, and a lagna lord there is considered a supportive placement in the tradition. The Sun, Mercury and Venus are in the same sign and house as Jupiter, so four planets occupy the 9th. Tradition reads a cluster there as a country whose identity is bound up with principle, with law and with its ancestors (Magha), and one that is much talked about abroad.

Rahu sits on the ascendant in Sagittarius at 22.9 degrees, with Ketu in Gemini in the 7th. Classically, Rahu in the 1st is read as a country that is restless and unusually visible, and the axis through the 1st and 7th is read as a theme of how the country defines itself in relation to neighbours and partners. This is a description of the tradition and not a statement about any neighbour.

The Moon is in Capricorn in Dhanishta, in the 2nd house of wealth, land and speech, with Saturn, who rules the sign. The Moon at 28.7 degrees is at the end of its sign. Moon with Saturn in the same sign is read as a serious and enduring public mood, shaped by hardship and by long attachment to the land. Mars is in Virgo in the 10th house, in Uttara Phalguni. Mars in the 10th is read as an active, combative public life, and Mercury, the 10th lord, is in the 9th with the Sun and Jupiter.

In the Western chart the Sun is in Virgo and the Moon in Aquarius, with Saturn also in Aquarius in the 1st house, and Capricorn rises. Western astrologers read Capricorn rising as a nation that has had to build itself against resistance. These are conventions and astrologers disagree among themselves.

## Testing the chart against history

We scored each timed chart with rules fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to the kind of event (whole sign, from the Vedic ascendant) or is the named planet. Constitutional change uses houses 1, 9 and 10. Territorial change uses houses 4, 7 and 12. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Only events dated to the day are scored, seven here, so the maximum is 14. "Chance" is the average score of random periods on the same events.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The referendum and 1918 charts have no published time and were not computed. The seven-event test is small. Nothing here describes the course of any war or the actions of any government, and the events are listed only with the dates given by the cited pages. No chart can predict how any conflict will end.
