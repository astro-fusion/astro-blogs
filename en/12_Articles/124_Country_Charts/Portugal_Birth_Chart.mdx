The research opened only two chart pages for Portugal. Astrotheme's 1910 entry gives 9:00 AM in Lisbon, contributed by Michael Keller with the source field blank. The 1143 entry for the Treaty of Zamora, which recognised Afonso Henriques as king according to the Astrotheme listing, was not opened. We know it only from a search summary, so it is unverified.

The 1179 and 1143 charts are dated before 1700 and were not computed for this article.

Three traps matter here.

**The time zone.** Lisbon mean time was in force in 1910, at 36.6 minutes behind GMT. Portugal adopted GMT only in 1912. Astrotheme gives no time zone, so the engine used -00:37.

**The calendar.** Medieval dates are Julian and need a seven-day shift to reach the Gregorian calendar. The 1179 source does not say which calendar it uses. The 1143 entry is listed as Julian.

**The event.** Portugal's origin can be placed at the kingdom (1128, 1143 or 1179), at the First Republic in 1910, or at the Carnation Revolution of 1974, for which no chart was found. Historians also differ on whether the 1910 uprising began on 3 or 4 October. The proclamation was on 5 October.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, and it is worded as tradition.

Libra rising is read as a state that looks for balance and good form. The lagna lord is Venus, and Venus sits in the 12th house in Virgo, in Uttara Phalguni. The 12th is the house of loss, withdrawal, foreign lands and the sea. A lagna lord there is read as an identity that is lived abroad or at a distance from home.
The 12th house is crowded. The Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter and Venus all sit in Virgo, in the 12th. The Sun and Mars are 2.6 degrees apart in the file. A cluster of five planets in the 12th is read as a great deal of energy that goes outward, into expeditions, colonies and foreign dealings, or inward, into private and hidden matters. The tradition also warns that a loaded 12th house can drain the 1st.

The Moon is in the 1st house, in Libra, in Swati, together with Ketu, in Vishakha. The Moon on the ascendant is read as a public mood that shows on the surface. Ketu beside it is read as a certain detachment. The Moon rules Cancer, the 10th sign, so the 10th lord is in the 1st. The file lists an opposition of 0.9 degrees between the Moon and Saturn.

Saturn and Rahu sit in the 7th house, in Aries, in Ashwini and Krittika. The 7th is the house of partners, treaties and open enemies. Saturn there, retrograde, is read as slow and hard bargaining, and Rahu as unusual alliances. None of this is evidence. The chart is traditional and its time is a quoted figure from a page graded C.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested the chart against dated events. The rules were fixed before it was cast. An event scores 2 points if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 point if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, or is the named planet. The houses are:

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
- death of a leader: 8, 10
- constitutional change: 1, 9, 10
- territorial change: 4, 7, 12
- natural disaster: 4, 8, 12

Only events dated to the day are scored, and only events after the chart date count. The 1755 earthquake, the 1807 occupation, Brazilian independence in 1822 and the regicide of 1 February 1908 all came before 1910 and are out of scope.
