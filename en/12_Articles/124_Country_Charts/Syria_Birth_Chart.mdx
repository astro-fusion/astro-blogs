# Syria Birth Chart: Virgo Rising at Midnight, Three Dates

The Syria birth chart most astrologers use is cast for 1 January 1944 at midnight in Damascus, the moment France agreed to hand over the remaining mandate powers. The Vedic ascendant is Virgo at 20.2 degrees and the Moon is in Aquarius in Purva Bhadrapada. Midnight is a convention, not a recorded moment, so the ascendant is only as good as that choice. We rate the confidence as medium. Two other dates are also in circulation, 27 September 1941 and 17 April 1946, and this page shows all three.

The three candidates are three different political moments. According to Wikipedia's [Independence Day (Syria)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_Day_(Syria)) article, Syria proclaimed independence on 27 September 1941, was recognised as independent on 1 January 1944, and the last French troops left on 17 April 1946. Which of these is the birth is a matter of choice, not fact.

The 1944 chart has the widest use, and several of its sources credit Nicholas Campion. Astrology King cites the Book of World Horoscopes and says midnight is consistent with transfers of mandate powers in other states. That is a reason for the convention, not a record of the hour. One source uses 12:01 AM. A forum poster gave a 06:43 variant with no source, which we do not use. The only Vedic page we found is Future Point, a commercial template page with no author and no reasoning. It lists the time as 00:00:00, which looks like a default.

The time zone is not in dispute here. Damascus was on +02:00 (Eastern European Time) on all three dates. The [tz database](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eggert/tz/main/asia) has no Syrian summer time between 1923 and 1962, so none applies. We rate the confidence as medium. Five of eight pages agree on the date and time, but the time is a convention, one source differs by a minute, and the only Vedic source uses a different date.

## What the chart says about the country

Virgo rises, so Mercury is the lagna lord. Mercury is in Capricorn in the 5th house, retrograde, in Uttara Ashadha, and shares the sign with Ketu. The tradition reads the 5th as the house of policy, intelligence and the young, and a lagna lord there as an identity expressed through ideas and administration. A retrograde lagna lord is read as a character that revisits its own questions. Ketu in the same sign is read as a theme of detachment from what the 5th house signifies. These are classical readings, offered as description.

The Moon, which signifies the people, is in Aquarius in the 6th house, in Purva Bhadrapada. The 6th is the house of service, debt, disputes and effort against obstacles, and Aquarius is a sign of groups and networks. The Moon also rules the 11th house (Cancer), the house of gains and alliances, so the people's mood is read as tied to both strain and association. Rahu sits in the 11th, in Cancer in Pushya, which the tradition reads as a strong pull toward outside alliances and sudden shifts among them.

The 9th house holds Mars and Saturn together in Taurus, with Saturn at 28.8 degrees at the very end of the sign. The 9th is the house of law, belief, fortune and foreign dealings. Mars and Saturn together there are read as a hard, disciplined and often contested relationship with law and belief. Saturn rules the 5th and 6th houses for Virgo rising, so the same planet that sits in the 9th also carries the lagna lord's house of policy and the Moon's house of disputes. The 10th house, Gemini, is empty, and its lord is Mercury, which is also the lagna lord. That ties the country's public standing to the lagna lord's placement in the 5th.

Jupiter, retrograde in Leo in the 12th house, is the other point worth noting. The 12th is the house of loss, distance and what lies beyond the border. Jupiter there is read as a protective force that works from outside the national frame. Venus in Scorpio in the 3rd and the Sun in Sagittarius in the 4th complete a chart in which the 3rd to 6th houses are heavily occupied. The tradition reads that as a country defined by effort, communication and home affairs more than by display.

## What this chart cannot tell you

The midnight time is a convention, so the Virgo ascendant, the houses and the dasha dates all depend on it. The dasha dates depend on the Moon's exact degree, which also depends on the time. Only the date is documented.

The choice of founding is open. The 1941 proclamation, the 1944 transfer and the 1946 evacuation each have a case, and the Vedic page we found uses 1946 with a placeholder time. We found no Vedic astrologer who discusses the 1944 chart.

Some dates of the events themselves differ between sources. Wikipedia gives 16 November 1970 for the Corrective Movement, while Astrology King and The Oxford Astrologer say 13 November. Wikipedia's article on Hafez al-Assad gives 10 June 2000 in its infobox and lead but 11 June in one passage. We used the first date in each case. A chart cannot attribute fault or intent to any government, group or leader, and nothing here does.
