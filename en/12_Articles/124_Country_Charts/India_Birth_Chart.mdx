# India Birth Chart: Taurus Rising at Midnight, and What Is Disputed

India's birth chart is cast for midnight at the start of 15 August 1947 in New Delhi, the moment the Dominion of India came into being. That is the chart most Vedic and Western astrologers use, and it has Taurus rising at 7.7 degrees in the sidereal zodiac. Nobody can call it a recorded birth time. Midnight is a convention, and some astrologers reject any 1947 chart. We rate confidence as medium, not high, and the rest of this page shows why.

Against nine dated events it scored 11 of 18 where a random chart would score 9.0. From 7 February 2026 to 25 February 2027 the Mars mahadasha runs with the Rahu antardasha. The research counts nine pages that cast a national chart. Eight use the 1947 midnight. The ninth, by Raghvendra Khare on the [pragyapath blog](https://pragyapath25.blogspot.com/2014/12/india-astrologically.html), argues that independence is not the birth of the nation and that deriving India's sign from this chart is "a fundamental error". That is an objection to the idea, not a different chart. The 1950 republic date is the other alternative. It is confirmed as a date on Wikipedia's [Constitution of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_of_India) page, but no time could be verified, and no Vedic practitioner page using it was found. We therefore have no engine chart for it and give no positions.

The research warns that many of the eight sites are popular sites that copy each other. Agreement among them is weaker evidence than it looks.

The time is where the argument sits. The Constituent Assembly session ended its "tryst with destiny" proceedings at about midnight, and the legal appointed day began at 00:00 on 15 August. Vaya adds that Pt. Suryanarayan Vyas advised an Abhijit muhurta of 11:51 PM to 12:39 AM. A popular story says astrologers shaped the midnight timing. It comes from Collins and Lapierre's "Freedom at Midnight" and later retellings, and the research notes that historians treat it with caution; the sources for it were search excerpts and were not fully read.

Calendar and zone traps:

- The same moment is the end of 14 August for Pakistan-based sources and the start of 15 August for India. 00:00 on 15 August 1947 is 18:30 UTC on 14 August.
- Delhi used Indian Standard Time, +05:30. Kolkata local time was +05:53:20, so check what your software defaults to.
- A Hindu day begins at sunrise, which is part of the astrologer story above.
- The ascendant sign is disputed: Taurus on most sites, Cancer on Vaya and on the earlier edition of Tempora, which Tempora corrected on 10 June 2026 to Taurus 8.86 using the True Pushya ayanamsa of PVRN Rao.

## What the chart says about the country

The Ascendant is read as the nation. Taurus rising is read as a land tied to the soil, to wealth and to endurance, and Astromedha and InstaAstro both mention Rahu in the first house. Rahu in the ascendant is read as a country that takes in foreign ideas and reinvents itself, and as a nature that is hard to categorise. Its Krittika position is the Sun's nakshatra. The lagna lord Venus is in the 3rd house, in Cancer, in Ashlesha.

The 3rd house is the house of effort, courage, communication, neighbours and short journeys. Five planets stand in it: Sun at 28.0, Moon, Mercury, Venus and Saturn. A stellium of this size is the most striking feature of the chart. It is read as a nation whose strength lies in its many voices, its trade, its media and its neighbourhood relations, and whose energy goes into exertion rather than rest. It also puts the lord of the 1st, the lord of the 3rd (Moon), the lord of the 4th (Sun), the lord of the 2nd and 5th (Mercury) and the lord of the 9th and 10th (Saturn) in a single sign. Many threads of the country's life are tied together in one place.

Saturn is the key planet in this reading. For Taurus rising it rules both the 9th and the 10th house, law and government. It is placed in the 3rd with four other planets, in Ashlesha. The Moon is in Pushya, a nakshatra that Saturn rules. Tradition reads this as a government shaped by patient, heavy, law-bound institutions, with change that comes slowly. The 10th house itself, Aquarius, is empty.

The Moon, in Cancer at 4.0 degrees in the 3rd house, is the public mood. It is the lord of the 3rd house and sits in its own sign, which is read as a population that is strongly rooted in family, home and culture. Mars, the lord of the 7th and 12th, is in Gemini in the 2nd house in Ardra. The 7th is read as dealings with other nations and the 12th as losses and distant lands. Ketu is in the 7th house in Scorpio. Jupiter, lord of the 8th and 11th, is in Libra in the 6th house, the house of service, disputes and the armed forces, and is read as a country that carries long-standing disputes and a large public service.

This is the tradition's reading of the placements. It is not a measurement of the country.

## Testing the chart against history

The scoring rules were fixed before the charts were cast. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event, counted whole-sign from the Vedic ascendant, or if it is the named planet. The houses are: war 6, 7, 8 or Mars; constitutional change 1, 9, 10; leader death 8, 10; economic crisis 6, 8, 12 or Saturn; natural disaster 4, 8, 12; pandemic 6, 8, 12. Only events dated to the day and with a rule are scored.

A chart whose score depends on that is not tightly pinned down. We do not call either chart verified. All nine events are used for both charts, and the 1950 republic chart could not be scored at all because it has no time.

## What this chart cannot tell you

Midnight is a convention. The chart is built on the legal start of 15 August, and no source records an instant at which India was "born". The ascendant moves from Taurus 7.7 at 00:00 to Taurus 8.0 at 00:01, so every minute changes the degree, and sites that disagree on the time also disagree on the sign: Taurus on most, Cancer on two.

The date itself is questioned. Khare argues independence is not the nation's birth, and the 1950 republic date has some Western support from a search excerpt we could not open. India's long history does not start in 1947, and a "birth chart" of the state is a chart of a government, not of a civilisation. Our test uses nine events and a simple scoring rule that gives a random chart 9 of 18 points. Neither is large enough to decide the question.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is India's rising sign?**
On the widely used midnight chart of 15 August 1947 the sidereal ascendant is Taurus, at 7.7 degrees. The tropical (Western) ascendant of the same chart is Gemini 0.9. A few sites give Cancer, so the sign depends on the time and the system used.

**What is India's zodiac sign?**
In the sidereal zodiac the Sun is in Cancer at 28.0 degrees in Ashlesha, and the Moon is in Cancer too, in Pushya. In the tropical zodiac the Sun is in Leo at 21.1.

**Which dasha is India running in 2026?**
On the c1 chart, the Mars mahadasha (11 September 2025 to 11 September 2032) with the Rahu antardasha, from 7 February 2026 to 25 February 2027. Jupiter antardasha follows.

**Why is India's chart set at midnight?**
Because the legal appointed day for the Dominion of India began at 00:00 on 15 August 1947 and the Constituent Assembly's midnight session marked it. The research records midnight as a convention, not a recorded moment.

**Does the 1950 republic chart give a different answer?**
We cannot say. Only a search excerpt mentions an astrologer who prefers it, no time is verified, and we did not compute it.
