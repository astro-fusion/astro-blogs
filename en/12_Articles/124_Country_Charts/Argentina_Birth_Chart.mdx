Two traps matter here.

**The event.** Argentina has two founding moments in common use: the May Revolution in Buenos Aires on 25 May 1810, when the first national government (the Primera Junta) was formed, and the declaration of independence at Tucumán on 9 July 1816. All the 1816 variants agree on the date and differ only in the time of day.

**The place and clock.** There was no standard time in 1816. The chart needs the local mean time of the city, and the city is Tucumán, not Buenos Aires. Local mean time in Tucumán is 4 hours 21 minutes behind UTC, while Buenos Aires would be 3 hours 53 minutes behind. Using the wrong city moves the ascendant.

## What the chart says about the country

This is a reading in the classical manner, worded as tradition. Because the time is noon by convention, the ascendant and the houses are a working frame.

Libra rising is read as a state that seeks balance, appearances and alliance. The lagna lord is Venus, and it sits in Gemini in the 9th house, with the Sun and with retrograde Mercury. Three planets in the 9th house, the house of law, belief, higher learning and long journeys, is read as a nation whose identity is tied to ideas, to the law and to a story about itself. The Sun is at 25.9 degrees and Venus at 19.8 degrees, so the group spans about 11 degrees, with Mercury in between at 15.3.

Jupiter is in the 1st house in Libra, in Swati. A benefic on the ascendant is read as an optimistic, expansive character and as protection for the whole chart. That is the standard tradition for Jupiter in the lagna, and it is colour only.

The Moon is in Sagittarius in the 3rd house, in Uttara Ashadha, at 27.9 degrees. The 3rd house is the house of communication, courage and the media. The Moon is the public mood and the 10th lord for Libra rising, so the tradition links popular feeling, the press and public standing. The Sun stands opposite the Moon, 1.9 degrees apart, which is close. A Sun-Moon opposition is read as a tension between those who govern and those who are governed.

The 10th house is Cancer, and Mars is in it, in Ashlesha. Mars is the planet of force and the army, and in Cancer the classical books call it weak. A weak Mars in the house of government is read as power that depends on soldiers or on strong figures, but this is a symbol and no more. Mars stands opposite Saturn, 6.1 degrees apart, and Saturn is retrograde in Aquarius in the 5th house. Rahu is in the 8th house in Taurus and Ketu in the 2nd, which the tradition links to hidden resources, debt and sudden changes of fortune.

None of this is evidence. The time is a convention, and the chart rests on one page.

- war: 6, 7, 8, or Mars
