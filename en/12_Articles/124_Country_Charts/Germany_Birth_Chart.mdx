# Germany Birth Chart: Cancer Rising at Reunification, 3 Oct 1990

There is no single Germany birth chart. Astrologers cast it for three different moments: the German Empire in 1871, the Federal Republic in 1949, and reunified Germany at 00:00 on 3 October 1990. We lead with the 1990 chart because it is the best documented and the most visible online, and because it describes the state that exists now. It has Cancer rising in the sidereal zodiac and Leo rising in the tropical one. Confidence is low: the sources disagree on the era, and the chart with the longest event record scores no better than chance.

We opened only seven pages that chart Germany, and they point at three eras. No Vedic astrologer was found; Lagna360 is software that lists all three charts without times. The research chose 1871 as its default only because it is tied to Campion and has the longest history to test. We lead with 1990 instead, and give 1871 as the historical chart.

Two time-zone traps matter. German summer time had ended on 30 September 1990, so Berlin was on Central European Time (UTC+1) at midnight on 3 October. Astrodienst uses CET. Wikipedia gives 00:00 CEST, a conflict the research could not resolve, and our engine used +01:00. For 1949, West Germany was on summer time that day, so the chart is computed at +02:00, though the research could not confirm that independently. Before 1893 Berlin used local mean time, 53 minutes 38 seconds ahead of Greenwich, which is what the 1871 chart uses. Which Germany counts is a second dispute: the Empire, Weimar, the Nazi state, West Germany or the reunified state.

## Testing the chart against history

The rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies a house linked to that kind of event, or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. An economic crisis uses houses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. A constitutional change uses houses 1, 9 and 10. Territorial change uses houses 4, 7 and 12. Only events dated to the day count. Vimshottari repeats every 120 years; for charts older than that the later cycles come from the same engine.

The 1990 chart can be scored on one event only: reunification itself, the moment the chart is cast for. It gets 2 points (Jupiter / Mercury / Jupiter). That is circular and tells us nothing: an event on the founding day always falls in the first mahadasha. The 1949 chart, scored on the same day, gets 2 points (Moon / Moon / Sun). ## What this chart cannot tell you

The choice of chart is the main limit. A reader who casts Germany for 1871 gets Virgo rising and a Moon in Aries; for 1949, Sagittarius rising and a Moon in Pisces. The sources disagree and there is no Vedic convention. The 1990 chart is the best documented but the youngest, so it has no event record to test it with.

Midnight is a legal moment, and the offset is a second limit. Whether midnight on 3 October 1990 was CET or CEST changes the instant by an hour, and the Wikipedia and Astrodienst figures conflict. The 1871 time has only one source, and the 18 January proclamation time of 1:00 pm from a search summary is unverified. The chart says nothing about any one government, and it gives no forecast.
