# Bangladesh Birth Chart: Taurus Rising on Victory Day, Low Confidence

The Bangladesh chart astrologers use most is Victory Day, 16 December 1971, when the Pakistani Eastern Command surrendered at Ramna Race Course in Dhaka. The time given is 17:00, which puts Taurus rising at 28.0 degrees in the sidereal zodiac and the Moon in Scorpio. Nobody can be sure of it. The time comes from a single Vedic author who credits Nicholas Campion, another site uses 11:00, and Bangladesh's own Independence Day is 26 March. Our confidence is low.

The two Malhotra pages are by one author, so c1 rests on one independent source. The research also read an [astroseekers](https://www.astroseekers.com/post/bangladesh-s-government-change-astrological-insights) piece that relies on Campion but states no date or time. A search excerpt said that the astrologer Anil Aggarwala uses 11:01 and cites Campion; his page was down when opened, so that is unverified. If it were true, it would contradict Malhotra's attribution of 17:00 to Campion. We could not read Campion directly.

The dispute is about what the "birth" is. Bangladesh celebrates 26 March, the day Sheikh Mujibur Rahman proclaimed independence, at 12:20 AM according to Wikipedia's page on the [Declaration of Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladeshi_Declaration_of_Independence). The Future Point chart uses a flat 00:00, not 00:20. The Mujibnagar government of 10 April 1971 is a defensible third choice, with no time. The majority of astrologers pick the surrender of 16 December, the date the Pakistani forces in Dhaka surrendered, and Wikipedia gives the place but no clock time.

The research flags that the sources contradict themselves on the rising sign. One page puts Taurus at the surrender moment and also calls the same chart an "Aquarius lagna foundation chart". In our engine, 17:00 gives Taurus rising and 11:00 gives Aquarius, so both signs exist in the data, but the research does not say which time the page meant.

The time-zone trap: Bangladesh used +06:00 (Dacca Time), while Pakistan Standard Time was +05:00. The Wikipedia pages on [Time in Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Bangladesh) and [Time in Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_in_Pakistan) do not confirm the offset for 16 December 1971 explicitly. The engine used +06:00 for all three charts. Check what your own software defaults to.

## What the chart says about the country

The ascendant is at Taurus 28.0, within two degrees of the border with Gemini. A small change in the time moves it across that border, which is one reason the chart must be held loosely. In our engine the 11:00 chart has Aquarius rising, so the sign changes within six hours.

On this chart Saturn is in the 1st house, in Taurus, retrograde, in Krittika. For Taurus rising, Saturn rules the 9th and the 10th house, law and government. Placed in the ascendant, it is read as a state whose identity is tied to burdens, to labour and to hard, slow construction. The lagna lord Venus is in the 8th house in Sagittarius, with the Sun. The 8th house is read as sudden change, shared resources and endings. A lagna lord there is classically read as a nation whose life has been shaped by crisis and recovery.

The most striking feature is the group in Scorpio in the 7th house: the Moon at 15.0, Mercury at 22.1 and Jupiter at 25.4. The 7th is read as relations with other nations. The Moon in Scorpio is the classical weak (debilitated) Moon. It rules the 3rd house, so the public mood is read as intense and strongly felt. Mercury (lord of the 2nd and 5th) and Jupiter (lord of the 8th and 11th) are with it, so wealth, learning, shared resources and gains are all tied to the 7th. This is read as a country whose fortunes are closely bound to its neighbours and to its partners abroad.

Mars is in Aquarius in the 10th house at the very end of the sign, in Purva Bhadrapada. It rules the 7th and the 12th. Mars in the 10th is read as a strong, assertive government, and the 7th-lord connection ties that government to foreign relations. Rahu is in Capricorn in the 9th house and Ketu in Cancer in the 3rd. These are readings from the tradition. They describe emphasis, and they are not verdicts on any government or people.

## What this chart cannot tell you

There is no recorded time. The surrender at Ramna Race Course has a date and a place, but no clock time in the source we could read, and 17:00 and 11:00 are both used. The Wikipedia page on the declaration of independence gives 00:20 for 26 March 1971, and the Future Point chart uses 00:00, so even that moment is not pinned down.

The ascendant sits at 28.0 degrees of Taurus, so a few minutes' difference could change the sign. The offset of +06:00 is likely but not confirmed for that day. The one author behind c1 wrote two pages, and we found no Western chart to compare. The surrender of 16 December 1971, the declaration of 26 March and the government of 10 April are three different answers to the question of when the country began, and the people of Bangladesh mark 26 March. Any chart for this country is a choice among them.
