# Thailand Birth Chart: Candidate Charts, No Agreed Founding

There is no agreed birth chart for Thailand. Western astrologers mostly use the constitutional revolution of 24 June 1932, with the time given as 05:00 in Bangkok. Thai tradition prefers the erection of the city pillar of Bangkok on 21 April 1782. This article sets out the candidate charts, uses the 1932 chart at 05:00 as its working example (Taurus rising at 26.3 degrees, Moon in Aquarius), and shows what the sources and the scores can and cannot support. We rate the confidence as low.

The sources disagree about what the country's founding is. The 1932 coup ended the absolute monarchy. The permanent constitution of 10 December 1932 is a second candidate. The 1782 pillar chart marks the founding of Bangkok as the Chakri capital and is recorded in the tradition as a "war victory" election with Aries rising. The 05:00 time for the coup has no explanation in the sources. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siamese_revolution_of_1932), troops reached the throne hall plaza at about 06:00 and the operation was over by 08:00, so 05:00 sits inside the event but is not a documented moment. The noon charts are a default for a missing time.

Two traps apply. The 1782 chart predates standard time. The engine file uses Bangkok mean time, +06:42, but the Thai court did not keep time as a clock offset, so the hour is uncertain. Thailand also used a solar calendar in which the new year began on 1 April until 1941, so dates from Thai sources must be checked against the Gregorian date given.

## What the chart says about the country

This reading applies to the 05:00 candidate only. It is a candidate chart, not a settled one.

Taurus rises, so Venus is the lagna lord. Venus is in Gemini in the 2nd house, retrograde, in Ardra, with the Sun and Mercury. The 2nd is the house of wealth, speech and family. The tradition reads this as an identity expressed through trade, speech and the arts, with the Sun and Mercury adding administration. Mars sits in the 1st, in Taurus, in Krittika. Mars in the 1st is read as a firm, defended national character.

The Moon is in Aquarius in the 10th house, in Shatabhisha, with Rahu at 28.1 degrees in Purva Bhadrapada. The Moon signifies the people, and the 10th is the house of government and standing. The tradition reads a Moon in the 10th as a public that is closely tied to its leadership. Rahu beside it is read as a theme of sudden or unconventional change in public life. This is a description of the tradition, not a claim about events.

Saturn is in Capricorn in the 9th, retrograde, in Shravana. The 9th is the house of law, belief and fortune, and Saturn there in its own sign is read as endurance and respect for hierarchy and custom. Jupiter is in Cancer in the 3rd at 27.4 degrees, in Ashlesha, a late degree. Ketu is in Leo in the 4th, the house of the homeland, which the tradition reads as detachment from comfort or from fixed ground.

The ascendant at 26.3 degrees of Taurus is 3.7 degrees from Gemini. A small shift in the time would move the whole chart to Gemini rising, which would put Mars in the 12th house instead of the 1st.

## Testing the chart against history

The scoring rules were fixed in advance. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses linked to the kind of event (counted from the Vedic ascendant), or is the named planet. The houses were 1, 9, 10 for a constitutional change and 4, 8, 12 for a natural disaster. Vimshottari repeats every 120 years; for charts older than that the later cycles come from the same engine. ## What this chart cannot tell you

There is no agreed chart. The 1932 time is a guess, the 1782 hour comes from a tradition that did not use clock time, and the Thai tradition and the Western tradition use different moments. A reading from one candidate does not carry over to another, because the rising sign and the houses change.

The test is also too small. Two events cannot separate charts, and the three charts that could be scored are at or below chance. We found no Vedic astrologer's own published analysis of Thailand, only Thai-tradition sources for the 1782 chart. Another author uses a chart for the 14 October 1973 democracy movement and states that it is not a national chart, which we do not use here.
