# Netherlands Birth Chart: Gemini Rising for 1815, and the 1581 Gap

The birth chart of the Netherlands is most often cast for the Act of Abjuration, signed on 26 July 1581 (Julian calendar, which is 5 August 1581 in the Gregorian calendar) in The Hague, at a conventional noon. That chart is the research consensus, but it is older than 1700 and our engine did not compute it, as with every pre-1700 candidate in this series. So the chart sections below are built on the second main candidate: the Kingdom of the Netherlands, 16 March 1815, 10:30 in The Hague. It gives Gemini rising in the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri). Research confidence in the 1581 consensus is medium, not high, because no time is recorded for it, the kingdom chart is a real rival, and no Vedic source was found.

The research found no Vedic source for the Netherlands. Every chart above is Western, and the sidereal reading here is our own computation, not an established Dutch practice.

The 1581 chart is the consensus because astro.com and Astrology Weekly both use it, and the Astrotheme page of the same date appeared in a search summary. Its time is a convention: no hour is recorded. The kingdom chart is used by the two Dutch-leaning sources. The Act of Abjuration is described on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Act_of_Abjuration) as the moment the States General abjured Philip II. William I declared himself king on 16 March 1815 according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom_of_the_Netherlands). The research also saw a forum thread mentioning a 1813 chart for William's return; it could not be verified and is left out.

There are two traps. The first is the calendar. 26 July 1581 and 23 January 1579 are Julian dates. Holland adopted the Gregorian calendar in December 1582, so the Gregorian equivalents are 5 August 1581 and, as an assumption, 2 February 1579. The astro.com page does not say which calendar it uses. The second is the clock. No standard time existed in 1581 or 1815, so all times are local mean time of The Hague, about +00:17. The two kingdom times, 10:30 and 11:47, are 77 minutes apart, and we show below how much that moves the ascendant.

## What the chart says about the country

Gemini rising is read as a trading, talking, information-handling nation. The lagna lord, Mercury, sits in Pisces in the 10th house, retrograde, with the Sun and Venus. In the tradition, the lord of the self placed in the house of government and public standing ties the national identity to its public institutions. Mercury also rules the 4th house of land and home. Here the Sun, Mercury and Venus together in the 10th make a crowded house of public life. We describe the pattern and do not claim it explains events.

The 10th house lord is Jupiter, which sits in Virgo in the 4th house, retrograde. Jupiter also rules the 7th house, the house of partners and foreign dealings. So the lord of government sits in the house of land and home, and also rules the house of dealings with others. That is a classical picture of a state whose public role and its territory are closely linked, and which looks outward through the 7th. The 7th house itself holds Mars and Ketu in Sagittarius, with Mars at 28.4 degrees, near the end of the sign.

The Moon, which stands for the people, is in Taurus in the 12th house, in Krittika. The 12th is the house of distance, loss and what lies beyond the borders. The Moon rules the 2nd house of resources and speech. Taurus is the sign of the Moon's exaltation in the classical scheme, which is read as a steady public mood even in the house of distance. Venus, lord of the 5th and 12th, is in Pisces at 22.9 in Revati, the sign of its exaltation.

Saturn, lord of the 8th and 9th, sits in Capricorn in the 8th house in its own sign. The 8th is read as the house of sudden change, shared funds and endurance, and the 9th as law and belief. Rahu is in Gemini in the 1st house with the lagna, and Ketu is in Sagittarius in the 7th. This axis of the nodes across the 1st and 7th is read as a theme of identity set against the outside world. All of this is description in the language of the tradition, not a forecast.

## Testing the chart against history

We tested each computed candidate against dated events. Rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the relevant houses (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. War uses houses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Economic crisis uses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. Leader death uses 8 and 10. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Territorial change uses 4, 7 and 12. Natural disaster uses 4, 8 and 12. Pandemic uses 6, 8 and 12. Only events dated to the day are scored. Four events before 1815 (1602, 1648, 1672 and 1795) have no period in a chart that starts in 1815, so eight events are scored.
