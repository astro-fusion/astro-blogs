Rao writes that among the different dates for the horoscope of Saudi Arabia, the most accepted is the one of 21 September, 12 LMT, Riyadh. His page gives no year. We assumed 1932 from the day and month, which is our assumption and not his statement, so treat c3 as unverified. Tempora's Cancer rising implies a time it does not state, so we could not recover it. Tempora also says the Saudi chart is not in its calibrated set.

The research flags two disputes. The first is the founding concept: the modern kingdom in 1932 against the capture of Riyadh in 1902, which began the Third Saudi State. The second is the day within 1932. Wikipedia gives 23 September for both the decree and the proclamation. Rao uses 21 September. One possible reason is a difference in converting between the Hijri and Gregorian calendars, but we could not verify that. Saudi Founding Day on 22 February refers to the First Saudi State in 1727 and no source we read uses it as a chart.

The clock is a trap. Saudi Arabia had no standard time in 1932 or in 1902, so Riyadh local mean time (+03:07) is the only coherent clock. Using a plain +03:00 would be wrong. Our engine used +03:07 for all three timed charts.

## Testing the chart against history

The rules were fixed before scoring. An event scores 2 if the antardasha lord qualifies and 1 if only the mahadasha lord does. A lord qualifies if it rules or occupies one of the houses for that kind of event (whole-sign, from the ascendant) or is the named planet. Boom uses houses 2, 5, 11 or Jupiter. Leader death uses 8 and 10. Constitutional change uses 1, 9 and 10. Economic crisis uses 6, 8, 12 or Saturn. War uses 6, 7, 8 or Mars. Only events dated to the day are scored. "Chance" is what a random pair of periods would score on the same events with the same chart.
