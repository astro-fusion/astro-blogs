# Jensen Huang: No Birth Time, Two Candidate Charts, One Mars With Rahu

Astrotheme casts its chart for noon and says so, and we found no Astro-Databank record. That means no Ascendant, no houses and no divisional charts can be stated as fact. This article shows what is fixed for the whole day, how we tried to recover the time, and why we stopped at two candidates instead of choosing one.

He is in the news for a reason that matters to the reading. In November 2026 he told Nvidia staff he had been unfaithful to his wife, as reported by the Washington Post, and the board opened an independent investigation. A second relationship, with an executive who reported to him, and a board plan to replace him have been reported from unnamed sources and are not confirmed. A chart cannot establish any of it and this one is not used to.

## Why we did not choose

Both charts account for the career and both account for November 2026. That is the weakness. A debilitated Mars with Rahu answers to a scandal from the 7th house as readily as from the 10th, so the present crisis cannot be used to pick between them. The two ways of scoring disagree. Taking the middle of each window and all ten events, A leads 18 to 15. Averaging over every three minutes of each window, B leads 15.7 to 15.5. On the five events dated to the day alone, B is clearly ahead, about 9.5 out of 10 against 6. That leans toward B, but five events is too few to call it.

A marriage date to the month, or either child's birth date, would likely settle it. Until one is sourced, every period table above is conditional on a time that is not recorded.
