# Paul McCartney: Venus Ran the Beatles, Mars Ran the Year Linda Died

Paul McCartney was born on 18 June 1942 at 14:00 in Liverpool. Astro-Databank rates the time A, from memory, and records the zone as BDST. In June 1942 Britain was on British Double Summer Time, two hours ahead of GMT, not the one hour of ordinary summer time. The chart is calculated at offset +2. Using Europe/London for that date would apply only +1 and shift the lagna.

Lahiri gives Virgo lagna, Moon in Cancer in Ashlesha pada 3, Sun in Gemini. Ashlesha is ruled by Mercury.

## A twenty-year Venus period, and four dates inside it

On 12 June 1965 the Birthday Honours named him a Member of the Order of the British Empire. The investiture was later. The period on the honours date is Venus / Rahu / Ketu. Rahu is in the 12th. The honor is a state ceremony in the antardasha of the 12th-house node, inside the 9th lord's major period.

On 12 March 1969 he married Linda Eastman. The period is Venus / Jupiter / Rahu. Jupiter rules the 7th and sits in the 10th. The marriage lord's antardasha falls in the same Venus mahadasha as the MBE.

On 10 April 1970 Paul McCartney announced that he had left the Beatles. The period is Venus / Saturn / Mercury. Saturn rules the 6th, the house of separation and of conflict, and sits in the 9th. Mercury, the pratyantar lord, is the lagna lord and the 10th lord. The band ends in the 6th lord's antardasha. The major period is still the one that built the band. Venus mahadasha has six years left.

On 15 April 1971 he won the Academy Award for best original score, for *Let It Be*. The period is Venus / Saturn / Moon. The prize for the work that belongs to the breakup arrives in the next pratyantar of that same Saturn antardasha.

## 17 April 1998 is not a divorce in the public record

The life-event table types 17 April 1998 as a divorce from Linda McCartney. Linda died that day. The row is the database's way of closing a marriage. The period is Mars / Venus / Saturn.

Mars mahadasha had been running since 30 June 1992. Mars rules the 8th and sits in the 11th. Venus, the antardasha lord, is the planet that occupies the 8th, and it rules the 2nd, the house of the family one keeps. The finest period is Saturn, 6th lord in the 9th. The reading is that an 8th-house topic, death, is timed by the 8th lord's major period and by the antardasha of the planet sitting in the 8th. Calling the row a divorce would hide the event the date actually is.

## The next marriage chapter is Rahu

The antardasha at the end is the 10th lord.

On 9 October 2011 he married Nancy Shevell. The period is Rahu / Venus / Moon. Venus has returned as antardasha lord, the same planet that timed Linda's marriage and that occupied the 8th at her death. Rahu mahadasha continues until 30 June 2017.
