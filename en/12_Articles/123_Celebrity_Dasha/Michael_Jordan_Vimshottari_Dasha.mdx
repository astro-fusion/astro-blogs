# Michael Jordan: One Mahadasha Held the Bulls Years

Michael Jordan was born on 17 February 1963 at 13:40 in Brooklyn. Astro-Databank rates that time AA, from a birth record. With Lahiri ayanamsa the chart is Gemini lagna, Moon in Scorpio in Jyeshtha pada 1, Sun in Aquarius. Jyeshtha is ruled by Mercury, so the opening mahadasha is Mercury, and only a short balance of it remains.

This reading uses that clock. A noon chart would move the lagna and is not used.

## The draft is Ketu. The titles are Venus

On 19 June 1984 the Chicago Bulls drafted him. The period is Ketu / Jupiter / Venus. Ketu mahadasha is the seven years that cover the University of North Carolina and the entrance into the NBA. Jupiter, the antardasha lord, is the 10th lord sitting in the 9th. The career lord's sub-period is the one that puts him into the league. Venus is only the pratyantar that day. Venus has not yet taken the major period.

Venus mahadasha begins on 2 January 1987. From there the dated events we can place are:

- 12 May 1988, NBA All-Defensive Team, the one award in our database with a real day: Venus / Venus / Rahu. Rahu is in Cancer, the 2nd, the house of the face the public pays for.
- 12 June 1991, the first NBA title: Venus / Moon / Moon. The Moon is the 2nd lord, in Scorpio, the 6th house of opponents. The finest period that day is the Moon's own sub-sub-period. The title is a 6th-house result inside the 5th lord's major period.
- 6 October 1993, the first retirement: Venus / Mars / Ketu. He leaves the game inside the same mahadasha that produced the titles. Mars rules the 6th, where the Moon sits. The antardasha changes. The mahadasha does not.
- 18 March 1995, the return to the Bulls: Venus / Rahu / Saturn. Rahu in the 2nd again, now as antardasha lord. Saturn, the pratyantar lord, rules the 8th and the 9th and sits in the 8th.

The second three-peat, through 1998, is still Venus. Wikidata stored most of the MVP and Finals awards as 1 January of the award year, which is a year bucket, not a day. Those rows are not used as day triggers. They do confirm that the award years 1988 through 1998 sit inside this one Venus mahadasha.

## What the period explains, and what it does not

Venus does not "cause" six championships. The structure is narrower than that. The 5th lord occupies the 7th, and the nineteen years of that planet contain the whole Chicago playing career: arrival as a finished player, titles, a retirement, a return, and the last titles. The draft, which is an entrance rather than a peak, falls in the previous mahadasha, in the sub-period of the 10th lord.

The database date for the marriage to Juanita Vanoy is 1 January 1989, and the divorce is 1 January 2002. Both are year-only. Both fall inside Venus by that coarse test, and the 1989 antardasha on 1 January is still Venus / Venus. A year bucket cannot time a wedding. The second marriage is stored as 1 January 2013, which is the hinge between Sun mahadasha and Moon mahadasha. That row is not a day.

Sun mahadasha, 2007 to 2013, is the 9th lord's period. The Sun sits in the 9th. The playing peak is already over. The honors that come later, including the Presidential Medal of Freedom stored at year-level in 2016, fall in Moon mahadasha.
