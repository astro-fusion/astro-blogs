## Ketu and the years before the breakthrough

The Ketu mahadasha (October 1888 to October 1895) covers the secondary school years in Munich, the failed entrance exam to ETH Zurich in 1895, and the year at the cantonal school in Aarau. Ketu is the planet of moksha, prior preparation, and hidden accumulation. In Gemini lagna charts, Ketu gives results coloured by its house and sign position. This was not a period of public achievement; it was a period of formation. The ETH Zurich entrance failure in October 1895 falls in the final weeks of Ketu mahadasha — a classic Ketu ending: the door closed before Venus could open the right one.

## Venus and the five extraordinary years within twenty

Venus mahadasha begins in October 1895. In October of the same month, Einstein enrolls at the cantonal school in Aarau. In 1896 he renounces German citizenship to avoid military service and enters ETH Zurich. In 1900 he earns his physics teaching diploma. In 1901 he publishes his first academic paper, on capillary forces, in the Annalen der Physik.

Then comes 1905. In a single calendar year — entirely inside Venus mahadasha — Einstein publishes four papers that are collectively called the Annus Mirabilis papers. The photoelectric effect (which will win the Nobel Prize sixteen years later), Brownian motion, special relativity, and mass-energy equivalence (E=mc²) are all submitted between March and September 1905. The 5th lord's period produces original theoretical work at a scale not seen in a single year by any physicist before or since.

Venus rules the 5th (intellect, authored works) and the 12th (isolation, inner work, the foreign land). Einstein wrote the 1905 papers while working six days a week as a patent examiner in Bern — a 12th-house occupation, examining the inventions of others in relative anonymity. The 5th lord rules what a person creates; the 12th lord rules where they retreat to do it.

By 1909, within Venus mahadasha, he receives his first university professorship. By 1911 he moves to Prague as a full professor. The Venus period closes in October 1915.

## Sun and the confirmation of general relativity

Sun mahadasha begins in October 1915. Within weeks, on 25 November 1915, Einstein presents the final field equations of general relativity to the Prussian Academy of Sciences. The timing is exact: the Sun's period opens and the most complete theory of his life is submitted.

The Sun rules the 3rd house for Gemini lagna (Leo is the 3rd). The 3rd is the house of communication, manuscripts, and declarations. The Sun is the natural karaka for authority and self-expression. The general relativity paper is the definitive communication of his life's theoretical work.

On 29 May 1919, the solar eclipse expedition led by Arthur Eddington confirms the bending of starlight around the Sun as predicted by general relativity. This is published in November 1919. Einstein becomes a household name globally. The period is Sun mahadasha. The 10th lord (Jupiter) in the Sun's mahadasha is confirming the career achievement through a celestial event — the Sun itself, bent into evidence.

## Moon and the Nobel Prize

Moon mahadasha begins in October 1921. The Nobel Committee had withheld the 1921 Prize. On 9 November 1922, in the second year of Moon mahadasha, the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics is officially announced and awarded to Einstein for the photoelectric effect — the paper he wrote in 1905, inside Venus, the 5th lord's period.

For Gemini lagna, the Moon rules the 2nd house (Cancer). The 2nd is the house of accumulated wealth, reputation, and the name. The Nobel Prize arrives as a 2nd-house event in the Moon's mahadasha. The recognition of the 1905 intellectual work (Venus, 5th) comes formally in the 2nd lord's period.

Einstein uses the Nobel Prize money to pay his first wife, Mileva, in accordance with their divorce settlement — a 2nd-house disbursement of name and money in one act.

## Rahu and the final chapter

Mars mahadasha (1931–1938) covers the period when Hitler rises to power in Germany. In January 1933, while Einstein is visiting the United States, the Nazis come to power. He does not return to Germany. He takes a position at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton, New Jersey. Mars is the 11th lord for Gemini lagna — the house of networks, groups, and the gains that come from them. Princeton's academic network becomes his permanent home.

Rahu mahadasha begins in October 1938. In August 1939, Einstein co-signs the letter to President Roosevelt warning that Germany may be developing atomic weapons — the letter that leads to the Manhattan Project. Rahu expands and amplifies; for Gemini lagna, Rahu's results depend on its house and sign, but the nodal period in this chart brings Einstein into the center of geopolitical consequence he had not sought.

In 1952 the Israeli government offers him the presidency. He declines. On 18 April 1955, seventeen months before Rahu mahadasha would have ended, Einstein dies at Princeton, age 76.
