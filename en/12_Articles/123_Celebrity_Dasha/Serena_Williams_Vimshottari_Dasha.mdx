# Serena Williams: Mars Won the First Slams, Rahu Held the Long Reign

Serena Williams was born on 26 September 1981 at 20:28 in Saginaw, Michigan. Astro-Databank rates the time AA, quoted from a birth record, in Eastern Daylight Time. The zone is America/New_York. Lahiri gives Aries lagna, Moon in Leo in Uttara Phalguni pada 1, Sun in Virgo. Uttara Phalguni is ruled by the Sun, so the chart opens on a Sun balance that lasts until August 1987.

The life-event table is almost entirely awards dated 1 January. The dates below are the public record of matches and of the wedding, run on this Moon. The database marriage row is 1 January 2017 and is not used as the wedding day.

The lagna lord in the 4th with Rahu puts the self in the house of the home crowd and of endings, conjunct the node. Ketu in the 10th is the career house occupied by the other node. ## The first slams are the lagna lord's major period

On 11 September 1999 she won the US Open, her first major. The period is Mars / Jupiter / Venus. Mars is the lagna lord and the 8th lord, in the 4th. Jupiter, the antardasha lord, rules the 9th and sits in the 6th, the house of opponents. Venus, the pratyantar lord, is the 7th lord in the 7th. The first slam falls in the major period of the lagna lord, in the sub-period of the 9th lord who sits in the house of the opponent, on a day ruled at the finest level by the planet in the 7th.

On 6 July 2002 she won Wimbledon for the first time. The period is Mars / Ketu / Mercury. The major period is still Mars. Ketu, the antardasha lord, occupies the 10th. Mercury, the pratyantar, sits in the 7th with Venus and rules the 3rd and the 6th. The first Wimbledon is a 10th-house antardasha inside the same mahadasha as the first US Open. The two breakthroughs are not two mahadashas. They are two antardashas of one Mars period, and Mars mahadasha ends on 20 August 2004.

## The long middle is Rahu, and Venus times both the 23rd slam and the wedding

Rahu mahadasha runs from 20 August 2004 to 20 August 2022. Rahu sits in the 4th with Mars. This is the period of the repeated titles, the comebacks, and the years the year-only ESPY and Laureus rows cluster. Those 1 January rows are not given antardashas here.

On 28 January 2017 she won the Australian Open, the 23rd major, while pregnant. The period is Rahu / Venus / Moon. Venus is the 7th lord in the 7th. The Moon, the pratyantar, rules the 4th, the house Rahu and Mars occupy, and the Moon itself sits in the 5th, the house of children. A major won in pregnancy falls on a day when the finest period is the 4th lord in the 5th, inside the 7th lord's antardasha.

On 16 November 2017 she married Alexis Ohanian. The period is Rahu / Venus / Jupiter. The antardasha has not changed. Venus is still the sub-period lord, and Venus rules the 7th and occupies it. The pratyantar has changed from the Moon to Jupiter, the 9th lord in the 6th. The slam and the wedding are the same Rahu / Venus. The database's 1 January 2017 marriage row happens to fall in Rahu / Venus as well, so the year bucket and the real day agree on the antardasha. They do not agree on the pratyantar, which is why the day is required.

## Jupiter opens on the last US Open

Jupiter mahadasha begins on 20 August 2022. On 10 September 2022 she played the US Open final, the match she had framed as her last. The period is Jupiter / Jupiter / Jupiter. Three weeks of the new mahadasha have passed. Jupiter rules the 9th and the 12th and sits in the 6th. Closing the competitive career falls in the opening triple period of the 12th lord, and that lord sits in the house of opponents. The reign itself was Rahu. The exit is the first season of Jupiter.

The time is AA, so the lagna and the Moon's nakshatra are on a birth record. Pratyantar edges can still move with a few minutes. Mars containing both first slams, and Rahu containing the 2017 slam and the wedding, are major-period facts.
