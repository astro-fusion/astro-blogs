## Office is a Rahu / Saturn period

On 13 December 1951 she married Denis Thatcher. The period is Sun / Jupiter / Saturn. The Sun is the 11th lord in the 12th. Jupiter, the antardasha lord, rules the 6th and sits in the 3rd. The marriage that funded and steadied the political career falls in a Sun major period, with Saturn, the exalted lagna occupant, as the finest period that day.

On 4 May 1979 she became prime minister. Rahu mahadasha had been running since 5 September 1972. The period is Rahu / Saturn / Sun. Saturn, exalted in the lagna and lord of the 4th and 5th, is the antardasha lord. The Sun, 11th lord in the 12th, is the pratyantar. On 24 October 1979 she was made a Fellow of the Royal Institute of Chemistry. The period is still Rahu / Saturn, now with Mars as pratyantar.
