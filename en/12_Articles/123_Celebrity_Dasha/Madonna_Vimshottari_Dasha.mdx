# Madonna: The First Marriage Is Moon, Both Divorces Bring in Saturn

Madonna was born on 16 August 1958 at 07:05 in Bay City, Michigan. Astro-Databank rates the time AA. The record labels the zone EST. August in Michigan is Eastern Daylight Time, and the civil clock of 07:05 is what the record gives, so the zone used is America/Detroit. Lahiri gives Leo lagna, Moon in Leo in Purva Phalguni pada 2, Sun in Cancer. Purva Phalguni is ruled by Venus, and a long Venus balance is still running at birth.

## The lords

Leo rises. The Sun rules the lagna and sits in Cancer, the 12th, with Venus. The Moon rules the 12th and sits in Leo, the 1st, in its own nakshatra lord's mansion: Purva Phalguni belongs to Venus. Mercury rules the 2nd and the 11th and sits in the lagna with the Moon. Mars rules the 4th and the 9th and sits in Aries, the 9th, with Ketu. Saturn rules the 6th and the 7th and sits in Scorpio, the 4th. Rahu sits in Libra, the 3rd, with Jupiter, who rules the 5th and the 8th.

The 7th lord in the 4th ties marriage to home and to the private house. The 12th lord, the Moon, in the lagna ties the body and the public self to foreignness, loss, and what is offstage. Venus, 3rd and 10th lord, is also in that 12th.

## Moon builds the first public self

On 12 November 1984 *Like a Virgin* was released. The period is Moon / Ketu / Mercury. The Moon is the 12th lord in the 1st. Ketu sits in the 9th with Mars. Mercury, the pratyantar lord, sits in the lagna and rules the 2nd, speech and the catalogue one sells. The record that made her a fixed public image falls in the major period of the planet that occupies the lagna.

On 16 August 1985, her twenty-seventh birthday, she married Sean Penn. The period is Moon / Venus / Rahu. Venus rules the 10th and sits in the 12th. The antardasha lord is the career lord, in the house of foreign places and of what is hidden. Rahu, the pratyantar lord, is in the 3rd, the house of performance and of nerve.

## Saturn is present every time a marriage ends

On 21 March 1989 *Like a Prayer* was released. The period is Mars / Jupiter / Moon. Mars mahadasha had begun on 31 January 1987. Mars is the 9th lord in its own 9th. Jupiter, the antardasha lord, rules the 5th and sits in the 3rd. The album that reset her public stance falls in a 9th-lord major period and a 5th-lord sub-period.

On 14 September 1989 she and Sean Penn divorced. The period is Mars / Saturn / Mercury. Saturn is the 7th lord, in the 4th. The marriage ends in the 7th lord's antardasha. The major period is still Mars. The pratyantar lord, Mercury, is in the lagna and rules speech: the divorce was a public argument as much as a legal end.

On 22 December 2000 she married Guy Ritchie. Rahu mahadasha had been running since 31 January 1994. The period is Rahu / Saturn / Moon. Saturn, the 7th lord, is again the antardasha lord, this time at the start of the marriage rather than at the end. The Moon, 12th lord in the lagna, is the pratyantar.

On 21 November 2008 they divorced. The period is Rahu / Sun / Rahu. The antardasha lord is the lagna lord, the Sun, placed in the 12th. The second marriage also ends inside Rahu mahadasha. Its antardasha is the 1st lord in the 12th, which is a separation of the self from the house, rather than Saturn's 7th-house signature. Saturn had opened that marriage. The Sun closed it.

Both marriages are inside a nodal or Martian major period. The planet that rules the 7th is the antardasha lord at the first divorce and at the second wedding. That repetition is the timing fact. It is not a claim that Saturn produces divorce.
