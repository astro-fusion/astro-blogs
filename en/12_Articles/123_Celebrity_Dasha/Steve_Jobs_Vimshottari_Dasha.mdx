# Steve Jobs: Apple Was Born in Ketu and Reborn in Venus

Steve Jobs was born on 24 February 1955 at 19:15 PST in San Francisco. Astro-Databank rates that time AA, from a birth record. With Lahiri ayanamsa the chart is Leo lagna, Sun in Aquarius in the 7th, and Moon in Pisces in the 8th in Uttara Bhadrapada pada 1. Uttara Bhadrapada is ruled by Saturn, so the opening mahadasha from the balance of that nakshatra is Saturn, running only a short stretch from birth.

This reading uses that clock. A noon chart would require a different lagna and is not used here.

<AIBlufSummary>
Saturn mahadasha is nearly exhausted at birth. Mercury runs through childhood and education. Ketu, the mahadasha that follows, holds Apple's founding, the Apple II, and the original Macintosh — the entire first creative burst. Venus then runs twenty years and contains the return to Apple, the iMac, and the iPod. The Moon mahadasha, which begins in 2008, is the period inside which he dies. For Leo lagna, the Moon rules the 12th and sits in the 8th.

The 8th house placement in Uttara Bhadrapada — a nakshatra whose deity is Ahir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep — is the signature of the life: immense creative force housed inside a body under recurring threat, and a career that repeatedly cycled through destruction and renewal.

The board that fired him in 1985 faced a 7th-house Sun — the lagna lord across the horizon, an equal, not a subordinate.

Venus rules the 10th for Leo lagna. The Venus mahadasha is the career mahadasha in the most literal sense: the planet that owns the house of profession runs for twenty years at the peak of his life.

## Ketu mahadasha: the first creative burst (1975–1982)

Ketu's seven-year period opens with Jobs dropping out of Reed College and drifting through India before returning to work at Atari. In 1976, at age 21, he co-founded Apple Computer with Steve Wozniak. In 1977, the Apple II launched — the product that turned a garage company into a business. The entire founding of the personal computer industry, as far as Jobs was involved in it, sits inside Ketu mahadasha.

Ketu is the planet of past karma, of detachment, of technical precision without an ego attached to the method. In the nakshatra framework, Ketu co-rules Ashwini, Magha, and Mula — the three nakshatra of roots, kings, and dissolution. For Leo lagna, Ketu does not rule a house in the traditional system, but its antardasha placements and the sign it tenants shape what the period delivers.

The Macintosh launch on 24 January 1984 falls just inside Ketu mahadasha, which ends January 1982 — wait, the Mac launched in 1984, which is Venus mahadasha. The Macintosh is a Venus event, not Ketu. Ketu closes in January 1982; the product that became the Mac was developed during those years, but the launch belongs to Venus.

## Venus mahadasha: the exile, Pixar, and the return (1982–2002)

Venus mahadasha begins on 31 January 1982. Venus rules the 10th for Leo lagna. The twenty years of this period contain the defining arcs of his professional life.

In 1984, the Macintosh launched — the first months of Venus mahadasha after Ketu had closed. In 1985, the Apple board forced him out. The Macintosh that became his identity was a Venus-period product, and the exile that followed was also Venus. In 1986, he bought The Graphics Group from Lucasfilm for \$10 million, renaming it Pixar. That acquisition — which would eventually become a \$7 billion sale to Disney — is a Venus-period investment. Venus, as the planet of art, aesthetics, and beauty, rules a studio that would redefine animated cinema.

By 1996, Apple was near collapse. Jobs returned as an advisor, then as CEO in 1997, still inside Venus mahadasha. The iMac launched in 1998, the first product designed under his return: a translucent plastic computer in five colors, a piece of object-based aesthetics as much as technology. The iPod launched in October 2001. Everything that re-established him as the defining figure of consumer technology falls within this one Venus mahadasha.

Venus does not cause these outcomes. It is the 10th lord's period — the mahadasha of the planet that owns the career house — running for twenty years at the precise moment when the career house's themes were active.

## Sun mahadasha: the final innovation arc (2002–2008)

Sun mahadasha begins in January 2002. The Sun is the lagna lord for Leo, sitting in the 7th. The Sun's period is the period of the self operating through the public-facing axis.

In April 2003, the iTunes Store launched. In January 2007, the iPhone was announced and launched — arguably the single most consequential product announcement in the history of consumer electronics. The Sun mahadasha closes in January 2008 and the iPhone launch falls at its end.

The Sun's six-year period contains the digital music ecosystem and the smartphone. These are not incidental events in a quieter period. They are the last peak before the Moon takes over.

## Moon mahadasha: the final years and death (2008–2018)

Moon mahadasha begins on 31 January 2008. The Moon rules the 12th for Leo lagna. It sits in the 8th house. These are the two most difficult houses in the Jyotish framework — the 12th is loss, liberation, and the foreign; the 8th is transformation, hidden things, chronic illness, and the lifespan itself.

In July 2008, the App Store launched — the first event of Moon mahadasha. The Moon's period opened with the last major platform expansion of his career and then narrowed toward illness. On 5 October 2011, Steve Jobs died of respiratory failure related to a pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor he had managed for eight years. The date is Moon mahadasha, with the Moon placed in the 8th in Uttara Bhadrapada.

Uttara Bhadrapada's ruling deity is Ahir Budhnya, associated with the depths, with the hidden, with what lies beneath the surface. The Moon in this nakshatra in the 8th house, ruling the 12th, is the whole story: a life built on making things visible, governed by a planetary signature that pointed toward concealment, transformation, and exit.

## What the chart holds

The structure is not that Ketu caused Apple or that Venus caused the return. The structure is that a Leo lagna chart with Moon in the 8th in Uttara Bhadrapada produced a dasha sequence where the 10th lord's period (Venus) covered the peak career and the 12th lord's period (Moon) covered the final illness. The founding was Ketu. The rebuilding was Venus. The exit was Moon. Each mahadasha found its own territory inside the same extraordinary life.
