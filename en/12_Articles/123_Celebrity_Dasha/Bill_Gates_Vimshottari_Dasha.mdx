# Bill Gates: Microsoft Was Founded in Ketu and Went Global in Venus

Bill Gates was born on 28 October 1955 at 21:00 PST in Seattle, Washington. Astro-Databank rates the time AA, from a birth record. With Lahiri ayanamsha, Cancer rises. The Moon is in Pisces in Uttara Bhadrapada nakshatra, ruled by Saturn. Uttara Bhadrapada gives a Saturn balance at birth: the chart opens on a short Saturn period, nearly exhausted.

<AIBlufSummary>
Ketu mahadasha, running from January 1975 to January 1982, holds the Harvard dropout, the founding of Microsoft, and the IBM deal that made MS-DOS the operating system of the personal computer era. Venus mahadasha then carries Microsoft from a startup to the most valuable company in the world. The Gates Foundation era begins in Sun mahadasha, the moment Gates steps back from the CEO role.
</AIBlufSummary>

## Lords that matter here

Cancer rises, so the Moon rules the lagna and sits in Pisces, the 9th house. This is a strong lagna lord: in a kendra-trikona relationship, the Moon in the 9th gives fortune, long journeys, and the ability to operate across borders. Jupiter rules the 6th (Sagittarius) and the 9th (Pisces); Jupiter is the dispositor of the Moon. Venus rules the 4th (Libra) and the 11th (Taurus). The Sun rules the 2nd (Leo). Mercury rules the 3rd (Virgo) and the 12th (Gemini). Mars rules the 10th (Aries) and the 5th (Scorpio).

For Cancer lagna, Venus is the 4th and 11th lord. The 4th is the house of fixed assets, long-term holdings, and the base of operations. The 11th is the house of income, networks, and the gains that accumulate over decades. A Venus mahadasha for a Cancer lagna chart is a period in which both the asset base and the income potential compound simultaneously.

## Mercury and the formation years

Mercury mahadasha covers January 1958 to January 1975 — the entire childhood, adolescence, and the first years at Harvard. For Cancer lagna, Mercury rules the 3rd (communication, early skill development, sibling networks) and the 12th (foreign institutions, hidden work, what goes on behind closed doors). Gates programs his first computer at age 13 at the Lakeside School in 1968, inside Mercury mahadasha. The 3rd is the house of hands and skill; Mercury is the planet of programming and analytical systems. The period produces the technical foundation.

In 1973, Gates enters Harvard. This is Mercury / Mercury or Mercury / Ketu — the final two years of Mercury mahadasha. The period that built the skill set closes at the institution's door.

## Ketu and the beginning that looked like an ending

Ketu mahadasha begins in January 1975. In January 1975, Gates and Paul Allen read about the Altair 8800 microcomputer in Popular Electronics and within weeks write a BASIC interpreter for it. Gates drops out of Harvard in his junior year. Microsoft is founded in April 1975.

Ketu is the planet of moksha and of past-karma activation. Ketu periods bring things that were already latent to the surface. The Harvard dropout looks like a loss from the outside. From the dasha perspective, Ketu simply moved what had accumulated during seventeen years of Mercury onto its proper stage. The 3rd lord's period (Mercury) built the skill; the South Node's period released it from the institution.

In 1980, IBM approaches Microsoft looking for an operating system for its new personal computer. Gates does not have one. He brokers the acquisition of QDOS from Seattle Computer Products and licenses it to IBM as MS-DOS. The deal closes in November 1980. This is the act that makes Microsoft the fulcrum of the personal computer revolution. It happens inside Ketu mahadasha.

In August 1981, the IBM PC launches with MS-DOS. The world's dominant computing platform is now gated through a software agreement negotiated in Ketu's period. Ketu runs until January 1982.

## Venus and the twenty years of Windows

Venus mahadasha begins in January 1982. Within three years, Windows 1.0 ships in November 1985. In March 1986, Microsoft holds its IPO. Gates becomes a billionaire at age 30. The period is Venus / Mercury / Moon.

Venus as the 4th and 11th lord for Cancer lagna means the IPO is a 4th-house event (fixed assets, ownership stake, permanent wealth) arriving via the 11th (income and gains on a large scale). Both houses activated simultaneously in Venus mahadasha: the company acquires permanent institutional form and the income begins compounding.

Windows 3.0 ships in May 1990 and achieves mass adoption — the first version that works well enough for ordinary users. By 1993 Windows NT launches, targeting enterprise clients. Windows 95 ships in August 1995 and becomes a cultural event: midnight launch queues, television coverage, and 7 million copies sold in the first five weeks. All of this is Venus mahadasha.

In 1996, Internet Explorer is bundled with Windows. By 1999, Microsoft's market capitalization exceeds \$600 billion, making it the most valuable company in the world. The Venus period that began at the IPO ends on 20 January 2002.

## Sun and the philanthropy turn

Sun mahadasha begins in January 2002. For Cancer lagna, the Sun rules the 2nd house (Leo). The 2nd is the house of family lineage, speech, and wealth held in reserve. The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, though formalized in 2000, begins its major operational scale under Sun mahadasha. Gates steps down as Microsoft CEO in January 2000, two years before Sun begins — but the institutional identity of the foundation grows through this period.

In January 2008, Gates steps down as Microsoft Chairman to work full-time on the Foundation. This is the final weeks of Sun mahadasha. The 2nd house period closes with the wealth being redirected from accumulation toward distribution.

## Moon and Mars

Moon mahadasha runs from January 2008 to January 2018. The Moon is the lagna lord — the self — sitting in the 9th, the house of philanthropy, long journeys, and foreign missions. The Gates Foundation expands to become the largest private charitable foundation in the world during this period. The Moon's self in the 9th is literally the person as a vehicle for 9th-house activity.

In 2014, Gates returns briefly to the Board as Technology Advisor. Moon mahadasha, the period of the self in the 9th, ends in January 2018.

Mars mahadasha runs from January 2018 to January 2025. Mars is the 10th and 5th lord for Cancer lagna. In May 2021, the divorce from Melinda Gates is announced. Mars as the 5th lord carries children and partnerships; the 10th lord's period reshapes the public role.

## What the dasha sequence explains

The Ketu period opened the company in an act that looked like a departure from one institution and was actually the founding of a new one. Venus, the 4th and 11th lord, then carried both the assets and the income for twenty years through Windows into planetary market dominance. Sun shifted the identity toward the 2nd-house use of that wealth. Each period carried what its lordship promised, and the company that runs the computers of the world was built in the seven years the South Node had to run.
