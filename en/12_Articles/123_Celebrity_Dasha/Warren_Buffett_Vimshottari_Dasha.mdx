## Mercury and the acquisition of the method

Mercury mahadasha runs May 1937 to May 1954 — the entire childhood and university career. For Scorpio lagna, Mercury rules the 8th (Gemini) and the 11th (Virgo). The 8th is the house of research, hidden structures, other people's money, and the depth that lies beneath the surface of things. The 11th is the house of income, networks, and the gains that accumulate. Mercury in mahadasha for Scorpio lagna produces the person who researches what is hidden in order to generate income.

At age 11, in 1942, Buffett buys his first shares: three shares of Cities Service Preferred at \$38. He is in Mercury mahadasha. The 8th-and-11th lord's period produces the first transaction in the market that deals in other people's assets.

In 1947, Buffett files his first tax return as a paperboy, claiming a \$35 deduction for his bicycle as a business expense. He is 16. The 11th lord's period is tracking income.

In 1950, he applies to Harvard Business School and is rejected. He then reads the catalogue at Columbia Business School and discovers that Benjamin Graham and David Dodd teach there. He applies and is admitted. This is the pivotal 8th-house event of Mercury mahadasha: the hidden structure of the opportunity — Graham's presence at Columbia — is what actually determines the direction. The rejected door (Harvard) sends him to the right one.

At Columbia, Buffett studies value investing directly under Graham. He earns the only A+ Graham ever awarded. In 1951, he reads Graham's Security Analysis and The Intelligent Investor and absorbs the methodology that will not change materially for the next seventy years. The 8th lord's period produces the method from depth: Graham's approach is precisely the 8th-house art — finding the intrinsic value hidden beneath the market price.

In 1954, Buffett goes to work directly for Graham at Graham-Newman Corporation in New York. Mercury mahadasha ends in May 1954.

## Ketu and the beginning of independence

Ketu mahadasha begins in May 1954. In 1956, Graham retires and closes the fund. Buffett returns to Omaha and, with \$174,000 from family and friends, starts Buffett Partnership Ltd. The company is founded in Ketu mahadasha.

Ketu is the planet of moksha, prior preparation, and the activation of accumulated karma. The seven years of Ketu carry forward everything the Mercury period deposited. The investment partnership compounds at approximately 30% annually during this period — the mathematics of the method are already proved.

In 1961, Ketu mahadasha ends. The partnership is worth several million dollars and is growing.

## Venus and the acquisition of Berkshire

Venus mahadasha begins in May 1961. For Scorpio lagna, Venus rules the 7th (Taurus) and the 12th (Libra). The 7th is the house of partners, public-facing relationships, and contractual agreements. The 12th is the house of foreign accumulation, hidden reserves, and what compounds out of view.

In 1962, the Buffett Partnership begins buying shares in Berkshire Hathaway, a struggling Massachusetts textile company. This is a Venus-period acquisition: the 7th lord's mahadasha produces the contractual acquisition that will define the next sixty years.

In 1965, Buffett takes control of Berkshire Hathaway. The company is now his primary vehicle. Venus mahadasha — the period of contractual authority and hidden accumulation (12th) — holds the controlling stake.

In 1972, Berkshire acquires See's Candies for \$25 million. This is the first major brand acquisition — Buffett pays above book value for a business with a durable competitive advantage, a departure from the strict Graham approach. The 7th lord's period produces partnerships with established franchises.

In 1977, Berkshire acquires The Washington Post stake. The Venus period compounds year after year through the 1970s. By the time Venus mahadasha ends in May 1981, Buffett's net worth is approximately \$150 million. The 7th-and-12th lord has built the hidden reserve (12th) through a series of contractual acquisitions (7th) that none of the period's contemporaries understood to be as transformative as they were.

## Sun and the name brands

Sun mahadasha runs May 1981 to May 1987. The Sun rules the 10th (Leo) and sits in the 10th — the 10th lord in its own sign. This is the period in which the public career identity consolidates into something that the world names. During Sun mahadasha, Buffett becomes a recognized figure in American finance, not merely a successful private fund manager.

In 1983, Berkshire acquires Nebraska Furniture Mart. In 1985, Berkshire closes the remaining textile operations — the original Berkshire business ends, leaving the investment vehicle. The Sun period sheds what does not belong to the 10th-house identity and retains what does.

## Moon and the decade of name brands

Moon mahadasha runs May 1987 to May 1997. The Moon is the 9th lord sitting in the 1st house. The 9th is the house of fortune, the foreign, and the broader moral and institutional framework. The Moon in the lagna means the period is the self as 9th-house actor.

In 1988, Berkshire begins buying Coca-Cola shares. By 1989, Berkshire holds \$1 billion of Coca-Cola stock. This is the most famous single investment of Buffett's career. The 9th lord's period produces the acquisition of the most globally recognized brand on earth — a 9th-house (foreign, universal) holding acquired in the period of the self-as-9th-lord.

In 1989, Berkshire invests in Capital Cities/ABC. The communications and media sector — a 3rd-house domain — enters the portfolio during the 9th lord's period.

By 1996, Berkshire's stock price is so high that the Board creates Class B shares to allow smaller investors to participate. The Moon mahadasha ends in May 1997 with Buffett's net worth above \$33 billion.

## Mars and the bridge decade

Mars mahadasha runs May 1997 to May 2004. Mars is the lagna lord for Scorpio, ruling the 1st and the 6th. The 1st lord's period is the period of the self operating directly. During Mars mahadasha, Berkshire's performance weathers the dot-com bubble — Buffett refuses to invest in technology companies he does not understand, and the 2000–2002 collapse validates the approach while the NASDAQ loses 78%. The 6th-house quality of Mars — the capacity to endure and outlast opponents — is the lived texture of this period.

## Rahu and the philanthropic peak

Rahu mahadasha begins in May 2004. Rahu expands and amplifies; it carries the person into the domain of the foreign, the large scale, and the institutional consequence that extends beyond the individual.

In June 2006, Buffett pledges to donate approximately 85% of his Berkshire shares to the Gates Foundation and four family foundations — at the time, approximately \$37 billion. This is the largest single philanthropic gift in American history. The Rahu period for the person who built the asset base in Venus now redirects that base toward the 12th-house domain: the foreign-and-outward, the surrender of accumulated wealth.

In 2008, the global financial crisis arrives. Berkshire profits from the dislocations through preferred share investments in Goldman Sachs and General Electric. Rahu as the planet of amplified opportunity operating through crisis produces the gains that come when others are selling.

In 2011, Buffett is diagnosed with prostate cancer and treated successfully. He discloses the diagnosis publicly. Rahu mahadasha ends in May 2022.
