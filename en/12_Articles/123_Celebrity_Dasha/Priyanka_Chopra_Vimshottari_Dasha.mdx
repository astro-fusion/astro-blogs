# Priyanka Chopra: Why 00:30 and 01:30 Are Different Charts

Priyanka Chopra was born on 18 July 1982 in Jamshedpur. Two clocks are in print, one hour apart, and they do not produce the same lagna or the same dasha calendar.

Astro-Databank rates 01:30 IST as A, from a close friend via the astrologer Ashish Seth. That is the time stored here, with Asia/Kolkata. Lahiri on that clock gives Taurus lagna and the Moon at 19.87° Taurus, Rohini pada 3. GrahAI publishes the same 01:30 and the same Taurus lagna. GrahaSutra also uses 01:30 and Taurus.

AstroSage and AstroKaya publish 00:30, source "Lagna Phal (Garg)", rating Reference. On that clock the lagna is Aries at about 22°, and the Moon is still Taurus, Rohini pada 3, but earlier in the nakshatra, near 19°09'. The Moon sign survives the hour. The ascendant does not.

## What an hour does, and what three degrees of Moon do

Taurus rises at 01:30, with the ascendant near 9° Taurus. Venus rules the lagna and the 6th and sits in Gemini, the 2nd, with Mercury and Rahu. The Moon rules the 3rd and sits in the lagna, exalted in Rohini. Mars rules the 7th and the 12th and sits in Virgo, the 5th, with Saturn, who rules the 9th and the 10th. Jupiter rules the 8th and the 11th and sits in Libra, the 6th. The Sun rules the 4th and sits in Cancer, the 3rd.

Rohini runs from 10°00' to 23°20' Taurus. Our Moon at 19.87° has about 3.5° of Rohini left. The Moon's own mahadasha is 10 years, so the balance at birth is about two years and seven months. Moon mahadasha ends on 20 February 1985. That arithmetic is the whole later calendar. Every subsequent period is counted from that date.

AstroSage's 00:30 Moon, near 19°09', leaves a longer Rohini balance. Their table gives Moon until 7 September 1985, a balance of 3 years, 1 month, 19 days, and Saturn from 7 September 2026. The lagna is Aries, so the house lords in their articles are not the lords used here.

GrahaSutra uses 01:30, the same clock as Astro-Databank, and prints the Moon near 22.78° Taurus, close to the end of Rohini. Their balance of Moon is about five months, Mars begins in December 1982, Jupiter runs 2007 to 2023, and they have Saturn already running. A later Moon inside the same nakshatra shortens the opening period and pulls every later mahadasha forward by years. Same clock, different published Moon, different marriage dasha.

We do not average these. The chart below is the 01:30 Moon our ephemeris returned, 49.873° sidereal.

## Miss World is Rahu / Mercury

On 30 November 2000 she won Miss World. The period is Rahu / Mercury / Venus. Rahu mahadasha had been running since 20 February 1992. Rahu sits in Gemini, the 2nd, with Venus and Mercury. Mercury, the antardasha lord, rules the 2nd and the 5th and sits in the 2nd. Venus, the pratyantar lord, is the lagna lord, also in the 2nd.

The contest that made her an international public figure falls in the major period of the node in the 2nd, in the sub-period of the 2nd lord, on a day when the finest period is the lagna lord. The 2nd is the house of the face, the voice, and the name. All three period lords occupy it.

The National Film Award and the Padma Shri are in the database as 1 January 2009 and 1 January 2016. Those are year buckets. 2009 is still Rahu on this clock. 2016 is Jupiter. They are not given antardashas.

## The marriage is the lagna lord's antardasha, inside Jupiter

On 1 December 2018 she married Nick Jonas. The period is Jupiter / Venus / Mars. Jupiter mahadasha had been running since 20 February 2010. Jupiter rules the 8th and the 11th and sits in the 6th. Venus, the antardasha lord, is the lagna lord in the 2nd. Mars, the pratyantar lord, is the 7th lord in the 5th.

The marriage day is the lagna lord's antardasha, and the finest period is the 7th lord. That is a specific combination: the 1st lord for the twelve years of the sub-period, the 7th lord for the weeks. Jupiter, the major lord, is the 11th lord of gains and the 8th lord of other people's families, placed in the 6th. A marriage that is also a move into another country and another industry sits under an 8th-and-11th lord, with the 7th lord as the short period.

On the AstroSage 00:30 calendar this date is also inside their Jupiter mahadasha, which runs until September 2026, but their Jupiter rules different houses because the lagna is Aries. On the GrahaSutra 01:30 calendar, Jupiter ended in December 2023, so December 2018 is still their Jupiter, and the antardasha boundaries are not ours. Publishing one of those timelines next to our lagna would mix two Moons.

## What is running now

On this chart Saturn mahadasha began on 20 February 2026. Saturn rules the 9th and the 10th and sits in Virgo, the 5th, with Mars. The 10th lord's major period is the career lord's period, and the planet sits in the 5th, the house of performance and of children. AstroSage's table starts that Saturn period in September 2026. GrahaSutra has been in it since December 2023. The three readings of "what is running today" disagree because the Moon's remaining Rohini does.

The time is Rodden A, from a friend, not from a birth certificate. An hour the other way is a published alternative with a different lagna, and it is the clock we did not use. Inside the hour we did use, a few degrees of Moon are enough to move the marriage from one antardasha to another. The Miss World combination, Rahu / Mercury / Venus, belongs to this Moon only.
