# Muhammad Ali: Rahu Took the Title and the Ban, Jupiter Fought Manila

Muhammad Ali was born Cassius Clay on 17 January 1942 at 18:35 in Louisville. Astro-Databank rates the time AA, from a birth record. The row previously stored 18:30. The record says 18:35, and that is the time used here. Louisville observed Central Time in 1942 and did not move to Eastern Time until 1961, so the zone is America/Chicago, which is CST in January. A modern Eastern zone would be the wrong offset for this birth.

## The lords

Four planets in the 7th make the opponent, the public partner, and the open contest the occupied house of the chart. The Moon is debilitated in Capricorn and is the lagna lord. ## Rahu: the title, then the ban

On 25 February 1964 he beat Sonny Liston and took the heavyweight title. The period is Rahu / Venus / Moon. Rahu is in the 2nd, the house of the name and of what is accumulated. Venus, the antardasha lord, sits in the 7th with the lagna lord and rules the 4th and the 11th. The Moon, the pratyantar lord, is the lagna lord, debilitated in that same 7th. The night he becomes champion, the finest period is the lagna lord, and the antardasha lord is a planet sitting with the opponent's house.

On 28 April 1967 the title was stripped after he refused induction into the Army. The period is Rahu / Sun / Venus. The major period has not changed. The antardasha lord is the Sun, which sits in the 7th and rules the 2nd, the house Rahu occupies. Venus has moved from antardasha at the Liston fight to pratyantar on the day the title is taken away. The gain and the loss of that championship are one Rahu mahadasha. The sub-period lord changes. Both sub-period lords sit in, or rule, the 7th and the 2nd.

Rahu mahadasha ends on 7 December 1969. The exile from the ring is inside it. The return is the next mahadasha.

## Manila is Jupiter, and the finest period is the exalted 10th lord

On 1 October 1975 he fought Joe Frazier in Manila. Jupiter mahadasha had been running since 7 December 1969. The period is Jupiter / Mercury / Mars. Jupiter rules the 9th and the 6th and sits in the 11th. Mercury, the antardasha lord, sits in the 7th with the Moon and rules the 3rd and the 12th. Mars, the pratyantar lord, is the exalted 10th lord in the 10th.

The fight that closed the Frazier rivalry falls in the major period of the 9th lord, and on a day when the finest period is the planet that rules the career house and occupies it in exaltation. Mars was the major lord only in childhood. At Manila, Mars is the pratyantar. The career lord does his most public work as a short period inside someone else's mahadasha.

## The death is Mercury / Rahu

On 3 June 2016 he died. Mercury mahadasha had been running since 7 December 2004. The period is Mercury / Rahu / Venus. Mercury is in the 7th and rules the 12th as well as the 3rd. Rahu, the antardasha lord, is the same node that was major lord when he won the title and when he lost it. Venus, the pratyantar lord, was the antardasha lord at the Liston fight. The planets of 1964 return as sub-period lords in 2016, under a different major lord, the 12th lord.
