# Yogini Dasha: 8 Yoginis, 36-Year Cycle & Free Calculator

---

## What is Yogini Dasha?

<AIBlufSummary>
Yogini Dasha is an authentic 36-year planetary cycle in Vedic astrology governed by eight divine Yoginis, each ruled by a specific Graha. Calculated from the birth Moon's nakshatra (by adding 3 and dividing by 8), this compact cycle repeats multiple times across a human lifetime to deliver precise, rapid timing of material and spiritual events. While Vimshottari spans 120 years, Yogini operates as a swift, practical lens for day-to-day and year-to-year life transitions.
</AIBlufSummary>

## How Yogini Dasha is Calculated

The starting Yogini at the moment of birth is determined through the Moon's longitude and nakshatra:

$$\text{Starting Yogini} = (\text{Birth Nakshatra Number} + 3) \pmod 8$$

Where:
* Ashwini = 1, Bharani = 2, up to Revati = 27.
* If the remainder is $1$, the native begins life in **Mangala** dasha.
* If the remainder is $2$, life starts in **Pingala**.
* If the remainder is $0$ (or $8$), life starts in **Sankata**.
* The balance of the first Yogini dasha is determined by the unexpired arc of the Moon in its birth nakshatra.

## Antardashas (Sub-Periods) in Yogini Dasha

Within each Yogini Mahadasha, all 8 Yoginis cycle in order as Antardashas, beginning with the Mahadasha lord itself. The duration of an Antardasha is proportional:

$$\text{Antardasha Duration (Years)} = \frac{\text{Mahadasha Years} \times \text{Antardasha Years}}{36}$$

For example, during the 6-year **Ulka (Saturn)** Mahadasha:
* **Ulka–Ulka**: $(6 \times 6) / 36 = 1\text{ year}$ ($12\text{ months}$)
* **Ulka–Siddha**: $(6 \times 7) / 36 = 1\text{ year } 2\text{ months}$
* **Ulka–Sankata**: $(6 \times 8) / 36 = 1\text{ year } 4\text{ months}$
* **Ulka–Mangala**: $(6 \times 1) / 36 = 2\text{ months}$
* **Ulka–Pingala**: $(6 \times 2) / 36 = 4\text{ months}$
* **Ulka–Dhanya**: $(6 \times 3) / 36 = 6\text{ months}$
* **Ulka–Bhramari**: $(6 \times 4) / 36 = 8\text{ months}$
* **Ulka–Bhadrika**: $(6 \times 5) / 36 = 10\text{ months}$

Total: $12 + 14 + 16 + 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 72\text{ months} = 6\text{ years}$.

## Yogini Dasha vs. Vimshottari Dasha

| Metric | Vimshottari Dasha | Yogini Dasha |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Cycle Length** | 120 Years | 36 Years |
| **Planets Included** | 9 Grahas (Ketu to Mercury) | 8 Grahas (Ketu combined with Rahu/Sankata) |
| **Speed of Transit** | Macro-life milestones (decades) | Micro-life timing & acute events (1–8 years) |
| **Calculation Anchor** | Moon's Nakshatra ruler | Moon's Nakshatra index $+ 3 \pmod 8$ |
| **Classical Authority** | Maharishi Parashara | Parashara & Rudrayamala Tantra |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long is the Yogini Dasha cycle?
The Yogini Dasha cycle lasts exactly 36 years. A person living 72 years will experience the full cycle twice.

### Which planet rules Sankata Yogini Dasha?
Sankata is ruled by Rahu (and in certain traditional Himalayan traditions, both Rahu and Ketu). Lasting 8 years, it is the longest Yogini period.

### Is Yogini Dasha more accurate than Vimshottari?
Vedic astrologers use both together. Vimshottari provides the strategic foundation of your life path, while Yogini acts as an acute tactical verification tool.
