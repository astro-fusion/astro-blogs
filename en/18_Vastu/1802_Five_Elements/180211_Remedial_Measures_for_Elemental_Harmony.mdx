# Balancing the Elements: Simple Vastu Remedies

## Introduction

When the five elements (earth, water, fire, air and space) fall out of step in a home or office, Vastu tradition holds that health, prosperity and peace of mind can be affected. This article describes remedies that need no major alteration or demolition: moving objects, choosing colours and making small improvements to the atmosphere of the home.

## What are elemental remedies?

These are corrections made to bring the five elements into balance in a building. According to Vastu tradition, they reduce the unfavourable influence that comes from an imbalance in the design or from elements placed in the wrong zone. Compared with major structural changes, they are quick, inexpensive and can be fitted to the energy of each space. The purpose is that traditional knowledge stays useful in a modern way of living.

## Key aspects

### Remedies without structural change

These are changes that need no walls to be built or demolished, so they are practical for most homes and offices.

- Arrange furniture so that obstructions are removed and energy can move freely.
- Use light partitions, screens or plants to shift the influence of the elements gently.
- Tradition places mirrors on walls such as the north or east, to improve the flow of favourable energy.

### Placing objects

Placing symbolic objects with thought is considered helpful for balancing the elements. For example:

- Put a water feature, such as a fountain, in the north-east, to support prosperity and emotional balance.
- Place lamps or sacred objects in a zone to make up for a deficiency or excess of its element.
- Set beds, desks or workstations facing auspicious directions, to support stability and productivity.

### Colours and ways of lifting the atmosphere

Colours are regarded as a means of bringing the elements of a space into harmony.

- Use cool colours (blue or green) in resting areas to soothe, and warm shades (red or orange) in active spaces for vitality.
- Tradition also lists aids such as fragrance and the chanting of mantras for lifting the atmosphere of a room.
- Indoor plants and natural materials are said to strengthen the earth and air elements and to add a feeling of calm and health.

## How to spot a shortfall

- Study the existing layout against a Vastu plan and find which element dominates or is lacking in each zone.
- Match the directions (north, south, east, west and their corners) with their elements.
- Use observation to find disturbances, such as a blocked passage, an overheated corner or a stagnant spot.

No complicated formulas are needed. In tradition, classical Vastu charts are consulted and the adjustments are decided from them.

## Reading the result

- An excess of an element (for example too much fire in the south-east) may bring agitation or discomfort. Simple means such as cool-toned decoration are used to balance it.
- A deficiency of an element (for example too little earth in the south-west) may bring instability or restlessness. It is made up by placing steady objects such as stones or heavy furniture.
- The key is to watch how the environment affects the people living in it and to make changes gradually and thoughtfully.

## Practical applications

### Bringing harmony to rooms in the home

Mirrors, water features and suitable colours can change the flow of energy in a home. According to tradition this improves comfort and family relationships and eases stress, without expensive renovation.

### The workplace

In offices, moving desks, adding plants and using colour well are considered good for productivity and for harmony among colleagues. Aids such as soft lighting also help make a balanced atmosphere.

## Common misconceptions

- **Myth: major construction changes are needed to correct the elements.** Most imbalances can be handled with decorative remedies such as the placing of objects and the choice of colours.
- **Myth: Vastu and elemental balance are strict rules.** They are flexible guidelines that can be adapted to a modern way of living, to personal needs and to the limits of the space.
- **Myth: only experts can apply these remedies.** Many are simple and can be done at home with a little guidance and observation.

## Examples

- **Example 1:** a family found their living room restless and untidy. A look at the Vastu plan showed a strong fire influence in the south-east. Cool-toned decoration was added there, and the room began to feel calmer. (The right place for a water feature is the north-east, not the south-east.)
- **Example 2:** an office had slow work and frequent disagreements. Plants were placed in the east zone and workstations were turned to face auspicious directions. The team is reported to have worked together better and to have felt more positive.

## Conclusion

These simple remedies show that the atmosphere of a home and a workplace can be improved without costly renovation. According to tradition, the placing of objects, the choice of colours and small improvements bring peace and balance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to break walls to balance the elements?

No. For most imbalances, simple remedies such as arranging furniture, plants, colours and the placing of objects are described.

### Where should a water feature go in the home?

According to tradition, in the north-east, for example a fountain. Avoid placing a water feature in the south-east, because that is the fire zone.

### How do I choose colours?

Cool colours such as blue or green are used in resting areas, and warm colours such as red or orange in active spaces.

### What if an element is lacking?

The element that is lacking is strengthened. For example, if earth is lacking in the south-west, steady objects such as stones or heavy furniture are placed there.

### Where should I start?

First look for places in your home that feel uncomfortable or blocked. Then use a simple Vastu plan to decide on the position of objects and on colours, or ask an expert.

### Do these remedies need a birth chart?

No. They are based on the directions of the space, not on anyone's birth chart.
