# Elemental Imbalance and Its Effects on a Home

## Introduction

Vastu tradition teaches that when the five elements (earth, water, fire, air and space) are in balance, life in the home and for those who live in it runs smoothly. When the balance is disturbed, the signs can show in the physical condition of the building, in the state of mind of the occupants and in the energy of the home. This article explains how to recognise such an imbalance and how to correct it.

## What is an elemental imbalance?

An imbalance means that one or more elements are present in a space in excess or in deficiency. In Vastu each element is linked with particular directions and functions. The disturbance can appear in three forms:

- **Physical:** for example poor air or dampness.
- **Energetic:** for example blocked or excessive flow of energy.
- **Psychological:** for example a disturbed mood.

Recognising the imbalance shows where in the home a correction is needed.

## Key aspects

### Physical signs in a building

- **Poor indoor air quality:** too much humidity, stagnant air or a high level of pollutants can cause headaches, fatigue, eye irritation and breathing difficulty. These are common signs of a stuffy building and are often linked to poor design or poor ventilation.
- **Temperature extremes:** too much fire (heat) or too little cooling (water and air) can cause discomfort, muscle cramps and a worsening of existing complaints.
- **Structural problems:** cracks, leaks, mould or persistent dampness. These are regarded mainly as signs of disturbance in the earth and water elements.

### Effects on mind and mood

- **Mental fatigue and irritability:** an imbalance of fire and air is said to cause restlessness, difficulty concentrating, disturbed sleep, irritability and anxiety.
- **Mood disturbance:** excess water may bring emotional overwhelm or apathy, while a lack of earth can bring insecurity and a feeling of being ungrounded.
- **Lower productivity:** a poor environment lowers morale, increases absence from work and reduces mental clarity.

### Blocked energy and subtle effects

- **Stagnant or turbulent flow of energy:** Vastu holds that energy that is blocked or too strong affects the atmosphere of a building and, through it, the people who live there.
- **Disturbed sleep:** an imbalance of fire (overstimulation) or air (restlessness) can bring insomnia and an irregular daily rhythm.
- **A feeling of unease:** unresolved disturbances of energy may create a vague sense of unease or agitation.

## How to identify an imbalance

- **Directional check:** each direction is linked with an element, for example the north with water and the south-east with fire. See whether the things belonging to that element are present in the right amount in each area, such as a water source in the north or the fire place in the south-east.
- **Environmental check:** pay attention to air quality, temperature, humidity, light and noise. This shows which element is out of balance.
- **Record of symptoms:** ask the members of the household which physical or mental complaints they have. From these you can judge which element has been disturbed.

## How to read the result

- **Balanced:** when all the elements are in fitting proportion, the environment is considered supportive and healthy.
- **Excess of an element:** if an element is in excess (too much heat or too much damp), its influence needs to be reduced.
- **Deficiency of an element:** if an element is lacking (poor air, little natural light), it needs to be increased.

Reach a conclusion by looking at signs together: the design of the building, the quality of the air and the mood of the occupants.

## Practical applications

### Vastu corrections for health and harmony

If there is too much water in the south-west, or too little fire in the south-east, a Vastu expert would suggest corrections such as:

- moving the water feature elsewhere;
- improving the lighting;
- rearranging the furniture;
- adding plants.

Such changes can make the atmosphere of the home more comfortable.

### Workplace and home environment

Improving air quality (balancing air), controlling damp (balancing water) and keeping enough light (balancing fire) can reduce complaints such as headaches and fatigue, and make work easier at home or in an office.

## Common misconceptions

- **Misconception: talk of imbalance is only superstition.** Elemental imbalance is the language of tradition. Even so, air, damp and light are directly tied to everyday comfort, so it is practical to take them seriously.
- **Misconception: only the body is affected.** Tradition holds that the state of mind and the flow of energy are affected as well, not only physical symptoms.
- **Misconception: every imbalance is obvious.** Mild low spirits or mild tiredness often go unnoticed, yet they are also linked in tradition with elemental disturbance.
- **Misconception: remedies are complicated or expensive.** Simple steps such as improving the flow of air, reducing clutter and adding plants help considerably.

## Examples

- **Example 1: an office with poor air.** Workers complain of headaches, dry skin and tiredness. A check finds many airborne particles and low-frequency noise. This would be read as a disturbance of the air element. Improving ventilation and adding plants is the usual remedy described for such a situation.
- **Example 2: a home with excess of the water element.** A family has frequent colds, a damp smell and a feeling of heaviness. There are leaks and poor drainage in the north (the direction of water). According to Vastu, fixing the leaks, drying out the area and strengthening the fire and earth elements is the remedy.

## Conclusion

The signs of elemental imbalance can appear at three levels: body, mind and energy. Recognising them and making simple corrections can turn a home into an environment that gives comfort and peace.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the signs of elemental imbalance in a home?

Physical signs such as damp, leaks, stagnant air, excessive heat, headaches and tiredness, together with mental signs such as restlessness, irritability and disturbed sleep.

### Where should I start checking for an imbalance?

Look at each area according to the element of its direction. Then pay attention to air, humidity, temperature, light and noise, and ask household members about their complaints.

### Are big changes needed to correct it?

No. Simple steps such as improving the flow of air, reducing clutter, fixing leaks and adding plants can be taken.

### What does an excess or a deficiency of an element mean?

With an excess, its effect is reduced, for example by cutting down damp. With a deficiency, it is increased, for example by improving ventilation and light in a place with poor air or little light.

### Do I need to know my birth time for this?

No. The elements of a building are assessed by looking at its directions, air, damp and light.
