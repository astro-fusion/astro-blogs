# Water Element in Vastu: North-East, Fountains, Kitchen, Bath

## Introduction

The water element, called Jal Tattva in Sanskrit, is one of the five fundamental elements (Pancha Bhoota) of Vastu Shastra, India's ancient science of building and design. This element is a symbol of purification, rejuvenation and vitality, that is, of the essence of life. In Vastu thought, water is not only physical sources of water. It also stands for fluidity of emotion, intuition and abundance. According to tradition, placing the water element properly in a living space is necessary for creating surroundings that favour emotional calm and good energy.

## What Is the Water Element?

In Vastu Shastra the water element is counted among the forces that shape the surroundings in which we live. As one of the Pancha Maha-Bhoota (five great elements), water stands for fluidity, adaptability and nourishment. In Vastu belief, water is linked with Varuna, the deity of the oceans and water bodies, who symbolises the flow of life, emotional stability and abundance.

In a home, the water element appears in many forms. There are direct sources of water such as fountains, pools and aquariums, and there is also its symbolic presence through colours, shapes and objects. According to Vastu principles, water is linked with several important aspects of life, such as the flow of opportunities in the form of money, jobs, clients and orders. It is believed that if the water element is out of balance in a home or workplace, health and state of mind can also be affected.

Water has a special relationship with directional energy. It is mainly linked with the north direction of any space. When placed according to Vastu guidelines, it is believed to open a path for the free flow of auspicious energy.

## Key Aspects of the Water Element

### Symbolic Meaning

In Vastu Shastra the symbolic meaning of the water element goes far beyond its physical properties. Water is a symbol of purity, vitality and fluidity, and is regarded as a medium of spiritual and emotional cleansing. It is also a symbol of transformation and adaptability, teaching us to flow around an obstacle rather than resist it. In Vastu thought, water symbolises abundance, prosperity and the continuous renewal of life energy.

The reflective nature of water also stands for understanding and introspection, letting us see ourselves more clearly. In tradition this element is linked with emotional calm, intuition and the ability to receive new ideas and opportunities. By understanding these symbols, the good qualities of water can be used in a living space, to create surroundings considered favourable for personal growth, emotional balance and financial stability.

### Directions

In Vastu Shastra, certain directions are fixed for the water element, and its effect on a space and its residents is held to depend on them. Water is mainly linked with the north direction, which is associated with career progress, opportunities and prosperity. According to the Vishwakarma Prakash, a book regarded as an authority on Vastu, a source of water can be placed auspiciously in several directions.

The sense of that text is that water may be placed in all directions except the north-west, south-west, south and south-east. Even the central part of the building should not hold a water source. Placing a water source in the north, north-east, east or west is considered auspicious.

In Vastu the north-east direction is considered especially auspicious for water, because it is the meeting point of the north (water) and the east (the rising sun). It is believed that a powerful confluence of energy forms there, drawing opportunities for growth and spiritual abundance. The east is linked with wealth and glamour, while water in the west is linked with financial prosperity.

Placing water in the wrong place, for instance in the south or the south-west, is held by tradition to cause serious imbalance, which can reach family problems or even a major crisis.

### Physical Forms

The water element appears in different physical forms in the places where we live and work. These forms are useful in practical terms as well as in terms of energy, and they contribute to the Vastu balance of a space.

- **Natural water bodies:** a river, pond, lake or stream near the property.
- **Swimming pools:** water structures that add comfort and beauty.
- **Fountains:** decorative items that create movement and sound.
- **Aquariums:** a combination of water and the life force of fish.
- **Waterfalls:** natural or artificial cascading structures of water.
- **Bathrooms and kitchens:** working areas where water is used regularly.
- **Water storage tanks:** essential facilities for storing drinking and everyday water.
- **Reflective surfaces:** mirrors and glass objects, which return light as water does.
- **Water-themed art:** paintings or sculptures showing water scenes.
- **Blue and black colours:** colours linked with the water element.

According to tradition, each of these forms carries the mark of the energy of water, and according to Vastu principles their correct placement balances the flow of energy in a space. For example, a small fountain in the north-east is considered to activate the energy of prosperity, and an aquarium with healthy fish in the north is considered favourable for career opportunities and the flow of money.

## How to Decide the Right Use of the Water Element

The correct use of the water element in Vastu depends on a careful check of direction, proportion and compatibility of energies. It is not a mathematical calculation in the strict sense, but this orderly method helps in deciding the right place for water features.

### Checking the Direction

1. Fix the cardinal directions properly with a compass.
2. Identify the north direction, which is said to be mainly linked with water.
3. Mark the north-east (Ishaan) corner, which is considered highly auspicious for water.
4. Treat the east and the west as second-ranking places for water sources.
5. Note the directions where water should be avoided: the south, south-west, south-east, north-west and the central part.

### Balance of Proportion

The amount of the water element in a space should be in proportion to the other elements (Earth, Fire, Air, Space), so that harmony is kept.

- For a home, small features such as fountains and aquariums are generally considered enough.
- In commercial spaces, larger water features may be suitable according to the size of the building.
- Avoid too many water features, especially in small spaces, or imbalance results.

### Compatibility With Other Elements

The way water goes with nearby elements is also looked at.

- Water should not be placed near sources of fire (kitchens, fireplaces), because their energies conflict.
- Water complements the Earth element, and a relationship of nourishment forms between the two.

## Signs of Balance and Imbalance

To understand the influence of the water element in Vastu, one looks at both the good effects of the right place and the troubles of the wrong place. The traditional corrections are decided from this.

### Traditional Results of the Right Place

According to Vastu belief, when the water element is in the right place, these good effects are believed to follow.

- **North:** Water features placed properly in the north are linked with career progress, financial prosperity and a steady flow of opportunities.
- **North-east:** Water in the north-east is linked with spiritual growth, clarity of thought and opportunities for personal advancement.
- **East:** Water features in the east are associated with success in studies, honour and a good public image.
- **West:** Water in the west is said to be linked with financial stability and material prosperity.

The quality of the water also matters. Clean, clear and flowing water is regarded as a symbol of lively energy, while still or dirty water is regarded as a sign of blocked opportunities or emotional stagnation.

### Beliefs About the Wrong Place

Tradition links these problems with a wrong place or a poor condition of water features.

- **South:** Water in the south is linked with troubles in the health of the women of the house or in relationships concerning women.
- **South-west:** Water in the south-west is believed to bring a serious crisis, loss of stability or a health problem for the head of the family.
- **Centre (Brahmasthan):** Water in the middle of the house is believed to cause loss of wealth and instability in the whole house.
- **North-west:** A water source here is believed to increase conflict with enemies or competitors.

Dripping taps, broken water features or a dirty aquarium are also regarded as signs of trouble in the areas of life linked with the water element.

### Principles of Balance

When the water element in a space is assessed, these points are kept in mind.

- The amount of water should be harmonious in proportion to the other elements.
- The quality and condition of the water should be very good, that is clean, clear and, where possible, flowing.
- The direction should agree with Vastu guidelines.
- The type of water feature should match the purpose of the space, for example a gentle fountain in a meditation area and a lively aquarium in a living room.

## Practical Applications

### Water Features and Their Placement

These are the traditional points on placing water features according to Vastu principles.

**Fountains and water features:**

- Placing a small, active fountain in the north-east corner is considered to activate the energy of prosperity.
- A water feature with movement in the north is considered to increase career opportunities.
- Choose water features with a slow and pleasant sound, not noisy ones or ones with a strong flow.
- Keep all water features clean and well looked after, and change the water regularly.

**Aquariums and fish tanks:**

- In tradition, placing an aquarium in the north or east is considered auspicious.
- Among fish, golden, orange or black ones are traditionally considered auspicious.
- The tradition is to keep an odd number of fish, with 9 considered auspicious.
- The aquarium should always be clean and the fish healthy and active.
- Avoid an aquarium in the bedroom, because more active energy can form there.

**Swimming pools and water bodies:**

- It is considered best to build a swimming pool in the north or north-east part of the property.
- A rectangular or square pool is considered better than an irregular shape.
- Proper care is needed to keep the water clean and clear.
- Avoid building a pool in the south-west or south parts.

The size of water features should be in proportion to the space. For small homes modest features are fine, while in large spaces larger features can also be placed.

### Planning the Bathroom and Kitchen

Bathrooms and kitchens are places with a large presence of the water element in the home, so Vastu gives them special attention.

**Bathroom:**

- It is considered best to build the bathroom in the north-west or the west.
- Avoid a bathroom in the north-east, because it is a spiritually important direction.
- Drainage should be very good and no tap should drip.
- Use white, light blue or pastel colours in the decor.
- Keep the bathroom door closed when it is not in use, so that the energy of water stays there.
- Mirrors are put up to balance energy, but not directly opposite the door.
- Keep cleanliness very good, so that negative energy does not collect.

**Kitchen:**

- The kitchen holds a balance of water and fire.
- It is considered best to build the kitchen in the south-east.
- Keep the water source, that is the sink, in the north-east part of the kitchen.
- Keep the sink and the fire source (the stove) apart, never side by side.
- Keep the plumbing in order and let there be no leakage.
- Use a water purifier for the quality of water.
- Keep drinking water in the north-east corner of the kitchen.

In both the bathroom and the kitchen, good ventilation is necessary so that steam spreads and goes out and the energy of water does not collect in the space.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Misconception 1, all water features are auspicious whatever their place:** The direction of a water feature matters a great deal. Water in the south-west or south is held by tradition to cause serious problems, while water in the north-east or north is generally considered auspicious. According to the sense of the Vishwakarma Prakash, water should be avoided in the north-west, south-west, south, south-east and the central part of the building.
- **Misconception 2, the more water features, the more prosperity:** Too much water causes imbalance, especially in small spaces. What matters is proportion with the other elements. A single well-placed water feature in the right direction is considered better than many placed in wrong places.
- **Misconception 3, any form of water works the same way:** The energy of different forms of water is held to differ. Flowing water (a fountain) gives dynamic energy, while still water (an aquarium) gives calm, reflective energy. Pictures of water scenes are considered to have a milder effect than real water features.
- **Misconception 4, Vastu water principles are the same all over the world:** The basic principles remain the same, but some Vastu scholars suggest small changes according to hemisphere and geographic location. Reverence for water goes beyond boundaries, but practical use can be adapted to local conditions.
- **Misconception 5, water features need costly construction:** Simple and cheap means, such as a small table fountain, a bowl of fresh water with floating flowers, or blue decorative items, are also considered to represent the water element when placed in the right place.

## Examples

Both examples are only to explain the point. No outcome can be promised with them.

### Example 1: Checking the Water Element in a Home

Suppose a family's home has some water-element points that do not fit Vastu.

1. The bathroom is in the north-east corner, which is considered an inauspicious place.
2. The water tank is in the south-west part.
3. Heavy furniture stands in the north, blocking the flow of energy.
4. There is no form of water in the auspicious directions.

Tradition describes these changes for it.

- If the bathroom cannot be moved, put up a mirror there and use light blue paint.
- Move the water tank to the north-west.
- Clear the north part of heavy furniture and place a small fountain there.
- Place an aquarium with nine goldfish in the north of the living room.
- Have all the dripping taps in the house repaired.

### Example 2: Checking the Water Element in an Office

Suppose the water element is distributed in an office in such a way that a Vastu check finds faults.

1. The water cooler is in the south.
2. The restrooms are in the north-east corner.
3. The reception area in the north has no form of water.
4. Pipes are leaking in several places throughout the office.

Tradition describes these changes for it.

- Move the water cooler to the north.
- If the restrooms cannot be moved, place blue-green plants for balance.
- Place an aquarium with black and gold fish in the reception area.
- Have all the pipe leaks repaired promptly.
- Place a small table fountain on the desk of the chief executive, who sits in the east part.
- Hang pictures of calm water scenes on the north and north-east walls.

## Conclusion

In Vastu Shastra the water element is counted among the main forces that bring harmony, prosperity and emotional balance into the surroundings where people live. As seen in this article, water is a symbol of purification, rejuvenation and vitality, and it is believed that when it is placed in the right place, these qualities touch our lives. The relationship of water with direction is very important. Its main direction is said to be the north, and its presence in the north-east and the east is considered auspicious. By keeping these directions in mind and avoiding the wrong places, the good influence of water can be used.

The forms of water, from fountains and aquariums to bathroom planning and colours, give many ways of bringing in this element despite limits of budget or space. The key is balance of proportion, keeping purity and paying proper attention to direction.

The water element also teaches adaptability and flow. As water flows easily around an obstacle, we too should learn to find a way with ease instead of hard collision. The essence of Vastu is to create balance, not perfection. Even small and thoughtful changes can make a difference to the atmosphere of a space.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### In which direction is it auspicious to place a water source in Vastu?

The north-east is considered the most auspicious. A water source can also be placed in the north, east and west. Water is mainly described as the element of the north.

### In which directions should water be avoided?

In the south, south-west, south-east, north-west and the central part of the building. Vastu practitioners link a water source in these places with imbalance.

### What should I keep in mind about water in the bathroom and kitchen?

It is considered better to keep the bathroom in the north-west or west and out of the north-east. The kitchen should be in the south-east, with the sink in the north-east part of the kitchen, apart from the stove. Keep leaks repaired in both places and arrange for ventilation.

### Is it better to have as many water features as possible?

No. Too much water causes imbalance, especially in small spaces. A single feature placed well in the right direction and kept clean is considered more suitable.

### What should I use to fix directions?

A proper compass is needed. Do not rely only on smartphone apps, because nearby metal can disturb them. If the direction is wrong, there is a risk of water features ending up in inauspicious directions.

### What should a beginner do?

Start with small changes that are not permanent. Place a small table fountain, or a glass bowl of clean water with floating flowers, in the north or north-east. Keep blue or black decorative items in the north, and make sure all taps and pipes are in order.
