# Air Element in Vastu: North-West and Airflow in the Home

## Introduction

The air element (Vayu) is one of the five basic elements of Vastu Shastra. It is believed that these five elements together decide the balance of the place where we live. The Vastu Purusha is called the cosmic being, and air is called his breath. For this reason, according to Vastu tradition, the balance of air is linked in a home with health, conversation and clarity of mind.

The importance of air is not limited to opening a window to let in fresh air. It is a symbol of movement, of communication and of the life energy called prana.

## What Is the Air Element in Vastu?

Air is one of the Pancha Maha-Bhoota, the five great elements. These elements are regarded as the basis of the Indian science of Vastu. Air represents movement, vitality and the breath of life. In Vastu belief, this element governs every kind of communication, the energy of contact, and the sensations of sound, touch and feeling.

Air is considered directly linked with our respiratory system and mental faculties. The body of the Vastu Purusha is the foundation of the rules of Vastu, and air is his breath. According to tradition, the balance of this element helps bring good health, clear thinking and easy conversation in a home.

In the order of the elements, air arises from space (Ether). It is the change from potential to movement, and it stands for every force and the motion that comes from it. For this reason it plays an important role in keeping the flow of positive energy going through the whole building, so that stagnation does not set in and fresh energy keeps moving freely.

## Key Aspects of the Air Element

### Movement and Circulation

Air mainly governs movement and circulation within a space. It is the moving flow of energy that prevents stagnation and keeps liveliness. In a house built according to Vastu, the path of air should not be a straight line but a zigzag. That is, the front and rear doors should not be directly opposite each other. In this way prana spreads through the whole house and does not simply pass straight out.

A proper flow of air is necessary for the balance of all the other elements. Even fire cannot burn without air, which shows how closely the five elements are linked. The plan of a house should be such that air reaches every corner and energy can move without obstruction.

### Link With the North-West Corner

In Vastu Shastra each element has its own direction. The air element is linked with the north-west corner of a house. In tradition this is considered the direction of Vayu Dev. This corner is considered linked with conversation and social relationships, so it is important for relationships and standing in society.

The north-west should be kept clean, airy and free of clutter. According to Vastu tradition, looking after this place keeps up mutual conversation, respect in society and harmony in relationships. Weakness of this corner can show itself as difficulties in conversation, loneliness or lack of respect in society.

### Influence on Health and Clarity of Mind

Vastu tradition links air with both physical health and mental clarity. It is believed that good ventilation helps respiratory health and gives the brain the fresh oxygen it needs. A balanced air element helps with alertness, clear thinking and concentration.

According to Vastu, lack of air can bring breathing trouble, heaviness of mind and obstacles in conversation. On the other hand, balanced air is considered helpful for deep breathing, mental clarity and being able to say what one means. This element is also linked with qualities such as cheerfulness, gentleness, neatness and courtesy.

## How to Check the Balance

According to the principles of Vastu, these points are looked at to check the balance of air in a space.

1. **Check of direction:** Look at the north-west part of the house or plot. This is the main area of the air element, and special attention is needed in its design and upkeep.

2. **Proportion of rooms:** Rooms should not be so cramped that air stops, nor so very large that energy is scattered.

3. **Assessment of ventilation:** In each room, compare the area of the windows with the area of the floor. Vastu suggests that the window area should be at least 20 percent of the floor area for natural ventilation.

4. **Quality of air:** Today, a device that measures air quality can also be used to check for a proper level of oxygen and low pollution.

The simple calculation of ventilation is this: divide the total area of the windows by the area of the floor and multiply by 100. If the result is 20 percent or more, it is considered favourable for the balance of the air element.

## How to Read the Signs

In a Vastu assessment, the air element is read in this way.

- **Strong air element:** According to Vastu belief, with balanced and strong air, conversation is easy, the mind is clear and respiratory health is considered good. Social relationships flourish and the home feels fresh and invigorating.

- **Weak air element:** Weakness of air is said to show itself often as difficulties in conversation, breathing trouble, heaviness of mind and social isolation. The space may feel stuffy, stagnant or oppressive.

- **Excess of air element:** Too much air can bring instability, restlessness and scattered thinking. The space may feel drafty, cold or unsettled.

The ideal state is balance, where air moves freely through the whole house but does not cause discomfort or obstruction. This balance helps both physical health and peace of mind, and keeps up easy conversation among the members of the household.

## Practical Applications

### Planning Ventilation for a Better Flow of Air

Building ventilation on the principles of Vastu means placing windows, doors and other openings thoughtfully. Large windows should be provided, especially in the north and east, so that plenty of light and fresh air can come in. This also reduces the use of energy and increases the flow of positive energy in the home.

In places where a window is not possible, such as interior bathrooms or corridors, a ventilator or skylight can be useful. Natural light coming from above can improve the atmosphere of such closed-in parts a good deal.

For a good flow of air, Vastu gives these suggestions.
- Place windows on opposite walls so that cross-ventilation forms.
- Keep the north-west corner well ventilated with enough windows.
- Do not keep things between rooms that block air.
- Keep a courtyard or an open space in the middle, so that air can move through the whole house.

### Planning Space for Mental and Respiratory Health

Because of the link of air with clarity of mind and respiratory health, it is necessary to keep it in mind in planning space. Rooms where work needing concentration is done, such as a home office or a study, should have very good ventilation and be built to take advantage of the natural path of air.

In the bedroom, give special attention to the quality and flow of air, because correct breathing during sleep affects overall health. According to Vastu, the bedroom should have windows that let in fresh air, but the bed should not be directly in the path of strong gusts, or sleep may be broken.

For a place of meditation and yoga, balance of the air element is considered very beneficial. Build such a place where natural air is plentiful, so that practice brings deep breathing and clarity of mind. The central part of the house is linked with the space element. Keep it open and airy so that energy flows unhindered through the whole house.

## Common Misconceptions

**Misconception 1: The more ventilation, the better**
Proper ventilation is necessary, but too much air can also bring imbalance. Vastu wants balance, not excess. Too many windows or constant gusts can create unstable energy and disturb the balance of the other elements. The aim is balanced and controlled air, not the maximum.

**Misconception 2: The rules of the air element apply only on the ground floor**
Many people believe that the rules of Vastu, including those of the air element, apply only to ground-floor houses. In fact the principles of Vastu are considered to apply to every floor of a building and to flats as well. Whether you live on the ground floor or an upper floor, the balance of the flow of air matters just as much.

**Misconception 3: The air element is only about air coming and going**
Physical ventilation is necessary, but in Vastu the scope of the air element is larger. It includes communication, mental processes and prana. Even a well-ventilated space can have an imbalance of air if it does not support healthy conversation or clarity of mind.

**Misconception 4: The rules of air cannot be applied in modern buildings**
Some people believe that it is impossible to adopt the rules of Vastu in modern construction. But in today's design too, proper ventilation, arrangement of directions and the flow of energy can be kept in mind, while following modern building standards as well.

## Examples

**Example 1: Improving a Home**

In one family's home there were breathing troubles, difficulty in conversation and an atmosphere of stuffiness. A Vastu consultant found several imbalances of the air element in the check.
- The north-west corner was packed with heavy furniture.
- Windows were hardly ever opened for fear of noise.
- Interior doors were often shut, so air did not circulate.

The suggested changes were these.
- Clear the things from the north-west corner and leave it empty.
- Arrange cross-ventilation with screens fitted, for the worry about noise.
- Keep interior doors open as far as possible, and fit a grille or vent for air when they are kept shut.
- Plant air-purifying plants in the house.

After these changes the family began to find the home fresh and conversation easier. Take this only as an example. It is no guarantee of any result.

**Example 2: Improving an Office Space**

In a small marketing agency there was a problem of communication among the staff and a lack of creative energy in the work. The Vastu check brought out these points.
- Storage dominated the north-west corner and nobody went there.
- Ventilation was weak, with windows shut and air only circulating and returning.
- The staff desks were arranged so that the natural path of air was blocked.

The suggested changes were these.
- Make the north-west corner a place for sitting and talking together.
- Fit an air purifier and set a time for opening windows.
- Arrange the desks so that air can move without stopping.
- As a tradition for awakening the air element, hang round metal wind chimes in chosen places.

After these changes, better conversation in the team and a more energetic atmosphere were reported. This too is only an example.

## Conclusion

In Vastu Shastra the air element is regarded as a basic force that affects health, conversation and clarity of mind in our homes and workplaces. It is the breath of the Vastu Purusha and the carrier of prana, so handling this element properly is necessary for creating surroundings that give comfort and peace at every level.

By understanding the link of air with the north-west corner and by adopting the right rules of ventilation, we can make a space that helps respiratory health, clear thinking and good conversation. These principles apply to both homes and commercial spaces.

Today, when the quality of air inside homes and the challenges of mutual communication are growing, this old teaching of Vastu gives useful guidance. Through thoughtful design we can make surroundings that are beautiful and that also support our basic needs for fresh energy, clear thinking and good relationships.

The air element is like a bridge between our physical and mental worlds. By giving proper attention to this essential element, we can invite balance, liveliness and clarity into life.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is the air element different from the other four elements?

Air represents movement and communication. Earth stands for stability, water for flow and purity, fire for transformation and energy, and space for openness and expansion. The direction of air is the north-west, while the other elements have different directions. Air is linked with our sense of touch and sound.

### What does Vastu say if the flow of air in a home is not right?

According to Vastu, imbalance in the north-west corner or poor ventilation can bring difficulties in conversation, breathing trouble or social obstacles. For this reason it is advised to correct these faults in advance.

### What should be kept in mind about the north-west corner?

Keep this corner clean, open and airy. Do not fill it with heavy things, and keep enough windows there. Also do not place things in the path that block air.

### Where should I start in correcting the air element?

Start with simple steps. Make cross-ventilation in the house, clear things from the north-west corner, plant air-purifying plants and, as tradition suggests, hang round metal objects or wind chimes. Regular cleaning and leaving open space for air are also an easy start.

### Do the rules of the air element apply in a flat or on an upper floor?

Yes. The principles of Vastu are considered to apply to every floor of a building and to flats. Whatever the floor, the balance of the flow of air matters just as much.

### Is it always good to put in more windows?

No. Too many windows or constant gusts can create unstable energy. In Vastu, balanced and controlled air is considered better, not the maximum.
