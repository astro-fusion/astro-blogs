# Exterior and Garden Vastu for Commercial Premises

## Introduction

One part of Vastu Shastra deals with the outer parts of a commercial building: the garden, the paths, the parking and the signboard. According to tradition, the aim of these rules is to create a setting favourable to the prosperity of a business and to invite positive energy. For people building a shop, an office or an industrial premises, these points can be useful when planning the outside space. Vastu is not a substitute for engineering, traffic and safety rules, or sound business practice.

## What Exterior Vastu for Commercial Premises Means

It is the method of applying the rules of Vastu to the outer parts of a commercial property. It covers the place and form of the garden, trees, parking space, paths, signboard and approach road. The aim is that the surroundings of the premises should suit the natural forces and that, by tradition, good fortune, prosperity and positivity should increase. It is believed that the right direction of the outer space supports the progress of the business and the efficiency of employees.

## Key Aspects

### Approach Paths and Roads

- The main road leading to the premises and the entrance gate should ideally come from the north or north-east. These directions are linked with prosperity and a positive flow of energy.
- If possible, the main entrance gate should be taller or more prominent than the other gates. It is believed that this draws auspicious energy into the premises.
- Paths should be gently winding rather than straight. Tradition says this slows the energy and lets it spread in balance.

### Garden, Trees and Plants

- Keep large or heavy trees and shrubs on the south and west of the building. Heavy things in these directions are said to act as a shield that protects the premises and keeps positive energy in.
- Keep small plants and flowering shrubs in the north and east, and water sources in the north-east, so that light and uplifting energy comes in and moves about.
- Do not plant thorny or withering plants. They are believed to attract stagnant and negative energy.
- Lawns or green areas should be spread out and tidy. By tradition they are more beneficial in the north or east.

### Parking and Signboard

- Keep the parking space towards the north-west or south-east. This keeps energy moving and is convenient for visitors and staff.
- The signboard should be clear, well lit and placed near the main entrance. It should draw attention but not block the flow of energy.

## How to Plan the Outer Space

- Fix the directions with a compass or the site plan.
- Look at the shape of the plot. A rectangular or square plot is considered better for stability and balance.
- Keep heavy things, such as tall trees and structures, in the south and west. Keep light things, such as lawns and small plants, in the north and east.
- Make sure the approach road, main gate and signboard are in auspicious places. For the gate, the north or north-east is mainly considered auspicious.
- Keep proportion and symmetry in mind throughout the premises, so that balance and an easy flow of energy are kept.

## Reading the Results

According to tradition, a premises with sound exterior Vastu shows liveliness, and the business and the people there stay energetic. If the exterior Vastu is out of balance, for example an entrance in the south-west or heavy construction in the north, tradition holds that stagnation and missed opportunities may follow. No medical claim is made here. This is only the belief of the tradition.

Each outer item, that is a tree, the lawn or the signboard, is judged by its position in relation to the main building. In this way an imbalance, called a Vastu dosha, is identified and put right accordingly.

## Practical Applications

### For the Prosperity of the Business

By keeping the main entrance and approach road towards the north-east and making open green areas in the north or east, positive energy is invited for the business, according to Vastu tradition. This is regarded as improving the chance of customer satisfaction and growth.

### For the Comfort of Staff

A garden laid out well, shaded sitting places and convenient parking make a stress-free setting for employees. This may improve their morale and their interest in the work.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Misconception:** Vastu applies only to homes.
  - **Fact:** Vastu is regarded as equally applicable to commercial and industrial places, especially for the outer parts and the garden.
- **Misconception:** Any plant or tree can be planted anywhere.
  - **Fact:** In Vastu the place of trees and plants is fixed. For example, large trees in the north are regarded as blocking opportunities.
- **Misconception:** Correction always means changing the structure.
  - **Fact:** Often a simple change in the arrangement of the garden gives an improvement in line with Vastu, without heavy cost.

## Examples

These examples are plain illustrations. No outcome is promised.

- **Example 1:** A retail building has its entry road and signboard in the north-east, its parking in the north-west and old trees on the western boundary. By Vastu this is regarded as a favourable arrangement.
- **Example 2:** An office complex moved its tall trees from the eastern boundary to the south-west. This is an example of a change in line with the traditional rule of keeping heavy things in the south and west.

## Conclusion

The exterior Vastu of a commercial premises is not limited to appearance. According to tradition, the right place for garden items, the direction of the entrance and paths, and a balanced spread of greenery keep harmony and an easy flow of energy in the premises. A commercial premises that follows these old rules is regarded as becoming a good centre of prosperity and work. Vastu is not a substitute for engineering, safety rules and sound business practice.

## FAQs

### In which direction should the main entrance of a commercial premises be?

Ideally in the north or north-east, because these directions are regarded as linked with prosperity and positive energy.

### Where should large trees and small plants be planted?

Large and heavy trees on the south and west of the building, where they act as a shield. Small plants and flowering shrubs in the north and east, and water sources in the north-east. Do not keep thorny or withering plants.

### On which side should parking be made?

Towards the north-west or south-east, so that energy stays in motion and coming and going is easy.

### Where and how should the signboard be placed?

Near the main entrance. It should be clear and well lit and should not block the flow of energy.

### Are major changes to the building needed for Vastu?

No. In many cases a simple change in the arrangement of trees, plants and the garden is regarded as enough for an improvement in line with Vastu.

### How should one begin?

Mark the directions of your premises on the plan. Keep the entrance in the north or north-east and keep heavy things in the south and west. If needed, take advice from an experienced Vastu consultant.
