# Commercial Building Entrance and Reception Vastu

## Introduction

The entrance and reception of a commercial building are not only a matter of the beauty of the design. They also play a part in setting the first impression of a business. According to Vastu tradition, the right position of the entrance and a well-thought-out arrangement of the reception are important for drawing positive energy (prana) and for making a good first impression. By understanding these details, a commercial space can be arranged to suit prosperity, harmony and the satisfaction of customers. Vastu guidelines should always be balanced with building codes, safety rules and accessibility standards, and they make no promise of results.

## What is entrance and reception design?

It is the planning, in terms of layout, space and energy, of the main entry of a building and the welcome area joined to it. In practical terms its purpose is to make a convenient and attractive first space for people who arrive. From the Vastu point of view its position, proportion and direction are thought to be closely tied to the flow of positive energy and the prosperity of the organisation. This thinking goes beyond beauty, because the energy that comes in through the main door is believed to affect the whole building and those who live and work in it.

## Key aspects

### Position of the entrance: north or north-east

- In Vedic tradition entrances facing north or north-east are specially recommended. These directions are linked with prosperity, growth and the drawing of auspicious energy.
- It is said that a north-facing door brings opportunity and financial gain, while the north-east invites wisdom and positive vibrations.
- Nearness to a main road and visibility from a distance are practical points that agree with this old advice and make it easier for customers and visitors to reach the place.

### Right proportion and height of the door

- The entrance should be in proportion to the overall size of the building, neither too grand nor too cramped, so that it neither overpowers the visitor nor looks dull.
- Ideally the main door should be the largest of all the doors in the building, which is a sign of welcoming energy and prominence.
- The height of the door should keep both usefulness and beauty in mind. It usually ranges between 7 and 8 feet, and the width is set to match, so that the space does not feel cramped.
- A vestibule or buffer area (a small open part inside the entrance) helps to manage the flow of air and the temperature, which adds to comfort and, it is held, keeps energy in.

### Lighting needs

- Good lighting of the entrance and reception area is necessary both for the flow of energy and for safety and accessibility standards.
- Give preference to natural light. If that is not possible, artificial light should give at least 200 lux in the lobby and shared areas, and 500 lux at the reception desk and general office areas.
- The light should be even, without harsh glare, and the fittings should be installed safely.
- Accent lighting can be used to bring out signboards, artwork or the reception desk, which adds to visual appeal and the identity of the brand.

### Direction of the reception desk: facing north or east

- Place the reception desk so that the receptionist faces north or east. It is believed that this welcomes positive energy, gives a good beginning to business dealings, and gives comfort and alertness to those who work there.
- The desk should be in a prominent place, easily seen from the entrance, and should give a feeling of security and openness.
- Keeping brand elements and accessibility features at the desk improves the visitor's experience and also agrees with good present-day practice.

## How to decide the direction and position

- With a reliable compass or a digital map, find true north for the plot.
- Mark the north-east part and plan the main entrance in this zone.
- See that there is a direct line of sight between the entrance and the reception desk, so that a visitor finds the way easily and the brand is seen.
- Set the height and width of the door in proportion to the front of the building.
- For the reception desk, check that the receptionist can face north or east directly. Adjust it if needed according to the interior plan and the movement of visitors.

## Reading the arrangement

- According to Vastu principles, a commercial building with a north or north-east entrance and a bright, open lobby is believed to receive positive energy steadily, which is taken as favourable to the standing of the business.
- If the reception desk faces south or west, or the entrance is cramped or poorly lit, it is believed that this may bring stagnation, missed opportunities or a poor first impression. Rearranging the space and better lighting can often improve these.
- Always keep the Vastu guidelines in balance with building rules and accessibility standards.

## Practical applications

### Office towers and corporate headquarters

- In a new office tower the entrance is placed on the north-east side of the plot, so that there is plenty of morning light and the Vastu principles are also followed.
- The reception area has a desk facing north, clear signboards and enough seating, which gives comfort and keeps the handling of visitors efficient.
- The light fittings are placed so that at least 200 lux is kept in the lobby and 500 lux at the desk.

### Service places such as hospitals and hotels

- In clinics and hotels an entrance in the north or the north-east is regarded as more reassuring for patients and guests.
- The reception desk is made to face east, which gives a feeling of openness and warmth.
- Natural materials, accent lighting and desks of accessible height make the space open to all and welcoming to look at.

## Common misconceptions

### Any direction of entrance is equally favourable

In Vastu tradition doors facing north and north-east are regarded as the most auspicious, while for other directions corrective measures to balance the energy are said to be needed.

### Lighting is only for looks

Enough light is necessary to comply with rules, and also so that the flow of energy is inviting and the workplace is safe and productive.

### The direction of the reception desk does not matter

According to tradition the direction of the desk affects the experience of both staff and visitors, and placing it to face north or east increases the arrival of positive vibrations.

## Examples

These are plain illustrations, not records of outcomes.

- Example 1: an information technology company in Bangalore placed the entrance of its headquarters in the north-east and fitted a glass door for natural light. The reception desk faces east, and the brand logo behind the desk is picked out with LED strip lighting. The light levels in the lobby were kept in line with safety standards. The company spoke of better client feedback and better staff morale.
- Example 2: a boutique hotel placed its entrance towards the north and made a vestibule to hold back the outside air. The reception area has a curved desk facing north-east, natural wood panelling and soft lighting. Guests describe the place as warm and inviting, which matches the intention of Vastu.

## Conclusion

The entrance and reception of a commercial building are not only a matter of looks. According to Vastu tradition they are a deliberate effort to join physical space with the flow of positive energy. Attention to the position of the door, its proportion, the lighting and the direction of the reception desk is believed to influence the first impression of the business, the flow of work and long-term prosperity. Joining these old principles with modern design standards makes a welcoming, useful and lively business space, and the Vastu advice never replaces building codes or safety rules.

## FAQ

### In which direction should the entrance of a commercial building be?

Vastu tradition recommends a door facing north or north-east. A north-facing door is linked with opportunity and financial gain, and the north-east with wisdom and positive vibrations.

### What should be the height and size of the main door?

The door should be in proportion to the size of the building, neither too grand nor cramped. Ideally it should be the largest of all the doors in the building. The height is usually 7 to 8 feet, and the width is set to match.

### Which direction should the reception desk face?

So that the receptionist faces north or east. The desk should be easy to see from the entrance and give a feeling of security and openness.

### How much light should there be at the entrance and reception?

Give preference to natural light. Artificial light should be at least 200 lux in the lobby and 500 lux at the reception desk, even and without harsh glare.

### What if the entrance is not in the north or north-east?

According to tradition, corrective measures are suggested to balance the energy in other directions. A new arrangement of the space and better lighting can also improve many difficulties. Keep the Vastu guidelines in balance with building rules and accessibility standards.

### What should a beginner do first?

First find the direction of your place with a compass, read the Vastu guidelines and, if needed, take advice from a practitioner, then arrange the entrance and reception according to the suggested directions. For quick improvement, concentrate on a bright, tidy and welcoming arrangement.
