# Water and Fire Element Placement in Commercial Vastu

## Introduction

Vastu Shastra holds that the position of the five elements in a space affects its working and its atmosphere. Among them, water and fire have a special place, because their natures are powerful and opposed to each other. According to Vastu tradition, the right place for these two in a commercial building is not just a question of decoration but a planned decision. This article explains where fountains, water pipelines, fire safety equipment, electrical panels and heating systems are placed according to Vastu, so that the setting is balanced and the work is not obstructed. Vastu is not a substitute for engineering, safety rules and sound business practice, and fire and electrical safety must always follow the rules and standards in force.

## What Water and Fire Element Placement Means

It means placing the things and systems that represent water and fire in a business space in the proper direction according to the rules of Vastu. This includes understanding the qualities of these elements, their directions and how they fit with the whole space.

In Vastu, water stands for flow, adaptability and prosperity, while fire stands for change, enthusiasm and energy. They are opposite by nature: water shows coolness and flow, fire shows heat and intensity. Tradition holds that when the two are balanced in a commercial space, the setting is favourable to business and operations. Placed wrongly, the two are said to clash, and the effects are described as problems with the flow of money, disagreements among people or inefficiency in the work.

Commercial Vastu differs from home Vastu in that its scale is larger and its attention is on business activity. It has to take account of the efficiency of employees, the impression on customers and the ease of operations.

## Key Aspects

### The Water Element: Prosperity and Opportunity

In Vastu, water is linked with wealth and opportunity. By tradition, water is connected with the flow of opportunities that come as money, jobs, clients and orders.

The north-east is regarded as the main direction of the water element, and the north is also linked with water. Together they are considered an auspicious area for water items in a commercial space. These directions are believed to bring business opportunities and help products reach the right customers. Flowing water is held to be more effective than still water, so water sources with movement are thought better.

Fountains and aquariums are regarded as things that awaken energy in a commercial space. It is believed that an aquarium with fish joins water and living energy and brings good fortune. But the size of a water source should be in proportion to the space. A very large water source is said to dominate the whole place and weaken the progress of the people who live or work there.

### The Fire Element: Cash Flow and Recognition

In Vastu, fire is the element of change and energy. In a modern business setting it is linked with cash flow and the recognition of a brand. The south-east is considered the ideal corner for the fire element. By tradition, this direction increases sales, and the south gives strength to the recognition and popularity of a brand.

In a commercial space fire appears as lighting, electrical systems, heating equipment and also certain shapes and colours. Red, orange and pink are regarded as colours of fire. The triangle is also a shape of fire. Tradition also links valuable objects, handsome artwork and pointed objects with this element. Placing them in the fire zone is considered proper.

For businesses that suffer a shortage of cash or delay in payments, Vastu tradition looks for an imbalance of the fire element as a possible cause.

### Balancing the Opposing Elements

The most important point in commercial Vastu is to prevent a direct clash between water and fire. According to Vastu, the two should not be kept on the same platform or stage. A wrong combination is said to show itself as quarrels in the business, problems in working or financial instability.

For example, a water source in the south-east, which is the fire zone, is said to affect finances badly and to cause disputes among partners, or between management and employees. In the same way, water running or a swimming pool towards the south-east of a plot is regarded as raising the risk of a fire accident.

## Deciding the Placement

This is not a mathematical process, but one can proceed in a few steps.

1. **Mapping the directions.** First fix the main directions of the premises with a compass. Without the right directions the right place for the elements cannot be decided.

2. **Identifying the zones.** Mark the zone of water (the north-east, with the north) and the zone of fire (the south-east, with the south).

3. **Checking the present positions.** Compare where the water sources, plumbing, electrical panels and heating arrangements are now with their ideal directions.

4. **Checking the symbols of the elements.** See where water and fire appear through colours, shapes and objects.

5. **Finding clashes.** Look for places where water and fire face each other directly.

6. **Checking the balance.** See that no single element dominates the whole space.

## Rules for Placing the Water Element

1. **Keep it in the north-east.** Water sources, water tanks and plumbing are kept in the north-east.

2. **Choose flowing water.** Flowing water sources are thought better than still water. A fountain placed at the entrance is held to hold back negative energy and call opportunities.

3. **Mind the proportion.** The water source should be in proportion to the space. A very large water source can upset the balance of a small premises.

4. **Avoid the south-east.** Do not place a water source in the south-east, because water puts out fire. Tradition regards this as a hindrance to financial progress and a cause of disputes at work.

## Rules for Placing the Fire Element

1. **Place it in the south-east.** Kitchens, heating arrangements, electrical panels and fire safety equipment are kept in the south-east part of the building.

2. **South for recognition.** Use the south for things that raise the visibility and recognition of a brand in the market.

3. **Use of colours.** Red, orange and pink are used in the south-east and south.

4. **Shapes.** Triangular shapes and dynamic designs are used in the fire zone.

5. **Electrical safety.** Keep the electrical equipment in the south-east well maintained, so that there is no real danger of fire. This is a safety need separate from Vastu.

## Preventing Clashes

1. **Keep them apart.** Keep the water and fire arrangements separate. Do not place them on the same platform or directly facing each other.

2. **Neutral space between.** If complete separation is not possible, leave a neutral space between the two.

3. **Corrective measures.** If the structure does not allow the ideal place, a balance is made through colours, shapes or symbolic objects.

## Practical Applications

### The Water Element in a Commercial Space

**A water source to welcome customers.** A water source in the north-east part of a reception or entrance makes a welcoming setting and, according to Vastu tradition, draws customers and opportunities. A fountain built with thought in a corporate lobby is regarded as an item of decoration and also as something that activates the water element.

The water source should have movement, because flowing water is held to be more effective than still water. In the northern parts, wavy shapes in the decoration or the design of walls are also taken as symbols of the flow of water.

**Aquariums.** An aquarium with healthy fish, well looked after, is considered auspicious in the north-east part of an office, shop or restaurant. Many hotels and restaurants keep one for this reason. But the aquarium should be in proportion to the space, because a very large water source can dominate the place.

### The Fire Element in a Commercial Space

**Kitchens and heating.** In a restaurant, cafeteria or commercial kitchen, placing the cooking area and the heating equipment in the south-east part is considered right for the fire element.

In an industrial premises, the main heating equipment, furnace or heat-producing machines are kept in the south-east. Heavy machinery is fixed in the south and south-west. Electric motors and the switches of fire appliances are kept in the south-east corner.

**Electrical panels and fire safety equipment.** In a professional, corporate or business premises, it is advised to keep the main electrical panel, the server room and computer stations in the south-east.

Fire safety equipment should be easy to reach and, for the most part, kept in the directions of the fire element. This brings the purpose of the equipment and the energy of the direction into line, it is believed. Its location must still follow the fire safety rules.

## Common Misconceptions

**Misconception 1: A water source anywhere will bring benefit.** Many business owners believe that fitting a fountain or aquarium is enough to bring prosperity. According to Vastu tradition, a water source in the wrong place, especially in the south-east, the zone of fire, creates a clash of energy.

**Misconception 2: Modern buildings do not need Vastu.** Some people think Vastu is only for traditional buildings. Vastu holds that the energy of the elements does not depend on the style of a building. So the position of the elements is considered in modern commercial buildings also.

**Misconception 3: The fire element means only fire.** The fire element also includes electrical systems, lighting, the colours red, orange and pink, the triangle and decorations such as artwork or pointed objects. Understanding all of these is necessary to balance this element in a commercial space.

**Misconception 4: Water and fire cannot be in one building.** The two should not face each other, but when placed correctly by the rule of direction they can be in one building. Their balanced presence is held to give the active energy needed for a successful business.

## Examples

These examples are plain illustrations. No outcome is promised.

### Example 1: Changes in a Production Unit

A company had machines that often broke down, disagreements among workers and uneven quality of production. A Vastu check showed that the heavy machinery stood in the north-east, the water zone, while the water store and the cooling arrangements were in the south-east, the fire zone. This kept a basic clash of the elements in place.

The heavy machinery was moved to the south and south-west, the water arrangements to the north-east and the electrical panel to the south-east.

### Example 2: Renovating a Corporate Office

A financial services company had a large decorative fountain in the south-east corner of its reception, while the accounts department and the main server room were in the north. In other words, the ideal places of the elements were reversed.

The renovation made these changes:

- Moving the water source to the north-east part of the reception.
- Moving the server room to the south-east zone.
- Adding red and orange elements in the south-east to support the fire element.
- Adding blue colours and wavy patterns in the north-east to support the water element.

## Conclusion

The place of water and fire in commercial Vastu is an aspect that often gets little attention. By understanding the directions, qualities and forms of these elements, business owners can create a setting that tradition regards as supporting efficient working and goodwill in the workplace.

According to Vastu tradition, water in the north-east increases the flow of opportunities and draws customers. Fire in the south-east, with the south, is linked with cash flow, the recognition of a brand and the energy of steady progress. Most important is to keep the two opposing elements apart so that their energies do not clash.

Vastu is not a substitute for business strategy, marketing, human resource planning, engineering or safety rules. It is taken up alongside them as a supporting arrangement.

## FAQs

### In which direction should a fountain be placed in a shop or office?

In the north-east. By tradition, a flowing water source in this direction draws opportunities and customers. Do not place a water source in the south-east.

### Where should the electrical panel, server room and kitchen be?

The main electrical panel, server room, computer stations, kitchen and heating equipment are placed in the south-east.

### Why are water and fire not kept close to each other?

According to Vastu the two are opposite elements and should not be on the same platform or face each other. A clash is said to bring business disputes and financial instability. If space is short, a neutral space is left between them.

### What can be done in a rented space where the structure cannot be changed?

Things that can be moved can be placed in the right position. A small flowing water source in the north-east, red, orange or pink items in the south-east and the right position of electrical equipment are considered helpful. For a thing that cannot be moved, balance is made through colours, shapes or symbolic objects.

### Is a Vastu expert needed for these rules?

The basics can be done by the rules of direction. For a large or complex premises it is considered useful to take the advice of a consultant experienced in commercial Vastu who understands both the traditional rules and modern needs.

### Where should one begin?

Mark the zones of the north, north-east, south-east and south with a compass. Then check the present position of the water sources, plumbing, electrical systems and heating. Small changes can be made at first, such as placing a movable water source in the north-east and bringing the colours of the fire element into the south-east.
