# Cash Counter and Safe Placement in a Shop by Vastu

## Introduction

Vastu Shastra gives great importance to the right position of the cash counter and of the places that deal with money in a commercial establishment. Vastu tradition holds that where the cash counter, the safe and the transaction areas are kept affects the prosperity of the business, the flow of money and its stability. For a shopkeeper or business owner these rules are practical guidance, which can be adopted to suit the layout of the shop. Vastu is not a substitute for proper accounting, security measures and sound business practice, and it makes no promise of results.

## What is meant by the cash counter and financial area?

It means placing the cash register, the safe and the places of transaction inside a shop or office with thought, according to Vastu principles. It is not only a matter of decoration or interior design. According to tradition it is an orderly method for keeping the flow of good energy, guarding against loss of money and letting the business grow steadily.

In Vastu tradition Kubera, the god of wealth, is regarded as the presiding deity of prosperity. It is believed that keeping the place of money in line with the auspicious directions brings the favour of Kubera and gives an establishment the possibility of wealth and stability.

## Key aspects

### Where to keep the cash counter and safe

- Keep the cash counter or cash box in the south-west corner of the shop or business premises. According to Vastu tradition this is regarded as best for financial security and stability.
- The cash drawer or locker should open towards the north. The north is regarded as the direction of Kubera, and in tradition it is taken as a symbol of the continuous arrival of wealth and blessings. Placing the cash box in the north itself is also acceptable.
- Avoid keeping the cash counter in the north-east or the north-west corner. According to Vastu tradition, keeping it in these places may lead to a leakage of money or financial instability.

### Which way to face for transactions

- Do all dealing in cash or valuables facing north. According to tradition this direction is auspicious for prosperity and keeps the flow of money easy.
- The owner or manager should ideally sit in the south or west, so that during the daily work the face is towards the east or north.

### Where to keep the safe and other items

- Keep a heavy safe, lockers or cabinets holding financial papers in the south-west part of the establishment.
- It is said that fixing a mirror inside a safe or in front of the cash counter shows a reflection of the cash and increases the energy of wealth. But do not fix a mirror in the south or the south-east.
- Do not keep the cash box or safe wholly empty, and do not leave it open in front of the entrance or the main passage. Tradition holds that doing so lets energy flow out.

## How to decide the direction

No astrological calculation is needed for the cash counter in Vastu. It follows the rules of space. They can be followed in order.

1. Identify the directions. With the help of a compass, fix north, south-west and the other directions properly in your shop or office.
2. Find the south-west corner. This is usually the corner farthest from the main entrance. In Vastu it is linked with stability and stored wealth.
3. Decide the direction of the drawer. Place the counter so that its drawer opens towards the north.
4. Decide where the owner sits. Seat the owner, or the person who takes money decisions, in the south or the west, so that the face is towards the north or the east.
5. Remember the places to avoid. Do not keep the place for handling cash near doors, windows, toilets or the north-east corner.

## Reading these rules

- In Vastu tradition a cash counter kept in the south-west, with the dealing done facing north, is taken as a sign of financial stability and of the easy arrival of money.
- If the financial area is in a wrong place, especially the north-east, the north-west or near the exits, tradition holds that money may get stuck, profit may fall or a sudden loss may be feared.
- An empty or exposed cash box is regarded as inauspicious. It is said that it keeps prosperity away.

These rules should be adapted to the layout of your establishment, but the basic Vastu principles should be kept.

## Practical examples

### A textile shop arranged by Vastu

In a textile shop the owner keeps the cash counter in the south-west corner, and the cash box opens towards the north. The owner sits in the western part of the shop, facing east. Financial papers are kept in a strong safe in the south-west. The place is cleaned regularly, and a small idol of Goddess Lakshmi is kept near the counter, so that a feeling of prosperity is kept up.

### Setting up a bank or financial institution

In a bank branch the main teller and the cash-handling area are placed in the south-west of the premises, and all the cash drawers open northwards. The main vault room is also here, and transactions are done facing north. The branch manager's room is in the south and faces north, so that the place of leadership is linked with the direction of prosperity.

## Common misconceptions

### Any corner will do for the cash counter

This is not right. In tradition the south-west is regarded as mainly auspicious, and the north is also considered suitable. Keeping it in other directions may hinder the flow of money.

### Vastu is not needed in rented premises

The rules of Vastu are not bound to ownership. It is believed that the layout of a place affects everyone who works there.

### Transactions can be done facing any direction

According to Vastu, dealing done facing north is regarded as more favourable for drawing wealth.

## Examples

These are plain illustrations, not records of outcomes or guarantees of any result.

- Example 1: a traditional gold and silver shop moved its cash counter from the north-west corner to the south-west corner, and the drawer of the counter began to open towards the north. The owner said that the flow of cash felt steadier and that the daily errors in the accounts were fewer.
- Example 2: a small pharmacy moved its safe and records cabinet to the south-west and turned the counter so that it opened towards the north. The owner said that the handling of money felt more settled.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, the right place for the cash counter and the financial area affects the financial health of a commercial establishment. Keeping the main money-related items in the south-west and doing the dealing facing north is regarded as auspicious for steady income, protection from loss and long-term prosperity. Business owners can pay attention to these points and arrange their shop or office accordingly, alongside proper accounting, locks and security.

## FAQ

### In which direction should the cash counter be kept?

According to Vastu tradition, the cash counter or cash box is best kept in the south-west corner. This is usually the corner farthest from the entrance. Keeping it in the north is also acceptable.

### Which way should the cash drawer open?

The drawer or locker should open towards the north. The north is regarded as the direction of Kubera and as a symbol of the arrival of wealth.

### Where should the cash counter not be kept?

Not in the north-east or the north-west corner. Do not make the cash-handling place near doors, windows or a toilet either.

### Where should the shop owner sit?

The owner, or the person who decides about money, should sit in the south or the west so that the face is towards the north or the east. Cash dealing is regarded as better done facing north.

### Do these rules apply in a rented shop?

According to the article, Vastu rules are not bound to ownership. In rented premises they can be followed as far as is possible.

### What else should be kept in mind about the safe or cash box?

Keep a heavy safe in the south-west. Do not keep it wholly empty, and do not leave it open in front of the entrance or the main passage. Keep the place clean.
