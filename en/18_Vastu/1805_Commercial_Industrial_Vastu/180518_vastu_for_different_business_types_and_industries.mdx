# Vastu for Different Businesses and Industries

## Introduction

Every business works in its own way, so the rules of Vastu are adapted to suit it. This information is for business owners, architects and new entrepreneurs who want to make a space that, according to tradition, is favourable to prosperity, progress and harmony. Every industry has its own needs, so when joining ancient knowledge with modern trade, it is necessary to choose the rules accordingly. Vastu is not a substitute for engineering, safety rules and sound business practice.

## What Vastu for Different Businesses Means

It is the application of the ancient Vastu tradition to commercial places such as retail shops, manufacturing, hotels, health services, education and the service sector. Instead of applying one rule to all, it holds that each industry has different needs of energy, a different way of working and different goals. With the rules of Vastu, based on the five elements (earth, water, fire, air and space), each business tries to build a setting that suits its purpose.

## Key Aspects

### Planning the Space by Industry

Different industries need different plans.

- **Manufacturing.** Attention goes to the place of heavy machinery, raw material and finished goods, so that the flow of energy stays balanced.
- **Retail shops.** The stress is on the direction of the entrance, the display area, the store and the cash counter, so that customers are drawn and stay.
- **Hotels and health services.** The entrance, service areas and resting places are arranged so that guests and patients are comfortable and operations run smoothly.

### Matching Directions

- **Entrance.** For a retail shop or an office an entrance facing north, east or north-east is regarded as auspicious. It is believed that these directions call opportunities.
- **Owner and management rooms.** These are placed in the south-west for authority and stability. The cash box or safe, and the billing or cash counter, are placed in the south-west, opening towards the north, or in the north.

### Balancing the Elements for Productivity and Well-being

- **Manufacturing.** The fire element, that is the south-east, is regarded as necessary for work such as metalwork or food processing.
- **Health services.** A water source in the north-east or east is regarded as giving a setting favourable to healing. Areas of harsh energy are advised against.
- **Education and services.** These need enough windows and open space for air and light, which suit learning and progress.

## How to Examine the Space

Vastu does not use mathematical formulas. It relies on the plan of the space and on observation.

- **Shape and slope of the plot.** A square or rectangular plot is regarded as better. The slope should fall from the south-west towards the north-east, that is, the north-east should be low.
- **Dividing the zones with a compass.** Fix the directions with a compass and decide the place of the main activities. For example, machinery is kept in the south and south-west and the customers' entrance in the north-east.
- **Checking the energy.** Look for obstructions in the space, such as uneven shapes, blockages or imbalance of the elements, and correct them according to the rules of Vastu.

## Reading the Results

- **A favourable position.** According to tradition, a space in line with Vastu is spoken of as having better efficiency, smooth operation and contented employees.
- **An unfavourable position.** Vastu defects such as an unsuitable entrance or an imbalance of the elements are said to bring a risk of delay in work, financial loss, discontent among employees or low sales.
- **Change to suit the business.** Vastu suggestions should always be adapted to the needs of the business and its way of working.

## Practical Applications

### Retail: Shops and Showrooms

- The entrance should ideally face east or north-east.
- The cash box or safe goes in the south-west, opening towards the north, or in the north.
- Use light, auspicious colours and keep the display clean and orderly, so that the setting is welcoming.
- Tradition speaks of keeping a store of raw material in the south-west.

### Manufacturing and Industrial Units

- Keep heavy machinery in the south and south-west zones.
- Raw material is stored in the south-west and finished goods in the north-west, so that dispatch is on time.
- Keep work involving fire or heat, such as metalwork or a kitchen, in the south-east.

### Hotels, Health Services, Education and Services

- **Hotels.** The entrance in the north or east for the coming and going of guests, the reception in the north-east and guest rooms in the south-west for rest.
- **Health services.** There is a tradition of keeping the patients' areas in the east or north-east for healing.
- **Education and services.** Classrooms and offices should face east for learning. Workplaces should be in the north or east for efficiency.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Vastu applies to all in the same way.** The plan for each industry should be different, according to its work and its challenges.
- **Vastu is only about directions.** It also includes the plan, colours, materials and the use of space, not only the directions of the compass.
- **Only new buildings benefit.** Even in places already built, changes or remedies can be adopted.

## Examples

These examples are plain illustrations. No outcome is promised.

### Example 1: A Clothing Boutique

A boutique had its main entrance in the east and its store in the south-west, and it set its billing counter in the south-west facing north. This is an example of an arrangement suggested by Vastu. In tradition it is regarded as favourable to the movement of customers and to operations.

### Example 2: A Production Unit

A food processing factory kept its heat-producing machines in the south-east and its finished goods in the north-west. This follows the traditional rule for the zone of the fire element and for the outflow of goods.

## Conclusion

Vastu for different businesses is a link between old knowledge and today's practice, giving suggestions to suit each kind of business space. According to tradition, matching the plan of a space, the entrance and the elements to Vastu makes a setting favourable to prosperity, harmony and progress. Whether starting a new business or improving one that is running, these rules serve as useful guidance. They do not replace engineering, safety rules or sound business practice.

## FAQs

### Is Vastu the same for all businesses?

No. The plan for each industry should be made according to its work and its challenges. The needs of manufacturing, retail, hotels, health services and education are different.

### Where should the entrance and the cash counter of a shop be?

The entrance should ideally face east or north-east. The cash box or safe is placed in the south-west, opening towards the north, or in the north. A clean display and light, auspicious colours are also suggested.

### Where should machinery and goods be kept in a factory?

Heavy machinery in the south and south-west. A store for raw material is placed in the south-west and for finished goods in the north-west. Work involving fire or heat is kept in the south-east.

### Where should the room of the owner or manager be?

There is a tradition of placing it in the south-west for authority and stability.

### Is Vastu only for new buildings?

No. In a place already built, changes and remedies can also be adopted.

### How should one begin?

Look at the plan of your business space, its entrance and the position of the elements according to the basics of Vastu. For advice specific to your industry, meet an experienced consultant, and try small changes.
