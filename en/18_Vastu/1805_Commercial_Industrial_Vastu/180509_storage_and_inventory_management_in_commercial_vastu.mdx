# Warehouse and Stock Storage Vastu: Direction and Layout

## Introduction

Vastu for warehouses and the storage of goods is an interesting meeting of ancient tradition and present-day business methods. Vastu Shastra is the ancient science of building, and commercial Vastu gives guidelines for the places where goods are kept and handled. Vastu tradition holds that following these principles keeps the flow of energy right, lets work run smoothly, lessens the wastage of goods and helps the prosperity of a business. Traders, warehouse managers and those who decide the layout of a shop can understand these principles and arrange their stock better. Vastu is not a substitute for sound stock management, fire safety, structural engineering or good business practice, and it makes no promise of results.

## What is meant by storage in commercial Vastu?

It means arranging commercial storage places with thought, according to Vastu Shastra. This method tells the directions for keeping different kinds of goods, the layout of racks and the arrangement of stock, so that a balanced flow of energy is kept in warehouses, godowns and the stockroom of a shop.

Present-day stock management systems look only at transport and efficiency. Commercial Vastu looks at storage from the point of view of energy, and considers how the five elements (earth, water, fire, air and space) act in a commercial space. According to Vastu tradition the place of goods, the direction of entrances and even the height of the racks influence the pattern of energy, which is thought to bear upon business, the efficiency of employees and prosperity.

## Key aspects

### Importance of the directions

The directions are held to play a large part in a storage space. Each direction is linked with a particular energy, and the corners and the centre with the elements. The north-east is linked with the water element and should be kept light and empty. The south-west is the direction of the earth element and of stability, so it is regarded as ideal for keeping heavy goods. The north and east are regarded as auspicious for the main entrance, because they are believed to bring positive energy and opportunity into the warehouse. Heavy machinery, raw material and large racks are advised to be kept in the south and the west.

### Points about the structure

The shape of a storage building affects its following of Vastu. According to Vastu principles a warehouse should ideally be square or rectangular, so that stability and balance are kept. An awkward shape, or a plot with too much slope, is held to bring an imbalance of Vastu. For a proper movement of energy the height of the ceiling is said to be kept at least 9 feet. Light colours are regarded as right for the walls and the ceiling, which make the atmosphere positive and open.

### Sorting the goods

Vastu Shastra has separate guidelines for keeping different kinds of goods.

- Heavy goods and raw material should be kept in the south, south-west or west, so that stability is kept and the weight is supported.
- Light goods and finished products are regarded as better kept in the north, north-west or north-east, so that energy flows easily and goods can be sent out with ease.
- Among food items, grain should be kept in the south-west part, which in tradition keeps abundance. Oil and ghee are advised for the south or the south-east. Dairy products such as milk and curd are regarded as better kept in the north or the north-west part.

## How to examine the space

Traditional Vastu has no complicated mathematics, but an orderly examination of the space is needed. Usually the following process is used.

1. Map the directions. First fix the exact directions of your store with a compass. All the rest of the arrangement depends on this.
2. Divide into zones. According to the grid of the Vastu Purusha Mandala, divide the warehouse or store into nine zones. Each zone is believed to be linked with a particular element and energy.
3. Check the proportion. Look at the ratio of the length and the width of your store. Ideally it should be 1 to 1 (square) or 1 to 2 (rectangle). An awkward shape needs special care and possibly corrective measures.
4. Set the order of rack heights. Keep the height of the racks so that it rises slowly from the north-east (lowest) to the south-west (highest). This makes a diagonal slope, which in tradition is regarded as good for the right flow of energy.
5. Place the entrance. The main entrance should be in an auspicious direction (north, east or north-east), and its width should be about one seventh of the length of the wall.

## Reading these rules

- Pattern of energy flow: see how goods, staff and energy move in the warehouse. The central part should stay fairly open so that movement is easy. According to tradition blocked passages hold energy back, and this is believed to bear on the speed at which goods sell.
- Distribution of weight: the heaviness of goods is seen not only by weight but by energy. Goods that are dense, dark or of strong effect are generally kept in the south and the south-west.
- Match by element: understand the element of the goods. Goods linked with water are kept in the north-east, those linked with fire in the south-east, those linked with earth in the south-west and those linked with air in the north-west.
- Nature of the business: the reading of the principles changes a little for different businesses. Food storage, industrial warehouses and retail stock each have their own special points.
- Flexibility: if it is not possible to keep all the rules because of the present structure, adopt the principles flexibly. Attend to the most important points only.

## Practical applications

### Layout of a warehouse

According to tradition, a warehouse laid out according to Vastu is regarded as helping efficiency in work and prosperity.

- Keep the main entrance in the north or the east, so that positive energy is welcomed.
- The administrative office should be in the north, east or north-east part of the warehouse, to keep up a feeling of clear thinking and good decisions.
- Keep raw material in the south, south-west or west, and finished goods in the north, north-west or north-east, so that sending out is easy.
- Keep the flow of goods clockwise, that is from receiving to storage and then dispatch. It is believed to resemble the natural flow of energy described in Vastu.
- Install heavy machinery in the south or the south-west, so that stability and efficiency of operation are kept.
- Keep the passages clean and orderly throughout the warehouse. Keep the central part especially clear, so that energy moves properly.

### Arranging a shop stockroom

In retail business the rules of Vastu can be applied both to the stock at the back and to the display in front of customers.

- The storeroom should be in the south-west or the north-west corner of the shop.
- Arrange the display of goods clockwise, starting from the right of the entrance. It is said that this keeps the movement of customers easy and raises the chance of sales.
- On racks, keep heavy goods on the lower shelves in the south and the west. Keep light and quick-selling goods on the upper shelves in the north and the east.
- According to tradition the cash box or safe of the shop is kept in the south-west and opens towards the north (the direction of Kubera), or it is kept in the north, so that the coming and going of money is good.
- Keep the store lit, clean and free of clutter and spider webs. According to tradition these bring negative energy and bear on prosperity.
- Keep cupboards and arrangements according to the Vastu directions. Cupboards for general storage should be in the west and the north, and a bin for waste or compost in the south-east.
- When seasonal goods are changed, put the old and the new stock in suitable places according to weight and speed of sale.

## Common misconceptions

### Vastu is only superstition and has no practical benefit

The energy principles of Vastu cannot be measured by ordinary scientific methods, but many of its suggestions agree with practical principles of handling goods and convenience of work. For example, keeping heavy goods in the south or south-west means they have to be lifted over a shorter distance, and gives stability to the structure.

### A full renovation is needed for Vastu

Many suggestions can be adopted by rearranging the existing space, without changing the construction. One can begin with the arrangement of goods and racks.

### The rules of Vastu are rigid and cannot be changed

Vastu guidelines can be adapted to the needs of present-day business and the limits of the existing structure. If the ideal situation is not available, the principles can be adopted in proportion.

### Only Indian businesses gain from Vastu

Any business can try the points about directions, the flow of energy and the organisation of space on its own premises. This is a traditional belief, not a proven rule.

## Examples

These are plain illustrations, not records of outcomes.

### A manufacturing company's warehouse

A manufacturing company faced problems with stock. Finished goods sold late and there were difficulties with the quality of raw material. After taking up Vastu principles it arranged its large warehouse in this way.

- The main entrance was moved to the north wall.
- The raw material store was made in the south-west part.
- Finished goods were kept in the north-west part.
- The administrative office was kept in the north-east corner.
- A small water feature was put in the north-east part.
- The order of work was made clockwise.

According to the company, goods stayed for a shorter time, the results of quality checks improved and the work of the staff was better. Space arranged according to the directions made the flow of work more efficient, and it was felt that the flow of energy through the whole premises also improved.

### A clothing shop's stockroom

A clothing shop had problems with stock and cash. On taking advice from a Vastu practitioner it made these changes in its stockroom.

- The stockroom was made in the south-west corner of the shop.
- Tall racks for off-season goods were put in the south-west.
- Quick-selling goods were kept on the lower shelves towards the north and the east.
- The cash counter was moved to the north.
- The display of goods was arranged clockwise.
- The lighting of the whole shop was improved.

According to the shop, stock moved faster after these changes, there were fewer shortages of popular goods and sales rose. The staff also said that it became easier to keep the stockroom in order and less time was spent in looking for things. These are accounts of experience given as examples only.

## Conclusion

Storage in commercial Vastu is a complete way of arranging a space that joins ancient tradition with present business needs. By linking storage places, the arrangement of goods and organisation with the principles of Vastu, greater efficiency of work and a chance of prosperity are hoped for, according to tradition. Keeping heavy goods and raw material in the south, south-west and west, and finished goods in the north and north-west, makes a natural order of work, which may make operations smoother.

Someone may question the energy basis of Vastu, but its way of using space in an orderly manner is practical in itself. Whether you build a new warehouse, improve a shop stockroom or change existing storage, these principles can offer useful suggestions alongside present-day stock management methods, fire safety rules and structural requirements.

## FAQ

### Where should heavy goods and raw material be kept in a warehouse?

In the south, south-west or west, so that stability is kept. Heavy machinery is advised to be installed in the south or the south-west.

### Which place is right for finished goods?

North, north-west or north-east is regarded as better, so that goods can be sent out easily.

### In which direction should the warehouse entrance be, and what should the north-east hold?

The entrance should be in the north or the east. Keep the north-east light and free of clutter, because it is the direction of the water element.

### Where should items such as grain, oil and milk be kept?

Grain in the south-west part, oil and ghee in the south or the south-east, and milk and curd in the north or the north-west part are regarded as better.

### Do I have to demolish or rebuild the whole warehouse for Vastu?

No. Many suggestions can be adopted by rearranging the existing space. Begin with the arrangement of goods and racks.

### How should one begin?

Find the directions with a compass. Keep heavy goods in the south or the south-west and keep the north-east light and empty. Keep the order of work clockwise and the central part open. These changes do not need any change in the construction.
