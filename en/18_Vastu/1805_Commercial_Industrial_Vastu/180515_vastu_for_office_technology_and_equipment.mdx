# Vastu for Computers, Servers and Office Equipment

## Introduction

In today's digital offices, bringing the old Vastu tradition and modern needs together has become a common question. Vastu Shastra gives rules of direction for placing computers, servers and technical infrastructure. According to tradition, following them supports innovation, progress and an easy flow of energy. This article explains how a workplace can be arranged according to Vastu so that work is done with concentration and the office remains harmonious. Vastu is not a substitute for electrical safety rules, proper cooling and ventilation, or sound IT practice.

## What Vastu for Office Technology and Equipment Means

It means applying the rules of Vastu to the position and arrangement of computers, servers, communication equipment and other technical infrastructure in a business space. The aim is to balance the energy of the workplace so that technology does not obstruct the natural flow of energy but supports it. The belief is that placing equipment in the right direction helps creativity and business progress, reduces obstacles and keeps employees in a good frame of mind.

## Key Aspects

### The Right Place for Computers and Digital Devices

- Keep the computer in the south-east corner of the desk. The person working should sit facing east or north. It is believed that this strengthens concentration and success.
- Avoid facing south while working at a computer. Tradition regards this as bringing negative energy or stagnation.

### Direction for Servers and Communication Equipment

- Keep servers, Wi-Fi routers and other equipment that uses a lot of electricity in the south-east part of the office. This is the direction of the fire element, which is considered favourable to technology and a strong communication system.
- Printers, scanners, camera monitors and network devices should also be kept in this area, so that the working of the equipment and the energy of Vastu are in step.

### Energy Flow in a Digital Workspace

- Keep both the desk and the desktop of the computer tidy and organised. It is believed that energy then flows without obstruction.
- To balance the dominance of electrical equipment, add natural elements such as plants or wooden decoration. This brings freshness to the space.
- Arrange the desk so that the person faces north or east. These directions are regarded as those of progress and new opportunities.

## How to Decide the Directions

This is not a matter of calculation. It is decided in these steps.

1. Assess the directions and zones of the office with the help of a compass.
2. Place the functions of the office, such as workstations, the server room and meeting areas, according to the Vastu zones.
   - East and north for innovation, learning and progress.
   - South-east for things linked with fire, that is, all the major electrical and electronic equipment.
3. Keep the north-east free of obstruction, heavy goods and clutter. This corner is regarded as the one for inspiration and clarity.

## Reading the Arrangements

- **Sitting in the east and north.** By tradition this encourages new ideas, innovation and financial success.
- **The south-east zone.** It is regarded as helping technical efficiency and reducing breakdowns of equipment or interruptions in work.
- **North-west and north-east.** Keep these for collaborative activities, or for work that involves movement and conversation. Do not keep heavy digital equipment here.
- **A clutter-free setting.** It is believed that energy then flows without hindrance, stress falls and employees concentrate better.
- **The right direction of the desk.** Employees should not work with their back to the main door, or facing south for long periods.

## Practical Applications

### Application 1: Office Layout and Desk Planning

- Arrange the workspace so that every computer user faces east or north.
- Keep digital equipment such as servers and routers together in the south-east corner of the office plan.
- Keep the north-east corner free of heavy equipment and goods. Elements that give inspiration, such as a small temple or a water source, can be kept there.

### Application 2: Upgrading the Technical Setup

- When changing the office technology, look at the Vastu plan and, if needed, move equipment to the south-east.
- Gather cables with cable organisers and cover them, so that there is no visual and energetic disorder. This also improves safety.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Misconception:** All the technology should be kept in one place, whatever the direction.
  - **Reality:** In Vastu each item of equipment has a preferred direction. Gathering everything in one place is said to upset the flow of energy.
- **Misconception:** Digital Vastu is limited to the places where things are kept.
  - **Reality:** Along with the physical arrangement, the digital arrangement, that is, a clear desktop and orderly data, is also counted.
- **Misconception:** Vastu does not go with modern technology.
  - **Reality:** The principles of Vastu can be adapted to a digital workspace, so that traditional knowledge and today's needs go together.

## Examples

These examples are plain illustrations. No outcome is promised.

### Example 1: The Office of a Technology Start-Up

A growing technology company set up all the computers in its main workspace so that they faced east. The server room was placed deliberately in the south-east corner, while meeting and collaborative spaces were kept in the north-west.

### Example 2: A Digital Workspace at Home

A freelancer placed the desk of a home office facing north, kept the laptop and printer at the left and right corners of the desk, and put plants in the north-east. The aim was to raise creativity and mental clarity.

## Conclusion

Placing computers, servers and digital equipment according to Vastu is one way of joining traditional knowledge with modern business needs. By stressing the east and north for desks and the south-east for technical infrastructure, tradition holds that innovation and the flow of energy are supported. The approach is regarded as leading towards an orderly, stress-free and harmonious workplace. It does not replace electrical safety, proper cooling or sound IT practice.

## FAQs

### In which direction should the computer be kept and which way should one face while working?

Keep the computer in the south-east corner of the desk and face east or north while working. It is advised to avoid facing south while working.

### Where should servers and routers be kept?

In the south-east part, because this is the direction of the fire element and is regarded as favourable to technology. Printers, scanners and network devices are also kept in this area.

### What should and should not be kept in the north-east corner of an office?

Keep the north-east free of heavy equipment, storage and clutter. Elements that give inspiration, such as a small temple or a water source, can be kept there.

### Is it all right to keep all the equipment in one place?

Not according to Vastu. Each kind of equipment has its own preferred direction, and keeping everything in one place is said to upset the flow of energy.

### Does digital tidiness matter in Vastu?

Yes. Along with the physical desk, it is advised to keep the desktop and data in order. Gathering cables with cable organisers is part of this too.

### Where should a beginner start?

First identify the directions of the workspace. Place computers and servers in the suggested areas, that is, east or north for desks and south-east for equipment. Remove clutter from both the physical and the digital space and keep natural elements such as plants for balance. For complex offices, take the advice of a Vastu consultant.
