# Simple Vastu Remedies for Existing Shops and Offices

## Introduction

When a shop or office is already built and the owner wants to correct its Vastu defects, the cost and disruption of heavy demolition is a major worry. Vastu tradition describes simple remedies that need no change of the structure. They speak of balancing the flow of energy with colour, mirrors, symbols and decorative items. Their purpose is to improve the atmosphere of the workplace and keep the morale of employees up. They are traditional practices and not a substitute for engineering, safety rules and sound business practice.

## What Vastu Remedies for an Existing Commercial Space Are

These are corrective measures carried out in a building that is already standing, without changing its structure. They include the choice of colour, the position of objects and the use of certain traditional items. They differ from the structural advice given while a new building is being constructed, because they involve surface changes and need no demolition or rebuilding.

The basic idea of Vastu is to bring balance among the five elements (earth, water, fire, air and space) and the directions. Remedies are regarded as steps towards that balance.

## Key Aspects

### Considered Use of Colour

- In Vastu remedies colour is held to have a role. Tradition suggests white or light colours for the east and north walls. Very dark colours are advised against, because they are believed to bring dullness and low spirits.
- Changing a colour needs no change in the structure. It is believed that this lightens the atmosphere of a space quickly.

### Position and Use of Mirrors

- Tradition regards a mirror as a device that changes the direction of energy. A mirror on a north or east wall that reflects an open space is considered auspicious.
- Avoid placing a mirror facing a work desk, because it is believed to make the burden of work and the stress seem doubled.
- Keep mirrors clean and free of marks.

### Symbols and Decorative Items

- There is a tradition of placing auspicious symbols such as the Swastika or the Shree Yantra at the main door, the cash counter or the meeting room.
- Plants, a water fountain and artwork showing prosperity are also regarded as items that improve energy without changing the structure.

## How to Examine the Premises

There is no mathematical formula for this. The premises are checked step by step.

1. **Check of place and direction.** Look at the direction of the building and the directions of the main door, the cash counter and the seating of employees.
2. **Identifying defects.** Identify points that do not match the rules of Vastu, such as a toilet in the north, a blocked eastern door or unsuitable colours.
3. **Remedies without changing the structure.** On the basis of the check, think of the position of mirrors, changes of colour and the arrangement of decorative items.
4. **Apply, observe and adjust.** After applying a remedy, watch for some time and make changes if needed.

## Reading the Results

Remedies are chosen by understanding the purpose and energy of each area.

- If the space feels stagnant or business is slow, look at the direction of the door, an excess of dark colours and a lack of natural light or auspicious symbols.
- Mirrors on the north or east, white or green colours and plants are regarded as signs of good Vastu. Clutter in the south-west, or dark wall colours, point to a need for correction.
- After a remedy, watch the movement of customers, the efficiency of employees and the atmosphere. Remember that Vastu remedies alone carry no assurance of results.

## Practical Applications

### For Cash Flow

- Tradition suggests placing a mirror inside or in front of the safe where cash is kept, whose symbolic meaning is a doubling of wealth. The mirror should show a clean and full space, not clutter. The cash box or safe itself sits in the south-west, opening towards the north, or in the north.

### For Efficiency and Harmony of Employees

- Keep white or light yellow on the north and east walls. It is believed that this keeps the mind clear and the team in step.
- Plants can be kept in the south-west corner.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Misconception:** The only way to correct a Vastu defect is a major structural change.
  - **Reality:** For most matters, methods without demolition, such as paint, mirrors and symbols, are described.
- **Misconception:** Vastu remedies can be carried out only at the time of construction.
  - **Reality:** These remedies are also adopted in buildings that are already in use.
- **Misconception:** Every mirror increases positive energy.
  - **Reality:** A mirror in the wrong place is believed to have the opposite effect, so fit one only after checking the rules of direction.

## Examples

These examples are plain illustrations. No outcome is promised.

- **Example 1:** The main door of an office faced south, which many Vastu beliefs regard as less favourable. The owner fixed a convex mirror outside the entrance and moved the reception desk towards the east. Green decoration was also added in the south-west. These were adopted as traditional remedies.
- **Example 2:** A shop with low sales painted its north walls white and placed plants at the entrance. This is also an example of traditional remedies.

## Conclusion

These remedies for an existing shop or office are regarded as simple and easy to adopt, because no demolition is needed. A considered arrangement of colour, mirrors and symbols lets business owners improve their surroundings with little cost and effort. Take them up as practices of the tradition, and not as an assurance of earnings or success. Vastu is not a substitute for engineering, safety rules and sound business practice.

## FAQs

### Does a building have to be demolished to correct Vastu defects?

No. Vastu tradition describes remedies that need no demolition, such as colour, mirrors, symbols and plants.

### Which colours are considered suitable for office walls?

White or light yellow is suggested for the north and east walls. Dark colours are advised against.

### Where should a mirror be placed and where not?

A mirror on a north or east wall that reflects an open and clean space is regarded as auspicious. Avoid placing a mirror in front of employees' desks.

### Is every mirror beneficial?

No. According to tradition, a mirror in the wrong place can have the opposite effect, so place it only after checking the rules of direction.

### Can these remedies be adopted after construction?

Yes. These remedies are described especially for buildings that are already built and in use.

### How should one begin?

First understand the basic rules of direction, colour and mirrors. Then make small changes, such as keeping plants, changing the colour of a wall or moving important items, and watch for some time. For deeper understanding, take advice from an experienced Vastu consultant.
