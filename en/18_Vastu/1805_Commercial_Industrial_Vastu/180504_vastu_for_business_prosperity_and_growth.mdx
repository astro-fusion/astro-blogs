# Vastu for Business Growth: Shop and Office Guidelines

## Introduction

Every trader and entrepreneur wants success, prosperity and stability in the business. Vastu suggests an orderly method towards this. According to tradition, the balance of natural energies, the right direction of the space and harmony among the elements of the workplace are held to help remove obstacles, make work more efficient and prepare favourable ground for financial progress. This article explains how to understand and apply Vastu advice in a shop, an office or a factory. Vastu is not a substitute for sound business strategy, leadership and hard work, and it makes no promise of results.

## What is Vastu for business?

It is the use of Vastu Shastra principles in commercial places, that is offices, shops, factories and business premises. Vastu Shastra is the ancient Indian science of building, which stresses balancing a building with the five elements: earth, water, fire, air and space. It is believed that such an environment suits health, wealth and happiness. In this view Vastu links the physical space with the laws of nature, gives direction to the flow of positive energy and supports business efforts.

## Key aspects

### Direction of the entrance

The entrance of a business premise is regarded as its mouth, through which energy comes in. According to tradition, a north-facing or east-facing entrance brings prosperity and new opportunities, because these directions are seen as the path by which positive vibrations arrive. The entrance should not be piled with goods, the light should be good and it should look inviting.

- North, north-east or east-facing entrances are regarded as the most auspicious.
- Avoid entrances in the south or south-west, because it is held that these may bring obstacles or financial instability.

### Place of the cash counter

In Vastu principles the position of the cash counter, the safe and the lockers is held to be important for financial stability.

- Place the cash counter or safe in the south-west corner so that it opens towards the north. Placing it in the north is also acceptable.
- Never leave the cash box or safe empty. Tradition speaks of keeping some cash and silver coins in it, with an image of Goddess Lakshmi or Lord Ganesha, which is regarded as auspicious.
- The cash counter should not be in line with the entrance, and should not be near a toilet, a storeroom or a staircase.

### Ways to keep the energy flowing

It is held that when energy flows well in the workplace, stagnation goes away and the paths to success open.

- Arrange the seating of employees and managers so that they face north or east. This is said to support concentration and decision making.
- Keep the workplace free of clutter, so that energy can move easily.
- Use the colours green, blue and yellow. Tradition links green with growth, blue with communication and yellow with clarity and prosperity.
- Keep thornless plants in the eastern or northern corners. It is believed that they purify the air and bring positive energy.
- Fixing a mirror on the northern wall is also mentioned, which is linked symbolically with reflecting wealth and doubling it.
- A water source such as a small fountain in the north or north-east is regarded as increasing abundance and the flow of opportunities.

## How to apply Vastu

1. Assess the site. With a compass, check the dimensions and direction of the plot and identify north, north-east, east and the other directions.
2. Fix the positions on the plan. On the building plan mark the main entrance, the cash counter, the main working areas and the utilities.
3. Corrective measures. If a Vastu dosha, that is an imbalance of energy, is seen, positional remedies are suggested, such as changing the entrance, moving workstations, changing colours or altering the decorative items.
4. Keep watching. After making changes, watch the atmosphere of the workplace to see whether everything runs smoothly and positively.

## Reading the arrangement

- According to tradition, good results are linked with offices and shops where the direction of the entrance and the cash counter is ideal, cleanliness is kept up and the five elements are in balance.
- Repeated financial setbacks, discord among employees or stalled growth may be taken as a sign of a Vastu dosha, for which corrective steps are taken.
- Treat Vastu as a supplement to good business strategy, leadership and hard work, never as a replacement for them.

## Practical applications

### Setting up a new office

When setting up a new office, consult the Vastu guidelines to decide the most auspicious direction for the entrance, to fix the place of large furniture such as the cash counter and the main desk, and to use the suggested colours. It is held that this keeps the energy flow favourable to a good start and later progress.

### Improving an existing business space

For established business places that are going through difficulties, Vastu suggests simple measures, such as moving the cash box, changing the direction of desks, placing particular plants or painting in new colours. According to tradition these small changes help to rebalance energy and remove blockages.

## Common misconceptions

- Vastu is superstition: its roots are ancient, but its guidelines are described as based on practical observations of space, light and surroundings.
- One rule applies to everyone: this is not so. Remedies and layouts must be adapted to the needs, space and activities of the business.
- Results come at once: according to tradition Vastu acts gradually, and its effects show over time.

## Examples

These are plain illustrations, not records of outcomes.

1. An office with an entrance in the south-west. A consulting firm with slow growth moved its entrance from the south-west to the north-east, and moved its cash counter to match. The firm felt that its client base and earnings had improved, which was in line with what Vastu expects.
2. A cluttered shop. A retail shop troubled by steady loss of stock and cash-flow problems cleared the clutter, put a mirror on the northern wall and placed green plants in the east. The shopkeeper felt that the atmosphere became more harmonious and that sales rose gradually.

## Conclusion

By paying attention to the entrance, the cash counter, the flow of energy and the balance of the elements, traders try to build a strong foundation for their business. By combining ancient knowledge with modern strategy, many business owners find Vastu helpful. It remains a supplement to sound business practice, never a replacement for it.

## FAQ

### Which direction of entrance is considered auspicious for a shop or office?

North, north-east or east-facing entrances are regarded as the most auspicious. Entrances in the south or south-west are advised against.

### Where should the cash counter or safe be kept?

In the south-west corner, so that it opens towards the north, or in the north. Do not place it in line with the entrance or near a toilet, a storeroom or a staircase. According to tradition the cash box should never be left empty.

### In which direction should employees and managers face while sitting?

North or east, so that concentration and decision making are supported.

### Which colours and plants are considered right for a business place?

Green, blue and yellow are held to be useful. Thornless plants in the eastern or northern corners are advised.

### Does Vastu give results at once?

No. According to tradition its effect shows gradually. It supplements business strategy, leadership and hard work and does not replace them. Market conditions and human effort also play a large part.

### What should a beginner do?

Identify the directions of your workplace, clear the clutter, make the entrance inviting and place key items such as the cash counter according to Vastu. For more involved remedies, consult an experienced Vastu practitioner.
