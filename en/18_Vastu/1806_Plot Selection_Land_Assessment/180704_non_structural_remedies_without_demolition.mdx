# Vastu Remedies Without Breaking Walls or Rebuilding

Building your own home takes a great deal of time, effort and money. If Vastu was not considered during construction, breaking walls later is often not possible or practical. Vastu tradition also describes measures that try to improve a home without touching a wall, door or window. This article describes those measures.

## What Remedies Without Demolition Are

These are corrections that try to put right an energy imbalance without breaking walls, removing doors or changing the structure. According to Vastu tradition, if Vastu is overlooked in construction it can affect peace of mind, money and general well-being. In such a case, measures without demolition can be carried out on the advice of an experienced Vastu practitioner.

Three aspects are traditionally associated with these measures:

- **The astrological side:** the relationship between the positions of the planets and the form of a building.
- **The Vastu side:** correcting a home or workplace without demolition.
- **The spiritual side:** connecting cosmic energy and the living space through light measures.

### Historical Background

The relationship between correcting energy and harmony with the surroundings is described in ancient Vedic texts such as the Brihat Samhita and the Vastu Shastra manuscripts. Present-day practitioners have adapted these principles to modern living, where changes to the structure are often not possible.

## Vastu and Astrology

### From the Astrological Side

- **Birth chart analysis:** Understanding how a Vastu correction is believed to bear on a person's fortune.
- **Choosing a muhurta:** Picking an auspicious time to carry out the light measures.
- **Planetary remedies:** Balancing the influence of the planets through subtle changes in the building. This is best understood as tradition.

### From the Vastu Side

In Vastu, measures without demolition relate to:

- **Directional alignment:** Deciding the use of rooms according to their direction.
- **Energy flow:** Increasing positive energy through the house.
- **Choice of materials:** Choosing suitable objects, colours and their placement for the correction.

Vastu practitioners advise correcting a dosha in a home or office through small changes. These include moving objects, changing the interior arrangement and adjusting the space. There is no need to break walls or remove doors and windows.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Understanding Vastu Correction

According to this principle a Vastu correction is seen in both the birth chart and the physical space, and demolition is not considered necessary for it.

**In practice:**

- Identify the energy imbalance in your living or working space.
- Apply the Vastu rules for the relevant directions of the house.
- Keep watching how the situation changes over time.

Tradition holds that in most existing houses and shops no major demolition or rebuilding is needed to bring them in line with Vastu. Vastu is based on the balance of the five elements: earth, fire, water, space and air.

### Principle 2: Improving the Energy

The aim is to create a balanced environment through light measures.

**Steps to follow:**

1. Examine the present flow of energy in the house using the principles of Vastu.
2. Mark the Vastu zones of your home or office on a plan.
3. Carry out the measures by moving things and by simple means such as cleaning.

### Principle 3: Pyramids and Yantras

Some people use pyramids, yantras and crystals as traditional or modern Vastu measures. These are sold products, and they are not part of classical Vastu. There is no independent verification of their effect, and this article does not advise buying them or spending much on them. It is better to try simple and free measures first.

## How to Check

### Information You Need

- The location of the house and its orientation
- The areas of concern or the goals of the correction
- If you wish, the birth chart details of the residents

### Steps

1. **Check the direction:** Fix the main directions of the house with a reliable compass.
2. **Map the elements:** Identify the element linked with each part of the house.
3. **Spot the imbalance:** Note the places where the prescribed elements are missing or wrongly placed.
4. **Choose the measure:** Choose measures that do not change the structure and that suit the fault found.

## Practical Uses

### 1. Ways of Cleansing Energy

Cleansing energy is a basic part of measures without demolition. Vastu tradition mentions these:

- **Salt:** There is a tradition of keeping sea salt in small bowls through the house. It is believed to absorb negative vibrations.
- **Plants:** Some indoor plants that freshen the air are considered to increase positive energy.
- **Light:** Placing lights properly to activate areas held to have dormant energy.

### 2. Placing Things Thoughtfully

Furniture and the arrangement of things are believed to play a large part in correction without demolition:

- Arrange the furniture according to the energy of the directions.
- According to Vastu tradition, sleep with the head towards the south or east and avoid the north.
- Remove electronic devices such as a television and a computer from the bedroom.
- Avoid placing water bodies or plants in some directions, as your Vastu check indicates.

### 3. Products Sold for Vastu

Pyramids, crystals and yantras are adopted by some people as a correction without demolition. They are things that cost money. They are mentioned here only as a popular practice, not as advice. The claims made for them are sellers' claims and are not classical Vastu, so do not spend heavily on them.

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Ignoring a Practitioner's Advice

You can carry out measures yourself, but for serious doshas a practitioner's check is often considered necessary. A practitioner can:

- Examine the whole house carefully
- Identify hidden doshas that are not easy to see
- Say where the remedial objects should be placed
- Give a solution that suits your own situation

### 2. Adopting Measures That Do Not Suit

Not every Vastu measure works the same for everyone. Common mistakes are:

- Doing a measure without considering the person's birth chart
- Placing remedial objects in the wrong position
- Mixing measures that conflict with each other
- Expecting immediate results and not being patient

### 3. Not Maintaining the Measures

Many people put a measure in place but do not maintain it properly:

- According to tradition the salt bowls should be changed every 1 to 2 days.
- Anything placed as a measure should be kept clean.
- Plants kept for Vastu need proper care.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Vastu doshas really be corrected without demolition?

According to Vastu practitioners, most doshas in existing buildings do not require demolition. Moving things and changes to the interior are believed to reduce the effect of many doshas.

### How soon do these measures show results?

It depends on how serious the dosha is and how accurate the measure is. Nothing can be promised, and these measures cannot replace medical care or repairs to a building.

### Is it necessary to spend money on pyramids and crystals?

No. Simple measures such as cleaning, the position of furniture, plants and light can be adopted first. Pyramids and crystals are only a popular practice.

### How do I know whether my home has a Vastu dosha?

Signs that are looked for include persistent money trouble, illness, conflict in relationships, trouble sleeping and a feeling of unease in some part of the house. For a firm check you can consult a practitioner.

### Can Feng Shui be used along with Vastu?

Many practitioners use ideas from both systems together. Both stress the flow and balance of energy, but their methods differ. When mixing them, pay attention to the ideas that complement each other and not those that clash.

## Conclusion

Measures without demolition are regarded as a practical and low-cost way of correcting a space in line with Vastu. By understanding the flow of energy, the importance of direction and the balance of the elements, you can improve your surroundings without costly repairs or building work.

The merit of these measures is that anyone can adopt them, whether the home is owned or rented. From easy steps such as placing things thoughtfully to popular devices, there are options for every situation and budget.

Remember that Vastu is not just about following rigid rules. It is about creating harmony between your living space and the energies said to influence it. Take up these measures thoughtfully and do not count on any result.
