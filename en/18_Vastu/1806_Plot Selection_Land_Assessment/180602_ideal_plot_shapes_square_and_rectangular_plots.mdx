# Ideal Plot Shapes: Square and Rectangular Plots

When buying a plot, the first thing people look at is its shape. Vastu Shastra considers square and rectangular plots the most auspicious. This article explains why they are considered so, what the right proportion is, and what is done when a plot's shape falls short.

You will learn:
- The basic principles behind square and rectangular plots
- The ideal ratio, direction and slope
- The order of directions for rooms
- Common mistakes and remedies for irregular plots

## What Is an Ideal Plot Shape

According to Vastu Shastra, square and rectangular plots are the most auspicious and balanced. Vastu tradition holds that these shapes are symbols of balance, stability and harmony. Energy is said to spread evenly across the whole plot, creating conditions of prosperity and comfort for the people who live there. Irregular shapes are said to carry an imbalance of energy, whereas in a square or a rectangle the natural forces stay in balance.

### Historical Background

The idea of an ideal plot shape is traced to old texts such as the Brihat Samhita and Vastu Shastra manuscripts. These texts discuss the relationship between the geometry of land and harmony with the environment. According to them, sunlight carries 32 kinds of energies, called devatas (deities), to the earth in a fixed pattern. The pattern depends on the directions, the landmass and the slopes. The same principle is held to apply to each individual plot, which is why the shape matters.

## Why Shape Matters in Vastu

In Vastu, the shape of a plot is held to affect the following:
- **Alignment with directions:** Square and rectangular plots can easily be lined up with the main directions.
- **Flow of energy:** Tradition holds that in such shapes magnetic waves and positive vibrations spread evenly.
- **Construction planning:** A proper shape makes the planning of materials and layout simpler.

A square plot, with equal length and width, is considered ideal for every kind of construction. Its perfect symmetry is believed to bring all-round success to those who live there. Rectangular plots in a proper ratio of 1 to 2 are also considered very good, and are described as bringing prosperity, well-being and happiness to the owner. In these shapes all the necessary Vastu zones are present within the plot, so it is easier to draw up a balanced layout.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: The Completeness of a Square Plot

All the sides of a square plot are equal, so it is a symbol of perfect balance. It is seen as a symbol of the energy of the earth element and as a solid base for a home. It is believed that when energy is shared equally in every direction, no area of life is neglected and development is all-round.

**In practice:**
- For balance of energy, choose a square plot whose four sides are exactly equal.
- Within a square plot, place the rooms according to the rules of Vastu.
- Watch how harmony and prosperity fare in the home.

### Principle 2: The Proportion of a Rectangular Plot

A square plot is ideal, but a rectangular plot with a width to length ratio of 1 to 2 is also considered highly auspicious. In this ratio the energies stay balanced and there are more choices for construction. Beyond 1 to 2 the plot becomes too elongated and the principles of Vastu are said to weaken.

**Steps to follow:**
1. When choosing a rectangular plot, work out the exact ratio of width to length.
2. Make sure the ratio does not go beyond 1 to 2.
3. Within the rectangle, place the rooms according to their directions.

### Principle 3: Alignment with the Main Directions

The direction of the plot affects the benefits of Vastu. Plots whose sides run along the four main directions, that is north, east, south and west, are considered favourable. It is said that such plots stay in line with the Earth's magnetic field.

**Suggestions:**
- Keep the sides of the plot parallel to the main directions.
- For a rectangular plot, practitioners may differ on whether the length should run north to south or east to west. Take alignment with the main directions as the basis.
- Keep the plot clean and well maintained.

## Method of Assessment

### Information Needed

- Exact measurements of the sides of the plot
- The precise orientation of the plot in relation to the main directions
- Measurements of the existing or planned construction

### Checking the Shape

**Square plot:** All four sides must be equal in length, and every corner must be a full right angle of 90 degrees.

**Rectangular plot:** Opposite sides should be equal and the ratio proper.
- Work out the ratio by dividing the length by the width.
- The ideal ratio is 1 to 1 (a square), or between 1 to 1 and 1 to 2 (a rectangle).
- Example: A plot of 30 ft by 60 ft has a ratio of 1 to 2, which is considered ideal.
- When the ratio goes beyond 1 to 2, the benefits of Vastu are said to fall.

### Assessing the Plot from the Vastu View

When judging a plot against Vastu, check the following:

1. **Completeness of shape:**
   - Is the shape a full square or rectangle?
   - Is any corner cut off, or any part projecting outward?

2. **Alignment with directions:**
   - Does the plot line up with the main directions?
   - Which direction does the plot face?

3. **Slope:**
   - According to tradition, the ground level should be higher in the south and west.
   - The north-east (Ishan) corner should be the lowest point.
   - A plot sloping towards the east is considered good for wealth.
   - A slope towards the south-east (Agneya) is said to bring sorrow, and a slope towards the south-west (Nairitya) is said to create fear.

## Practical Use

### Residential Use

Square and rectangular plots are held to have several advantages for a home:

1. **Spread of energy:** The symmetry of these shapes lets energy flow evenly through the whole house and keeps the atmosphere of the home balanced.

2. **Placing of rooms:** When the directions are clear, it is easier to follow the Vastu rules for the place of rooms:
   - North-east (Ishan): Prayer room or water storage
   - East: Main entrance or living room
   - South-east (Agneya): Kitchen or other fire-related places
   - South: Storage or heavy goods
   - South-west (Nairitya): Master bedroom or heavy furniture
   - West: Dining room or children's room
   - North-west (Vayavya): Guest room or storage
   - North: Safe, study room or water-related places

3. **Health and prosperity:** Tradition holds that a square plot is linked with all-round growth, happiness and prosperity.

### Commercial Use

For shops and offices:

1. **Business:** A square plot is believed to bring balance to the running of a business and steady progress.

2. **Teamwork:** A balanced shape is said to help harmony among the people who work there.

3. **Effect on clients:** A well-built commercial space on a plot that follows Vastu makes a good impression on clients and partners.

## Common Mistakes

### Not Taking Accurate Measurements

The commonest mistake is to assume that a plot is a square or a rectangle without measuring it properly. Even a small difference can reduce the Vastu benefits.

- **Mistake:** Judging by eye or taking rough measurements
- **Remedy:** Always take exact measurements and angles with professional surveying instruments

### Overlooking Cuts and Projections

Many plots look square or rectangular but have small cuts or projections at the corners.

- **Mistake:** Not noticing small irregularities in a plot that looks like a square or rectangle
- **Remedy:** A plot with a cut, especially in the north-east corner, should be corrected before building begins

### Misjudging the Ratio

Not every rectangle is equally good.

- **Mistake:** Choosing a very long rectangular plot whose ratio goes beyond 1 to 2
- **Remedy:** For the greatest Vastu benefit, keep the ratio of width to length at 1 to 2 or less

### Ignoring Direction

Even a perfect square or rectangle must lie in the right direction.

- **Mistake:** Building without checking how the plot lines up with the main directions
- **Remedy:** Keep the sides of the plot in line with the true north to south and east to west lines

An office stood on a triangular plot. Vastu tradition links a triangular shape with fire-like energy and with trouble from government authorities. ### Suggestions from Practitioners
- Always have the measurements of the plot confirmed by a professional survey
- In existing homes on irregular plots, mark the boundaries with plants and fencing
- Look at the slope of the ground along with the shape
- After correcting the shape, keep a written record of the changes

### About Square Plots

A square plot is considered beneficial to everyone for these reasons:

1. It has no incomplete corner, so Vastu regards it as especially fortunate
2. It is believed to share magnetic waves evenly
3. It is a symbol of stability, giving equal weight to all areas of life

For the greatest benefit, practitioners suggest the following:
- Choose a square plot that faces north-east, east or north
- Make all the corners exact right angles
- Keep the ground level, or give it a gentle slope from the south and west towards the north and east

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why are square and rectangular plots considered better than other shapes?

According to Vastu tradition, these shapes share magnetic waves and energies evenly, whereas irregular shapes such as triangular or circular plots create an imbalance of energy. The square and the rectangle are symbols of stability and balance.

### What is the ideal ratio for a rectangular plot?

The width to length ratio of an ideal rectangular plot should be 1 to 2 or less. This both follows Vastu and leaves practical choices for construction. When the elongation goes beyond 1 to 2, the Vastu benefits are said to fall.

### Can a house be built on an irregular plot?

Yes, with proper Vastu correction. The suggested method is to give the usable part the form of a square or rectangle with a fence, landscaping or a building line, and to build only within that part.

### Which direction should a square or rectangular plot face?

Plots facing north, north-east and east are generally considered the most auspicious. This direction is described as especially beneficial for these shapes.

### Is any shape other than a square or rectangle considered good?

Yes, in some cases. Circular plots are considered good for educational institutions and financial institutions, because they are said to promote knowledge and the growth of wealth. A building on such a plot should also be circular. Octagonal plots are also considered auspicious.

### What should I do if a plot has cut corners?

They should be corrected before building starts, especially a cut in the north-east corner. Mark out the full shape with a fence or boundary and build within it.

## Conclusion

In Vastu tradition, square and rectangular plots are considered the most auspicious for both homes and business. Because of its perfect symmetry, a square plot is ideal for every kind of construction. A rectangular plot in the proper ratio of 1 to 2 is a practical choice and also remains in keeping with Vastu. In both, energy is believed to spread evenly.

When choosing a plot, give weight to the direction, the slope and the surroundings along with the shape. On an irregular plot, measures such as fencing and landscaping can be used. The shape of your plot is regarded as the base of the energy of your property, so choose it with care.
