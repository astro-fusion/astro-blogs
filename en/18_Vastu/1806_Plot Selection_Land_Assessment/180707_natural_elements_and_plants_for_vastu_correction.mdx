# Natural Elements and Plants in Vastu: Tulsi to Water

A gentle way of improving the balance of a home without changing its structure is to use the elements of nature and plants. This article explains the Vastu tradition of balancing the five elements through plants and natural materials, in which the Tulsi has a special place.

## What Correcting With Natural Elements and Plants Means

It is a way of balancing the energy of a home with the help of the elements of nature and plants, without altering the construction. Three aspects are described:

- **The astrological side:** It is believed that the choice and placing of natural elements relates to the positions of the planets.
- **The Vastu side:** Bringing the five elements (the Pancha Bhoota) into the home through plants and natural materials.
- **The spiritual side:** A relationship between cosmic energy and the living space without heavy construction.

According to Vastu Shastra, faults in the design of a building can cause an imbalance in the five elements of air, water, fire, earth and space (akash), which is held to hinder a person's progress. Correction with natural elements is described as a gentle way, without demolition, of restoring this balance.

### Historical Background

It is said that this idea comes from Vedic texts such as the Brihat Samhita and the Vastu Shastra manuscripts, in which the sages described the relationship between the natural elements and harmony with the surroundings. These texts stress that the five elements, earth, water, fire, air and space, should be in proper proportion and in the proper place.

## Natural Elements in Vastu and Astrology

### From the Astrological Side

- **Birth chart:** It is believed that the dominance of an element influences a person's fortune.
- **Muhurta:** Choosing an auspicious time to bring plants and natural things into the home.
- **Planetary measures:** It is said that cosmic influences are balanced by placing plants and symbols of the elements with thought.

### From the Vastu Side

- **Directional alignment:** Placing plants and natural things according to the directions.
- **Energy flow:** Creating a positive atmosphere in the home without changing the structure.
- **Choice of materials:** Choosing natural materials that match the energy of each element.

It is believed that balance among the five elements is good for health, money and relationships. By this method, even modern homes try to make Vastu-friendly changes without demolition or major repair.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Harmony of the Five Elements

Earth, water, fire, air and space are the foundation of Vastu Shastra, and they should be kept in balance in their related directions.

**How to do it:**

- Identify the imbalances of the home through a Vastu check.
- Place the related natural element in the suitable directions.
- Keep watching how things change over time.

Each element has its own direction and meaning:

| Element | Direction | Attribute | Natural measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | South-west (Nairitya) | Stability, grounding | Yellow plants, stones |
| Water | North-east (Ishan) | Prosperity, clarity | Water features, blue items |
| Fire | South-east (Agneya) | Energy, passion | Red flowers, candles, sunlight |
| Air | North-west (Vayavya) | Growth, creativity | Wind chimes, light moving decorations |
| Space | Centre | Expansion, wisdom | Open space, light colours |

### Principle 2: Balancing Vastu Through Plants

Plants regarded as sacred and auspicious are held to be important in creating a balanced atmosphere and in correcting Vastu faults.

**Steps to follow:**

1. Choose plants according to the need of the direction and the Vastu fault.
2. Place the plants according to their element.
3. Look after them properly so that their Vastu benefit is kept.

**Tulsi (holy basil):** It is regarded as the most sacred plant in Vastu Shastra. It is believed that Tulsi creates a positive atmosphere and reduces stress. It is said to be kept in the east, north-east or north corner of the house. According to tradition this:

- Keeps the surroundings and the air pure.
- Balances the five elements.
- Creates a peaceful atmosphere.
- Is held to protect against negative energy.

**Money plant:** It is regarded as raising the energy of prosperity when kept in the south-east or east.

**Other plants regarded as helpful:**

- **North-east:** Peace lily, jasmine
- **East:** Bamboo, morning glory
- **South-east:** Sunflower, marigold
- **South:** Thorny plants and cactus (in limited number)
- **South-west:** Jade plant, snake plant
- **West:** Aloe vera, rubber plant
- **North-west:** Bonsai, small flowering plants

### Principle 3: Natural Materials

The use of natural materials is also described for correcting Vastu faults.

**Suggestions:**

- Where the earth element is to be increased, stone sculptures or heavy natural objects are placed.
- Use natural materials such as wood, stone and metal according to the quality of their element.

## How to Check Direction and Element

You need this information:

- The existing plan of the house and its orientation.
- The areas of concern or the aims of the correction.
- The natural elements and plants already in the space.

**Method of checking:**

1. **Map the directions:** Identify the main directions of the house accurately with a compass.
2. **Analyse the elements:** See which element:
   - is too much and creating imbalance,
   - is too little and needs increasing,
   - is placed in the wrong direction.
3. **Identify the fault:** Common Vastu faults are:
   - cut corners, especially in the south-west,
   - a missing part of a direction,
   - wrongly placed rooms,
   - blocked flow of energy.
4. **Choose the measure:** Choose natural elements according to the fault and its place:
   - For a cut corner in the south-west: yellow plants and heavy objects.
   - For a blockage in the north-east: a water feature or blue-flowering plants.
   - For an imbalance in the south-east: bright red flowers and things that increase light.

## How to Use Them in the Home

### The Water Element

In Vastu, water is linked with prosperity and emotional balance. When placed properly it is regarded as important in the balance of energy.

**Suitable places:**

- A small fountain in the north-east, which is believed to increase the energy of prosperity.
- A fish tank in the north, regarded as increasing business opportunities.
- Pictures on a water theme in the north or north-east.

**Cautions:**

- Do not keep a water element in the south-east (the direction of fire), because it creates opposing energy.
- Keep the water clean and flowing, not still.
- Never place a water feature in the south-west, as this weakens stability.

### The Earth Element

The earth element gives stability and grounding, so it is needed for the south-west corner. This corner is regarded as that of relationships and overall stability.

**Effective measures:**

- Place heavy stone sculptures in the south-west.
- Use plants and decoration in yellow, golden or earthy colours.
- Put up pictures of mountains or natural scenes, which are believed to increase the energy of steadiness.

For a south-west corner that is cut or missing, it is said that balance can be restored without changing the structure by using yellow colour and golden light.

### The Fire Element

Fire is a symbol of energy and change and is regarded as strongest in the south-east.

**Natural measures:**

- Place bright red or orange flowering plants in the south-east.
- Make sure this place has enough natural light.
- Use a candle or an oil lamp only with full care for safety.

### The Air and Space Elements

Air is regarded as linked with communication and creativity, and space with expansion and knowledge.

**For the air element:**

- Hang wind chimes in the north-west or west.
- Keep good ventilation, along with plants that clean the air.
- Place light and moving decoration in these spots.

**For the space element:**

- Keep the centre of the house relatively open.
- Keep light colours and little decoration.
- Plants with rounded leaves are kept, and they are believed to bring harmony to the space.

## Common Mistakes

### Too Many Plants

Plants are good, but too many can create still energy and too much damp. Keep a few well-chosen plants and do not turn the house into a jungle.

### Placing Elements in the Wrong Place

Placing elements in opposing directions can worsen a Vastu fault:

- Do not place a water feature in a fire-dominant place (south-east).
- Do not place a fire element in a water-dominant place (north-east).
- Do not keep thorny plants in the south-west or the north-east.

### Not Looking After the Plants

Dry, wilted or dead plants are regarded as giving negative energy. According to Vastu belief, the Tulsi should be looked after properly and never allowed to dry out or get spoiled, or it is regarded as inauspicious.

### Ignoring the Whole Check

Vastu doshas are rarely formed by an imbalance in one direction alone. Several directions together can make a problem, so make the correction by looking at the whole house.

### Practical Advice

- Combine plants with the colours of their element.
- Change flowering plants with the season to keep them fresh.
- It is said to keep the number of plants odd (1, 3, 5).
- Keep the space around the Tulsi especially clean and keep it raised on a platform.
- In cities where natural light is low, vertical gardens and rooftop water features are used to keep the balance of the elements.
- If you notice damp or cracks in a wall, call an engineer. Plants do not repair a building.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Vastu be improved without demolition?

Vastu tradition says yes. The attempt is made to restore balance by placing the five elements thoughtfully in their directions, with no need to change the construction.

### In which direction is the Tulsi best kept?

The most auspicious place for the Tulsi is said to be the east, north-east or north corner of the house. In Vastu Shastra these directions are linked with the deities and positive energy.

### How do the five elements of Vastu affect my home?

Earth, water, fire, air and space are regarded as linked with different sides of life, such as stability, prosperity, energy, growth and knowledge. According to tradition, keeping them balanced brings harmony to the home.

### Can plants really correct Vastu faults?

In tradition every plant is thought to hold the energy of an element, and placing it properly is said to reduce faults. This is a traditional belief and should not be taken as proven fact.

### Which plants are regarded as best for prosperity in Vastu?

A money plant in the south-east or east, a jade plant in the south-west and bamboo in the east are regarded as good for prosperity. A Tulsi in the right place is also regarded as creating an auspicious atmosphere.

### How can the fire element be balanced without real fire?

It is said that the fire element can be balanced by placing red or orange flowering plants, sunlight, triangular shapes and pictures of sunrise or a flame in the south-east.

## Conclusion

Natural elements and plants are regarded as a gentle way of improving the balance of a home without heavy construction. By understanding earth, water, fire, air and space and their directions, you can place plants and natural materials with thought.

The sacred Tulsi is regarded in Vastu as the cornerstone of measures with plants. With plants and symbols of the elements suited to each direction, it makes up the whole approach.

Vastu is not a name for rigid rules but a way of making harmony with the forces of nature. The merit of these measures is that they can be changed, removed and improved. Start with small changes, watch the effect and gradually make your home balanced. Treat them as traditional belief, which does not ensure any benefit.
