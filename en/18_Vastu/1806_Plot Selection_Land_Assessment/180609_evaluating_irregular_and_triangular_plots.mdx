# Evaluating Irregular and Triangular Plots in Vastu

In Vastu the shape of a plot is held to be as important as its direction. Square and rectangular plots are considered ideal, while triangular, L-shaped or many-cornered plots need to be judged with care. This article explains how to assess such plots and which remedies tradition describes.

## What Evaluating an Irregular Plot Means

It means judging land of a shape other than a square or a rectangle in a systematic way within the framework of Vastu. It has three sides:

- **Spread of energy:** According to Vastu tradition, the shape of a plot decides how energy is shared out within it.
- **Use in Vastu:** Making changes in the plan of the house or office that reduce the effect of the faults of the shape.
- **Spiritual side:** The relationship between the geometry of the plot and the flow of energy.

### Basis in the Texts

This idea is found in the Brihat Samhita and in Vastu Shastra manuscripts, where the relationship between the shape of a plot and harmony with the surroundings is described. According to these texts, square and rectangular plots are ideal because they share energy in a balanced way. Irregular shapes are taken as an obstacle to the natural flow of energy.

## Why the Shape of a Plot Matters in Vastu

Checking the shape helps to decide these matters:

- **Harmony with direction:** Fixing the place of rooms according to the irregularity of the plot.
- **Flow of energy:** Keeping positive energy even in a place of odd shape.
- **Choice of materials:** Choosing suitable building materials and colours to reduce negative effects.

According to Vastu tradition, energy in an irregularly shaped plot is considered weak, and it is linked for the residents with sadness, health trouble, financial loss and unrest. It is held that land of different shapes gives different kinds of energy.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Identifying the Shape of the Plot

First identify the shape of the plot, such as triangular, L-shaped or polygonal. Then see what Vastu holds about it according to its direction, and mark on the plan the imbalance of energy created by crooked corners or projecting parts.

### Principle 2: The Triangular Plot

In Vastu a triangular plot is generally considered unfavourable, because an imbalance of energy can arise in it. According to tradition, such a plot often brings trouble with government departments and other difficulties. For this:

1. Decide in which direction the pointed tip of your triangular plot lies.
2. Adopt remedies according to the direction (north, east, south or west facing).
3. Create balance with a thoughtful plan for the boundary wall and landscaping.

### Principle 3: Increasing the Balance of Energy

- With the help of landscaping, give the irregular shape a more balanced form.
- Fix the place of the entrance according to the direction of the plot.
- For parts considered negative, use natural means such as trees, plants and a water source.

## How to Assess a Plot

**Information needed:**

- The exact measurements of the plot and a plan of its shape
- The position of the plot in relation to the four directions
- Where the projecting parts, cut parts or missing corners are

**Steps of assessment:**

1. **Identify the shape**
   - Square or rectangle: the ideal shape, a symbol of stability and balance.
   - Triangle: a difficult shape, in which remedies must be applied with care.
   - L shape: a missing corner creates an imbalance of energy.
   - Polygon: a complex shape, to which several kinds of effects are attributed.
   - Circular or semi-circular: suitable only for certain kinds of buildings.
2. **Analyse the direction**
   - Plots facing north or east are generally considered more favourable.
   - Irregular plots facing south or west need additional remedies.
   - Look at the choices for the entrance according to the direction of the plot.
3. **Check the projecting parts**
   - Identify any projecting or extended part of the plot.
   - Only an extension towards the north, north-east (Ishan), east and east-north-east is considered beneficial.
   - An extension towards the south-east (Agneya), north-west (Vayavya) or south-west (Nairitya) needs remedies.
4. **Check the corners**
   - A missing corner creates a void of energy, which needs attention.
   - The sharp angles of a triangular plot create concentrated points of energy.
   - Record the fault at each corner so that remedies can be fixed for it.
5. **Check the slope**
   - A gentle slope towards the north or east is favourable.
   - Note an unfavourable slope so that a plan for correcting it can be made.

## Practical Use and Remedies

### Remedies for Triangular Plots

**West-facing triangular plot:**

- Build a boundary wall around the rectangular part of the land.
- Plant trees and plants outside the boundary wall at the south-west and south-east corners.
- Concentrate the landscaping work on the north side.
- Keep a place for parking and make a lawn around it.

**North or east-facing triangular plot:**

- Plant trees and plants in the south-west and north-west directions.
- Keep the plants outside the boundary wall, leaning towards the east.
- Keep a separate place for parking in the plan.

**South-facing triangular plot:**

- Plant trees in the south-west and south-east directions.
- Keep the plants outside the boundary wall, towards the east.
- Include a place for parking in the plan.

### Remedies for L-Shaped Plots

An L-shaped plot usually has one or more important corners missing, which creates an imbalance of energy. For this:

1. **Fixing the boundary:**
   - Imagine a rectangular boundary that encloses the whole L shape.
   - Treat the missing part as a separate zone for remedies.
2. **Treating the missing corner:**
   - If the north-east corner is missing, make a water source in the north-east part that is available.
   - If the south-west corner is missing, put heavy objects or dense landscaping there.
   - If the south-east corner is missing, arrange a fire element or proper lighting there.
3. **Place of rooms:**
   - Keep important rooms such as the master bedroom and the prayer room in the regular part of the plot.
   - Keep the extended parts for places used less, such as storage, a garage or a garden.

### Remedies for Other Irregular Shapes

**Pentagonal plot:**

- If the fifth corner projects into the north-east, it is considered highly unfavourable.
- Make an imaginary rectangular or square boundary within the pentagon.
- With the help of landscaping, make the shape look ordinary.
- Fix the entrance according to the main direction of the plot.

**Quadrilateral plot:**

- Give it the form of a square or rectangle according to Vastu guidance.
- Correct the angles with landscaping and boundary walls.
- Create visual balance with a thoughtful placing of elements.

### Gomukhi and Shermukhi Plots

A Gomukhi (cow-faced) plot is narrow at the front and wide at the back. It is considered auspicious for residence. A Shermukhi (lion-faced) plot is wide at the front and narrow at the back, and is considered suitable for commercial construction.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Ignoring the effect of the shape of the plot**
   - Many people look only at the location and forget so basic a matter as shape.
   - An irregular plot should be judged with care before it is bought.
   - Take the advice of a Vastu practitioner when considering such a plot.
2. **Placing the entrance wrongly**
   - Placing an entrance without regard to the irregularity of the plot increases bad effects.
   - Every irregular shape needs a different place for the entrance.
   - In spite of the limits of the plot, the entrance should face the flow of auspicious energy.
3. **Ignoring remedies by direction**
   - Different directions need different remedies.
   - Applying general remedies without looking at direction can make the problem worse.
4. **Ignoring projecting and cut parts**
   - An extension in some directions is considered beneficial and in others harmful.
   - Cut corners create a void of energy, which must be removed.
   - Dealing with all extensions and cuts in the same way leaves an imbalance.
5. **Ignoring the slope**
   - The slope affects the flow of energy a good deal.
   - If the slope is not right, the problem of shape grows.
   - For a full solution, use remedies for both shape and slope together.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are all irregular plots inauspicious according to Vastu?

Square and rectangular plots are considered ideal, but irregular plots too can be made favourable with proper Vastu remedies. The main point is to make the right correction according to the shape and direction of the plot.

### What is the best approach for a north-facing triangular plot?

Plant trees and plants in the south-west and north-west directions, outside the boundary wall, leaning towards the east. Keep a separate place for parking and fix the entrance according to the rules of Vastu.

### Can a house be built on an L-shaped plot?

Yes, if proper Vastu remedies are adopted, an L-shaped plot can be suitable for residence. Make imaginary boundaries, put suitable elements in the missing corners, and fix the place of rooms in such a way that the flow of energy stays balanced.

### What are Gomukhi and Shermukhi plots?

A Gomukhi plot is narrow at the front and wide at the back, and is considered auspicious for residence. A Shermukhi plot is wide at the front and narrow at the back, and is considered suitable for commercial construction.

### What effect is the slope of the plot held to have in Vastu?

A gentle slope towards the north or east is considered auspicious. A slope in other directions, especially south or west, needs additional remedies, particularly in an irregular plot.

### Can landscaping correct the faults of an irregular plot?

Yes, a thoughtful landscaping plan is counted among the most effective remedies that can be carried out without changing the construction. With trees, plants, a water source and the layout of a garden, the shape can be made to look ordinary.

## Conclusion

Irregular plots do bring challenges, but it is not necessary to give them up altogether. With proper assessment, planning and remedies, even a plot of difficult shape can be made suitable for living or working. The main point is to understand what kind of energy different shapes are held to create and to apply remedies accordingly.

Every plot has its own character. For your situation, take guidance from an experienced Vastu consultant. By joining traditional knowledge with an understanding of modern construction, you can build a place that is favourable to comfort and peace in spite of the first limits of the plot.
