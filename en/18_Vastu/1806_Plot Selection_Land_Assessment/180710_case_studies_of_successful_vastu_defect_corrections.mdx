# Vastu Defect Corrections: Plain Illustrations

How a Vastu defect is corrected in practice is understood best through illustrations. This article gives some illustrations from a home, an office, a temple and a healthcare business, the corrections that tradition names for each direction, common mistakes and a method of checking. The illustrations are only descriptions of what is named in Vastu tradition for each fault. They state no outcomes and give no guarantee.

## What the Illustrations Are

These are illustrations in which the Vastu defects of homes, offices and business places are looked at and the correction that tradition names is described. They show how the principles of Vastu Shastra are used to address imbalances of a building and place that are believed to affect health, money, relationships and general well-being.

Three aspects are described:

- **The astrological side:** It is believed that the positions of the planets influence the form of a building and the timing of a correction.
- **The Vastu side:** The practical carrying out of corrections in homes and workplaces.
- **The spiritual side:** The relationship between cosmic energy and the living space.

### Historical Background

It is said that the idea of correcting Vastu defects comes from Vedic texts such as the Brihat Samhita and the Vastu Shastra manuscripts, in which the sages described the relationship between harmony of a building and the energy of its surroundings. These texts are said to describe how imbalance of a place creates disturbance in energy and which measures restore balance.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Identifying and Correcting a Directional Defect

How to identify and correct faults linked with the main and intermediate directions.

**How to do it:**

- Identify the cut corners or extended parts of your property.
- Carry out the measure according to the direction affected.
- Keep watching how things change over time.

For example, a cut north-east (Ishan) corner is held to bring financial and health problems. As a measure, growing a Tulsi plant near the cut part or making a strong water source there is named.

### Principle 2: Restoring the Balance of Elements

The part played by the five elements, earth, water, fire, air and space, in creating a balanced environment.

**Steps to follow:**

1. See which elements are low in energy because of Vastu faults.
2. Carry out particular measures to increase the elements that are low.
3. Create a balance for the elements that are too strong.

For example, when a kitchen (the fire element) has been wrongly placed in the north-east, tradition names moving it to the south-east (Agneya).

### Principle 3: Correction Without Changing the Structure

Ways to turn the flow of energy without altering the structure.

**Suggestions:**

- Careful placing of mirrors to turn the flow of energy.
- Symbolic correction with yantras and sacred geometry, as described in tradition.

A toilet in the south-west (Nairitya) is regarded as a major fault. Where it cannot be moved, the advice is to consult a knowledgeable practitioner and not to rely on devices sold with claims that have not been independently verified.

## How to Check for a Defect

### Information Needed

- A plan with accurate compass directions.
- The particular concerns or problems being faced.
- The history of the construction and any changes made earlier.

### Steps of Checking

1. **Analysis of direction:** Identify the exact compass orientation of the property.
2. **Checking the placing of rooms:** See whether the rooms are in their ideal directions.
3. **Checking cuts and extensions:** Identify cut corners or extended parts.
4. **Map of the elements:** See whether the symbols of water, fire and so on are in the right place.
5. **Digital mapping:** Software that overlays the Vastu grid on the plan of the property can help to identify faults accurately.

## Use in Homes and Workplaces

### Corrections in a Home

**Faults in the kitchen:**

According to tradition, fire (the stove) and water (the sink) are kept apart in the kitchen. If the stove is in the centre of the house, the measures named are:

- Move the gas stove to the south-east corner.
- Keep a distance between the stove and the sink, so that fire and water are not close together.
- If the structure cannot be changed, a distance or a screen between the stove and the sink is advised.

**Faults in the bedroom:**

Where the head of the bed is towards the north, which is regarded as inauspicious:

- Turn the bed so that the head is towards the east or the south.

**Wrong placing of a toilet:**

When a toilet is in an inauspicious place, the measures named are:

- Put in a mirror to increase positive energy, taking care that it does not show the toilet seat.
- Keep plants that clean the air, such as a spider plant.
- Keep sea salt in a bronze bowl.

### Corrections in a Business Place

**Layout of an office:**

For offices with Vastu faults the measures named are:

- Change the direction of desks to suit favourable directions.
- Cover beams with a false ceiling.
- Keep the south-west strong and steady in its use.

**Reception area:**

- Plan the reception and the main office in a rectangular Vastu shape.
- Keep the north-east corner clean and open.
- Keep the balance of the elements in the entrance area.

## Common Mistakes

### Wrongly Identifying a Vastu Defect

Many owners of property make these mistakes:

- Leaving aside the major faults and paying attention to small matters.
- Using general measures without a proper check.
- Ignoring how several faults act on each other.

**Right approach:** Do a full Vastu check before any measure, because faults that are connected need a combined solution.

### Ineffective Measures

Common mistakes in correction:

- Using wrong materials for a measure.
- Placing a corrective item in a wrong place.
- Expecting immediate results and not being patient.

**Strategy for correction:** Carry out the measures that are named exactly as stated, with the right materials and the right place.

### Ignoring Personal Astrological Factors

A major oversight is:

- Doing Vastu corrections without considering the birth charts of the occupants.
- Not looking after the timing of the correction.
- Ignoring the person's own balance of elements.

**Combined approach:** Tradition says it is better to join a Vastu correction with astrological timing and personal factors.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Vastu defects be corrected without changing the construction?

According to tradition and Vastu practitioners, many faults are handled by measures that do not alter the structure, such as the thoughtful placing of elements and mirrors. No demolition or construction is needed for these. For a serious construction fault these measures should not be taken as a firm solution.

### How soon do the results of a Vastu correction show?

It is said to depend on the seriousness of the fault and the accuracy of the correction. No fixed time or result can be promised. After a measure, patience is advised, along with writing down any changes.

### Are some Vastu defects impossible to correct?

Practitioners say that almost every fault has some measure, even a toilet in the north-east, which is traditionally regarded as beyond correction. The main thing is said to be a correct diagnosis and a suitable measure. Take this as a belief of the tradition.

### Can a Vastu correction help with particular problems such as money or health?

According to tradition the energy of each direction is linked with some aspect of life, so the correction is made in that direction. This is a belief, and it is not a promise. For health, see a doctor.

### What should I do first when I find several defects?

Make a full Vastu check, then correct the major defects first and the minor ones afterwards, because connected faults need a combined solution.
