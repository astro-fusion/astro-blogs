# Colours in Vastu: Which Colour for Which Direction

In Vastu every direction of the home is linked with an element, and each element is associated with certain colours. On this basis Vastu tradition regards colour as a simple means of correcting a fault. It needs no demolition: changing the wall colour, curtains, cushions and decorative items is enough.

This article explains the idea behind colour correction, the colours suggested for each direction, how to apply them at home and the mistakes to avoid.

## What Colour Correction in Vastu Means

It means using colour with thought so that the directions and elements of a home or office are balanced. According to Vastu tradition, a wrong colour in a direction, or a lack of its element, leaves the atmosphere there unbalanced. The aim is to correct that imbalance by using the right colour.

### Main Aspects

- **Astrological link:** Tradition connects colours with the energy of the planets. It is held that a weakness linked with a planet can be handled on the physical level with the help of colour.
- **Use in Vastu:** The colours prescribed for each direction and element are held to balance the flow of energy in the home.
- **Subtle effect:** According to tradition, colours are not only pleasing to look at; they are also held to act on the mind and the atmosphere.

### Historical Background

It is said that this practice is connected with ancient Vedic texts and old traditions of colour therapy. In these the sages are believed to have described the use of colour for balancing the surroundings and correcting Vastu doshas.

## Colour in Vastu and Astrology

### From the Astrological Side

- **Birth chart:** Tradition holds that the weakness of a planet in a chart can appear as a disharmony in the home. In that case colours are suggested according to the planet concerned.
- **Auspicious time:** It is believed that choosing a good time for painting or decorating increases the effect of the measure.
- **Planetary measures:** For example, Mars is regarded as a fiery planet. So in the south-east (Agneya), the zone of fire, it is said that cool colours are mixed in to balance its effect.

### From the Vastu Side

- **Directional alignment:** Every main and intermediate direction is linked with an element (earth, water, fire, air, space) and its colour.
- **Energy flow:** Colours are held to raise or calm energy, so as to create a positive atmosphere in the home.
- **Choice of materials:** Wall paint, upholstery, rugs and decorative items together strengthen the colour measure.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Joining Colour Therapy to Vastu

Colour therapy means using a colour directly as a measure to bring a space in line with Vastu.

**How to do it:**

- Identify the Vastu zone and see which element is lacking there.
- Choose the colour that Vastu gives for that direction or element.
- Apply it as wall colour, decorative fabric, upholstery or small decoration.

**Example:** If there is an imbalance in the north-east (Ishan), it is said that light blue or white should be used there. The direction is regarded as linked with water and spirituality, and these colours are considered calming and clarifying.

### Principle 2: Balancing the Elements Through Colour

Each direction is held to belong to one of the five elements. Colours are seen as a way to increase or calm that element.

**Steps to follow:**

1. Use a compass to see which direction is the problem.
2. Identify the element of that direction, such as fire, water, earth, air or space.
3. Correct an excess or lack of that element with the colour of the balancing element. For example, green in the east is said to raise the energy of the sun.

### Principle 3: Balancing the Colours

Colour balancing means using opposing or neutral colours to correct an excess of energy or of an element.

**Suggestions:**

- Use complementary colours to make a space calm or energetic. For example, cool blue curtains in a south-east kitchen that has too much fire.
- Bring colour in through several means: walls, furniture, curtains, bedspreads, cushions, murals or decorative items.
- Where the colour Vastu gives is not possible, choose neutral colours such as cream, white or beige.

**Remember:** Balance is the main point. Using too much of a single colour, or mixing opposing colours such as blue and red in one zone, is held to disturb the flow of energy.

## How to Decide the Colour for a Direction

You need this information:

- The exact directions of the home or office, fixed with a compass.
- The parts where trouble with health, money or relationships keeps returning.
- The element and colour linked with each direction.

The order is:

1. Identify all main and intermediate directions with a compass.
2. Mark the problems of the house on those directions.
3. See the element and colour of the problem direction.
4. Decide the right colour for that area.
5. Apply it as wall colour, fabric, pictures and decoration.

Example: If the south-east (Agneya, the fire zone) of a home is blue, which is the colour of water, Vastu tradition holds that this creates disharmony. Repainting it in red or orange is believed to bring balance.

## Applying Colour at Home

- **Paint:** Apply the suggested colours on the walls, ceilings and doors of the direction concerned.
- **Fabrics:** Choose curtains, bedsheets, sofas, cushions and rugs in colours suited to the zone.
- **Decoration:** Hang pictures and place vases and lampshades.
- **Lighting:** Bulbs and fittings with coloured filters or shades can change the mood lightly.
- **Pictures and murals:** Have figures and symbols painted in colours suited to the direction.
- **Small items:** Place coloured bowls, mats or candles in the right spot. These are temporary and easy measures.

**Practical examples:**

- Light blue walls and sheer white curtains in a north-east puja room, to keep the room calm.
- Orange kitchen towels and utensils in the south-east, to strengthen the fire element.
- Beige or light brown upholstery in the south-west, to give stability to family relationships.

## Common Mistakes

- **Using too many dark colours:** Black, deep red and other dark shades are held to create stagnant or negative energy in most Vastu zones.
- **Ignoring the direction:** A wrong colour in a wrong direction, such as dark blue in the fire zone of the south-east, is held to create disharmony.
- **Mixing conflicting colours:** Mixing the colours of water and fire, such as blue and red, in one zone is believed to cancel the effect of both.
- **One colour everywhere:** Painting the whole house one colour without considering the energy of the direction can dull the positive effect.
- **Overlooking the ceiling:** It is advised to keep ceilings white, so that the space feels open and clear.
- **Sharp contrast:** Avoid sudden changes of colour between neighbouring zones. Keep gentle changes or a neutral colour between them, so that the flow continues.

A family faced repeated health and money problems. The north-east, regarded as linked with peace of mind, had a dull and dark colour, which was taken to suppress the water element. It was repainted in light blue and white, and blue accents were added. This is only an illustration and not a promise of any result. Persistent illness needs a doctor.

In an office the staff were low in spirit and there was little team feeling. The north-west, regarded as linked with the air element and communication, had no white or light colour. White blinds, light grey decoration and cream seating were put there.

### Practical Advice

- Always use a reliable compass to fix directions.
- You may adjust for regional or cultural preference, but do not compromise the basic colours of the directions.
- If in doubt, keep the walls neutral (white, cream, beige) and use colour in soft furnishings such as cushions and curtains.
- Keep these areas clean and tidy so that the improvement lasts.
- Keep a record of the changes made so that you can refine your colour plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can colours correct all Vastu doshas without changing the structure?

No. Colours cannot correct serious faults of construction. They are considered a useful means for small doshas and for increasing positive energy, and no demolition is needed. Cracks and damp need an engineer.

### Is it necessary to repaint a whole room for a Vastu benefit?

Not always. Using colour thoughtfully through fabrics, pictures and decoration is considered just as effective.

### What if my favourite colour does not match the Vastu zone?

Balance can be created. Keep your favourite colour only as decoration, and keep the main colour of that direction on the walls and large surfaces.

### Are neutral colours always safe?

Yes. Off-white, cream and beige are generally regarded as neutral in terms of energy and are acceptable in any zone.

### Should blue and red be used together in one zone?

No. It is advised not to mix these colours of water and fire in one zone.

## Conclusion

In Vastu tradition, colours are regarded as an easy means of bringing a home and an office in line with the directions. By understanding the element of each direction and using colour through wall paint, fabrics or decoration, one can try to correct an imbalance without changing the structure. Colour used with restraint and balance can make a home feel pleasant, but it should not be taken as a promise of any dramatic result.
