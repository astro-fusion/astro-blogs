# Wind and Air Flow Testing on a Plot

When choosing land for a house, Vastu also looks at how the air moves there. Air that moves freely is taken as a sign of a healthy and pleasant place to live. This article describes the traditional and modern ways of testing air flow, how to read the results and what use Vastu makes of them.

## What Air Flow Testing Is

Air flow testing means judging the movement, quality and circulation of air around a piece of land or inside a building. In Vastu these methods are used to decide whether a place or a building is fit to live in comfortably.

The best known traditional method is to **drop fine soil from a height** and watch the way it scatters. This old method gives a quick, visible sign of the movement of air and energy.

- **Use in Vastu:** A simple test on the spot to see whether the place agrees with the principles of Vastu.
- **Spiritual side:** Tradition takes the flow of air as a sign of the ability of the land to receive energy.

### Basis in the Texts

Vastu tradition, in the Brihat Samhita and other Vastu texts, speaks of building in line with the elements of nature. It is believed that the ancient builders and teachers carried out such tests for this purpose.

## Why Air Flow Matters in Vastu

In Vastu, the air test helps to decide these matters:

- **Harmony with direction:** The entrance, the windows and the main rooms should be where good air comes in.
- **Flow of energy:** Prana energy should move smoothly through the whole building. Tradition links this with good health and comfort.
- **Choice of materials:** Avoid materials or forms of building that block the path of air or hold negative influence in.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: The Traditional Soil Particle Test

This simple test is still considered useful. At the plot or the site of the house, take a handful of fine, dry soil. On a calm day, release it from shoulder height and watch its movement.

- If the particles stay afloat and drift slowly away, it is a sign of good air flow and of receptivity of the land.
- If the particles fall straight to the ground at once, the air is considered stagnant or blocked, and the place is thought less favourable.

### Principle 2: Harmony of Air Quality

A flow of clean air is good for physical health, and in Vastu it is also linked with the balance of energy. For this:

1. Carry out the soil test several times at different places and at different times of the day.
2. Read the results together with observation of the local wind and modern readings of air quality.
3. Mark the findings on the Vastu grid of the site, so that remedies can be applied in the right place.

### Principle 3: Improving the Air Flow

For a full test, both traditional methods (the soil test, watching smoke) and modern methods (tracer gas, hot wire anemometer, digital visualisation) can be used.

- Use incense or a smoke stick to watch the air, especially indoors.
- For figures, use instruments such as an anemometer or a pitot tube, if available.
- Keep repeating the test when changes are made to the landscape or the building.

## How to Do the Test

**Information needed:**

- The location, direction and layout of the site or building
- The date, time and weather conditions of the test

**The soil particle test, step by step:**

1. Collect fine, dry soil from the plot.
2. Stand in the middle of the plot, or at a main spot inside.
3. Hold the soil at shoulder height and release it gently.
4. Watch and write down:
   - **Floating and drifting:** Favourable, a sign of active and healthy air.
   - **Falling at once:** Unfavourable, a sign of stagnation or blocked energy.
5. For complete information, repeat the test at the four main directions of the plot.

**How to read the results:**

- Particles float and scatter widely: the place is suitable for living or construction.
- Particles fall quickly and straight down: the place may need Vastu remedies, or it should be reconsidered.

**Modern methods:** For deeper analysis, combine it with tracer gas or smoke visualisation of the air, and for exact figures use instruments that measure air.

## Practical Use

- Test the air before fixing a plot for a house or a shop.
- Place the main entrance, the windows and the outlets for air in the directions where the air moves without obstruction.
- Where the soil test shows a blockage, apply measures such as landscaping, a water source or changes in construction.

Examples of use:

- If air stagnates in the south-west (Nairitya), do not place an entrance or an open space there. Strengthen that part with heavy materials or plants.
- On a plot where the air comes strongly and steadily from the north or east, allow more openness and more windows in those directions.

## Common Mistakes

- Doing the soil test on a stormy or very windy day, which gives wrong results.
- Relying on one place or one time only, and not repeating the test under different conditions.
- Overlooking modern causes of stale air indoors, such as closed rooms, heavy curtains or clutter.
- Ignoring the part played by nearby buildings, trees or the lie of the land, which can turn or block the air.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I do the soil particle test myself?

Yes, anyone can do this simple test in the way described. For reading the results, the help of a Vastu practitioner is useful.

### When should the test not be done?

Do not test on a stormy or very windy day, because the results will be misleading. Choose a calm day.

### What should I do if my plot fails the test every time?

Measures such as planting, changes in ventilation and improvements in construction often improve the flow of air and energy.

### Are modern instruments necessary?

They are not necessary for traditional Vastu, but for a large or complicated property they give useful figures.

### At how many places and when should the test be repeated?

Test in the middle of the plot and at the four main directions. Repeat it at different times of the day and in different seasons too.

## Conclusion

Air flow testing joins an old tradition with modern instruments. By combining the traditional soil test with today's methods of measuring air, you can reach a decision that rests on both Vastu and practical understanding of the environment. This helps to make a place to live or work healthy and pleasant.
