# Modern Vastu Devices: What the Sellers Claim

Alongside the traditional Vastu measures, some modern devices and technical methods have come into use. In their favour it is claimed that they measure the subtle energy of a building and correct Vastu faults without demolition. This article introduces these devices, describes how their users say they are used and gives some cautions. These devices are not part of the classical Vastu texts. The claims made by those who sell and use them should be read as claims, and no independent verification of them is given here. This article does not advise buying any of them.

## What Modern Vastu Devices Are

These are a newer class of devices and tools of which it is said that they bring scientific precision to the ancient art of Vastu. They are described as blending traditional Vastu knowledge with present-day technology. Their supporters say that with them a practitioner can:

- Detect, measure and check the subtle energy field of a built place.
- Adopt virtual measures, without demolition, to correct Vastu imbalances.
- Judge the improvement before and after a measure.

### Background

It is said that the roots of these devices are in the texts of classical Vastu and Vedic astrology. In recent decades it is claimed that inventions such as the H3 antenna and the energetic shifter give measurable and repeatable results and bridge the gap between ancient intuition and modern science. This is the claim of the makers and users of these devices.

## Modern Devices in Vastu and Astrology

### From the Astrological Side

- **Birth chart:** Joining the energy of the planets with the arrangement of a place to create harmony.
- **Muhurta:** Choosing an auspicious time to apply a measure on the basis of changes in measured energy.

### From the Vastu Side

It is claimed that these innovations add the following to Vastu practice:

- **Directional alignment:** Mapping and balancing the energy of the directions of a place with tools such as the H3 antenna.
- **Energy flow:** Changing areas of negative or weak energy without altering the structure, using energetic shifters and tools of virtual correction.
- **Choice of materials:** The help of measuring instruments in checking the effect of Vastu aids such as metals or pyramids.

## Key Principles

Of measuring devices such as the H3 antenna it is said that they allow an objective assessment of the energy of a building, the identification of Vastu faults and the choice of precise measures.

- Using the H3 antenna to measure the energy of the five elements and of the 16 Vastu directions.
- Mapping the strong and weak points of energy before and after Vastu measures are applied.
- Preparing reports of energy analysis to follow progress.

### Principle 2: Virtual Correction With Energetic Shifters

Of energetic shifters it is said that they move wrongly placed parts of a building (such as a kitchen in the wrong direction) virtually, and so balance the energy without demolition.

**Steps as the sellers describe them:**

1. Identify the wrongly placed part (such as a toilet in the south-west).
2. Place pairs of energetic shifters or a Shift Max pyramid at the suitable corrective spot.
3. Measure the energy level before and after placing to confirm the improvement.

### Principle 3: Other Modern Innovations

Of technologies such as pyramid antennas, space purifiers and power crystals it is said that they strengthen a Vastu measure and help in checking it.

**What is claimed:**

- Pyramid devices such as the Shift Max pyramid yantra are said to move energy fields in homes and offices virtually, especially in flats where the structure cannot be changed.
- Power crystals and colour therapy are used in the areas identified by the energy-measuring instruments.

## Common Mistakes

- Depending entirely on traditional measures without checking the results with modern instruments.
- Not looking after the proper place and direction of technical devices, so that the expected result does not come.
- Keeping gadgets, an inverter or heavy equipment in an important energy zone such as the Brahmasthan, which is believed to disturb the flow of energy.
- Not measuring the energy level before and after a correction and not comparing, so that no objective confirmation is possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can modern Vastu measures work without changing the property?

Their supporters claim that devices such as energetic shifters and the Shift Max pyramid make a virtual correction, and so they are convenient for tenants and for flats where changes to the structure are not allowed. The claim has not been independently verified.

### Is there independent evidence for these technical measures?

Many devices such as the H3 antenna and pyramids are described as based on the principles of radiesthesia, which some circles of alternative science regard as a way to measure and change subtle energy. No independent verification is known, so such claims should not be taken as established fact.

### How often should the energy measurement be repeated?

The supporters of these devices say it is good to measure before and after every major change, or at least once a quarter, to see the stability of the energy.

### Do these measures interfere with everyday life or electronic goods?

They are described as suited to a modern lifestyle, and it is said that they may even help reduce the stress caused by gadgets giving off EMF. This is the claim of the makers.

### Is it fine to keep gadgets or an inverter in the Brahmasthan?

No. Keeping gadgets, an inverter or heavy equipment in an important zone such as the Brahmasthan is believed to disturb the flow of energy, so it is advised to avoid it.

Modern Vastu devices are a newer trend in the field of Vastu Shastra. No independent confirmation of the results of these devices is given in this article. Before taking up any device, weigh its claims with care, and do not stop attending to the basic rules of Vastu such as direction, the form of the building and cleanliness.
