# Vastu Shanti Puja and Rituals: Steps and Tradition

Vastu tradition describes many measures in the form of worship and ritual. The most prominent of these is the Vastu Shanti Puja. This article gives in plain language its traditional method, sequence, materials, timing and the beliefs about the Vastu Purusha Mandala. Read it as a description of tradition. It does not promise any benefit.

## What Vastu Rituals Are

Vastu rituals are sacred acts of the Vedic tradition performed to correct an imbalance in the energy of a home or workplace. Three aspects are described:

- **The astrological side:** It is believed that the positions of the planets influence the form of a building and the timing of a ritual.
- **The Vastu side:** A practical attempt to correct an energy imbalance in the construction of homes and workplaces.
- **The spiritual side:** A relationship between cosmic energy and the living space through ritual.

At the centre of these traditions is the Vastu Shanti Puja. This ancient Hindu ritual is performed to pacify the nine planets and to invite positive energy into a building, especially a home or office. According to tradition it is performed before a house or building is constructed, so that any negative energy or Vastu dosha present in the land is removed.

### Historical Background

It is said that the idea of Vastu rituals comes from Vedic texts such as the Brihat Samhita and the Vastu Shastra manuscripts. At the centre of this tradition is the idea of the Vastu Purusha, who is regarded in Vastu Shastra as the presiding being of the energy of a building.

According to legend, the Vastu Purusha was a powerful being born from the sweat of Lord Shiva during a cosmic battle. To prevent disorder the gods subdued him and laid him face down on the earth. Lord Brahma appointed him the guardian of all constructions. Since then he has been worshipped as the divine power behind Vastu Shastra, and many rituals have come into use to bring harmony with his energy in the home.

## Rituals in Vastu and Astrology

### From the Astrological Side

- **Birth chart:** Tradition holds that the Vastu Shanti Puja influences a person's fortune by pleasing the planets.
- **Muhurta:** Choosing an auspicious time for construction, repair and ritual.
- **Planetary measures:** Balancing cosmic influences through adjustments made in the building and through rituals.

Vedic astrology holds that rituals such as the Vastu Shanti Puja bear more fruit if performed on a particular lunar day and time. In the puja the Vastu Purusha, regarded as dwelling in the house, is invoked, and the aim is to please him with prayers and offerings so that the negative effect of the heavenly bodies on the home is reduced.

### From the Vastu Side

- **Directional alignment:** Deciding the placement of rooms on the basis of the Vastu Purusha Mandala.
- **Energy flow:** Creating a positive atmosphere throughout the house.
- **Choice of materials:** Choosing suitable building materials and colours.

The foundation of these traditions is the Vastu Purusha Mandala. It is a cosmic diagram in which the body of the Vastu Purusha is divided into 45 deities, with Brahma at the centre, 12 in the inner ring and 32 on the perimeter. Each square is regarded as belonging to a deity or an energy field. Understanding this mandala is said to be necessary for effective Vastu rituals and measures.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: The Vastu Shanti Puja

The Vastu Shanti Puja is a comprehensive ritual in which mantras are chanted, prayers are offered and special procedures are carried out as given in the Vedic scriptures. Its aim is described as balancing the energy of a place and joining it with cosmic forces.

**Sequence of the puja:**

- Fix an auspicious time for the puja on the basis of the birth chart.
- Keep the correct order of the rites, beginning with the Ganesh Puja to remove obstacles.
- In the main ritual, invoke the Vastu Purusha with special offerings.
- At the end, perform the sacred fire ritual (havan), in which ghee, grains and herbs are offered.

### Principle 2: The Balance of the Vastu Purusha Mandala

The Vastu Purusha Mandala is regarded as playing a part in creating a balanced atmosphere. It is divided into several energy zones, each with its own qualities and presiding deities:

1. **Brahma Vithi:** The middle part, regarded as the most sacred part of the building. All possibilities of creation and existence are held to be in it.
2. **Deva Vithi:** The directions around the centre, linked with four deities:
   - Bhudhar: regarded as giving the energy to begin any task.
   - Aryama: regarded as giving skill in building relationships.
   - Vivasvan: regarded as giving the energy of success and renown.
   - Mitra: regarded as giving the drive for positive action.
3. **Manushya Vithi:** The diagonal directions, each with its own energetic qualities.

**Steps to follow:**

1. Understand the Vastu Purusha Mandala in relation to your property.
2. Identify an imbalance or dosha in a zone.
3. Carry out the ritual suited to each zone to balance it.

### Principle 3: The Timing of the Ritual

The benefit of a ritual is held to depend on its timing.

**Suggestions:**

- Consult the Panchang (almanac) to fix an auspicious muhurta for a Vastu ritual.
- Look at the positions of the planets, especially at the time of Griha Pravesh (the house-warming ceremony).
- For positive energy, small daily acts are done, such as lighting a lamp in set directions.

## Preparation and Method of the Puja

### Information Needed for Preparation

- The position and orientation of the house.
- The birth details of the owner of the house.
- The areas of concern or the aims of the correction.

### Steps of Preparation

1. **Fixing an auspicious time:**
   - Look for an auspicious lunar day (tithi) and lunar mansion (nakshatra).
   - Avoid inauspicious periods such as Rahu Kalam.
   - Find the most favourable muhurta from the Panchang.
2. **Gathering the materials:**
   - A Vastu yantra, images of deities, ghee, seven kinds of grain or pulses and sesame.
   - Urad dal, wheat, rice, sticks of wood (samidha) and havan materials.
   - Lamps, candles, sandalwood paste, turmeric, kumkum and incense sticks.
   - Camphor, coconut, jaggery, a bell, moli (sacred thread), betel nuts and betel leaves.
3. **Preparing the place:**
   - Clean the whole premises thoroughly.
   - Decorate the main door with a toran (a decorative door hanging).
   - Prepare the place of worship according to the Vedic method.

### The Sequence of the Vastu Shanti Puja

The full Vastu Shanti Puja is done in several steps in a fixed order:

1. **Sankalpa (resolution):** The first step, in which flower petals, rice and grains are offered to Mother Earth. It is usually done by the head of the family.
2. **Ganesh Puja:** Invoking Lord Ganesh to remove obstacles and for the success of the ritual.
3. **The story of the Vastu Purusha and recitation of the mandala:** The priest tells the story of the Vastu Purusha and explains the Vastu mandala.
4. **Installing the kalash and the Navagraha Puja:** Installing the sacred vessel and worshipping the nine planets.
5. **Vastu Purusha Puja:** The main ritual, in which the Vastu Purusha is invoked and pleased.
6. **Havan:** The sacred fire ritual with special mantras and offerings.
7. **Conclusion:** Prayer, resolutions and sprinkling of Ganga water with mango leaves through the premises.

## Points About Rituals in the Home

### Where the Picture of the Vastu Purusha Goes

The picture of the Vastu Purusha is placed in one of the directions regarded as sacred: south-east (Agneya), south-west (Nairitya) or north-east (Ishan). The place should be kept clean and tidy.

### In Different Situations

**Attached houses:** If two flats are joined by a shared wall, it is said that the Vastu puja is done separately in each flat to keep the balance of energy.

**Renovated or second-hand homes:** The Vastu Shanti Puja is mentioned especially in these situations:

- A change to an old building that alters the structure.
- When buying an old house.
- When correcting a faulty arrangement inside a house.
- After living in a house for ten years or more.
- When returning to a house that has been locked up for a long time.

### Special Parts of the Puja

**Kalash:** An inseparable part of the Vastu Shanti Puja, regarded as a symbol of the universe and the source of life. It is usually of brass or copper, filled with water, decorated on top with a coconut and mango leaves, and placed in the middle of the place of worship.

**Yantra:** An important part, regarded as a vessel of divine energies and a means of invoking and harmonising cosmic forces in the place. The yantra puja includes preparation, purification, installation, invocation of the deity, offerings and a closing prayer.

**Sandalwood paste:** It has a special significance in the puja. Because of its divine fragrance and its coolness it is believed to purify the atmosphere and bring peace to the place of worship.

## Common Mistakes

### Rituals Not to Mix

1. **Doing different pujas together:** The Satyanarayan Puja and the Vastu Puja are different. To keep the purity of each rite it is said that they should not be done on the same day.
2. **Wrong acts:** When doing the Vastu Puja in your own house, it is said to avoid acts such as Sutrabandhan, placing a curd-and-rice offering towards the eight corners of the house, and driving nails into the floor.
3. **Needless complication:** In the Vastu Puja, Sutraveshtan and Gruhapradakshina are not said to be needed, because they make the ritual complicated without adding to it.

### Preparing the Mind

The state of mind of the participants is regarded as important in a ritual. At the time of the Vastu Puja keep the mind fresh and cheerful.

### Mistakes of Timing

A ritual done at an inauspicious time is regarded as having less effect. To know the right time for the Vastu Shanti Puja, consult the Panchang or a knowledgeable astrologer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should the Vastu Shanti Puja be performed?

According to tradition the Vastu Shanti Puja is performed before entering a new house, after major repair, when trouble persists in a house or when an old property is bought. An auspicious day fixed by Vedic astrological calculation is kept for it.

### What materials are needed for the Vastu Shanti Puja?

The main materials include a Vastu yantra, images of deities, ghee, various grains and pulses, a lamp, candles, sandalwood paste, turmeric, kumkum, incense sticks, camphor, coconut, jaggery, a bell, moli, betel nuts and leaves. The needs can change according to the tradition of the region and the advice of the priest.

### Can I perform the Vastu Shanti Puja myself, or is a priest necessary?

The small daily Vastu acts can be done by oneself. The full Vastu Shanti Puja is complex and needs knowledge of particular mantras and procedures, so in tradition it is done by a priest who knows Vastu rituals.

### How is the Vastu Purusha Mandala related to the Vastu Shanti Puja?

The Vastu Purusha Mandala is the basic diagram on which the Vastu Shanti Puja is based. In the puja the aim is to balance the different energy zones of this mandala, and the 45 deities presiding over the different parts of the plot are pleased.

### What benefits are said to come from the Vastu Shanti Puja?

According to tradition it removes Vastu doshas, asks forgiveness for harm done to nature during construction, is held to calm disturbed energy and expresses a wish for harmony and good health for the residents. These are beliefs and not guarantees.

### How often should Vastu rituals be performed?

A major Vastu Shanti Puja is usually performed once, when moving into a new place or after major repair. Small maintenance rituals can be done once a year or when there is a major change in the family or the property. Some practitioners advise a full puja every 10 years.

## Conclusion

Vastu rituals give a traditional framework for joining a living space with cosmic energy. The Vastu Shanti Puja is the cornerstone of these traditions and is regarded as an orderly way of correcting an energy imbalance. By understanding the principles of the Vastu Purusha Mandala and keeping the correct order of the rite, both householders and businesses try to make their place positive.

These rituals have been in use before construction, at the time of repair or as a measure for an existing problem. Tradition holds that the effect of a ritual depends on the right time, the right method and a sincere resolve. Take them up with reverence and an open mind, but do not rely on any fixed result.
