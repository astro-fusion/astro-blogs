# Three Illustrations of Astro-Vastu Principles

## Introduction to the Illustrations

A good way to understand a tradition is to see how it is applied. The three illustrations below only show how the tradition of Astro-Vastu relates a planetary position in the birth chart to a direction of the home. They are illustrations. They are not records of real clients, and no result is claimed for any of them.

## Illustration 1: Difficulty in Business

- **The client**: A young entrepreneur who is finding it hard to make his business profitable.
- **The situation**: His birth chart shows a weak Mercury, which is regarded as the planet of business and communication. His office has a Vastu defect in the north, the direction of Mercury.
- **The traditional approach**: The tradition looks at both the chart and the Vastu. On the Vastu side it speaks of strengthening the north of the office, for example with a green plant or a picture of a lush green landscape. Gemstones, mantras and yantras are also mentioned in the tradition, but they are not offered here as advice.

## Illustration 2: Health and the Bedroom

- **The client**: A middle-aged woman.
- **The situation**: Her birth chart shows a weak Sun, which is regarded as the planet of health and vitality. Her bedroom is in an unsuitable direction and has several Vastu defects.
- **The traditional approach**: The tradition speaks of moving the bedroom to a more suitable direction and correcting the Vastu defects. For any health problem the right course is to see a doctor. Vastu is not a substitute.

## Illustration 3: A Business Shared by Two Brothers

- **The clients**: Two brothers who run a business together.
- **The situation**: The business is not going well and the brothers argue constantly. Both birth charts show a weak Jupiter, which is regarded as the planet of wisdom and prosperity.
- **The traditional approach**: The tradition speaks of strengthening the north-east of the office, the direction of Jupiter, with a water feature and a picture of a deity.

## Conclusion

The illustrations show that the tradition of Astro-Vastu looks at a weak planet and its direction together. A person facing a challenge can consult a knowledgeable practitioner, but no one can promise a result.

## FAQ

### Which direction is looked at for a business owner with a weak Mercury?

The north, which the tradition regards as the direction of Mercury. A green plant or a picture of a green landscape is mentioned there.

### What is looked at in the home in the weak Sun illustration?

The direction of the bedroom and the Vastu defects in it.

### Which direction is strengthened in the two brothers' illustration?

The north-east, which the tradition regards as the direction of Jupiter.

### Are these real success stories?

No. They are illustrations given only to show how the tradition works. No result or figure is claimed.

### What should a person do about a health problem?

See a doctor. Vastu concerns the arrangement of a home and is not a treatment.
