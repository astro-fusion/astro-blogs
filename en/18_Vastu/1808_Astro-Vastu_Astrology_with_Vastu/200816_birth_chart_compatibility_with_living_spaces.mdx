# Birth Chart and Living Space: How Astro-Vastu Matches Them

## Introduction

In the tradition of Astro-Vastu, every living space is held to have its own energy, just as every person has a birth chart of their own. The tradition holds that how well the two suit each other can matter a great deal in a person's life.

It is said that a compatible home supports the favourable features of a birth chart, and that an incompatible home can make the difficult planetary positions of the chart feel heavier. These are traditional beliefs, not certain outcomes.

## How Compatibility Is Assessed

In the tradition, an Astro-Vastu practitioner looks at three things.

- **The Vastu of the property**: The Vastu of the building or plot is assessed first, to identify its strengths and weaknesses.
- **The birth chart**: The birth chart is then analysed to identify which planetary positions are strong and which are challenging.
- **The correlation**: Finally the Vastu of the property is set against the birth chart to judge whether the property suits the person.

## Supporting Favourable Planetary Positions

According to the tradition, a property that is compatible with the birth chart can support its favourable potentials.

### Example: a Strong Jupiter

Suppose Jupiter is strong in the birth chart, and the north-east of the home, which is the direction of Jupiter, is Vastu-compliant. The tradition holds that the favourable effects of Jupiter are then supported, with a leaning towards wisdom, knowledge and prosperity.

## When Challenging Positions Feel Heavier

The tradition also says that an incompatible property can make the challenging planetary positions of a chart more pronounced.

### Example: a Weak Saturn

Suppose Saturn is weak in the birth chart, and the west of the home, which is the direction of Saturn, has a Vastu defect. According to the tradition, the delays, obstacles and struggles linked with Saturn may then be felt more strongly.

## Conclusion

In the tradition, the choice of a living space is counted among the important decisions of life. A compatible space is held to be a source of support, and an incompatible one a source of stress. For that reason it is considered wise, before buying or renting a property, to consult a knowledgeable practitioner and compare its Vastu with the birth chart.

## FAQ

### What does matching a birth chart with a home mean?

It means comparing the Vastu of a property with a person's birth chart, to see whether the energy of the home supports the favourable positions of the chart or makes the challenging ones heavier.

### Who carries out this assessment?

In the tradition, an Astro-Vastu practitioner assesses the Vastu of the property first, then the birth chart, and then correlates the two.

### Which direction is linked to Jupiter?

The north-east.

### Which direction is linked to Saturn?

The west. According to the tradition, a Vastu defect in the west is a concern for a person with a weak Saturn.

### What should be done before buying or renting a property?

The tradition advises consulting an Astro-Vastu practitioner first to assess how well the property suits the birth chart.
