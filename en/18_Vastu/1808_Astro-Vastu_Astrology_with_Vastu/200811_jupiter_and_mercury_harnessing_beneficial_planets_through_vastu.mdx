# Jupiter and Mercury in Vastu: North-East and North

## Introduction to Jupiter and Mercury in Vastu

In Vedic astrology, Jupiter (Guru) and Mercury (Budha) are counted among the two most benefic planets. Jupiter is the planet of wisdom, expansion and good fortune. Mercury is the planet of intelligence, communication and commerce.

The tradition that joins astrology with Vastu holds that the positive qualities of these two planets are supported by keeping their corresponding directions in a home or workplace in good order. What follows is what that tradition holds. It is not a promise of any result.

## Jupiter (Guru) in Vastu

- **Direction**: North-east
- **Represents**: Wisdom, knowledge, spirituality and prosperity.
- **Vastu significance**: The north-east is considered the most sacred and auspicious direction in Vastu. It is regarded as the direction of divine energy and is considered ideal for a puja room, a meditation room or a study.

Traditional suggestions linked to Jupiter are these.

- **North-east placement**: Keep the north-east of the home clean, open and free of clutter. Avoid heavy objects, toilets or kitchens in this direction.
- **Supporting Jupiter's energy**: The tradition mentions a water feature or a picture of a deity in the north-east, and the colour yellow in this direction.

## Mercury (Budha) in Vastu

- **Direction**: North
- **Represents**: Intelligence, communication, business and education.
- **Vastu significance**: The north is regarded as the direction of wealth and career. It is considered ideal for an office, a study or a library.

Traditional suggestions linked to Mercury are these.

- **Northern orientation**: Keep the north of the home clean and well lit. Avoid clutter and obstructions in this direction.
- **Supporting Mercury's energy**: The tradition mentions a green plant or a picture of a lush green landscape in the north, and the colour green in this direction.

## Bringing the Two Planets Together

Jupiter and Mercury are described as friendly planets whose energies complement each other. For that reason the tradition holds that it is good to look after both the north-east and the north of a home.

- **For students**: A clean and well-lit north and north-east are regarded as favourable for concentration and memory in study.
- **For business people**: A well-balanced north and north-east are regarded as favourable for business.
- **For spiritual seekers**: A sacred and quiet north-east is regarded as favourable for a meditation practice.

## Conclusion

Jupiter and Mercury are the two great benefics of the zodiac in Vedic astrology. According to the tradition, keeping the north-east and the north clean, open and well lit is the way to honour their qualities in a home. These are traditional beliefs, not proven outcomes.
