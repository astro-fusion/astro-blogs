# Planetary Influences on Directions in Vastu

## Introduction to Planetary Influences in Vastu

Vastu Shastra and astrology (Jyotish) are often described as two sides of the same coin in the world of Vedic sciences. Vastu Shastra, the science of architecture, is held in tradition to be closely tied to the nine planets (Navagraha) of Vedic astrology. Each planet is linked with a direction, and the energy of that direction is said to be influenced by the strength and placement of its planet in a person's horoscope.

Knowing these links lets a home owner make deliberate choices about the design and layout of the home. The remedies below are traditional practices, not assured results, and some traditions differ on Rahu and Ketu.

## The Sun (East)

- **Direction:** east
- **Represents:** health, vitality, leadership and success.
- **Tradition holds:** a well-balanced east, together with a strong Sun in the horoscope, is associated with recognition and a sense of well-being. A defect in the east, or a weak Sun, is taken to weaken these qualities.
- **Traditional remedies:** keep the east open and well lit, place a copper Sun ornament on the east wall and chant the Surya Mantra.

## The Moon (North-West)

- **Direction:** north-west
- **Represents:** mind, emotions, relationships and travel.
- **Tradition holds:** a harmonious north-west, together with a strong Moon, is associated with mental peace, emotional steadiness and good relationships. A defect there, or a weak Moon, is taken to weaken these qualities.
- **Traditional remedies:** place a water fountain or a picture of a full moon in the north-west, use calming colours such as white and light blue and chant the Chandra Mantra.

## Mars (South)

- **Direction:** south
- **Represents:** courage, energy, ambition and determination.
- **Tradition holds:** a strong south, together with a strong Mars, is associated with success in career and courage to face challenges. A defect in the south, or a weak Mars, is taken to dull motivation.
- **Traditional remedies:** place heavy furniture in the south, use warm colours such as red and orange and chant the Mangal Mantra.

## Mercury (North)

- **Direction:** north
- **Represents:** intelligence, communication, business and education.
- **Tradition holds:** a well-balanced north, together with a strong Mercury, is associated with success in business, good communication and academic strength. A defect in the north, or a weak Mercury, is taken to weaken these.
- **Traditional remedies:** keep the north clean and free of clutter, place a green plant or a picture of a lush green landscape there and chant the Budha Mantra.

## Jupiter (North-East)

- **Direction:** north-east
- **Represents:** wisdom, knowledge, spirituality and prosperity.
- **Tradition holds:** a harmonious north-east, together with a strong Jupiter, is associated with spiritual growth and wisdom. A defect there, or a weak Jupiter, is taken to lessen the inclination towards spirituality.
- **Traditional remedies:** keep the north-east clean and sacred, place a water feature or a picture of a deity there and chant the Guru Mantra.

## Venus (South-East)

- **Direction:** south-east
- **Represents:** love, beauty, creativity and luxury.
- **Tradition holds:** a vibrant south-east, together with a strong Venus, is associated with happiness in relationships and creativity. A defect there, or a weak Venus, is taken to weaken these qualities.
- **Traditional remedies:** place a crystal or a beautiful piece of art in the south-east, use elegant colours such as pink and white and chant the Shukra Mantra.

## Saturn (West)

- **Direction:** west
- **Represents:** discipline, responsibility, justice and stability.
- **Tradition holds:** a well-balanced west, together with a strong Saturn, is associated with discipline, stability and success in career. A defect in the west, or a weak Saturn, is taken to bring delays.
- **Traditional remedies:** place heavy furniture or a large plant in the west, use earthy colours such as brown and grey and chant the Shani Mantra.

## Rahu (South-West)

- **Direction:** south-west
- **Represents:** ambition, desire and materialism.
- **Tradition holds:** a balanced south-west is associated with worldly success and the fulfilment of desires. A defect in the south-west is taken to bring unexpected difficulties.
- **Traditional remedies:** keep the south-west heavy and closed, place a Rahu yantra there and chant the Rahu Mantra.

## Ketu (Centre)

- **Direction:** centre (Brahmasthan)
- **Represents:** spirituality, intuition and liberation.
- **Tradition holds:** a clean and open Brahmasthan is associated with spiritual growth and intuition. A cluttered or obstructed Brahmasthan is taken to bring confusion and lack of clarity.
- **Traditional remedies:** keep the Brahmasthan open, clean and free of clutter, avoid heavy objects or construction in the centre of the house and chant the Ketu Mantra.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which planet is linked with which direction?

The Sun with the east, the Moon with the north-west, Mars with the south, Mercury with the north, Jupiter with the north-east, Venus with the south-east, Saturn with the west, Rahu with the south-west and Ketu with the centre.

### Why does the horoscope matter in directional Vastu?

The energy of a direction is said to be influenced by the strength and placement of its ruling planet in a person's horoscope.

### What is usually advised for the centre of the house?

Keep the Brahmasthan open, clean and free of clutter, and avoid heavy objects or construction there.

### What is advised for the south-west?

The tradition advises keeping the south-west heavy and closed. A Rahu yantra and the Rahu Mantra are traditional practices for it.

### Are these remedies assured to work?

No. They are practices described by the tradition. Treat them as optional customs and not as promised results.

## Conclusion

The links between planets and directions are a rich and complex subject in Vastu Shastra. By understanding them and applying the principles of Vastu, a home owner can aim for a living space that is in tune with the cosmic energies as the tradition describes them.
