# Astrological Dashas and Their Place in Vastu

## Introduction to Astrological Dashas and Vastu

In the tradition of Astro-Vastu, it is held that the effect of a home's Vastu arrangement is linked to the astrological dasha, or planetary period, that the occupants are running. The tradition holds that a Vastu-compliant home may not show its full benefits while its occupants are going through a challenging dasha.

From this thinking comes the idea of making temporary or permanent adjustments to a living space once the dasha is understood. The aim is to ease the weight of a challenging dasha and to keep the support of a favourable one. What follows is what the tradition holds, not a promise of any result.

## What Are Astrological Dashas?

Astrological dashas are planetary periods that indicate which planets are most active in a person's life at a particular time. The most commonly used dasha system in Vedic astrology is the Vimshottari Dasha system, a 120-year cycle of planetary periods.

Each dasha period is ruled by a specific planet. The nature of the dasha is held to be determined by the strength and placement of that planet in the birth chart.

## How Dashas Relate to Vastu

The tradition looks at this relation in two ways.

- **Favourable dashas**: It is held that during a favourable dasha a Vastu-compliant home gives its maximum benefit, and that the occupants may feel peace, prosperity and happiness.
- **Challenging dashas**: It is held that even a Vastu-compliant home cannot completely shield its occupants from the effects of a challenging dasha. Such a home is still said to reduce the intensity of the difficulties and to give the occupants a supportive environment.

## Temporary Vastu Adjustments for Challenging Dashas

The tradition says that during a challenging dasha some temporary adjustments can be made, with the aim of settling the dasha lord and softening its effects.

### Example: the Dasha of Saturn

In the dasha of Saturn, it is held that a person may meet delays, obstacles and struggle. The adjustments the tradition describes are these.

- Strengthen the west direction of the home, which is the direction of Saturn.
- Use earthy colours such as brown and grey in the home.

The tradition also mentions a yantra and donations on Saturdays. These are only a record of tradition and are not advice for anyone to follow.

## Permanent Vastu Solutions for Long-Term Influences

For long-term influences, such as a malefic planet standing in a particular house of the birth chart, the tradition speaks of permanent Vastu solutions.

### Example: Mars in the Fourth House

It is held that a person who has a malefic Mars in the fourth house of the birth chart may meet problems connected with property and domestic happiness. The permanent solutions the tradition describes are these.

- Make sure the south direction of the home, which is the direction of Mars, is Vastu-compliant.
- Avoid Vastu defects such as a toilet or a kitchen in the south direction.

A Mars yantra is also mentioned in the tradition, but this is only a record of tradition.

## Conclusion

According to the tradition, dashas affect how a Vastu arrangement is experienced. Understanding this is meant to help a household adjust its home so that it stays in harmony with cosmic energies, whatever dasha is running. Treating Vastu as something that is attended to over time, not fixed once for all, is the heart of this thinking. These are traditional beliefs, not proven outcomes.
