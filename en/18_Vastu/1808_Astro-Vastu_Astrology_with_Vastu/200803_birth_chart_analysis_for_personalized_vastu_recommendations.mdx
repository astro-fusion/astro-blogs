# Birth Chart Analysis for Personalised Vastu Advice

## Introduction to Personalised Vastu

Personalised Vastu is an application of Vastu Shastra that takes a person's birth chart into account to give customised recommendations. General Vastu principles apply to everyone. Personalised Vastu goes a step further and tries to align the energy of the living space with the planetary energies in the owner's horoscope. Tradition holds that this allows Vastu remedies to be applied in a more targeted way.

## The Importance of the Birth Chart

A birth chart, also called a horoscope or Janam Kundli, is a snapshot of the heavens at the time of birth. It shows the positions of the planets in the zodiac signs and houses, and astrology reads from it a person's character, strengths, weaknesses and life path.

In Vastu, the birth chart is used as a tool for understanding which planetary energies are strong or weak in a person's life and how, by tradition, they are expected to show up in the living environment.

## Analysing the Birth Chart for Vastu

An Astro-Vastu practitioner studies the birth chart to identify the following.

- **The Ascendant (Lagna):** the Ascendant represents the self and is the most important point in the birth chart. The Vastu of the home should be in tune with the Ascendant to support personal growth and well-being.
- **The Moon sign (Rashi):** the Moon sign represents the mind and emotions. The Vastu of the home should be in tune with the Moon sign to support mental peace and emotional steadiness.
- **The strengths and weaknesses of the planets:** the practitioner notes which planets are strong (exalted, or in their own sign) and which are weak (debilitated, or in an enemy's sign).
- **The Dasha period:** the practitioner also considers the Dasha (planetary period) that is running, since it shows which planetary energies are most active at present.

## Strengthening Dominant Planets

Based on this reading, the practitioner suggests Vastu measures to strengthen the dominant planets in the horoscope.

- **Example 1:** if Jupiter is a strong and beneficial planet in the birth chart, the practitioner may suggest enhancing the north-east of the home, the direction of Jupiter, to support its qualities further.
- **Example 2:** if Saturn is weak and difficult in the birth chart, the practitioner may suggest specific Vastu measures for the west of the home, the direction of Saturn, to soften its effects.

## Two Illustrations

These are plain illustrations of how the method is applied, not reports of results.

### A writer and Mercury

A writer's birth chart shows a weak Mercury, the planet of communication. A practitioner might suggest enhancing the north of the home, the direction of Mercury, with a green plant and a picture of a lush green landscape. The writer might also be advised to wear green on Wednesday.

### A couple and Venus

A couple's birth charts show a weak Venus, the planet of love and relationships. A practitioner might suggest enhancing the south-east of their home, the direction of Venus, with a crystal and a beautiful piece of art. They might also be advised to wear white or pink on Friday.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is personalised Vastu?

It is the use of a person's birth chart to give customised Vastu recommendations, aligning the living space with the planetary energies in the horoscope.

### What does a practitioner look at in a birth chart?

The Ascendant (Lagna), the Moon sign (Rashi), which planets are strong or weak, and the Dasha period that is running.

### Which direction goes with Jupiter and which with Saturn?

Jupiter is linked with the north-east and Saturn with the west.

### Which direction is linked with Mercury and with Venus?

Mercury is linked with the north and Venus with the south-east.

### Is a birth chart necessary for Vastu?

General Vastu principles apply to everyone. The birth chart is used when the advice is to be personalised.

## Conclusion

Birth chart analysis is the method by which Vastu advice is personalised in this tradition. By aligning the living space with the planetary energies in the horoscope, the practice aims at a more harmonious home.
