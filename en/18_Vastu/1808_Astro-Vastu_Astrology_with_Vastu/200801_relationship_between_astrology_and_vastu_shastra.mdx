# Astrology and Vastu Shastra: How the Two Connect

## Introduction to Astro-Vastu

Astro-Vastu is the combined use of two ancient Vedic sciences: astrology (Jyotish) and Vastu Shastra. Astrology studies the influence that celestial bodies are believed to have on our lives. Vastu Shastra is the science of architecture and spatial arrangement. Tradition holds that together they offer a way to build a life of harmony, prosperity and well-being.

- **Astrology:** provides a map of a life based on the positions of the planets at the time of birth.
- **Vastu Shastra:** provides the tools to shape a living space that is in tune with cosmic energies.

The belief is that taking the principles of both sciences together makes it easier to face challenges, work towards goals and live a more fulfilling life.

## Key Characteristics of Astro-Vastu

Astro-Vastu rests on the idea that a home is a reflection of its owner's horoscope. In this tradition, defects in a home's Vastu are often seen as matching difficult planetary influences in the birth chart.

- **Personalised Vastu:** recommendations are based on an individual's birth chart.
- **Holistic approach:** a problem is looked at from both the astrological side and the side of the home environment.
- **Targeted remedies:** the tradition holds that remedies work better when they address the root cause of a problem.

## Planetary Influences in Vastu

In Vastu Shastra each direction is linked with a planet. The tradition holds that the position of these planets in a birth chart can influence the energy of the matching direction in the home.

- **Sun (east):** represents health, vitality and leadership. A strong Sun in the birth chart is said to support the positive energy of the east.
- **Moon (north-west):** represents the mind, emotions and relationships. A strong Moon is said to bring peace and harmony to the north-west.
- **Mars (south):** represents courage, energy and ambition. A strong Mars is said to bring dynamism to the south.
- **Mercury (north):** represents intelligence, communication and business. A strong Mercury is said to favour business and education in the north.
- **Jupiter (north-east):** represents wisdom, knowledge and spirituality. A strong Jupiter is said to support spiritual growth in the north-east.
- **Venus (south-east):** represents love, beauty and creativity. A strong Venus is said to bring happiness and creativity to the south-east.
- **Saturn (west):** represents discipline, responsibility and justice. A strong Saturn is said to bring stability to the west.

## Astro-Vastu and the Twelve Houses

The tradition links the twelve houses of the birth chart with different parts of the home, so that the good qualities of each house are supported and its weaknesses softened.

- **First house (Lagna):** represents the self. The home's Vastu should be in tune with the Lagna lord to support personal growth and well-being.
- **Second house (Dhana Bhava):** represents wealth and family. The kitchen and dining room should be in tune with the second lord for financial stability and family harmony.
- **Third house (Sahaja Bhava):** represents communication and siblings. The study room and library should be in tune with the third lord to support learning and good relations with siblings.
- **Fourth house (Sukha Bhava):** represents happiness and the mother. The living room and the mother's bedroom should be in tune with the fourth lord.
- **Fifth house (Putra Bhava):** represents children and creativity. The children's room and entertainment room should be in tune with the fifth lord to support the children and creativity.
- **Sixth house (Ripu Bhava):** represents health and enemies. The bathroom and storeroom should be in tune with the sixth lord.
- **Seventh house (Kalatra Bhava):** represents marriage and partnership. The master bedroom should be in tune with the seventh lord for harmony in married life.
- **Eighth house (Randhra Bhava):** represents longevity and transformation. The staircase and underground water tank should be in tune with the eighth lord.
- **Ninth house (Dharma Bhava):** represents religion and spirituality. The puja room should be in tune with the ninth lord.
- **Tenth house (Karma Bhava):** represents career and profession. The office or workplace should be in tune with the tenth lord.
- **Eleventh house (Labha Bhava):** represents gains and aspirations. The balcony and terrace should be in tune with the eleventh lord.
- **Twelfth house (Vyaya Bhava):** represents spirituality and foreign lands. The meditation room and bedroom should be in tune with the twelfth lord.

## Remedies and Solutions

Astro-Vastu describes a range of remedies for correcting Vastu defects and softening difficult planetary influences. Treat them as practices described by the tradition, not as assured outcomes.

- **Mantras and chants:** chanting the mantras of the planet that rules the troubled direction is traditionally held to calm it.
- **Yantras:** placing the yantra of the ruling planet in the troubled direction is traditionally held to settle negative energy.
- **Colours:** using the colours of the favourable planets in the matching directions is held to strengthen their positive energy.
- **Plants:** placing particular plants in the troubled directions is held to create a more positive atmosphere.

## Compatibility and Relationships

The tradition also uses Astro-Vastu to look at relationships between family members.

- **Marital compatibility:** by studying the birth charts of husband and wife, a practitioner may suggest changes to the master bedroom for a more harmonious relationship.
- **Parent and child:** by studying the charts of parents and children, a practitioner may suggest changes to the children's room for better understanding between them.

## Career and Professional Life

Astro-Vastu is also applied to work and career.

- **Office Vastu:** by studying the business owner's birth chart, a practitioner may suggest changes to the office to support the growth of the business.
- **Career guidance:** by studying a person's birth chart, a practitioner may point to the direction considered most favourable for that person to work in.

## Health Considerations

Vastu tradition links the home environment with a sense of well-being. It is not a substitute for medical care. For any health problem, consult a qualified doctor.

- **Health and Vastu:** a practitioner reading a birth chart may point out defects in the home that the tradition regards as a source of discomfort.
- **Improving matters:** correcting those defects and using other Astro-Vastu remedies is regarded in the tradition as supportive of well-being.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between astrology and Vastu Shastra?

Astrology studies the influence of celestial bodies on our lives, while Vastu Shastra is the science of architecture and spatial arrangement. Astrology gives a map of a life, and Vastu Shastra gives tools to shape a living space that is in tune with cosmic energies.

### How can Astro-Vastu help me?

By tradition, Astro-Vastu gives personalised Vastu suggestions based on your birth chart, which is believed to make it easier to face challenges, work towards goals and live a more fulfilling life.

### Do I need major structural changes to follow Astro-Vastu?

Not necessarily. In many cases simple and inexpensive measures such as colours, plants and yantras are considered enough to correct a Vastu defect.

### Which planet is linked with which direction?

The Sun with the east, the Moon with the north-west, Mars with the south, Mercury with the north, Jupiter with the north-east, Venus with the south-east and Saturn with the west.

## Conclusion

Astrology and Vastu Shastra are held to be deeply connected. Taking the principles of both together offers a path to a life of harmony, prosperity and well-being, according to tradition. Astro-Vastu gives a holistic and personal approach to making a living space that is not only beautiful but also uplifting in energy and spirit.
