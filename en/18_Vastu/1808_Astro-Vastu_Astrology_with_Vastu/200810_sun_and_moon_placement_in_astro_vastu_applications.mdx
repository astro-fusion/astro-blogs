# Sun and Moon in Astro-Vastu: East and North-West

## Introduction to the Sun and Moon in Astro-Vastu

In Vedic astrology the Sun and the Moon are the two most important celestial bodies. They are known as the luminaries and stand for the basic dualities of life: masculine and feminine, light and dark, consciousness and emotion. In Astro-Vastu, the tradition gives weight to the placement of the Sun and Moon in a birth chart and to how a home is aligned with their energies.

## The Sun (Surya) in Vastu

- **Direction:** east
- **Represents:** masculine energy, consciousness, vitality and leadership.
- **Vastu significance:** the east is the direction of the rising Sun. It is regarded as the source of life-giving energy (prana) and as the most auspicious direction in Vastu.
- **Astro-Vastu applications:**
    - **East-facing entrance:** an east-facing entrance is considered very auspicious because it lets morning sunlight into the home, bringing with it positive energy.
    - **Strengthening the Sun:** if the Sun is weak in the birth chart, the tradition suggests enhancing the east of the home. Keep this area open, clean and well lit, and place a copper Sun ornament on the east wall.

## The Moon (Chandra) in Vastu

- **Direction:** north-west
- **Represents:** feminine energy, emotions, intuition and relationships.
- **Vastu significance:** the north-west is the direction of the Moon. It is associated with the element of air and with movement and change.
- **Astro-Vastu applications:**
    - **North-west placements:** the north-west is considered a suitable location for a guest room, a garage or a finished goods store, because the energy of this direction is unstable and not suited to long-term settlement.
    - **Strengthening the Moon:** if the Moon is weak in the birth chart, the tradition suggests enhancing the north-west of the home. Place a water fountain or a picture of a full moon in this direction and use calming colours such as white and light blue.

## Balancing the Masculine and Feminine Energies

A harmonious home, in this tradition, is one where the masculine and feminine energies are in balance. In Astro-Vastu this is done by balancing the energies of the Sun and the Moon.

- **East (Sun):** the east should be open and light so that the masculine energy of the Sun can flow freely into the home.
- **North-west (Moon):** the north-west should be used for activities in tune with the feminine energy of the Moon, such as relaxation, creativity and socialising.

By balancing these two energies, the tradition aims at a home that is a place of peace and harmony.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which direction is linked with the Sun?

The east, the direction of the rising Sun, regarded as the most auspicious direction in Vastu.

### Which direction is linked with the Moon?

The north-west, which is associated with the element of air and with movement and change.

### Why is an east-facing entrance considered auspicious?

Because it lets morning sunlight into the home, bringing with it positive energy.

### What rooms suit the north-west?

A guest room, a garage or a finished goods store, because the energy of the north-west is unstable and not suited to long-term settlement.

### How is the east kept in good order?

Keep it open, clean and well lit, so that the Sun's energy can flow into the home. A copper Sun ornament on the east wall is a traditional suggestion.

## Conclusion

The Sun and the Moon are the two luminaries of Vedic astrology. By aligning a home with their directions, the east and the north-west, Astro-Vastu aims at a home that reflects the cosmos and is balanced between masculine and feminine energies.
