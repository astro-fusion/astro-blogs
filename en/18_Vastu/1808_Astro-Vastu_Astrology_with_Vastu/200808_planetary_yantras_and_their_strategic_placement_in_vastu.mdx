# Planetary Yantras and Where to Place Them in Vastu

## Introduction to Planetary Yantras in Vastu

In Astro-Vastu, planetary yantras are traditional aids for harmonising the energy of a living space. The tradition places the yantra of a planet in the direction linked with that planet, in the belief that this appeases the planet and supports its positive side. This guide describes the nine planetary yantras and where the tradition places them.

## What Are Yantras?

Yantras are sacred geometric diagrams used to represent the energies of deities and planets. They are made of specific shapes such as triangles, circles and squares, arranged in a precise and meaningful way. The tradition holds that a yantra that is properly energised and worshipped acts like an antenna, drawing in positive energies and keeping out negative ones.

## The Nine Planetary Yantras

- **Sun (Surya) Yantra:** for health, vitality and success.
- **Moon (Chandra) Yantra:** for mental peace, emotional steadiness and relationships.
- **Mars (Mangal) Yantra:** for courage, energy and ambition.
- **Mercury (Budha) Yantra:** for intelligence, communication and business.
- **Jupiter (Guru) Yantra:** for wisdom, knowledge and spirituality.
- **Venus (Shukra) Yantra:** for love, beauty and creativity.
- **Saturn (Shani) Yantra:** for discipline, responsibility and justice.
- **Rahu Yantra:** for worldly success, recognition and the fulfilment of desires.
- **Ketu Yantra:** for spirituality, intuition and liberation.

## Strategic Placement of Yantras

Traditions vary on the directions of Rahu and Ketu. The common scheme is as follows.

- **Sun (Surya) Yantra:** east
- **Moon (Chandra) Yantra:** north-west
- **Mars (Mangal) Yantra:** south
- **Mercury (Budha) Yantra:** north
- **Jupiter (Guru) Yantra:** north-east
- **Venus (Shukra) Yantra:** south-east
- **Saturn (Shani) Yantra:** west
- **Rahu Yantra:** south-west
- **Ketu Yantra:** centre (Brahmasthan)

## Activation and Worship of Yantras

In the tradition, a yantra is activated by a simple puja. The usual steps are as follows.

1. **Cleansing the yantra:** wash it with milk, water and sandalwood paste.
2. **Placing the yantra:** place it on a clean cloth in its direction.
3. **Offering flowers and incense:** offer fresh flowers and light an incense stick in front of it.
4. **Chanting the mantra:** chant the mantra of the planet 108 times.
5. **Praying:** offer your prayers to the planet and ask for its blessings.

The tradition adds that the yantra should be worshipped regularly to keep its energy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a yantra?

A sacred geometric diagram, made of shapes such as triangles, circles and squares, used to represent the energies of deities and planets.

### Where is the Saturn yantra placed?

In the west.

### Where are the Rahu and Ketu yantras placed?

The Rahu yantra in the south-west and the Ketu yantra in the centre, the Brahmasthan, following the common scheme.

### How is a yantra activated?

By a simple puja: cleansing it with milk, water and sandalwood paste, placing it on a clean cloth in its direction, offering flowers and incense, chanting the planet's mantra 108 times and praying.

### Does a yantra need regular worship?

Yes, according to the tradition it is worshipped regularly to maintain its energy.

## Conclusion

Planetary yantras are a simple and long-standing traditional practice in Astro-Vastu. They are used with faith and devotion, as customs rather than assured outcomes, to balance the planetary influences in a home.
