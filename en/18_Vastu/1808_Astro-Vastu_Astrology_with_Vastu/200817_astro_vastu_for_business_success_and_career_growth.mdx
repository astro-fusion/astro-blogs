# Astro-Vastu for Business and Career: A Traditional View

## Introduction to Astro-Vastu for Business

In the competitive world of business, every support counts. The tradition of Astro-Vastu holds that a commercial space can be arranged in line with the energies of the planets, so that the environment helps the work along. This is a traditional belief. It is not a promise of any result.

## Aligning Business Activities with Planetary Energies

In the tradition, an Astro-Vastu practitioner reads the birth chart of the business owner to identify which planets are favourable for that person. The business activities are then arranged in line with those favourable planets.

### Example

If Jupiter is a favourable planet for a business owner, the owner can keep the north-east of the office, which is the direction of Jupiter, strong and well ordered. The tradition holds that this creates an atmosphere of wisdom, knowledge and opportunity.

## Industry-Specific Planetary Rulers

The tradition assigns each industry a ruling planet. By knowing the ruler of an industry, the commercial space can be given considered Vastu adjustments.

- **Education**: Jupiter
- **Finance**: Mercury
- **Real estate**: Mars
- **Information technology**: Rahu
- **Manufacturing**: Saturn

## Directional Emphases for Professional Growth

The tradition regards each direction as suitable for particular departments of a workplace.

- **North**: The direction of wealth and career. Considered suitable for the accounts department and the marketing team.
- **East**: The direction of new beginnings and growth. Considered suitable for the sales team and the research and development department.
- **South**: The direction of fame and recognition. Considered suitable for the CEO's office and the boardroom.
- **West**: The direction of gains and profits. Considered suitable for the production department and the warehouse.

## Conclusion

The tradition of Astro-Vastu offers a way of arranging a workplace according to the owner's birth chart, the ruling planet of the industry and the directions. It can be taken as one traditional point of view. The progress of a business still depends in the end on planning and hard work.
