# Planet Gemstones in Astro-Vastu: Traditional Associations

## Introduction to Gemstones and Crystals in Astro-Vastu

In the tradition of Astro-Vastu, gemstones and crystals are not seen only as objects of adornment. They are said to be linked with the energy of living spaces. This article only describes those traditional associations.

It is not a recommendation to buy, wear or place any stone. Gemstones can be costly, no stone has been shown to correct a Vastu defect, and any decision about one should be made carefully and with the advice of a knowledgeable person.

## Gemstones and the Nine Planets

In Vedic astrology each of the nine planets is associated with a particular gemstone. The tradition links each stone with the Vastu direction of its planet, on the idea that the planet's energy is connected with that direction.

- **Ruby (Sun)**: East
- **Pearl (Moon)**: North-west
- **Red coral (Mars)**: South
- **Emerald (Mercury)**: North
- **Yellow sapphire (Jupiter)**: North-east
- **Diamond (Venus)**: South-east
- **Blue sapphire (Saturn)**: West
- **Hessonite (Rahu)**: South-west
- **Cat's eye (Ketu)**: Centre (Brahmasthan)

## Crystals in the Tradition

Crystals are regarded in the tradition as amplifiers of energy, linked with a particular Vastu zone.

- **Clear quartz**: Linked with amplifying the energy of any direction.
- **Rose quartz**: Linked with the south-west, which is regarded as the direction of relationships.
- **Amethyst**: Linked with a calm and spiritual atmosphere in the north-east, the direction of spirituality.
- **Citrine**: Linked with wealth and prosperity in the north, the direction of wealth.
- **Black tourmaline**: Linked with protection against negative energy and with Vastu defects.

## Placement and Activation in the Tradition

According to the tradition, a gemstone or crystal is first cleansed and charged with the mantra of its planet, and then placed in its direction on a clean cloth. The tradition also speaks of cleansing and charging the stone again from time to time. This is a description of tradition only.

## Conclusion

In the tradition, gemstones and crystals are linked with planets and directions in the way set out above. These are traditional beliefs and not proven remedies. The tidiness of a home, a sound layout and open air and light matter regardless of any stone.
