# Zodiac Signs and Their Vastu Zones

## Introduction to Zodiac Signs and Vastu Zones

In Astro-Vastu, one tradition holds that each of the twelve zodiac signs has a natural affinity with a particular Vastu zone. The idea is that a living space in tune with a person's zodiac energy supports a more balanced life. This guide lists that scheme sign by sign, with practical tips for each zone.

The list is one tradition's scheme. The zone given for a sign is not always the direction of its ruling planet, and other traditions map signs differently.

## Aries (Mesha)

- **Vastu zone:** east
- **Ruling planet:** Mars
- **Characteristics:** Energetic, ambitious and courageous.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the east of the home to support your energy and your endeavours. Keep this area open and well lit. Use the colour red in moderation.

## Taurus (Vrishabha)

- **Vastu zone:** south-east
- **Ruling planet:** Venus
- **Characteristics:** Practical, reliable and artistic.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the south-east of the home in line with wealth, luxury and creativity. Keep this area clean and beautiful. Use elegant colours such as pink and white.

## Gemini (Mithuna)

- **Vastu zone:** north
- **Ruling planet:** Mercury
- **Characteristics:** Intelligent, communicative and versatile.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the north of the home to support communication skills, business and education. Keep this area clean and free of clutter. Use the colour green.

## Cancer (Karka)

- **Vastu zone:** north-west
- **Ruling planet:** Moon
- **Characteristics:** Emotional, nurturing and intuitive.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the north-west of the home to support emotional well-being and relationships. Keep this area clean and calming. Use the colour white.

## Leo (Simha)

- **Vastu zone:** east
- **Ruling planet:** Sun
- **Characteristics:** Confident, generous and charismatic.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the east of the home to support confidence and recognition. Keep this area open and well lit. Use the colour orange.

## Virgo (Kanya)

- **Vastu zone:** north
- **Ruling planet:** Mercury
- **Characteristics:** Analytical, practical and detail-oriented.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the north of the home to support analytical skills and your work. Keep this area organised and free of clutter. Use the colour green.

## Libra (Tula)

- **Vastu zone:** south-east
- **Ruling planet:** Venus
- **Characteristics:** Diplomatic, charming and artistic.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the south-east of the home to support harmony in relationships and creativity. Keep this area beautiful and elegant. Use the colour pink.

## Scorpio (Vrishchika)

- **Vastu zone:** south
- **Ruling planet:** Mars
- **Characteristics:** Passionate, determined and intuitive.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the south of the home to support energy and career. Keep this area powerful and dynamic. Use the colour red.

## Sagittarius (Dhanu)

- **Vastu zone:** north-east
- **Ruling planet:** Jupiter
- **Characteristics:** Optimistic, adventurous and philosophical.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the north-east of the home to support spiritual growth. Keep this area clean and sacred. Use the colour yellow.

## Capricorn (Makara)

- **Vastu zone:** west
- **Ruling planet:** Saturn
- **Characteristics:** Ambitious, disciplined and responsible.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the west of the home to support stability and career. Keep this area organised and free of clutter. Use earthy colours such as brown and grey.

## Aquarius (Kumbha)

- **Vastu zone:** west
- **Ruling planet:** Saturn
- **Characteristics:** Independent, humanitarian and innovative.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the west of the home to support innovation and social and humanitarian endeavours. Keep this area open and airy. Use the colour blue.

## Pisces (Meena)

- **Vastu zone:** north-east
- **Ruling planet:** Jupiter
- **Characteristics:** Compassionate, intuitive and artistic.
- **Vastu tips:** Enhance the north-east of the home to support spiritual growth and creativity. Keep this area clean and serene. Use the colour yellow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How are the zodiac signs linked with Vastu zones?

One tradition maps each of the twelve signs to a zone of the home. For example, Taurus and Libra are linked with the south-east, Sagittarius and Pisces with the north-east, and Capricorn and Aquarius with the west.

### Which signs are linked with the east?

Aries and Leo. The tips for both are to keep the east open and well lit.

### Which signs are linked with the north?

Gemini and Virgo. The tips are to keep the north clean, organised and free of clutter, and to use the colour green.

### Is this the only way to map signs to directions?

No. It is one tradition's scheme, and the zone for a sign does not always match the direction of its ruling planet.

### How should I use this guide?

As a starting point. Try the colours and tidiness tips for your zone and see what suits you.

## Conclusion

Aligning a living space with the zone linked to your sign is a way, in this tradition, of making a home reflect the person who lives in it. Use this guide as a starting point for exploring the connection between your sign and your Vastu zone.
