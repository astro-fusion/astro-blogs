# Rahu and Ketu in Vastu: The Shadow Planets

## Introduction to Rahu and Ketu in Vastu

In Vedic astrology Rahu and Ketu are not physical planets. They are mathematical points in the sky where the paths of the Sun and Moon intersect. They are known as the shadow planets or the lunar nodes, and astrology gives them a strong influence.

In Vastu Shastra, Rahu and Ketu are linked with particular directions. Traditions vary on this, and the account below follows the commonly cited scheme. The tradition holds that their energies are best kept in balance through the way those parts of a home are used.

## Rahu (The Dragon's Head)

- **Direction:** south-west
- **Represents:** ambition, desire, materialism and sudden events.
- **Tradition holds:** when Rahu is well placed in a horoscope and the south-west of the home is balanced, it is associated with worldly success, recognition and the fulfilment of desires. A defect in the south-west, or a difficult Rahu, is taken to bring unexpected setbacks.
- **Vastu challenges:** a toilet, kitchen or main entrance in the south-west is said to strengthen the difficult side of Rahu.

## Ketu (The Dragon's Tail)

- **Direction:** centre (Brahmasthan)
- **Represents:** spirituality, intuition, liberation and detachment.
- **Tradition holds:** when Ketu is well placed in a horoscope and the Brahmasthan is open and clean, it is associated with spiritual growth, intuition and a sense of detachment from worldly affairs. A cluttered or obstructed Brahmasthan, or a difficult Ketu, is taken to bring confusion and lack of clarity.
- **Vastu challenges:** heavy objects, construction or a toilet in the Brahmasthan are said to strengthen the difficult side of Ketu.

## Remedial Measures for Rahu and Ketu

These are traditional practices, not assured results.

### For Rahu (South-West)

- **Keep it heavy and closed:** the south-west should be the heaviest and most closed part of the house. Use heavy furniture and avoid large openings.
- **Place a Rahu yantra:** a Rahu yantra can be placed in the south-west, as the tradition suggests.
- **Use earthy colours:** brown, grey and beige suit the south-west.
- **Chant the Rahu mantra:** chanting it is traditionally held to appease Rahu.

### For Ketu (Centre)

- **Keep it open and clean:** the Brahmasthan should be open, clean and free of clutter. Avoid heavy objects or construction in the centre of the house.
- **Place a Ketu yantra:** a Ketu yantra can be placed in the Brahmasthan, as the tradition suggests.
- **Use light colours:** use light and soothing colours in the Brahmasthan.
- **Chant the Ketu mantra:** chanting it is traditionally held to support the spiritual and intuitive qualities of Ketu.
