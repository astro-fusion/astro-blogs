# Mars and Venus in Vastu: South and South-East

## Introduction to Mars and Venus in Vastu

In Vedic astrology, Mars (Mangal) and Venus (Shukra) are two powerful planets that stand for the dynamic and the harmonious sides of life. Mars is the planet of action, courage and energy. Venus is the planet of love, beauty and creativity.

The tradition that joins astrology with Vastu holds that the energies of these two planets should be kept in balance, so that a household has both drive and harmony. What follows is what that tradition holds. It is not a promise of any result.

## Mars (Mangal) in Vastu

- **Direction**: South
- **Represents**: Action, courage, ambition and determination.
- **Vastu significance**: The south is regarded as the direction of fame, recognition and career success. It is considered suitable for the bedroom of the breadwinner of the family.

Traditional suggestions linked to Mars are these.

- **South placement**: Keep the south of the home strong and lively. The tradition mentions warm colours such as red and orange in this direction.
- **Strengthening Mars**: If Mars is weak in a birth chart, the tradition suggests improving the south of the home. It is held that this supports a person in dealing with obstacles, working towards goals and building a career.

## Venus (Shukra) in Vastu

- **Direction**: South-east
- **Represents**: Love, beauty, creativity and luxury.
- **Vastu significance**: The south-east is regarded as the direction of wealth, luxury and creativity. It is associated with the element of fire and is considered ideal for a kitchen or a creative workspace.

Traditional suggestions linked to Venus are these.

- **South-east placement**: Keep the south-east of the home beautiful and elegant. The tradition mentions colours such as pink and white in this direction, and a beautiful piece of art.
- **Strengthening Venus**: If Venus is weak in a birth chart, the tradition suggests improving the south-east of the home. It is held that this supports relationships and creativity.

## Balancing the Energies of Mars and Venus

Mars and Venus are described as complementary planets whose energies need to be balanced for a harmonious life.

- **Passion and harmony**: A balanced Mars and Venus are regarded as the basis of both passion and harmony in a relationship.
- **Action and creativity**: A balanced Mars and Venus are regarded as helpful in acting on creative ideas and carrying them through.
- **Vastu for balance**: To keep the two planets in balance, make sure that both the south and the south-east of the home follow Vastu principles. Avoid Vastu defects in these directions, such as a toilet or a septic tank.

## Conclusion

According to the tradition, a home in which Mars and Venus are in balance supports a blend of passion and harmony, action and creativity, and ambition and love. These are traditional beliefs, not proven outcomes.
