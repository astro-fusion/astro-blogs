# Vastu Dosha Nivaran with Astrological Remedies

## Introduction to Vastu Dosha Nivaran

Vastu Dosha Nivaran is the process of correcting Vastu defects (doshas) in a building to restore its balance. Vastu Shastra offers a variety of corrections for this purpose. A more holistic approach, in this tradition, combines them with astrological remedies, so that both the environmental and the astrological side of a concern are looked at together.

## Identifying Vastu Doshas Through Astrology

The tradition holds that a birth chart can give clues about the Vastu defects in a home. An experienced Astro-Vastu practitioner studies the chart to see which planets are weak or afflicted and how these challenges might show up as Vastu defects in the living space.

- **Example:** if Saturn is afflicted in the birth chart, the practitioner may look for a Vastu defect in the west of the home, the direction of Saturn.

## Astrological Remedies for Vastu Doshas

Once the Vastu doshas have been identified, the tradition uses astrological and Vastu corrections together. The practices below are described as traditional customs, not assured results.

### Mantras

- **Principle:** chanting the mantra of the planet that rules an afflicted direction is traditionally held to appease that planet.
- **Example:** for a Vastu defect in the south, the direction of Mars, the Mangal mantra is chanted to pacify Mars.

## Holistic Solutions for Vastu Doshas

Combining an astrological remedy with a Vastu correction is meant to address the root of a concern as the tradition sees it.

- **Example:** a person faces financial worries and the birth chart shows an afflicted Mercury. A practitioner may suggest chanting the Budha mantra as the astrological side. On the Vastu side, the practitioner may suggest enhancing the north of the home, the direction of Mercury, with a green plant, a picture of a lush green landscape and a Budha yantra. Financial worries also deserve practical attention, such as sound budgeting and, where needed, professional financial advice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Vastu Dosha Nivaran?

It is the process of correcting Vastu defects in a building to restore its balance.

### How can astrology help find a Vastu defect?

An Astro-Vastu practitioner studies the birth chart to see which planets are weak or afflicted. A weak Saturn, for example, points the practitioner towards the west of the home.

### What mantra is used for a defect in the south?

The Mangal mantra, to pacify Mars, the planet linked with the south.

### What is suggested for the north when Mercury is afflicted?

Enhance the north with a green plant, a picture of a lush green landscape and a Budha yantra, and chant the Budha mantra.

### Are these remedies assured to work?

No. They are traditional practices and should be treated as customs, not promised results.

## Conclusion

Vastu Dosha Nivaran with astrological remedies is a holistic approach in tradition. It looks at both the environmental and the astrological side of a concern, and offers practices meant to bring a home into better balance.
