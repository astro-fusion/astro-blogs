# Vastu Remedies for Difficult Planetary Positions

## Introduction to Vastu as a Remedial Measure

In the Vedic sciences, Vastu Shastra is seen as more than a way to make a living space beautiful and functional. Tradition also treats it as a means of responding to difficult planetary positions. By making specific changes to the home or workplace, the tradition holds that the effect of difficult planets, weak planets and demanding transits in a birth chart can be softened.

This approach is known as Astro-Vastu. The measures below are traditional practices, not assured results.

## Understanding Astrological Challenges

A birth chart is said to show a number of difficulties that may appear as concerns in different areas of life.

- **Malefic planets:** Saturn, Mars, Rahu and Ketu are considered natural malefics. When they are poorly placed in a birth chart, tradition links them with obstacles, delays and struggles.
- **Planetary debilitation:** when a planet is in its sign of debilitation, it is said to be weak and unable to give its positive results.
- **Difficult transits:** the transit of malefic planets over sensitive points in a birth chart is said to mark demanding periods.

## Vastu Remedies for Malefic Planets

### Saturn (Shani)

- **Themes:** delays and obstacles.
- **Traditional Vastu measures:** enhance the west of the home, the direction of Saturn. Keep this area clean and free of clutter. Use earthy colours such as brown and grey. Place a Shani yantra. Light a sesame oil lamp on Saturdays.

### Mars (Mangal)

- **Themes:** anger, aggression and conflicts.
- **Traditional Vastu measures:** enhance the south of the home, the direction of Mars. Use warm colours such as red and orange. Place a Mangal yantra. Grow a pomegranate tree in the garden.

### Rahu

- **Themes:** sudden problems, unexpected setbacks and confusion.
- **Traditional Vastu measures:** enhance the south-west of the home, the direction usually linked with Rahu, though traditions vary. Keep this area heavy and closed. Place a Rahu yantra. Avoid an underground water tank in this direction.

### Ketu

- **Themes:** detachment, isolation and spiritual confusion.
- **Traditional Vastu measures:** enhance the centre of the home, the Brahmasthan, usually linked with Ketu though traditions vary. Keep this area open, clean and free of clutter. Avoid heavy objects or construction in the centre. Place a Ketu yantra.

## Vastu Remedies for Planetary Debilitation

When a planet is debilitated in a birth chart, its natural significations are said to suffer. Vastu measures are used in the tradition to support the debilitated planet.

- **Example:** if Venus is debilitated in a birth chart, tradition links it with concerns in relationships and finances. A traditional measure is to enhance the south-east of the home, the direction of Venus, by placing a crystal and a beautiful piece of art there and using elegant colours such as pink and white.

## Vastu Remedies for Difficult Transits

Difficult transits, such as Sade Sati, the seven-and-a-half-year transit of Saturn, are regarded as demanding periods in a person's life. Vastu measures are used in the tradition to ease them.

- **Example:** during Sade Sati, one traditional measure is to enhance the west of the home, the direction of Saturn, to appease Saturn.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which planets are considered natural malefics?

Saturn, Mars, Rahu and Ketu.

### Which direction do I enhance for Saturn?

The west. Keep it clean and free of clutter, use earthy colours such as brown and grey, place a Shani yantra and light a sesame oil lamp on Saturdays.

### What is traditionally advised for Rahu?

Enhance the south-west, keep it heavy and closed, place a Rahu yantra and avoid an underground water tank in that direction.

### What is advised for a debilitated Venus?

Enhance the south-east by placing a crystal and a beautiful piece of art there and using colours such as pink and white.

### What is Sade Sati and what does Vastu say about it?

Sade Sati is the seven-and-a-half-year transit of Saturn. The tradition suggests enhancing the west of the home during it.

## Conclusion

Vastu Shastra offers, in tradition, a practical way to respond to difficult planetary positions. By changing a living space with care, a home owner follows a long-held practice meant to support well-being. These are customs and not assured outcomes.
