# Astro-Vastu Consultation: Steps and How They Fit Together

## Introduction to the Astro-Vastu Consultation Process

An Astro-Vastu consultation is a detailed process that draws on the traditions of Vedic astrology and Vastu Shastra to bring a living space into balance. It involves a detailed analysis of the client's birth chart and of their living space. Recommendations are then drawn up that take both the astrological and the architectural factors into account.

## Client Interview

The first step is a detailed interview with the client. Its purpose is to understand the client's problems, goals and aspirations. The practitioner asks questions about the client's health, finances, relationships and career.

## Birth Chart Analysis

The next step is to analyse the client's birth chart. The practitioner prepares a detailed horoscope and looks at the strengths and weaknesses of the planets, the current dasha period and the major events of the client's life.

## Property Evaluation

The third step is to evaluate the client's property. The practitioner visits the client's home or workplace and assesses how far it follows Vastu. The practitioner checks the orientation of the property, the placement of the rooms and the flow of energy.

## Integrated Recommendations

On the basis of the interview, the chart analysis and the property evaluation, the practitioner draws up a set of integrated recommendations. These combine astrological and Vastu measures.

- **Astrological measures**: The tradition mentions gemstones, mantra chanting and rituals. These are only a record of tradition. Measures that cost money or carry risk need careful thought and should not be taken on lightly.
- **Vastu measures**: The tradition mentions the use of colours, the rearrangement of furniture, and the placement of yantras and crystals.

## Conclusion

An Astro-Vastu consultation is a process that joins the traditions of astrology and Vastu in order to bring a living space into balance. A person facing a challenge can consult a knowledgeable practitioner to learn what the tradition suggests in their case. No practitioner can promise a result, and a health problem needs a doctor.

## FAQ

Four: the client interview, the birth chart analysis, the property evaluation and the integrated recommendations.

### What is asked in the client interview?

The practitioner tries to understand the client's problems, goals and aspirations, and asks about health, finances, relationships and career.

### What is looked at in the birth chart analysis?

The strengths and weaknesses of the planets, the current dasha period and the major events of the client's life.

### What is checked in the property evaluation?

The practitioner visits the home or workplace and checks the orientation of the property, the placement of the rooms and the flow of energy.

### What do the recommendations contain?

A combination of astrological and Vastu measures. On the Vastu side the tradition mentions colours, rearranging furniture, and yantras and crystals.
