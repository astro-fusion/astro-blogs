# Auspicious Timing for Construction in Vastu

## Introduction to Muhurta in Vastu

In the Vedic sciences, Muhurta is the practice of choosing an auspicious time to begin any important activity. In Vastu Shastra, tradition gives Muhurta an important place in a building project. The idea is that starting construction activities at an auspicious time puts the project in tune with favourable cosmic energies.

## The Importance of Auspicious Timing

The tradition compares this to a seed planted at the right time, which grows into a healthy and fruitful tree. In the same way, a building begun at an auspicious time is held to be strong and durable and to suit those who live in it. The timing of construction activities is believed to influence the energy of a building.

## Foundation Laying Ceremony (Bhoomi Pujan)

The foundation laying ceremony, also known as Bhoomi Pujan, is the first and most important step in the construction process. It is performed to seek the blessings of Mother Earth for the stability and longevity of the building.

- **Auspicious months:** Baisakh, Shravan, Margshirsh and Paush.
- **Auspicious nakshatras:** Rohini, Mrigashira, Chitra, Hasta, Jyeshtha and Anuradha.
- **Auspicious tithis:** Dwitiya, Tritiya, Panchami, Saptami, Dashami and Trayodashi.

## Roof Completion Ceremony

The roof completion ceremony marks the completion of the roof, a major milestone in construction. It is performed to seek the blessings of the deities for the protection and well-being of the occupants.

- **Auspicious months:** Kartik, Margshirsh and Paush.
- **Auspicious nakshatras:** Rohini, Mrigashira, Chitra, Hasta and Anuradha.
- **Auspicious tithis:** Dwitiya, Tritiya, Panchami, Saptami and Dashami.

## House Warming Ceremony (Griha Pravesh)

The house warming ceremony, also known as Griha Pravesh, is performed when moving into a new home. It is performed to purify the energy of the home and to invite the blessings of the deities.

- **Auspicious months:** Magha, Phalguna, Baisakh and Jyeshtha.
- **Auspicious nakshatras:** Rohini, Mrigashira, Chitra, Hasta and Anuradha.
- **Auspicious tithis:** Dwitiya, Tritiya, Panchami, Saptami, Dashami and Ekadashi.

## Avoiding Inauspicious Planetary Conditions

Tradition advises avoiding the start of construction activities under certain planetary conditions.

- **Retrograde planets:** some traditions avoid starting important work while a planet is retrograde.
- **Combust planets:** a planet that is combust, meaning too close to the Sun, is considered to lose its strength.
- **Eclipses:** eclipses are considered highly inauspicious, and important activities are avoided during an eclipse.

## Favourable Configurations

To make the most of an auspicious time, tradition looks for favourable planetary configurations.

- **Benefic planets in Kendras:** the presence of benefic planets such as Jupiter, Venus and Mercury in the Kendra (angular) houses is considered very auspicious.
- **A strong Lagna lord:** the Lagna lord should be strong and well placed in the chart for the Muhurta.
- **A favourable nakshatra:** the nakshatra of the day should suit the activity being performed.

The foundation laying ceremony (Bhoomi Pujan), the roof completion ceremony and the house warming ceremony (Griha Pravesh).

Baisakh, Shravan, Margshirsh and Paush.

### Which months are considered auspicious for Griha Pravesh?

Magha, Phalguna, Baisakh and Jyeshtha.
