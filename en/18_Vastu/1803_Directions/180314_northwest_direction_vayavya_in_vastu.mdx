# Northwest Direction in Vastu: Guest Rooms, Children and Air

## Introduction

The northwest direction, Vayavya Kon, holds an important place in Vastu Shastra. It lies between the north and west directions, and Vastu tradition links it with social connections, the flow of air and the balance of movement in a living space. It is associated with the Moon, which is held to be its ruling planet, and with Lord Vayu, the deity of wind and movement. Tradition holds that understanding this corner helps bring harmony and good relationships to a home or workplace.

## What Is the Northwest Direction (Vayavya)?

The northwest, known as Vayavya Kon or Vayu Kon, is the space between the north and west cardinal points. It is one of the eight main directions of Vastu, and each has its own character. The word Vayavya comes from Vayu, the Sanskrit name of the wind deity, which shows the strong link of this direction with the air element.

Tradition regards the northwest as the meeting point of hot and cold regions, which makes it a good entry point for air. The direction is ruled by the Moon (Chandra), which is said to rise in the northwest as the Sun sets in the west, marking the change from day to night. For this reason the northwest is seen as a point of transformation of energy in any structure.

Other directions give stability, but the northwest is marked by movement and change. Tradition holds that, depending on how the space is arranged and used, it can either bring abundance or create difficulties. That is why correct arrangement of the northwest is considered especially important.

## Key Aspects of the Northwest

### Element and Planetary Influence

The northwest is governed mainly by the air element (Vayu), which stands for movement, communication and the free flow of energy. This is why the northwest is considered the right place for ventilation and air circulation. The Moon adds emotional sensitivity, intuition and a calming quality. Together, the two are held to make a zone of lively yet soothing energy that touches the mental state of the family and its relations with outsiders.

### Social Connections and Support

One of the main aspects of the northwest is its link with social connections, networking and personal relationships. Vastu tradition holds that a well-balanced northwest opens the way to opportunities, helps communication and strengthens support systems. When it is arranged correctly, it is said to help a person build and keep useful relationships, both personal and professional. Defects in this corner are linked with social isolation, communication problems and a lack of support in time of need.

### Resources and Stability

Traditionally the northwest was used to store the grain and supplies that would sustain the household through the year. In modern terms this stands for financial resources and support systems such as banking relationships. Tradition holds that a balanced northwest keeps support and resources available when needed. The combination of open space and the air element in this direction is said to keep resources flowing, which also makes it a good place to store finished goods that are ready for sale or distribution.

## How to Find and Check the Northwest

To identify the northwest of a home or workspace, follow these steps.

1. **Find true north.** Use a reliable compass, and allow for the magnetic declination of your area.
2. **Map the floor plan.** Draw a simple sketch of the floor plan and divide it into 8 equal segments, representing the 8 directions, with the centre of the property as the reference point.
3. **Identify the northwest quadrant.** The northwest lies between north and west, at about 315 degrees on the compass.
4. **Measure the proportion.** Ideally the northwest sector should take up about one eighth of the total area of the property. Measure to see whether it is properly proportioned, extended or cut off.
5. **Check the flow of air.** The northwest should have enough ventilation without being too open or completely blocked.
6. **Look for obstructions.** Note any heavy furniture, built-in structures or other things that block the free flow of energy in this direction.

Tradition holds that the influence of the northwest is strongest when it keeps its proper proportion to the rest of the building and lets air and energy move freely.

## How to Assess the Northwest

**Balanced northwest.** A well-balanced northwest is linked with social harmony, strong support networks and financial stability, along with good relations with people outside the family and clarity of mind.

**Extended northwest.** When the sector is larger than the ideal proportion, tradition links it with too much movement and instability, such as frequent moves, difficulty in settling down or financial ups and downs.

**Cut or missing northwest.** A cut or missing northwest corner is considered a serious Vastu defect. It is linked with a lack of social connections, difficulty in keeping relationships, financial strain and a lack of support in hard times.

**Heavy northwest.** If the northwest carries heavy structures, tall buildings or too much weight, tradition holds that this suppresses the air element and is linked with mental confusion and difficulty in thinking clearly.

**Polluted northwest.** Toilets, garbage or other polluting things in the northwest are considered especially inauspicious. They are linked with disturbed social connections, legal problems and conflicts.

Tradition holds that imbalances in the northwest show first in the mental state of the family, particularly of women and children. When assessing the effects, pay attention to how these family members fare in social relationships and clarity of mind.

## Practical Applications

### Guest Rooms and Social Spaces

Vastu principles consider the northwest highly suitable for guest rooms. Because the direction is linked with the air element and the Moon, it is said to give a fresh and soothing atmosphere that suits visitors. Placing guest rooms here serves several purposes:

- Guests receive enough sunlight and fresh air, which adds to their comfort.
- The calming influence of the Moon makes a peaceful place to rest.
- It encourages friendly contact between hosts and guests.
- The natural instability of this direction means, as tradition has it, that guests do not become permanent residents.

Social spaces such as living rooms also suit the northwest, because the energy of the direction encourages communication, pleasant exchange and the building of relationships. These spaces should use light colours, especially soft pastels, blues and greens.

### Children's Areas and Creative Spaces

The northwest is considered a good place for children's bedrooms and play areas. The air element is linked with mental clarity and creativity, and the Moon with emotional balance and comfort. Tradition links rooms here with:

- imagination and creativity
- communication skills
- balanced emotional growth
- closer social bonds with peers

For creative professionals, a studio or workspace in the northwest is held to support innovation and artistic expression. The lively yet harmonious energy is said to suit the creative process and to encourage productive collaboration with others in the field.

## Common Misconceptions

**Misconception: The northwest is always unsuitable for toilets or bathrooms.**
**Reality:** Many Vastu practitioners advise against a toilet in the northwest, but context matters. A small powder room without heavy plumbing may be acceptable. A main bathroom with heavy water use should be avoided, because it conflicts with the air element.

**Misconception: The northwest matters only for social relationships.**
**Reality:** Social connections are a key aspect of this direction, but tradition also links the northwest with mental clarity, financial support and legal matters. Its influence is not limited to one area of life.

**Misconception: A cut northwest always brings bad results.**
**Reality:** Correcting the structure is ideal, but the tradition also describes remedies and alternatives that balance the energy despite architectural limits. The effect is said to depend on the size of the defect and the remedies applied.

**Misconception: The northwest is unsuitable for kitchens.**
**Reality:** The northwest lies diagonally opposite the southeast, which is the ideal place for a kitchen. A kitchen in the northwest can therefore be acceptable if it is well designed. The key is good ventilation and no heavy storage that suppresses the air element.

## Conclusion

The northwest (Vayavya Kon) is an important energy centre in Vastu Shastra, linked with social connections, mental clarity and support systems. Governed by the air element and the Moon, it calls for thoughtful handling. Tradition holds that a well-balanced northwest supports relationships and peace of mind.

Because tradition describes this direction as one that can bring abundance or difficulty, following Vastu principles in designing it matters, whether in a home or a workplace. Keeping the northwest clean, well ventilated and suitably used is the way tradition advises to work with its energy.

## FAQs

### Which rooms are considered suitable in the northwest?

Guest rooms, living rooms, children's bedrooms or play areas, and studios for creative work are considered suitable in the northwest. Storing finished goods that are ready for sale is also mentioned as good here.

### Why is the guest room placed in the northwest?

Tradition holds that the air element and the Moon give freshness and calm here, and guests get sunlight and air. It is also believed that the natural instability of this direction keeps guests from becoming permanent residents.

### Can a toilet or kitchen be built in the northwest?

Many practitioners advise against a toilet, and garbage or polluting things are also called inauspicious here. A small powder room without heavy plumbing may be acceptable. A kitchen can be acceptable with good ventilation and without heavy storage.

### What if the northwest corner of my house is cut?

A cut northwest is considered a serious Vastu defect, but the tradition says that remedies and alternatives can be used even then. For firm advice on your home, consult a qualified Vastu practitioner.

### How large should the northwest part be?

Ideally the northwest takes up about one eighth of the total area of the plot. A larger part is linked with instability, and a cut part is considered a serious defect.

### What can a beginner do about the northwest?

Identify the northwest part of the home or workplace and keep it clean. Give it good ventilation and do not load it with heavy weight. Light colours and small indoor plants are held to improve its energy. Before major structural changes, take advice from a qualified practitioner.
