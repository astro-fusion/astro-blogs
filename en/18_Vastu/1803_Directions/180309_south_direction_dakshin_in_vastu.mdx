# South Direction in Vastu: Yama, Stability and Security

## Introduction

The south has a special place in Vastu Shastra. The common belief is that the south is only a direction of unfavourable energy, but tradition links it with stability, protection and discipline. Its lord is held to be Yama, the god of death, and as a planet it is linked with Mars. Vastu tradition holds that the south plays a large part in deciding the overall energy, security and prosperity of a building. By understanding it properly, its supposed difficulties can be turned into strengths in homes and workplaces alike.

## What Is Vastu of the South?

Vastu of the south means the rules and principles of Vastu Shastra for the southern part of any building. Vastu Shastra is the ancient Indian building tradition. In tradition each direction is given its own energy and character.

The lord of the south is Yama. He is a symbol of the end of life and also of the importance of right conduct. As a planet it is linked with Mars, a symbol of energy and discipline. It is believed that when the qualities of this direction are respected through layout and use, the direction becomes a source of both protection and standing.

## Key Aspects

### Stability and a Strong Structure

- In Vastu the south is regarded as the direction that gives a building strength, both physical and energetic.
- To add to this stability, it is advised to make the south and west walls thicker and higher than the walls in other directions. It is believed that this holds back negativity and gives the building a base.
- The planet Mars is a symbol of courage and firmness, and by tradition it strengthens this sense of security and lasting power.

### Security Measures

- Yama, the guardian of the south, is a symbol of guarding boundaries and of discipline.
- Security measures such as secure fencing, strong doors and clear boundaries are regarded as working best when stressed along the southern boundary.
- A main entrance in the south should be placed in the auspicious padas (segments) of the Vastu Purusha Mandala. By tradition this brings in positive energy and adds to safety.

### Discipline and Suitable Activities

- The southern part of a home or building is regarded as suitable for activities of rest, discipline or serious decision-making.
- A master bedroom in the south is taken to give deep and restful sleep, and under the influence of Yama it carries the idea of closing and renewal.
- Placing heavy and important things such as a safe or professional tools in the south or southwest (Nairitya) is in line with tradition and is regarded as giving a solid base to family and personal life.

## How the Padas of the South Are Fixed

To fix the proper place for rooms, doors and other parts, Vastu divides the plot into a grid called the Vastu Purusha Mandala, which has 32 padas (segments) on the outer edge. Not all the padas on the southern side are regarded as auspicious for the main entrance or for major construction. Only some are. For example:

- Divide the south wall into eight equal parts. The third and fourth segments (Vitatha and Gruhakshata) are described as favourable for an entrance.
- Check the thickness of the walls, the position of windows and the position of heavy goods to judge the balance and strength.
- Avoid water features or open space in the south, because they can interfere with the protective nature of this direction.

## How to Assess the South

- By tradition a balanced south part is linked with stability, good health and material success.
- Neglect or weakness of the south is said to bring a risk of insecurity, financial instability or health trouble.
- Too much openness in the south, thin walls or an entrance in the wrong place can upset the energy. They should be corrected according to Vastu principles.
- These points should be seen together with an analysis of the whole plot and with your own life goals.

## Practical Applications

### Structure and Security

- Keep the southern and western walls thicker and higher than the other walls, to add to security and to guard the home from outside negativity.
- Place the main door in the auspicious segments of the south. By tradition it should be the largest door of the building, opening inward and with a clear threshold.
- Put secure fencing, CCTV or protective planting along the southern boundary.

### Placing Rooms and Activities

- Place the master bedroom in the south or southwest for the most rest and stability.
- Keep storage, a safe or heavy goods in the south. This is regarded as a symbol of a firm foundation.
- Do not place the kitchen or water tank in the south. The kitchen is regarded as proper in the southeast (Agneya) and the water tank in the northeast (Ishana).

## Common Misconceptions

- Many people believe the south brings misfortune by itself because of its link with Yama. By tradition, when properly balanced, this direction supports prosperity and moral strength.
- A second misunderstanding is that every south-facing entrance is bad. Tradition asks only that some padas be avoided, while other padas are regarded as very auspicious.
- Some people think the south is not suitable for any important activity. In fact it is regarded as suitable for places of rest, security and decision-making.

## Examples

- **A sample home layout.** In a house where the master bedroom is in the southwest, the walls on the southern boundary are thick and the main entrance is in the Gruhakshata pada, Vastu experts say that family harmony and financial stability are better. This is a traditional belief.
- **An office.** An office with its records room or safe in the south is regarded as good for security and smooth working. A southern boundary that is open or lightly built is believed to let energy leak, and so this arrangement is avoided.

## Conclusion

The south is not an inauspicious direction. By tradition, when its rules are properly followed, it becomes the direction of stability, security and disciplined progress. By understanding its link with Yama and Mars, and by making careful decisions about the strength of the structure, security measures and the placing of the main rooms, the supposed difficulties of a home or workplace can be turned into long-term benefit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the south direction inauspicious?

Vastu tradition does not regard it so. Yama, the lord of the south, is called a symbol of discipline and right conduct, and the direction is linked with stability and security. When properly balanced it is regarded as a support for prosperity.

### Is every door in a south-facing house bad?

No. By tradition only some padas should be avoided, while others are very auspicious. The third and fourth segments of the south wall (Vitatha and Gruhakshata) are described as favourable for a door.

### Which rooms are regarded as proper in the south?

A master bedroom in the south or southwest is regarded as proper. Storage, a safe and heavy goods can also be kept here. Do not place the kitchen or water tank in the south.

### What does Vastu say about the walls of the south?

It is advised to make the south and west walls thicker and higher than the walls in other directions. It is believed that this gives the building strength and security.

### Why are water features or open space not placed in the south?

By tradition, water features or open space in the south interfere with the protective quality of the direction. For this reason fencing and solid construction are stressed on the southern boundary.

### What should a beginner look at in the south?

First look at the existing structure, such as the thickness of the walls, the place of the door and the use of the southern part. Then make small changes, such as strengthening the boundary or moving the bedroom according to Vastu. For firm advice on your own house, consult a knowledgeable Vastu expert.
