# North Direction in Vastu: Kubera, Water and Room Placement

## Introduction

In Vastu Shastra the north (Uttar disha) is counted among the most respected directions of the Indian Vastu tradition. Vastu tradition holds that it is the direction of Kubera, the god of wealth, and it is linked with prosperity, new opportunities and financial progress. This article explains in plain language the principles of the north, its use in homes and businesses, and some common misunderstandings.

Each direction in Vastu is given its own energy, which is said to be linked with different sides of life. The north is linked with wealth, prosperity and opportunity. Kubera is called the treasurer of the gods, and so the northern part of any building is regarded as having special importance for financial progress and livelihood. Tradition holds that keeping this direction arranged according to Vastu rules keeps the flow of energy in the home balanced.

## What Is the North Direction?

The north is one of the eight main directions of Vastu. By Vastu tradition it is linked with the energy of wealth, prosperity and the fulfilment of wishes. Its lord is Kubera, and as a planet it is linked with Mercury (Budha).

The element of the north is water. Water stands for flow, movement and abundance, and so the direction too is linked with flow and expansion. Tradition holds that energy coming from the direction of the Earth's north pole enters a building from this direction. For this reason the north is taken as the direction of beginnings, from which the process of any undertaking taking shape starts. When the direction is open, clean and balanced, it is regarded as the path by which wealth and opportunity come into the home.

## Key Aspects

### Link with Wealth and Prosperity

The north is mainly linked with wealth, fortune and material comfort. It is believed that a house with a balanced north keeps the favour of Kubera, and that effort bears fruit as financial stability. The direction is regarded as controlling the flow of money coming and going.

When the north is open and has no obstruction, tradition holds that the chances of earning and saving increase. In contrast, a Vastu defect in the north is said to bring a risk of financial difficulty or loss.

### Career and New Opportunities

Besides wealth, the north is also linked with jobs, promotion, the growth of a business and new markets. For people who want to move ahead in their career, Vastu tradition regards following the rules of the north as useful. Many Vastu experts advise those who want progress in their career to make the bedroom in the north or to take a north-facing house.

### Flow of Energy and Fulfilment of Wishes

In Vastu the north is regarded as the starting point of anything taking shape. For people who work with a clear goal in mind, this direction is held to be special. Tradition says that when the energy here flows without obstruction, thoughts begin to turn into concrete results. For this reason it is said to be necessary to keep the north clean and free of unneeded things.

## How to Find and Check the North

The rules of Vastu fit only when the north has been identified correctly. Even a small deviation can change the assessment.

**Step 1: Find true north.** Stand outside the main door with the house behind you. Hold a reliable compass level at chest height and wait until the needle settles. When the zero-degree mark and the north end of the needle line up, that is magnetic north.

**Step 2: Divide the north wall into nine padas.** Once the north wall is identified, divide it into nine equal parts. These are called padas. Tradition gives each pada its own energy and its own deity.

**Step 3: Identify the auspicious padas.** According to Vastu, the fifth pada of the north is regarded as the most auspicious, because it is linked directly with Kubera and the drawing of wealth. The third (Mukhya), fourth (Bhallat) and eighth (Diti) padas are also described as favourable for a door, a window or activities linked with wealth.

**Step 4: Check the present condition.** See whether there is a toilet, heavy goods, clutter or any obstruction in the north. Write down what needs correction.

**Step 5: Apply remedies.** After the check, removing obstructions is the simplest step. Tradition also mentions placing a water symbol or fixing a Vastu yantra, but these should be understood only as traditional practices and not as necessary remedies.

## How to Assess the North

When assessing the north of your home, pay attention to these points.

**Balance of elements.** The element of the north is water. Water-related symbols or facilities can be placed in a suitable spot in this direction. Excess of water should be avoided, because it can upset the balance.

**How the energy feels.** A balanced north feels open, light and lively. If heaviness, stagnation or unease is felt there, tradition regards it as a sign of held-up energy that needs attention.

**Value of the property.** It is said that a property with a correct north by Vastu keeps its value better. When looking at a property, the condition of the north can be considered as one point.

**Condition of the occupants.** By tradition, if the north is in order, the occupants' financial path is smooth and more opportunities come. This should be seen only as a belief and not as a guarantee.

**Changes with the season.** It is said that the energy of the north changes a little with the seasons. In winter, extra light or warmth in the north keeps the balance, while in summer adding a water symbol is regarded as proper.

## Practical Applications

### Home Layout and Arrangement

**Position of the main entrance.** If possible, place the main entrance in the third, fourth, fifth or eighth pada of the north wall. The fifth pada is regarded as especially auspicious, because it is linked directly with the energy of Kubera.

**Bedroom.** For people who want growth in their career and new opportunities, a bedroom in the north is regarded as beneficial.

**Study room and office.** The north is described as good for a study room or home office, because it is linked with creativity, expression and communication.

**No kitchen.** By the rule of Vastu, a kitchen should not be built in the north or the northeast (Ishana). This place is regarded as inauspicious, and it is said that it blocks the flow of wealth and opportunities.

**Water source.** A small fountain or aquarium in the north is described in tradition as auspicious. The flow of water is a symbol of the flow of wealth.

### Business and Workplace

**Direction of the office.** Business people should face north while working, according to traditional advice. It is believed that this brings clarity in taking financial decisions.

**Safe and cash counter.** In a shop or office, it is described as proper to keep the cash counter, safe and items for keeping money in the north. It is believed that this keeps the flow of money continuous.

**Growth of the business.** Companies that want to expand should keep their north in order by Vastu. Removing obstructions and defects in this direction is regarded as opening the way to new markets.

**Reception.** Placing the reception or the customers' seating area in the north is said to make a good first impression and to help good business relationships form.

**Staff seating.** Arrange the desks of staff in finance or revenue so that they face north. By tradition this adds to efficiency.

## Common Misconceptions

**Misconception 1: Every north-facing property is equally auspicious.** A north-facing property is generally regarded as favourable, but its auspiciousness depends on other things too. In which pada the main door lies, what the whole layout of the house is like and how the other Vastu elements stand all decide the matter together. Even a north-facing property with major Vastu defects can have an imbalance.

**Misconception 2: Water can be placed anywhere in the north.** The element of the north is water, but even so a water source should be placed with thought. Ideally the place for water is the northeast part of the north direction. It is said that placing water in the wrong spot, even in the north, can have the opposite effect.

**Misconception 3: Vastu of the north concerns only money.** The chief link of the north is with wealth, but in tradition its influence extends to career, opportunity, creativity and the ability to communicate. Looking at it only from the financial side makes its scope too small.

**Misconception 4: Vastu defects in the north cannot be corrected.** Some people believe that structural defects in the north are permanent. By tradition this is not so. Balance can be brought by simple steps such as removing obstructions, cleaning, light and a water symbol. Aids such as yantras and mirrors are also described in tradition, but treat them only as traditional practices.

## Examples

These examples are only for illustration.

**Example 1: Setting right a north-facing home.** A family lived in a north-facing house. The members worked hard, yet a financial standstill was felt. On examination, a heavy storage cabinet was found in the north, and the main door was in an inauspicious pada of the north wall.

The following steps were suggested for correction:

1. Remove the heavy cabinet from the north.
2. Place a small fountain in the northeast corner of the north.
3. Place a Kubera Yantra on the north wall.
4. Fix a mirror on the north wall to enlarge the space symbolically.

Of these, the most practical step is removing the heavy goods. Measures such as the yantra are traditional practices and should not be treated as necessary.

**Example 2: Layout of a shop.** The sales of a shop were falling. When a Vastu expert looked at the layout, the cash counter was found in the south and the north was full of heavy stock and unused equipment.

The following steps were suggested for correction:

1. Move the cash counter to the north.
2. Clear the clutter and heavy goods from the north area.
3. Put proper lighting in the north.
4. Place a money plant in the north corner.
5. Place a small water source in the northeast part of the shop.

This is an example only, and no result is promised.

## Conclusion

In Vastu Shastra the north is regarded as the zone of energy linked with wealth, prosperity and new opportunities. Kubera is its lord, and when the direction is properly arranged and kept clean, it is regarded as the way to financial progress and professional advancement.

The basic principles of the north are that it is linked with wealth, progress in career and the taking shape of wishes. By fixing the right place for the entrance, the water source and activities linked with money, these principles can be applied in today's homes too.

Whether you are building a new house, rearranging your present home or putting your business in order, the rules of the north can be kept in mind. Remember that Vastu is a holistic discipline. The north is regarded as important for wealth, but it creates a balanced atmosphere only together with the other directions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is the lord of the north and what is its element?

By Vastu tradition the lord of the north is Kubera, the god of wealth, and the direction is linked with the planet Mercury. Its element is water, which is regarded as a symbol of flow and abundance.

### Where in the north is the main door regarded as proper?

The north wall is divided into nine equal padas. The third, fourth, fifth and eighth padas are described as favourable for a door. The fifth pada is regarded as the most auspicious, because it is linked with the energy of Kubera.

### Is it proper to build a kitchen or toilet in the north?

No. By the rule of Vastu, the kitchen should not be in the north or the northeast. It is also advised to avoid a toilet and heavy storage in this direction, because they are believed to block the flow of wealth and opportunities.

### Where should a water source be placed in the north?

A small fountain or aquarium can be placed in the north. Water in the northeast part of the north is regarded as most proper. Excess of water should be avoided.

### What can a beginner do about the north?

First find true north with a compass. Then clear clutter and heavy goods from the north area and see that natural light and air reach it. By tradition, a small water source can be placed there, and keeping communication devices such as a computer or phone in the north is regarded as good for career. Do not keep a toilet, kitchen or heavy storage in the north.

### Is the influence of the north limited to money?

No. In tradition it is linked with career, opportunity, creativity and communication as well as wealth. For this reason it should not be seen only from the financial side.
