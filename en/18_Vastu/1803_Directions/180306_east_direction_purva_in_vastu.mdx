# East Direction in Vastu: Rules for the Rising Sun

## Introduction

In Vastu Shastra the east is counted among the most auspicious directions. It has deep importance in the ancient Vedic building tradition. It is regarded as the direction of new beginnings, vitality and prosperity. Each day it welcomes the rising sun, and so it is taken as a symbol of fresh opportunity and the rise of positive energy. For home builders, architects and anyone interested in Vastu, it is worth understanding the principles of the east. Vastu tradition holds that they help make a living space harmonious. This article looks at the different sides of the east direction and how they are used in present-day life.

## What Is the East Direction in Vastu?

The east (Purva disha) is one of the main directions of Vastu Shastra, and it is the place where the sun rises. In Vastu philosophy the east is regarded as a centre of positive energy and cosmic forces. Two chief deities of the Vedic texts are its lords: Indra, the king of the gods, and Surya, the sun god. By tradition this double rulership makes the direction especially strong for the fulfilment of wishes, for health and for spiritual growth.

The east is the direction of the rising sun, and it stands for growth, creativity and movement. It is the main way in which solar energy enters any home. For this reason it carries great weight in planning a building by Vastu. The east is not merely a geographical direction. It is regarded as a cosmic gateway through which life-giving energies enter and affect those who live in the place.

## Key Aspects

### Divine Rulership and Symbolic Meaning

The lords of the east are Indra, the king of the gods, and Surya, the sun god. Indra is known for his skill in forming relationships. He is believed to give this direction the power to link a person with supportive people who help fulfil the purposes of life. Surya is the source of universal energy and gives the direction vitality and strength. For this reason the east is regarded as important for attracting good relationships, for finding a suitable life partner and for making useful social contacts.

As a symbol, the east is the direction of new beginnings, the light of knowledge and spiritual awakening. It is linked with intellectual growth, creative work and clarity of mind. The sun rising in the east signals the end of darkness and ignorance. For this reason the direction is thought especially useful for work that needs concentration and mental sharpness.

### Health and Well-Being

In tradition the east is tied to physical and mental health through sunlight. It is a traditional belief that the morning sun coming from the east gives the body energy and eases stress. Of homes that make good use of the east, Vastu tradition holds that these things are kept up:

- Physical energy and stamina
- Clarity of mind and concentration
- A better sleep routine
- Less low mood
- General support for health

The link of this direction with health is held so strongly that Vastu experts often advise placing a healing room, meditation room or wellness area in the east. This is a traditional belief and not a substitute for medical care.

### Prosperity and Career Progress

The east is closely tied to professional progress, prosperity and leadership. By tradition its energies are considered helpful for these matters:

- Moving ahead in a career and gaining professional recognition
- Qualities of leadership
- Opportunities for progress
- Success in study and in intellectual work
- Financial stability and prosperity

The east is regarded as especially suitable for people in intellectual, creative or leadership roles, because it strengthens the qualities that bring success in these fields. Many Vastu experts advise placing the study area, home office or work desk so that it faces east.

## How to Find the East Direction

To find the correct east for Vastu, careful measurement and correct setting of direction are needed. The standard method is this:

1. **Use a reliable compass:** Use a good-quality compass to find the true directions. The digital compass of a smartphone can serve, but it is not as accurate as a professional instrument.
2. **Allow for magnetic declination:** Magnetic north and true north differ according to where you are. Correct your reading for the magnetic declination of your area.
3. **Divide the plot into nine equal parts:** Following the principle of the Vastu Purusha Mandala, divide the property into a 9x9 grid (81 equal squares).
4. **Identify exact east:** East falls at exactly 90 degrees on a compass. Pure east is taken to span 75 to 105 degrees on the compass.
5. **Mark the east zone of the property:** Once exact east is found, mark the eastern part of the property according to the Vastu grid.

For plots of irregular shape, consult a Vastu expert who can use special methods to fix the correct direction and the east zone.

## How to Assess the East Zone

**Best use:** The east should ideally hold the spaces that benefit from morning sun and positive energy, such as the prayer room, meditation space or study room.

**Checking the energy:** See whether the eastern part of the property is balanced, improved or blocked. Vastu tradition holds that a balanced east zone goes with good health, friendly relationships and regular chances of progress.

**Common Vastu defects in the east:**

- Heavy structures or load-bearing walls
- Water leakage or dampness
- Dark, crowded or closed-in spaces
- Toilets or a sewage system
- Heavy metal objects or machinery

**Need for correction:** After the defects are identified, decide on suitable corrections. These may be changes in the structure, a change in the position of things, or elements that balance the energy.

**Planning ahead:** When building new, plan the east zone correctly from the design stage.

By tradition, a properly balanced east is regarded as good for the health, social relationships, career progress and general well-being of the occupants.

## Practical Applications

### Home Design and Layout

The east plays a very important part in the layout of a home according to the principles of Vastu. Some specific uses are these:

**Main entrance:** An east-facing main entrance is considered highly auspicious and is often called the "victory door". It is believed to give the occupants name, fame, success and social connection. The door should open through 90 degrees without any obstruction.

**Windows and air:** Large windows or ventilated openings in the east let in more morning sun and fresh air, which keeps the whole house fresh.

**Placing rooms:** The east is regarded as ideal for these uses:

- Prayer or meditation room
- Study space for children
- Living room, where the family gathers
- Bedrooms for elderly members, who benefit from the morning sun

**The kitchen:** The kitchen ideally lies in the southeast (Agneya). Even so, a window or air opening in the east wall of the kitchen is considered good for natural light and freshness.

**Do not place in the east:** Do not put a toilet, a store of heavy goods, a staircase or clutter in the east, because these are believed to block the flow of auspicious energy.

### Workplace and Office Layout

By tradition the east affects productivity and success in professional places:

**Office entrance:** An east-facing entrance to an office or shop is regarded as attracting customers, opportunities and professional progress.

**Desk position:** Placing the work desk facing east is believed to improve concentration, decision-making and leadership qualities. Business people and executives often prefer to sit facing east.

**Meeting areas:** A conference room or shared working space in the eastern part is believed to help clear communication, new ideas and successful negotiation.

**Display and branding:** The east wall is regarded as ideal for showing the company's achievements, vision statement or important branding, because the direction is thought to add to visibility and recognition.

In this way, careful use of the east in the design of a workplace can give a setting that encourages progress, innovation and goodwill among team members.

## Common Misconceptions

**Misconception 1: All east-facing properties are equally auspicious**

*Reality:* A property is considered to benefit from eastern energy only when it lines up properly with the directions. Many so-called "east-facing" properties actually face the southeast (Agneya) or the northeast (Ishana), whose energy is held to have different qualities. True east lies between 75 and 105 degrees on the compass.

**Misconception 2: Vastu for the east cannot be applied in modern buildings**

*Reality:* Even in modern construction with a fixed layout, the eastern part can be improved without changing the structure, through furniture arrangement, colours and things that add to the energy.

**Misconception 3: Defects in the east can be put right only by costly rituals or repairs**

*Reality:* Many Vastu defects in the east can be improved by simple changes, such as keeping the place clean, well lit and uncluttered, or placing things like plants that add to eastern energy.

**Misconception 4: East is always better than the other directions**

*Reality:* The east is highly auspicious, but Vastu Shastra stresses balance among all the directions. Contrary to common belief, west-facing and south-facing entrances can also be beneficial if properly built and balanced.

Use a compass. East lies at 90 degrees, and pure east is taken to span 75 to 105 degrees. Correct the reading for the magnetic declination of your area. The compass of a smartphone is not as accurate as a professional instrument.

### What are the common Vastu defects in the east?

Heavy structures or load-bearing walls, water leakage or dampness, dark or crowded spaces, a toilet or sewage system, and heavy metal objects or machinery.

No. By tradition many defects improve with simple changes, such as keeping the place clean, well lit and uncluttered, keeping windows open in the morning, and not placing heavy furniture or electronic equipment there.

Find the exact east of your home with a compass. Keep that area clean, well lit and uncluttered. Place plants with round leaves there, open the windows at sunrise, and keep heavy furniture and electronic equipment out of it.
