# The Four Corner Directions in Vastu: NE, SE, SW and NW

## Introduction

The four directions that lie between the four main directions on a compass are called the corner directions. They are the northeast, Ishana; the southeast, Agneya; the southwest, Nairitya; and the northwest, Vayavya. In Vastu tradition each corner is given its own energy. Understanding these directions is regarded as useful for placing rooms correctly in a home or workplace, for a balanced flow of energy and for peace of mind.

## What Are the Corner Directions?

The corner directions are the points exactly midway between north, south, east and west. They are also called intermediate or auxiliary directions. In Vastu tradition each corner has its own deity, its own planet and its own energy, which is said to be linked with different sides of life.

## The Main Points of the Four Corners

### Northeast (Ishana)

- **Nature of the energy:** Spirituality, knowledge, purity and clarity. This direction combines the purifying energies of the north (prosperity) and the east (vitality).
- **Ruling deity:** Lord Shiva (Ishana), a symbol of higher consciousness and spiritual wisdom.
- **Planetary influence:** Jupiter (Guru), the planet of wisdom and expansion.
- **Vastu guidelines:** Keep this zone open and free of goods. It is regarded as ideal for a meditation room, prayer place or study room. A water source here is regarded as adding to positive energy.

### Southeast (Agneya)

- **Nature of the energy:** Fire, transformation, energy and creativity. It joins the dynamic energies of the south (strength) and the east (growth).
- **Ruling deity:** Agni, the god of fire, a symbol of passion, power and movement.
- **Planetary influence:** Venus (Shukra), an indicator of beauty, comfort and material prosperity.
- **Vastu guidelines:** The best place for a kitchen or for work that needs active energy. Do not place a bedroom or water source here, to avoid conflict and health problems.

### Southwest (Nairitya)

- **Nature of the energy:** Stability, strength and protection. It joins the south (discipline) and the west (materiality).
- **Ruling deity:** Nairuti, who is linked with security and the well-being of the family.
- **Planetary influence:** In some traditions Rahu, or the indirect influence of Mars and Saturn.
- **Vastu guidelines:** Proper for the master bedroom or storage. Do not place an entrance here, because it is believed that this unsettles the energy of the home. Keep this part heavy and closed.

### Northwest (Vayavya)

- **Nature of the energy:** Movement, flexibility, communication and social connection. It joins the intellectual north with the communicative west.
- **Ruling deity:** Vayu, the god of wind, a symbol of change and the flow of resources.
- **Planetary influence:** The Moon (Chandra), regarded as adding to emotions, adaptability and getting along with others.
- **Vastu guidelines:** Suitable for a guest room, storage or places linked with movement, such as a bathroom. This direction is not regarded as proper for a kitchen or heavy storage.

## How to Find the Corner Directions

The corner directions can be identified in this way:

- **Dividing the compass:** Divide the compass or the plot into 8 or 16 equal parts. The northeast lies at 45 degrees, the southeast at 135 degrees, the southwest at 225 degrees and the northwest at 315 degrees. Use a compass or a digital tool for accurate measurement.
- **Mapping by Vastu:** First set the structure against the four main directions, then identify the corner points in between, and fix the proper place for rooms and objects accordingly.

## How to Assess Them

- **Understand the mixed energy:** Each corner is a blend of the energy of the two neighbouring main directions, so its effect is regarded as more subtle.
- **Use of Vastu:** By tradition, placing the main rooms, objects or activities in the right corner is regarded as a way to gain from the energy of that direction or to balance its shortcomings.

## Practical Applications

### Vastu in Home Design

Understanding the corner directions lets architects and home owners build homes that tradition regards as supportive of health, wealth, peace and harmony. For example, a kitchen in the southeast fits the energy of the fire element, while a meditation room in the northeast is regarded as good for spiritual progress.

### Places of Worship and Practice

Tradition mentions that some practices are done facing a particular corner. Examples are meditating in the northeast for wisdom and carrying out social activities in the northwest to build contacts. Understand these only as traditional practices.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Only the main directions matter:** Many people think Vastu rests only on the four main directions, but the corner directions add important layers of energy and meaning.
- **Deities and planets are always fixed:** Some people believe these links never change, but different traditions show small differences. For the southwest, for example, Rahu is named in one place and Mars in another.
- **All places are equally beneficial:** Each direction suits certain functions. Placing a room in the wrong place, such as a kitchen in the northeast, can reduce harmony.

## Examples

These examples are only for illustration.

- **Example 1:** A family builds its prayer room in the northeast and feels peace and clarity in the home. This matches the belief of Vastu tradition that the northeast is the direction of knowledge and purity.
- **Example 2:** A business owner moves the office pantry to the southeast and speaks of better energy among the staff and fewer disputes between them. This is in line with the traditional belief linked with the energy of Agni and Venus.

## Conclusion

The corner directions are not mere points on a compass. In tradition they are regarded as living energy fields made up of the blend of the main directions, the planets and the deities. By understanding their importance, spaces can be built that fit the traditional idea of balance and prosperity. Recognising the separate qualities of each corner gives a deeper understanding of harmony in a home built by Vastu.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which are the four corner directions, and who are their deities?

The deity of the northeast (Ishana) is Shiva, of the southeast (Agneya) Agni, of the southwest (Nairitya) Nairuti, and of the northwest (Vayavya) Vayu. As planets, the northeast is linked with Jupiter, the southeast with Venus and the northwest with the Moon. For the southwest, some traditions give Rahu or the influence of Mars and Saturn.

### In which corner should the kitchen be built?

By tradition the southeast (Agneya) is the best place for a kitchen, because it is the direction of fire. A kitchen in the northeast is not regarded as proper, and a kitchen in the northwest is also advised against.

### Where should a prayer or meditation room go?

The northeast (Ishana) is regarded as ideal for a meditation room, prayer place and study room. This part should be kept open and free of goods.

### In which corner is the master bedroom placed?

The southwest (Nairitya) is described as proper for the master bedroom or for storage. This part is kept heavy and closed, and placing an entrance here is not regarded as right.

### How do I identify the corner directions with a compass?

Divide the compass into 8 or 16 equal parts. The northeast lies at 45 degrees, the southeast at 135 degrees, the southwest at 225 degrees and the northwest at 315 degrees. For this, set the plan of the house against the main directions.

### What should a beginner do?

Identify the four corners in your home with the help of a compass. See which room lies in which corner, and then gradually make small changes according to the Vastu suggestions.
