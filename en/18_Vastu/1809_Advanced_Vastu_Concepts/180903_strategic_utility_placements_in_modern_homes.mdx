# Vastu for Electricity, Water and AC in Modern Homes

## Introduction: Vastu in the Age of Technology

Vastu Shastra is the traditional knowledge of building in harmony with the order of nature. A modern home is recognised by its conveniences: complex electrical wiring, water management systems, and appliances such as air conditioners that control the temperature. Where these are placed is not only a question of convenience or engineering. In modern Vastu it is an important consideration.

According to Vastu tradition, utilities placed in the wrong position upset the delicate balance of the elements in a house. It is believed that this leaves a lasting negative atmosphere, which may show up as unrest, financial instability or health worries. This article describes where the utilities of a home should be placed according to the tradition. Wherever a utility involves safety, such as wiring, plumbing or the weight of a tank on a roof, the work must follow the advice of a licensed electrician, plumber or structural engineer, and Vastu preferences come second to that.

## Elemental Harmony: The Core Principle

The foundation of Vastu is the balance of the five elements, the Pancha Bhutas: fire (Agni), water (Jal), air (Vayu), earth (Prithvi) and space (Akasha). Every object and utility in a home has one dominant element. The core principle is to place an object in the zone of its own element.

- **Fire element utilities** (heaters, electrical panels) belong in the south-east.
- **Water element utilities** (tanks, borewells) belong in the north-east or in other compatible zones.
- **Air element utilities** (AC units and air exhausts) belong in the north-west.
- **Earth element utilities** (heavy structures, overhead tanks) belong in the south-west.

A mismatch of element and direction, such as an electrical panel in the north-east, the water zone, is considered a major Vastu defect.

## Electrical Systems: The Fire Element

In a modern home the electrical system is the main form of the fire element.

### Electrical Panels, Inverters and Generators

- **Ideal zone, the south-east (Agneya)**: this is the corner of fire. Placing the main electrical panel, inverter or generator here is considered very auspicious, and tradition says the equipment works well there.
- **Alternative zone**: the north-west is the second option, especially for backup equipment such as inverters, because the air element supports fire.
- **Zones to avoid**:
    - **North-east (Ishanya)**: placing a heat-producing electrical panel here is a major Vastu defect. It is said to "burn" the sacred water element of this direction.
    - **South-west (Nairutya)**: tradition links this position with frequent equipment failure and disputes.
    - **Brahmasthan (the open centre)**: clogging the central part of the house with tangled wiring is said to disturb the energy of the whole house.

## Water Systems: The Water Element

Water is the source of life, so Vastu treats its correct placement as important.

### Overhead Water Tanks

- **Ideal zone, the south-west**: this is the most important rule for overhead tanks. The south-west is the zone of the earth element and should be the highest and heaviest part of the building. A heavy tank here is said to give the building the weight and stability it needs. Whether a roof can carry a tank is for a structural engineer to decide.
- **Alternative zones**: the west or the south are the next best places.
- **Zones to avoid**:
    - **North-east**: placing a heavy tank here is considered a serious Vastu defect. It is believed to put heavy pressure on the "head" of the Vastu Purusha.
    - **South-east**: fire and water clash here, and tradition links this with accidents and legal disputes.
    - **Brahmasthan (the open centre)**: this is regarded as placing a heavy burden on the heart of the home.

### Underground Water Sources (Borewell, Sump)

- **Ideal zone, the north-east**: in contrast to overhead tanks, an underground water source is best in the north-east, the natural zone of the water element. The tradition regards this as favourable for prosperity and health.
- **Alternative zones**: north or east.
- **Zone to avoid**: the south-west. An underground water source here is considered a major Vastu defect.

### Septic Tanks and Drainage

- **Ideal zone, the north-west (Vayavya)**: the north-west is considered best for septic tanks and wastewater outlets. The air element of this direction is said to help disperse the negativity linked with waste.
- **Zones to avoid**: the north-east, south-west, south-east and the Brahmasthan. According to tradition a septic tank in these zones pollutes the auspicious energy.

## AC and Ventilation: The Air Element

Heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems are considered to belong to the air element.

- **Ideal zone for outdoor units, the north-west**: the outdoor units of air conditioners and the main exhaust fans are best placed in the north-west, the corner of the air element.
- **Alternative zone**: the south-east is also acceptable for AC units that give off heat, because it matches the fire element.
- **Placing indoor AC units**:
    - They are best fixed on a north or west wall.
    - Keep the airflow towards the south or the east.
    - Do not fix an AC directly above a bed, because it can disturb sleep.

## Electromagnetic Fields and Plumbing Layout

### Electromagnetic Fields (EMF)

Modern Vastu also draws attention to the electromagnetic fields from electrical wiring, Wi-Fi routers and appliances.

- **Wi-Fi routers**: place them in the south-east corner of the house or room. Avoid bedrooms and the Brahmasthan.
- **Green plants**: some people keep plants such as the snake plant or spider plant near electronic equipment.

### Plumbing Layout

- **Direction of flow**: all drainage pipes should flow from south to north or from west to east.
- **No leaks**: a dripping tap or pipe is considered a major Vastu defect, and tradition treats it as a symbol of wealth draining away. Have leaks repaired at once.
- **Avoid diagonal lines**: pipelines should not cross the plot diagonally and should not pass under the Brahmasthan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My Wi-Fi router is in a north-east bedroom. What can I do?

The best solution is to move it to the south-east. If that is not possible, switch it off at night.

### Can I keep a washing machine in the bathroom?

Yes, provided the bathroom is in a direction that Vastu considers suitable for a washing machine, such as the north-west or the south-east. Avoid keeping it in a bathroom in the north-east or south-west.

### The main water tank of my apartment building is in the north-east. What is the effect?

You cannot change the structure of the building, but you can strengthen the Vastu of your own flat. Keep the north-east corner of your flat light and clean, and keep a small water feature inside the home.

### Can the water inlet and the drainage outlet be in the same direction?

This is not ideal. Water should enter from the north-east, north or east, and drainage should leave from the north-west. If both are in the same place, tradition sees a conflict of energy.

## Conclusion: Modern Living and Traditional Knowledge

For modern homes, the correct placement of utilities is a point Vastu does not ignore. Electricity is matched with fire, water with its natural directions, and air and earth with their own places, so that the house is planned with thought. Balancing technology with tradition is the main purpose of these rules, and safety and engineering advice always come first.
