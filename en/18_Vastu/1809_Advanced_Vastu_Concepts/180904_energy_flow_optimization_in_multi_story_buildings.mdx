# Vastu for Multi-Storey Buildings and Apartments

## Introduction: The Third Dimension of Vastu

Traditional Vastu Shastra guided the design of single-family homes on a flat, two-dimensional plan. Skylines everywhere are now dominated by multi-storey buildings and high-rise apartments, which calls for a more complex application that some call 3D Vastu.

In a multi-storey building, energy is said not only to move sideways across a floor plan but also to travel vertically and join every level. Vastu tradition treats this vertical flow as the lifeblood of the whole structure and holds that a disturbance in it creates Vastu doshas that affect every resident. This article describes the principles for the vertical circulation of a building, wherever your flat is.

## Foundational Vastu for the Whole Building

Before looking at an individual apartment, the Vastu of the whole building is considered. The energy of a flat is linked to the energy of the structure it sits in.

- **Plot and shape**: the plot on which the building stands should ideally be a square or a rectangle. The building itself should follow the same regular shapes, for stability.
- **Main entrance**: the main entrance of the whole complex is important. The most auspicious directions are the north-east, east or north. An entrance in the south-west is considered a major defect that brings instability to the whole building.
- **Open spaces**: it is considered very beneficial to have more open space, such as lawns or gardens, on the north and east sides of the plot, so that positive energies can enter and gather.
- **Building height**: the south and west sides of the building should ideally be taller and heavier than the north and east sides, which creates a protective barrier and a favourable slope.

## The Spine of the Building: Vertical Conduits

Staircases and lifts are the main channels of vertical movement, so their placement and design are given great weight.

### Staircase Placement and Design

Staircases are compared to arteries that carry prana from one floor to the next. Wrongly placed, they can block energy or drain it away.

- **Ideal placement**: the best zones for a staircase are the south, west or south-west. These zones are associated with the earth element, and a staircase in them is said to give stability and a smooth, controlled flow.
- **Direction to avoid, the north-east (Ishanya)**: a staircase in the sacred north-east corner is considered a severe Vastu dosha. It is likened to placing a heavy, constant burden on the "head" of the Vastu Purusha.
- **Design principles**:
    - **Clockwise turn**: a staircase should turn clockwise as it rises. A counter-clockwise staircase is said to create a whirl of negative energy.
    - **Odd number of steps**: the number of steps in a flight should be odd, for example 15, 17 or 19. A person who starts with the right foot then ends the flight on the right foot, which is considered auspicious.
    - **Solid and safe**: spiral staircases are avoided because they are said to make energy chaotic. Stairs should be solid and well lit, and should not be left in disrepair.

### Lift Placement

A lift is a large metal object with electromagnetic fields, and its movement is said to create a strong vertical current.

- **Ideal placement**: the south-east is a good location for a lift, because the fire element of this zone is said to handle the electrical energy of the motor. The north-west is another suitable option.
- **Zones to avoid**: a lift should never be in the north-east, which it would afflict. The Brahmasthan, the open centre, is also completely off limits, because a moving shaft at the centre is said to unsettle the energy of the whole building.

## Vastu for the Individual Apartment

Even if the building as a whole has flaws, the tradition says the energy inside your own flat can be improved.

- **Entrance of the flat**: it follows the same rules as the main door of a house. The north-east, east and north are best.
- **Master bedroom**: it should be in the south-west corner of the flat. Tradition links this with stability for the head of the family.
- **Kitchen**: it should be in the south-east, the fire zone. The cooking stove should be placed so that the person cooking faces east.
- **Children's room**: the north-west or the west is ideal.
- **Puja room or altar**: the north-east is the only correct place for a prayer space.
- **Toilets and bathrooms**: they should be in the north-west or the west.

## Alignment Between Floors

This is an important idea in 3D Vastu. What lies on the floor above and below you is said to affect your own flat.

- **Toilets**: a toilet on the floor above should never be directly over the kitchen or the puja room on the floor below. Tradition treats this as a serious contamination of energy.
- **Kitchens**: in the same way, a kitchen on the floor above should not be directly over a bedroom or study, because fire energy is said to cause agitation.
- **Brahmasthan**: the central area of your flat should not have a heavy pillar or a toilet on the floor directly above it.

When choosing a flat, try to see the floor plans of the units above and below, so that you can check for these conflicts.

## Traditional Remedies for High-Rise Living

The structure of a flat often cannot be changed. For such cases the tradition describes the following practices.

| Defect | Traditional remedy |
| --- | --- |
| Staircase or lift in the north-east | This affects the whole building. In your own flat, keeping a small water feature such as a small fountain running in the north-east is thought to strengthen the water element. |
| Flat entrance in the south-west | Place two tiles with the image of Hanuman outside the door and use a brown or peach doormat. |
| Kitchen in the north-east | Paint the kitchen walls light yellow. Keep a bowl of water with fresh flower petals in the north-east corner of the kitchen. |
| Toilet above the kitchen or puja room | This is considered a serious flaw. The tradition mentions keeping camphor in both spaces. If possible, avoid it when choosing the flat. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which floor is best in a multi-storey building according to Vastu?

Lower floors, from the ground to the third floor, are considered more connected with the earth element and are said to give more stability. Higher floors get better light and air and less noise, which is also good from a Vastu point of view. The choice is yours. For energetic stability, lower floors are often preferred.

### My flat has a balcony in the south-west. What can I do?

A balcony in the south-west makes an opening and makes this zone lighter, which is considered a Vastu defect. Tradition speaks of placing heavy pots with large plants on the balcony. Dark, earthy colours can be chosen for the balcony tiles and railing, and heavy curtains can be hung on the balcony door. Check with a structural engineer before adding weight to a balcony.

### The water tank of the building is directly above my north-east flat. Is that a problem?

Tradition treats it as a major Vastu defect, because weight and the water element are not considered favourable in the north-east. The traditional suggestion is to strengthen the south-west of your flat with heavy furniture and to keep the north-east light and clean. Questions about the load of the tank belong to a licensed structural engineer.

### Can I use mirrors to correct Vastu defects in a flat?

A mirror on a north or east wall makes the space look larger and is considered auspicious in the tradition. Never place a mirror opposite the main door, because it is said to send the incoming energy straight back out. Avoid keeping a mirror in a bedroom where it reflects the bed.

## Conclusion: Harmony at Height

Living in a multi-storey building does not mean giving up Vastu. With a three-dimensional view you can understand the vertical flow that marks high-rise living. Pay attention to the core structure of the building, its staircases and lifts, and to the Vastu of your own flat.

Choose a flat in which the conflicts between floors are fewer, and apply the traditional practices for defects that cannot be avoided. In this way the old understanding of Vastu can be used even in buildings that reach for the sky.
