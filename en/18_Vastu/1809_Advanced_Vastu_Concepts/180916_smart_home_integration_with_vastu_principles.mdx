# Smart Home Devices and Vastu: Where to Place Them

## Introduction: Old Tradition and Today's Technology

The Internet of Things (IoT) has turned homes into networks of connected, intelligent devices. When we fill a home with smart devices, a web of electromagnetic waves (EMF) also forms there. The ancient teachers of Vastu did not know of these devices. The question for today's home owner is how to enjoy the convenience of a smart home without upsetting the balance that Vastu speaks of.

This article explains where main devices such as a Wi-Fi router and a smart hub can be placed, and how shielded cables are used to keep wireless signals down, so that the flow of energy in the home is not hindered by technology. Smart-home ideas are modern, not classical Vastu. What follows applies Vastu tradition to them and should be read as belief.

## The Energy Effect of IoT Devices

From a Vastu point of view, every object in the home has its own energy effect. IoT devices give off wireless signals all the time and draw electricity, so their effect is considered more active. There are two main concerns.

1. **Interference from EMF**: Tradition holds that a large amount of EMF can disturb the natural energy field of a place and give a feeling of stress, tiredness and imbalance.
2. **Clash of elements**: Electronic devices are linked with the fire element. Placing them in the wrong Vastu zone is believed to create a clash between elements.

The aim is to place and handle devices according to Vastu rules, so that their benefit is kept and any unfavourable effect on the energy is as small as possible.

## Where to Place IoT Devices

### 1. Wi-Fi router: the centre of the fire element

A Wi-Fi router is the heart of a smart home's connectivity and gives off a strong signal all the time.

- **Preferred place**: The south-east (Agneya) corner is considered best for a router. It is the zone of the fire element, so it suits heat-producing, energy-heavy devices. Placed there, the router's fire energy stays in its own zone and does not disturb the zones of other elements.
- **Places to avoid**: The north-east (Ishanya) is the sacred zone of water. A router there is believed to set fire against water, so do not put it in the north-east. Do not put it in the Brahmasthan, the centre of the home, either, because that is meant to be kept as open and empty as possible.

### 2. Smart hubs and voice assistants: the control centre

Smart hubs (such as Amazon Echo or Google Home) work as the control centre of a smart home.

- **Preferred place**: So that voice commands can be heard from several rooms, the centre of the home (Brahmasthan) is often convenient in practice. Vastu generally says to keep the Brahmasthan empty, but one small, neat smart hub can be treated as an exception, as a symbol of the home's brain. If the centre is not possible, the next best place is the south-east.
- **Keep in mind**: The device should not be large or bulky, and the space around it should be clean and tidy.

### 3. Automation systems (lighting, blinds and so on)

The controllers of automated systems should also follow the rule of the fire element.

- **Place**: The central control panels for smart lighting, automated blinds and similar systems are best fitted on walls in the south-east part of the home, or in the south-east part of the room they control.

## Keeping EMF Low with Shielded Cables

Wireless connection is convenient, but Vastu encourages keeping EMF low where possible, especially in sleeping areas.

- **Shielded Ethernet cables**: For devices that stay in one place, such as a smart TV, a desktop computer and a gaming console, consider shielded Ethernet cables instead of Wi-Fi. The connection stays stable and fast, and there are fewer wireless signals in the room.
- **Planning cable routes**: When wiring a home, plan the cable routes according to Vastu. Do not run heavy electrical or data cables through the Brahmasthan or under the main bedroom. Take them along the edges of rooms.
- **Wi-Fi sleep mode**: Many modern routers can switch off Wi-Fi during the night, for example from 11 PM to 6 AM. According to tradition, this keeps the atmosphere calm at sleeping time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where should a smart TV go according to Vastu?

A smart TV is an electronic device, so it is considered better in the south-east or north part of the living room. Do not put it on the north or east wall, because then the viewer faces south or west, which is not considered good.

### Is it all right to keep a smart speaker in the bedroom?

Vastu advises keeping electronic devices out of the bedroom so that sleep stays calm. If one must be kept, put it as far from the bed as possible, in the south-east corner of the room. Switch off or cover all the lights on the device at night.

### My router has to stay in the north because of the provider's connection point. What can I do?

If the router cannot be moved, keep it away from the place where you sleep, and use the Wi-Fi sleep setting at night. These are practical steps and they do not depend on any belief.

### Do smart security cameras have a Vastu place?

Yes. Outside cameras are placed to watch the main entrance, ideally looking from the south-east towards the north or west. Inside cameras should be fitted discreetly and kept away from sensitive places such as the bedroom and the puja room.

## Conclusion

Making a smart home that fits Vastu does not mean refusing technology. It means taking it up thoughtfully. By placing IoT devices according to their element and taking conscious steps to keep EMF low, a home can be modern and also balanced in the view of Vastu tradition. Such a lifestyle brings the convenience of connection, and the home can still stay a place of peace.
