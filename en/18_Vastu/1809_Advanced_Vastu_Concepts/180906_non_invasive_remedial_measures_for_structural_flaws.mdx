# Traditional Vastu Remedies Without Demolition

## Introduction: Correction at the Level of Energy

Many people believe that correcting a major Vastu dosha, such as a wrongly placed toilet or a pillar in the middle of the home, needs expensive and disruptive demolition and rebuilding. Removing a structural fault is the most direct solution, and that is true. Vastu tradition, however, also describes another way, which it calls correction at the level of energy.

According to the tradition, these practices work on a subtle level to balance the negativity said to arise from a defect. Their aim is to restore the harmony of the five elements in the home. The approach is convenient for rented houses, flats and anyone who wants to improve a home without the cost and trouble of renovation. These are traditional practices, not a promised solution. Structural matters, such as a crack, a beam, a pillar or any wall that might carry load, must be judged by a licensed engineer before anything is altered, and a health complaint should be taken to a doctor.

## Structural Flaw and Energetic Imbalance

It helps to understand the difference between a physical flaw and the energy problem it is said to create.

- **Structural flaw**: this is the physical reality, such as a toilet in the north-east, a kitchen in the south-west or a beam running over a bed.
- **Energetic imbalance**: this is the consequence, according to the tradition. A toilet in the north-east is said to contaminate the sacred water element. A kitchen in the south-west is said to unsettle the earth element. A beam is said to create a heavy, pressing energy, which is linked with stress.

Practices that need no demolition do not change the physical structure. The tradition holds that they address the energetic imbalance directly.

## Traditional Means That Need No Demolition

- **Mirrors**: used to spread, correct and return energy. They are believed to "cut" the extended parts of a plot and "fill" the cut parts in a virtual sense.
- **Colours**: each colour is linked with an element and a planet. Painting a wall a particular colour is said to strengthen a weak zone or calm an overactive one.
- **Light**: brightly lit places are regarded as dispelling negativity. Lamps and good lighting can bring life to dark, stagnant corners.
- **Sound**: the sound of a bell or the chanting of mantras is regarded as breaking negative patterns of energy.
- **Plants**: living plants are regarded as a source of lively prana.
- **Sea salt**: raw sea salt is regarded as absorbing negative energy and is used to cleanse a space.

## Traditional Practices for Common Structural Flaws

### Defect: Toilet or Septic Tank in the North-east (Ishanya)

This is considered a serious Vastu dosha. The tradition describes the following practices.

1. Keep the toilet lid down and the door shut at all times.
2. Place a bowl of raw sea salt in the toilet and change it every week.
3. Paint the outer walls of the bathroom light yellow or cream.
4. As far as possible, use this toilet as little as you can.

### Defect: Kitchen in the North-east

This is regarded as a clash of the fire and water elements.

1. Paint the kitchen walls light yellow.
2. Keep a small pot of water with fresh flowers in the north-east corner of the kitchen.
3. If possible, place the stove in the south-east corner inside the north-east kitchen.

### Defect: Exposed Beam

A beam passing over a bed, desk or sofa is linked with pressure and stress.

1. In some traditions two bamboo flutes are hung from the beam with a red ribbon, with the mouthpieces pointing down. It is believed that this disperses the heavy energy.
2. The simplest practical step is also to move the bed or the seating from under the beam.

### Defect: Pillar in the Brahmasthan (the Centre)

It is believed that this blocks the flow of energy through the whole house.

1. Covering the pillar with mirrors on all sides makes it appear to "disappear", and according to the tradition energy then flows around it.
2. Keep the space around the pillar open, clean and free of clutter.

Never cut, chip or alter a pillar or a beam yourself. Whether either can be touched is a question for a licensed structural engineer.

## The Role of Colours

Using colours is a simple way to balance the elements of a home.

| Direction | Element | Colours considered auspicious in the tradition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North-east | Water | Light blue, yellow |
| South-east | Fire | Orange, pink, silvery |
| South-west | Earth | Peach, beige, brown |
| North-west | Air | White, light grey, cream |

If a zone has a defect, it is strengthened with the colour of that zone, or calmed with a balancing colour.

## Vastu for Renters

Practices that need no demolition suit renters best, because the space can be arranged without any change to the structure.

- **Focus on movable things**: use plants, bowls of salt, lamps and small decorative items.
- **Use furnishings for colour**: if you cannot paint, coloured curtains, bedsheets, rugs and cushion covers can bring in the colour of the element you need.
- **Take them with you**: all of these can be carried along when you move, so the same arrangement can be set up in the new place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can many practices be used at once?

No. Too many objects add clutter, which is itself regarded as a disturbance of energy. A few well-chosen practices in the right place are better than filling the house with things. Start with the most serious defects.

### Are these practices a permanent substitute for structural change?

For serious defects the tradition regards them as means of balance. A structural change is the final solution. Where demolition is not possible, these practices are adopted as a practical alternative.

### What can I do if I live in a rented house?

Choose practices that can be removed, such as plants, bowls of salt, lamps, coloured curtains and rugs. They can be taken with you when you move.

### What does the tradition suggest if the kitchen is in the north-east?

It speaks of painting the kitchen walls light yellow, keeping a pot of water with fresh flowers in the north-east corner and, if possible, placing the stove in the south-east corner of the kitchen.

### What should I do if there is a pillar in the middle of the house?

The tradition speaks of covering the pillar with mirrors on all sides. The space around it should also be kept open, clean and free of clutter. Have an engineer confirm that nothing structural is disturbed.

## Conclusion: Bring Harmony to Your Home

You are not helpless in front of the structural flaws of your home. Vastu Shastra offers an accessible store of traditional practices that need no demolition. By working with colour, light, sound and simple objects, you can try to manage the imbalance.

Take up these practices thoughtfully and with faith. A harmonious home is regarded as the foundation of a harmonious life.
