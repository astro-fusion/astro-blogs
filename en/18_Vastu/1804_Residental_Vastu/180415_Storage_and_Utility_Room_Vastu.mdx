# Storage and Utility Room Vastu: Direction and Organisation

## Introduction

Storage and utility room Vastu looks at how the position and organisation of storage spaces are said to affect the flow of energy in a home. Vastu Shastra, the traditional Indian system of architecture, holds that a storage room kept in the right place and in good order helps a household stay steady and settled, instead of being filled with clutter and stagnation.

## What Is Storage and Utility Room Vastu

It is the application of Vastu Shastra principles to the parts of a home set aside for storing goods, tools, seasonal items and other belongings. Vastu tradition holds that the position of every room affects the flow of energy in the house, and with it the well-being and clarity of the people who live there. A storage room built according to Vastu is seen as more than a practical need. It is also a factor in keeping the energy of the home balanced.

## Key Aspects of Storage Room Vastu

### Which Direction Is Right

The ideal position for a storage or utility room is the south-west or the north-west of the house.

- **South-west:** This is the steadiest and most secure part of the house. It suits heavy or seldom-used items and is said to lend stability to the property.
- **North-west:** This part is linked with movement and turnover of goods. It suits items that are used often, or food storage.

Directions to avoid: the north-east, which is considered sacred and meant to stay free of clutter, and the centre of the house, the Brahmasthan, should never be used for storage. The belief is that storing things there clouds the flow of auspicious energy.

### Order of Items: Nature and Frequency of Use

- Store heavier and less-used items, such as seasonal tools, spare tiles and paint cans, towards the south-west or the southern part of the storage area.
- Items used more often, or lighter ones such as household supplies or grains, should go towards the north-west or on higher shelves.
- Do not let clutter build up. Clear out unused, broken or expired items regularly, so that stagnant or negative energy does not collect.
- Keep the room clean at all times and avoid keeping empty containers, which are believed to attract negative energy.

### Shelves, Light and Air

- **Shelving:** Place shelves and cupboards along the south and west walls. Heavy cabinets should be in the south-west, and lighter shelves can be in the north or east.
- **Lighting:** A well-lit storage area is essential. Avoid dark, dingy corners, which are believed to become stores of stagnant energy. Use natural light where possible, especially from east-facing or north-facing windows, and add warm artificial light to brighten the space.
- **Ventilation:** Good airflow is important. Provide at least one window or ventilator, ideally in the east or north, so that fresh air circulates and the room does not turn musty or damp.
- Tradition also mentions the practice of burning incense from time to time, or keeping a bowl of sea salt and changing it regularly, to keep the space fresh and positive.

## How to Decide the Direction

Vastu Shastra is more a set of rules than a calculation. To decide the best direction for storage and utility rooms:

- Map the floor plan of your house with a compass.
- Identify the main directions and the key zones, such as the Brahmasthan, the north-east and the south-west.
- Allot storage spaces in the south-west or north-west zones.
- Make sure windows, doors and shelving follow the recommended orientation: windows on the east or north walls, doors on the north or east side, and shelves on the south or west walls.

## What the Position of a Storage Room Is Said to Mean

- A storage room in the south-west is held to bring stability and to keep clutter from growing, which is thought to support the household.
- Storage areas in the north-west suit frequent movement and the circulation of energy, and are ideal for food or items used daily.
- Storage in the forbidden areas, the north-east or the centre, is believed to impede the flow of positive energy, with mental fog and obstacles as the result.
- Disorder or poor upkeep is linked by tradition with money blocks, family friction and persistent trouble in the house.

## Putting It into Practice

### For a Store Room Already Built in the Wrong Direction

If a store room already stands in a direction that Vastu does not favour, such as the north-east or the centre of the house, tradition describes these simple measures.

- Keep a bowl of sea salt, said to absorb negativity, and change it regularly.
- Add light to dark corners and clean them often.
- Carry out purifying acts from time to time, such as burning incense or chanting mantras.

These are described as traditional practices. No costly item needs to be bought for them.

### For Positive Surroundings and Good Order

- Keep heavy or seldom-used items on lower shelves or in the south-west.
- Keep light items used every day on higher shelves or towards the north-west.
- Keep all containers covered and do not store useless clutter.
- Let air into the room. Indoor plants in suitable zones are also thought by tradition to balance the flow of energy.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Any room can be used for storage.** According to Vastu this is not right. The right position is considered necessary to avoid stagnation and negative energy.
- **A store room needs no upkeep.** A store room collects disorder like any other room. If it is neglected, the energy is believed to become blocked.
- **Light and air are optional.** In Vastu, poor lighting and stale air are held to cause stagnant energy, so they should never be ignored.
- **Sleeping in a store room does no harm.** Tradition strictly forbids it, because the atmosphere of a store room is regarded as heavy and stagnant, which is not right for rest.

## Examples

**Example 1:** A family had its main store room in the north-east. They felt that quarrels and delays over money were increasing at home. After learning the Vastu suggestions, they moved the storage to the south-west and adopted simple cleaning and purifying practices. Tradition holds that the atmosphere of a home can improve after such a change.

**Example 2:** A home owner arranged a utility room by keeping seldom-used tools in the south-west and light items for daily use in the north-west. A window was fitted in the north wall for ventilation and warm lighting was added. The room became more useful and pleasant, and household work became easier.

## Conclusion

Storage and utility room Vastu is more than a guide to tidiness. It is a whole approach to keeping a home in energetic balance. By placing storage and utility rooms in the recommended directions, arranging items sensibly, providing good shelving, lighting and ventilation, and keeping up simple purifying practices, you fit your living space to the principles of Vastu Shastra. These long-held practices also make the home more useful in everyday terms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### In which direction should a store room be built?

The south-west and the north-west are recommended. The south-west is the steadiest part of the house and suits heavy or seldom-used items. The north-west suits items that are used often and food storage.

### Where should a store room not be built?

Not in the north-east, which is meant to stay free of clutter, and not in the centre of the house, the Brahmasthan.

### Where should heavy and light items be kept in a store room?

Keep heavier and less-used items towards the south-west or the southern part of the room, or on lower shelves. Keep lighter and more-used items towards the north-west or on higher shelves.

### Why do light and air matter in a store room?

Vastu holds that dark corners and stale air lead to stagnant energy. Use natural light from east-facing or north-facing windows, add warm artificial light, and provide at least one window or ventilator in the east or north.

### What can be done if the store room is already in the wrong direction?

Tradition describes keeping a bowl of sea salt and changing it regularly, brightening dark corners and cleaning often, and carrying out purifying practices such as burning incense. No costly item is needed.

### Where should I start?

Declutter and clean the storage area, then find its direction with a compass. Rearrange items by the principles above, improve the lighting and ventilation, and see how the room feels.
