# Home Office Vastu: Room Direction and Desk Position

## Introduction

According to Vastu tradition, the place of the office in a home and the direction of the desk affect concentration and productivity in work. Understanding the energy of the directions, paying attention to the layout of the room and choosing colours well are said to turn an ordinary room into one that suits work. With more people working from home, arranging the workspace by this old guidance has become relevant for many households. This article explains the rules in plain terms.

## What are home office placement and desk position?

This is the practice of placing the work area and the desk inside the home with thought, based on Vastu Shastra, the Indian tradition of arranging space. It is believed that the right place keeps the flow of positive energy good, opens the way to prosperity and helps a person reach his or her best in work.

## Key points

### Direction of the home office

- The north, the east or the north-east zone is recommended for a home office. These directions are linked with new beginnings, knowledge and prosperity.
- If the entrance of the office faces north or east, it is believed that the inflow of positive energy and opportunities is greatest. The tradition links this with growth of income and success at work.
- The general advice is that the office should not be near the bedroom or in the south-west. It is believed that this slows down work and fails to keep the places for work and for rest apart.

### The right direction of the desk

- The desk should ideally face north, north-east or east. The east is linked with creativity and growth of income, the north-east is said to increase clear thinking, and the north is the direction of progress in a career and mental focus.
- Place the desk so that you can see the door while seated, but are not directly in line with it. This position is regarded as a sign of being in control and ready for opportunities.
- A solid wall behind the chair gives the mind a feeling of support and steadiness.

### Colours for focus

- Use green, blue, yellow or light gold in the decor of the office. Green calms the mind, reduces nervousness and is good for sitting and working for long hours. Blue adds to focus and mental clarity. Yellow supports hope and creativity. Light gold gives a touch of abundance and warmth.
- Avoid very strong red or dull, flat white. It is believed that these can increase restlessness or reduce attention.
- These colours can be used on walls and furniture, or in small items such as cushions, curtains and desk accessories. This keeps the space harmonious and the mind on the work.

## How to decide the direction

Vastu has no calculation in the mathematical sense. The process is as follows.

- Decide the best direction for the home office by looking at your goals in work and the present layout of the house.
- Confirm the exact direction of the room and the desk with a compass or a mobile app.
- Look at the sources of natural light and the position of the entrance, so that the flow of energy is not blocked.
- Arrange the entrance and the desk according to the north, east or north-east. The desk should face one of these directions, and there should be a solid wall behind your back.
- Choose colours according to the nature of the work, for example blue for work that needs new ideas and green for administrative work.

## How to read the results

When the workspace is arranged by these rules:

- **Facing north, east or north-east**: it is believed that mental concentration grows, the way to prosperity opens and professional contacts become stronger. An east-facing set-up is said to be especially good for entrepreneurs and for creative workers.
- **Colours**: a space in which blue dominates points to focus and new ideas. Green calms and sustains creativity. Yellow gives enthusiasm and light gold gently suggests abundance.
- If you see no improvement in your mood or results, look again at the accuracy of the direction, the depth of the colour and the clutter in the room, so that the flow of energy improves.

## Practical use

### Entrepreneurs and business owners

Entrepreneurs can make their home office in the north-east or east and keep the desk facing east or north. According to tradition this supports the growth of business. Green or gold colours can help stir creativity and bring in useful opportunities.

### Remote employees and creative professionals

People who work from home and want focus can keep the desk facing the north-east for clear thinking, or the east for creative work. Blue and green tones give a calm, stress-free atmosphere and keep distractions away during long hours.

## Common misconceptions

- **Misconception:** the direction of the desk is only a matter of decoration.
  - **Clarification:** in Vastu the position is regarded as important for energy, not only for looks.
- **Misconception:** any room can be a home office.
  - **Clarification:** it is better if the chosen place is separate from the places for sleeping and resting. This keeps the balance of energy and clarity of mind.
- **Misconception:** any colour will do.
  - **Clarification:** colours matter for productivity. Some colours add to focus and calm, while others can distract or cause restlessness.

## Examples

- **Example 1:** a freelance writer sets up her office in the east corner of the house. Her desk faces east and there is a blue wall behind her.
- **Example 2:** an IT professional sets up his workplace in the north. His desk faces a window, and around him are green plants and light gold decorative items.

These examples are only meant to show how the tradition can be put into practice.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, the right room, a suitable direction for the desk and a good choice of colours can make working from home better. An office in the north, east or north-east is said to make a workplace that is favourable for concentration and productivity. Applying these old and practical ideas can help anyone make a home office that works for them.

## FAQs

### In which direction should a home office be built?

According to Vastu the north, the east or the north-east is considered good. These are linked with new beginnings, knowledge and prosperity. It is better if the door of the office also faces north or east.

### Which way should the desk face?

Keep the desk facing north, north-east or east. The east is linked with creativity, the north-east with clear thinking and the north with progress in a career and focus. There should be a solid wall behind the chair, and you should see the door without sitting in line with it.

### Which colours are good for a home office?

Green, blue, yellow and light gold are suggested. Very strong red and dull, flat white should be avoided.

### Where should a home office not be built?

The general advice is that the office should not be near the bedroom or in the south-west. When the places for work and rest are kept apart, the balance of energy and clarity of mind are said to remain.

### Where should I begin?

First find the north, east and north-east in your home. Keep the desk facing one of these directions, bring in green, blue, yellow or light gold colours, and do not let the workspace fill up with things.
