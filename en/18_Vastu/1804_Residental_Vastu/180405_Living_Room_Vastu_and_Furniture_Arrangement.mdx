# Living Room Vastu: Direction and Furniture Arrangement

## Introduction

According to Vastu Shastra, the direction of a house and the position of its furniture affect the flow of energy and the well-being of the family. The living room is special in this respect, because both the family and guests sit here. Vastu tradition holds that arranging the living room according to the direction the house faces, and placing furniture by Vastu principles, supports prosperity, harmony and a positive atmosphere. This article sets out those placement rules.

## What are living room Vastu and furniture arrangement?

This is the application of the directional and spatial rules of Vastu Shastra to the layout and furnishing of the living area. The rules come from the Vedic tradition of building. Their purpose is to bring the five elements (earth, water, fire, air and space) into harmony by positioning the living room and its furniture in a way that is held to make the most of beneficial energies. According to the tradition, this arrangement touches relationships, wealth, health and general satisfaction in life.

## Key aspects of living room Vastu

### Place of the room according to the direction the house faces

- **North or east-facing houses**: place the living room in the north-east. It is believed that this zone draws in clarity, prosperity and positive energy.
- **West-facing houses**: the north-west or the west is ideal for the living room, which is thought to support stability and social connections.
- **South-facing houses**: put the living room in the south-east or the south. In the south-east the fire element is linked with activity and liveliness.
- Do not place the living room in the south-west. Tradition does not consider it right for this purpose, and it is said that it may bring stagnation.

### Rules for placing furniture

- Place heavy furniture such as sofas, cabinets and tables in the south or west corners of the room, to give the room stability.
- Keep the north-east corner open and clear, so that energy can flow.
- Arrange the seating so that people face north or east while sitting. This is believed to support good conversation and clarity of mind.
- Place the TV and other electrical appliances in the south-east and not in the north-east, so that the calm and clarity of that corner are not disturbed.

### Other useful rules

- Do not push the sofa right against the north or east wall. Leave a small gap so that energy can move.
- Put mirrors on the north or east wall, which is said to spread positive energy. Do not put a mirror on the south or west wall.
- Keep the living room square or rectangular, to keep balance and symmetry.
- Choose light, soothing colours for the walls, such as white, cream, pastel green or blue.

## How to decide the place of the living room

Take these steps to decide the place of the living room and the arrangement of its furniture.

1. Identify the main entrance of the house and the direction the house faces (north, east, south or west).
2. Look up the Vastu rule for the place of the living room.
   - North or east-facing: living room in the north-east
   - West-facing: living room in the north-west or west
   - South-facing: living room in the south-east or south
3. For furniture, keep these points in mind.
   - Put heavier items in the southern and western parts.
   - Keep the north-east open.
   - Arrange the seating so that guests face north or east.
4. Do not place the living room in the south-west, and avoid dark, harsh wall colours.

## What a Vastu-based arrangement means

- According to tradition, a living room placed by Vastu is held to support prosperity, harmony among family members and a pleasant atmosphere for guests.
- A living room in the wrong place or with an unsuitable furniture arrangement is said to bring a chance of disagreement and stagnation.
- Heavy furniture in the south or west is said to steady the energy, while openness in the north-east keeps the flow and clarity.

## Practical applications

### Bringing harmony in the family

It is said that families who follow the Vastu rules feel an improvement in their relationships and in the calm of the home. Guests feel more at ease and meetings are pleasant.

### For financial prosperity

In houses facing north or east, a living room in the north-east is considered good for prosperity and new opportunities. A clean and well-lit space is thought to add to this. This too is a belief of the tradition and not a promise.

## Common misconceptions

- **Vastu is only superstition**: many Vastu rules are linked with practical matters such as light, air and the flow of space.
- **Furniture can be placed any way**: the position of heavy items and of open space is said to affect the flow of energy, so do not ignore it.
- **Colours and shapes make no difference**: according to Vastu, dark colours or irregular shapes can upset balance and harmony.

## Examples

- **Example 1**: In a north-facing house the living room was placed in the north-east and heavy cabinets were fitted in the south-west part of the room. This is an illustration of the arrangement, not a promise of any result.
- **Example 2**: In a west-facing house the living room was moved to the north-west and the sofas were placed properly.

## Conclusion

The Vastu of the living room and the arrangement of its furniture is considered important in shaping the atmosphere of a home. By choosing the place of the living room according to the direction the house faces, and by placing furniture by Vastu, you can make a space that, according to the tradition, supports happiness and peace for the residents and for visitors.

## FAQs

### In which direction should the living room be?

It depends on the direction the house faces. For a north or east-facing house it is the north-east, for a west-facing house the north-west or west, and for a south-facing house the south-east or south. The living room should not be in the south-west.

### Where should heavy furniture go?

Place heavy furniture such as sofas, cabinets and tables in the southern or western corners. Keep the north-east corner empty and tidy.

### Which way should people face while sitting?

Arrange the seating so that people face north or east. Do not push the sofa against the north or east wall; leave a small gap.

### Where should the TV and the mirror go?

Put the TV and other electrical appliances in the south-east, not in the north-east. Put a mirror on the north or east wall, not on the south or west wall.

### Which colours are good for the living room walls?

Light colours such as white, cream, pastel green and blue are considered good. Avoid dark and harsh colours.

### How do I begin?

First find the direction your house faces, clear unneeded things from the living room, and place heavy furniture by the Vastu rules. Then slowly follow the suggestions on colour and light.
