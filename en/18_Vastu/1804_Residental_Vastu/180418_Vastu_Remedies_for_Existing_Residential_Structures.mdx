# Simple Vastu Remedies for an Existing Home

## Introduction

Many home owners want to bring more harmony into a home that is already built, without costly renovation. Vastu tradition describes a number of non-structural measures for this, based on the careful use of colours, symbols, mirrors, plants and everyday objects. This article describes those traditional measures and the way they are meant to be applied.

## What Remedies for an Existing Home Are

These are corrective measures from Vastu Shastra that do not need walls to be broken or the main structure of the building to be changed. They involve placing objects with thought, choosing particular colours and adding natural elements such as plants. The tradition holds that these measures reduce negative energy and improve the harmony of the home. They are a way of adapting a fixed structure to the Vastu ideal, so that people who live in a finished home can also follow Vastu.

## Key Aspects of the Remedies

### Careful Use of Colours

Colours are given an important role in Vastu. Each direction in the home is said to have a colour that helps the good flow of energy. For example:

- Yellow and brown bring stability in the south-west.
- Green and lemon yellow bring energy in the south-east.
- Avoid red or blue where harmony may be disturbed.

When these colours are used on walls, upholstery or décor, the tradition holds that they have a light but deep effect on the energy of a room.

### Placing Symbols and Mirrors

- **Auspicious symbols:** It is a custom to place auspicious symbols at the entrance or at sacred places. They are believed to bring prosperity and keep negativity away.
- **Mirrors:** A mirror is regarded as something that increases energy, so its position matters. Place it on the north or east wall, which is believed to attract prosperity. Do not place a mirror directly facing the bed or in the south or west, because it is believed to bring imbalance and restlessness.

### Natural Elements: Plants and Decorative Objects

- Keep healthy, lush plants such as basil in key parts like the north or east. Tradition holds that this purifies the surroundings and brings liveliness.
- A tortoise figurine, of wood or glass, placed in the south-west or east is seen as a symbol of stability and protection.
- Auspicious paintings of calm nature scenes or deities are believed to add to spiritual balance.
- Bowls of salt, camphor balls and horseshoes are traditional objects, believed to absorb negativity and encourage good fortune.

## How to Decide on the Remedies

There is no complex mathematics here, but attention to direction, proportion and balance is necessary. The usual order is as follows.

1. **Look at the layout of the home.** Use a compass to find the main directions inside the house.
2. **Identify the Vastu defects.** Look for problem areas such as extended or missing corners, a bathroom or kitchen in the wrong place, and cluttered spaces.
3. **Match the element and colour to the direction.** Fit the element and colour to each part of the home.
4. **Apply the remedies.** Choose objects and changes to suit the particular imbalance, such as keeping salt in the corners.

According to tradition the effects of remedies are subtle at first, such as a better mood, better relationships or fewer daily obstacles. Different parts of the home are held to be linked with different areas of life. Correcting the north-east is thought to help finances, while the south-west is linked with stability and health. Remedies should be matched to the Vastu defects thought to affect the household most, and reviewed from time to time.

## Putting It into Practice

### A Toilet in the North or a Kitchen in the South

If a home has a toilet in the north or a kitchen in the south, both counted as defects in Vastu, there is no need to demolish it. Tradition describes reducing the effect with coloured tape, metal strips and suitable colours used in the area.

### Improving the Main Entrance

There is a tradition of placing auspicious symbols at the main entrance, or a horseshoe above the door, keeping the area free of clutter and keeping it well lit and welcoming. It is believed that good pictures or symbols placed there protect the home from bad influences and invite prosperity.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Vastu can be applied only during construction.** Many people think so. In fact, remedies of this kind are described for homes that are already built, and they need no structural change.
- **Major renovation is always needed.** Structural change is not compulsory. Tradition describes simple adjustments with objects, colours and placement.
- **Vastu changes are mere superstition.** Vastu is rooted in tradition, and its attention to the flow of energy, light and cleanliness also fits modern ideas about healthy living spaces.

## Examples

- **Example 1:** A home has a missing south-east corner, which is counted as the fire zone, and the family feels money is unstable. Without changing the building, they paint that part pistachio green and hang a picture of the sun on the east wall. Tradition describes such changes as a way to hope for lower expenses and better opportunities.
- **Example 2:** People in a home feel restless because a mirror faces the bed. By moving the mirror as the Vastu guidelines say and putting up a calm piece of art, sleep and peace are said to return.

## Conclusion

Remedies for an existing home are a practical and affordable way, in Vastu tradition, of correcting imbalances without major renovation. By using colours, auspicious symbols, mirrors and plants with care, a home owner can harmonise the home and answer Vastu defects within the long tradition of Vastu Shastra. These measures are within the reach of any home owner who wants to bring more balance into the living space.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is demolition needed to correct Vastu defects in a home that is already built?

No. Tradition describes non-structural measures such as colours, symbols, mirrors and plants, which need no breaking of walls.

### Where should a mirror be placed in the home?

On the north or east wall, which is believed to attract prosperity. Do not place it facing the bed directly, or in the south or west.

### Which colours are suggested for the south-west and the south-east?

Yellow and brown for stability in the south-west, and green and lemon yellow for energy in the south-east. Avoid red or blue where harmony may be disturbed.

### What can be done if there is a toilet in the north or a kitchen in the south?

There is no need to demolish it. Tradition describes reducing the effect with coloured tape, metal strips and suitable colours in the area.

### What is suggested for the main entrance?

Auspicious symbols at the entrance or a horseshoe above the door, an entrance free of clutter, and good lighting that makes it welcoming.

### Where should I start?

Start by decluttering and finding the main directions of your home. Remove broken or unwanted objects, and introduce suitable colours, symbols and decorative items gradually.
