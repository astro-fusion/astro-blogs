# Main Entrance Door Vastu: Direction, Opening and Design

## Introduction

The main door of a house is more than a gap in the wall between your home and the outside world. According to Vastu tradition it is the main path by which energy enters the house, and it is believed to affect wealth, health, relationships and prosperity. The direction the main door faces, the way it opens, its size and even its decoration are all held to matter for the energy that comes into the home. This guide sets out the traditional teaching on the direction and design of the main entrance door, with practical points for making a harmonious home.

## What is main entrance door Vastu?

It is the systematic use of Vastu Shastra rules for the position, direction, size and decoration of the main entrance of a house. Vastu, the ancient Indian science of building, looks at a building as a living body in which energy flows all the time. In this body the main door is regarded as the mouth, which decides what energies are welcomed in and what stays outside.

In the Vedic tradition each direction has its own deities and planets, and each has features that are linked with different sides of life. It is said that the right position and design of the door keep the house in line with these energies.

## Key aspects of main entrance door Vastu

### Direction and position

In Vastu the direction in which the main door faces is considered the most important point. According to tradition the most auspicious directions for an entrance are these.

1. **North-east (Ishanya)**: considered the most auspicious. A north-east entrance is said to invite spiritual energy, wisdom and clarity of mind, and to support peace, harmony and prosperity in the house.
2. **North**: the ruler of this direction is Kubera, the god of wealth. A north-facing door is believed to be good for prosperity, financial stability and success in a career. It is called especially favourable for those in business and those who want financial progress.
3. **East**: this direction is linked with Surya, the Sun god. An east-facing door is believed to bring health, positivity and new beginnings. Morning sunlight entering through an east door is considered to purify the atmosphere of the house.

The south, the south-west and the west are directions for caution, or for which remedies are advised. In the tradition these are generally linked with financial instability, disagreements and health trouble.

### Direction in which the door opens

How the door opens is also considered equally important.

- **Opening inward**: the door should ideally open inward and not outward. A door that opens inward is a symbol of calling opportunity, wealth and success into the house.
- **Opening clockwise**: the door should open clockwise, from the right-hand side, because this is thought to match the natural flow of positive energy and the movement of the sun. It is believed to support prosperity and warm relationships.
- **Opening anticlockwise**: a door that opens to the left (anticlockwise) is generally not considered right. It is said that it can disturb the flow of energy and bring misunderstandings in the house.

### Design of the threshold

The threshold of the main door is considered important for both practical and symbolic purposes.

- **Height and material**: the threshold should be strong and well sealed, so that water does not come in. It can be made of good wood or stone with a durable material such as metal.
- **Ease of passage**: in modern construction the threshold should reduce the risk of tripping. A height of half an inch or less is good for easy access.
- **A boundary of energy**: in Vastu the threshold is regarded as a boundary that stops negative energy and welcomes positive energy. This is why in many traditions people touch or honour the threshold when entering the house.

## How to decide the right door

Several things have to be looked at to decide the direction and design of the main door.

1. **Assess the direction of the plot**
   - Find true north with a compass.
   - Look at your plot according to the Vastu Purusha Mandala.
   - Identify the ruling planet and deity of each direction of the property.
2. **Position of the door**
   - Measure the wall of the house.
   - Divide each wall into 9 equal parts.
   - The middle third of a wall facing the north-east, north or east is considered the most auspicious for the door.
   - The door should generally not be at a corner of the house.
3. **Size of the door**
   - The main door should be larger than the other doors of the house.
   - The width should be at least 36 inches, more than that of inner doors.
   - The height should also be more than that of inner doors.
4. **Height of the threshold**
   - Measure the height of the inside floor and the height of the outside step or platform.
   - Keep the threshold at half an inch or less.
   - Keep the difference in height as small as possible while keeping a barrier against water.

## What is believed for each direction

In Vastu tradition these effects are described for the direction and design of the door.

**North-east entrance**: it is believed to support spiritual growth, clarity of mind and balanced prosperity. It is called especially good for people in education, spiritual practice and creative work.

**North entrance**: it is considered good for saving wealth, for business and for progress in a career. Attention to spiritual balance is also advised, so that materialism does not take over.

**East entrance**: it is considered favourable for health, vitality and new opportunities. It is linked with new beginnings and success in education, and is called especially good for families with children and older people.

**Opening inward or outward**: a door that opens inward is a symbol of accepting new opportunities and of saving wealth. A door that opens outward is regarded as a sign of energy flowing outward.

**Opening clockwise or anticlockwise**: a door that opens clockwise is considered to support the natural flow of energy, warm relationships and progress. A door that opens anticlockwise is said to bring misunderstanding or disagreement in the house. All of these are beliefs of the tradition and not a promise of any result.

## Practical applications

### When building or improving a home

When building a new home or changing an old one, follow these points for the main door.

1. **Choice of direction**: whenever possible choose the north-east, north or east for the main door.
2. **Size of the door**: make the main door at least 36 inches wide and taller than the other doors of the house, so that energy has plenty of room to flow.
3. **Threshold**: the threshold should be strong, watertight and easy to cross (half an inch or less in height). Natural materials such as wood or stone can be used with the strength of metal.
4. **Space outside the door**: keep an open, well-lit area of at least 5×5 feet outside the main door, so that positive energy can pause there before coming in.
5. **A clear path**: the door should be able to open fully to 90 degrees with nothing in its way.

### Traditional remedies if the direction is not favourable

If the main door is not in the ideal direction, these remedies are described in the tradition.

1. **Use of colours**: paint the door in the colour linked with the planet of its direction.
   - North: green (Mercury)
   - East: wood colour, yellow, gold or light blue (Sun)
   - South: coral red, pink or orange (Mars)
   - West: blue (Saturn)
2. **Auspicious symbols**: put auspicious symbols near the door.
   - Swastika (an ancient symbol of well-being)
   - Om symbol (the primal sound)
   - A nameplate with the family name
   - Toran (a decorative garland over the door)
3. **Plants**: keep plants suited to the direction near the door, which are thought to balance the elements.

## Common misconceptions

**Misconception 1: all doors must open inward.** Vastu generally advises a door that opens inward, but practical reasons such as lack of space or building rules may mean that a door opening outward has to be used.

**Misconception 2: the same rules apply to all entrances.** Different entrances serve different purposes. The Vastu rules are strictest for the main door, while service doors, garage doors and emergency exits may follow modified rules according to their use.

**Misconception 3: traditional rules and modern design cannot go together.** In modern architecture the Vastu rules can be followed without losing beauty or convenience. Keeping the threshold below half an inch for easy access does not clash with Vastu advice.

**Misconception 4: remedies completely remove the fault of an inauspicious door.** Remedies are considered to reduce the effect, not to remove it entirely. In some cases major changes may be needed for a better arrangement.

## Examples

### Example 1: A traditional north-east entrance

A family built their house with a north-east main door and used traditional Vastu elements.

- Door size: 42 inches wide and 8 feet tall.
- Material: solid teak wood with brass fittings.
- Threshold: a low natural stone threshold of 0.3 inches.
- Opening: inward and clockwise.
- Decoration: a traditional nameplate, a brass bell and a toran.

This is an illustration of a traditional arrangement, not a promise of any result.

### Example 2: A modern adaptation for a south-facing entrance

Another family took a house with a south-facing entrance, which the tradition regards as less auspicious. They made these adjustments.

- Door colour: coral red, the colour of Mars for the south direction.
- Threshold: an aluminium threshold with a wooden layer, below half an inch.
- Path to the door: a slight curve in the path as it approaches the door.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu, the direction and design of the main door are considered important for a harmonious home. The north-east, north and east are given as the most auspicious directions for the main door. Besides direction, a door that opens inward and clockwise, a strong but easy threshold and suitable decoration are considered part of a good flow of energy.

Traditional knowledge gives the base, and it can be combined with modern needs and standards of easy access. Whether you are building a new home or improving an old one, these rules, applied with thought, are regarded as helping to make a place that is favourable for happiness at home.

## FAQs

### Which is the best direction for the main door?

The north-east is considered the most auspicious. The north is linked with wealth and career, and the east with health and new beginnings. Caution or remedies are advised for the south, the south-west and the west.

### How should the main door open?

Inward and clockwise. A door that opens to the left or outward is generally not considered right, but if an outward-opening door has to be used for practical reasons, it can be used.

### What size should the main door be?

The door should be wider and taller than the other doors of the house, and at least 36 inches wide. Keep an open, well-lit area of at least 5×5 feet outside.

### Where on the wall should the door be placed?

Divide the wall into 9 equal parts. The middle third of the north-east, north or east wall is considered auspicious. The door should not be at a corner of the house.

### What can I do if the direction of the door is not ideal?

The tradition describes painting the door in the colour linked with its direction, putting up auspicious symbols such as the swastika, Om, a nameplate and a toran, and keeping plants. These are considered to reduce the effect and not to remove it entirely.

### What simple changes can I make to begin with?

Choose the colour of the door by its direction, keep the entrance clean, put good lighting there, add auspicious symbols such as a nameplate or toran, and keep the door in good condition so that it opens and closes easily.
