# Guest Room Vastu: Direction, Door and Decor

## Introduction

Guest room Vastu is the part of Vastu Shastra that tells where the room for visitors should be placed in a house and how it should be arranged. The aim is that the room feels lively, harmonious and welcoming. According to Vastu tradition, these guidelines make the guest room a place that shows the spirit of hospitality and also carries natural energies that bring cheerfulness. Understanding them can help a home owner make the room pleasant for visitors and for the rest of the household.

## What is guest room Vastu?

Guest room Vastu means the ancient Indian guidelines that decide the position and design of the room for visitors in a house. Their aim is comfort in the room, a good atmosphere and warm relations with guests. In Vastu Shastra, the science of building, the right direction of a room and a match with the elements are believed to bring in beneficial energies, and to support peace, prosperity and harmonious dealings in the household.

The position of the guest room, especially the north-west corner, is linked with the air element. Air stands for freshness, movement and temporary stay, and these are the qualities considered most suitable for a room for visitors.

## Key points

### The north-west corner and the air element

- The north-west corner is the place where the north and west directions meet. It is ruled by the air element (Vayu), and it is regarded as the most auspicious place for a guest room.
- Fresh air, sunlight and a cheerful atmosphere are said to stay constant here. Guests feel welcome, but they do not become too attached to the room.
- The air element encourages movement and change. According to the tradition, this is why guests do not become permanent members of the household, and during their stay they are given positivity and clarity of mind.

### Direction of the entrance

- According to Vastu the door of the guest room should face east or north.
- An east door brings in the sunlight of the rising sun and the life energy linked with it. A north door is linked with prosperity and growth.
- With a door in these directions the flow of prana (life energy) stays natural, which keeps the room bright, fresh and balanced.

### Arrangement and decoration of the room

- It is considered right to put the bed in the south-west or south part of the room. The guest's head should point south or east.
- Choose light and calm colours for the walls, such as white, light blue or grey. These are said to add a feeling of comfort and a good mood.
- Keep heavy furniture in the south or west part of the room. Leave the east and north part relatively open, so that energy flows better.
- Windows and open spaces in the north and east walls bring in more sunlight and air. This is considered good for the whole atmosphere of the room.

## How to decide the direction

Take these steps to decide the most suitable place for a guest room.

1. With a compass, find the main directions of the house (north, south, east and west) and the corners in between (north-west, north-east, south-east and south-west).
2. Find the north-west corner, that is the meeting point of the north and west sides. According to Vastu this is the preferred place for a guest room.
3. Put the door of the room on the east or north wall. The south-west corner is kept for the bedroom of the head of the household, so do not put the door of the guest room there.
4. Place furniture and windows according to the Vastu rules, so that air moves well and the room has a bright, open feeling.

## How to read the results

- **A place in the north-west**: this is taken as a sign of temporary stay. It is ideal for guests who should feel welcome but should not stay for an uncertain length of time.
- **A door facing east or north**: this is considered a sign of goodwill, growth and new opportunities. Guests feel fresh and at ease.
- **A place in the south-west or north-east**: this is regarded as against the Vastu rules. According to the tradition, guests may stay longer than needed or the harmony of the house may suffer.

With these points you can judge an existing guest room, or plan a new one that gives a positive atmosphere and keeps relations warm.

## Practical use

### A welcoming atmosphere

The Vastu rules can be used to make a guest room that is cheerful, airy and full of natural light. This helps every guest feel respected and at ease.

### Avoiding negative influences

According to Vastu tradition, choosing the right place is said to reduce the chance of strained relations, guests staying too long and bad effects on the family atmosphere.

## Common misconceptions

- **Misconception:** any room can be a guest room if it is clean.
  - **Reality:** in Vastu the direction and the match with the elements are considered important. They are what keep the balance of energy that Vastu Shastra speaks of.
- **Misconception:** the south-west corner is fine for guests.
  - **Reality:** in Vastu this place is meant for the head of the family. Guests should not be put up there.

## Examples

1. **Example 1:** a family makes its guest room in the north-west corner. The door faces east and the walls are light blue. This is an illustration of a room made according to Vastu.
2. **Example 2:** in another home the guest room is in the south-west, it is full of things and has few windows. According to Vastu tradition, guests in such a room may feel uneasy and stay longer than needed. This is regarded as an imbalance in Vastu.

## Conclusion

Guest room Vastu gives a practical frame for making a pleasant and welcoming place for visitors. When the room is in the north-west corner, the door opens to the east or north, and the arrangement inside matches the natural elements, tradition holds that the home has freshness, comfort and good cheer. Following these guidelines is said to enrich hospitality and the well-being of the household.

## FAQs

### Which is the best direction for a guest room?

According to Vastu the north-west corner is the most auspicious. It is the place of the air element, which is linked with freshness, movement and temporary stay. That is why it is considered suitable for a room for visitors.

### Which way should the door of a guest room face?

The door should face east or north. The east brings morning sunlight and energy, and the north is linked with prosperity and growth. With both, the flow of prana stays natural.

### Where and in which direction should the guest's bed be placed?

Put the bed in the south-west or south part of the room, with the guest's head to the south or east. Keep heavy furniture in the south or west part, so that the east and north part stays open.

### Which colours are good for a guest room?

Light and calm colours such as white, light blue or grey are suggested. They are said to add comfort and a good mood.

### Can a guest room be built in the south-west corner?

In Vastu the south-west corner is meant for the head of the family. According to the tradition, a guest room there may make guests stay longer than needed and may disturb the harmony of the house.

### Where should I begin?

First find the directions with a compass and locate the north-west corner. Then arrange the door, windows and furniture by the rules given above.
