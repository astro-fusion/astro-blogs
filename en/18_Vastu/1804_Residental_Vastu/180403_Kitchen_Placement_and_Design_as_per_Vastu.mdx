# Kitchen Vastu: South-East Placement, Stove and Sink

## Introduction

The kitchen is often called the heart of the home. Food is prepared here and family bonds grow stronger. Vastu Shastra gives the kitchen special importance because it is dominated by the fire element (Agni). Kitchen placement and design in Vastu is not only a matter of looks. It is about keeping the energies of a cooking space in balance for everyone in the household. This ancient science of building gives specific guidelines for the location, layout and organisation of the kitchen, and tradition holds that following them supports the quality of life of those who live in the home.

## What is kitchen Vastu?

Kitchen Vastu means applying the principles of Vastu Shastra, an ancient Indian building science, so that energy flows in a balanced and positive way in the cooking space. Vastu Shastra aims to bring the five elements of nature (earth, water, fire, air and space) into harmony inside living spaces.

Since the kitchen is ruled by the fire element, its correct placement and organisation are considered important for keeping the elements in balance. Vastu gives the ideal direction of the kitchen within the home and also rules for the position of appliances, storage units and sinks, and for the direction the cook faces while preparing meals. These rules are meant to encourage positive energy flow and reduce negative influences.

## Key aspects of kitchen Vastu

### Direction and location

The south-east corner is considered the most auspicious place for the kitchen in Vastu Shastra. The reason is that the south-east is ruled by Agni, the god of fire, so it matches the fire element of the kitchen. Tradition says that this placement helps to control and strengthen the energies of fire, which are linked with digestion, metabolism and health. If the south-east corner is not available, the north-west corner is the second best choice, though good ventilation becomes even more important there to balance the fire element.

The kitchen should never be placed in the north-east corner, because that area belongs to the water element and would clash with the fire element of the kitchen. The south-west corner is also discouraged, because tradition says it can lead to health problems and disharmony in the family.

### Layout and dimensions

For a good flow of energy Vastu suggests particular dimensions for the kitchen. Ideally the kitchen should be rectangular, at about 10×12 feet or 8×10 feet, which leaves enough room to move while cooking. The kitchen should never be irregular in shape, because this is said to upset the balance of energy.

The entrance of the kitchen is equally important. It should be on the east, north or west side, never in the south. The door should also not be placed in any corner of the kitchen. Ventilation is essential, and windows should ideally face east so that morning sunlight and fresh air can enter the cooking space.

### Organising the cooking zone

The cooking zone is the centre of the kitchen, and Vastu gives these directions for it.

1. Place the stove or cooking range in the south-east corner of the kitchen, a few inches away from the wall.
2. The person cooking should face east while preparing meals, because this direction is linked with positive energy and good health. If facing east is not possible, facing north is the next best choice.
3. Build the kitchen platform or counter along the east and south-east walls, so that the work follows a natural order.
4. Electrical appliances that produce heat, such as microwaves and ovens, should also go in the south-east or south part of the kitchen.

### Keeping water and fire apart

One of the most important Vastu principles for kitchen design is to keep the fire and water elements apart.

1. Place the sink and all water-related fittings in the north-east corner of the kitchen, which is the natural position of the water element.
2. Keep enough distance between the sink (water) and the stove (fire), so that the two elements do not clash.
3. Keep water storage, such as drinking water containers, water purifiers and water bottles, in the north-east of the kitchen.

## How to decide the direction and plan the kitchen

Vastu does not involve complicated mathematics, but there is a systematic way to decide the right place and layout of the kitchen in a home.

1. **Identify the directions**: first use a compass to find the main directions (north, east, south and west) and the in-between directions (north-east, south-east, south-west and north-west) of the house.
2. **Look at the available space**: examine the spaces in the home, especially the south-east corner, which is the first choice for the kitchen.
3. **Measure the area**: measure the possible kitchen space. The ratio of length to width should be about 1:1.2 or 1:1.5.
4. **Check for obstructions**: make sure that no structure, such as a pillar or a beam, blocks the flow of energy in the kitchen.
5. **Mark the position of each element**: mark the places inside the kitchen.
   - South-east corner for the stove and cooking range
   - North-east corner for the sink and water storage
   - South and west walls for storage cabinets and the pantry
   - North-west corner for a dining area, if the kitchen has one

This approach helps to place every part of the kitchen according to Vastu.

## Understanding and applying the rules

When you apply the Vastu rules to a kitchen, keep these points in mind.

1. **Give priority to the fire element**: the most important point is to put the stove in the south-east corner. If you can make only one Vastu change in your kitchen, make this one.
2. **Aim for balance, not perfection**: if the structure of the home does not allow ideal positions, keep fire and water balanced by placing them at opposite corners.
3. **Look at the direction the cook faces**: Vastu tradition holds that the direction a cook faces affects the energy of the food. Facing east is considered best.
4. **Think about the flow of work**: a well-planned kitchen lets work move without obstruction from storage to preparation to cooking.
5. **Observe the effects with patience**: according to tradition, a kitchen planned by Vastu supports digestion, the family's finances and harmony in the family. This is a belief of the tradition and not a promise.
6. **Adapt to modern kitchens**: respect the traditional rules, and adapt them thoughtfully to modern designs such as open-plan kitchens and island kitchens.
7. **Colours**: colours play a part in Vastu. Warm colours such as yellow, orange and red strengthen the fire element and suit a south-east kitchen, while black and dark grey are best avoided.

## Practical applications

### Harmony in modern homes

In modern homes, especially flats and ready-built houses, it is hard to change the structure. Even so the Vastu rules for the kitchen can be followed.

- In an open-plan kitchen, mark a boundary between the kitchen and the living area with a different flooring material or a divider.
- If the kitchen cannot be in the south-east corner, balance the fire element with light touches of yellow, orange or red.
- In a kitchen with limited space, put the stove in the south-east part of the available space, even if the whole kitchen is not in the south-east corner of the home.
- Keep good lighting in the east and north-east parts of the kitchen so that the flow of energy continues.

### For health and prosperity

According to tradition, a kitchen planned by Vastu is believed to affect the health and finances of the people of the house.

- Placing the cooking range in the south-east is held to strengthen the fire element, which is linked with wealth.
- Keeping the kitchen clean and organised, especially around the stove, removes blockages and is considered good for health.
- Placing the refrigerator in the south-west corner is advised, so that stability stays in the family.
- Materials such as granite or marble for countertops and ceramic tiles for the floor are considered to add to the steadiness of the earth element.
- Keep good ventilation in the east or north walls, so that fresh air flows and smoke goes out.

## Common misconceptions

Several misunderstandings about kitchen Vastu need clearing up.

1. **Myth: only the south-east is acceptable for a kitchen.** The reality is that the south-east is ideal, but the north-west is also considered suitable. The condition is that the other rules, such as ventilation and the position of appliances, are followed within the available space.
2. **Myth: all the traditional rules must be followed exactly.** Vastu can adapt to modern life. The rules can be applied with thoughtful changes that keep the essence of balance between the elements and meet today's design needs.
3. **Myth: a kitchen that does not follow Vastu certainly brings bad luck.** This is not so. Vastu is an attempt to improve the flow of energy. Awareness and intention in the home also matter, and even partial corrections are considered helpful.
4. **Myth: a kitchen needs big changes to follow Vastu.** Simple steps such as moving the stove, reorganising storage or changing the direction the cook faces can be done without a major renovation.
5. **Myth: kitchen Vastu is only about direction.** Besides position, the choice of colours and materials, cleanliness and the state of mind of those who prepare the food are also part of the atmosphere of the kitchen.

## Examples

### Example 1: A traditional kitchen in the south-east

In a traditional home with the kitchen in the south-east corner, the layout might look like this.

- A rectangular space of 10×12 feet with the entrance on the north wall.
- The cooking range in the south-east corner, with the cook facing east.
- The kitchen counter along the east and south walls.
- The sink and water storage in the north-east corner.
- The refrigerator along the west wall.
- Storage cabinets on the south and west walls.
- Windows on the east wall for natural morning light.
- A warm colour such as yellow or light orange for the walls.
- Ceramic tile flooring in cream or light green.

According to Vastu this arrangement is considered favourable for the flow of energy.

### Example 2: Following Vastu rules in a flat kitchen

In a flat where the kitchen cannot be moved, this approach can be taken.

- The kitchen is in the northern part of the flat.
- The stove is in the south-east part inside the kitchen.
- The cook faces east while preparing food.
- The sink is in the north-east part of the kitchen, at a distance from the stove.
- The refrigerator is along the west wall.
- Light colours are used, with a light touch of warm colour near the cooking area.
- A good ventilation system is in place, with an exhaust fan in the east or north wall.
- Small plants are kept in the east.
- Counters are kept tidy and storage is organised.

This example shows that when the position of the kitchen in the home cannot be changed, the basic Vastu rules can still be followed by arranging the elements inside the available kitchen space.

## Conclusion

Kitchen Vastu is a thoughtful mix of ancient tradition and practical needs. Putting the kitchen in the south-east corner of the home, placing the stove in the south-east part of the kitchen with the cook facing east, and keeping fire and water apart make up the core of the guidance. Tradition holds that such a kitchen supports not only physical nourishment but also harmony in the home.

Vastu reminds us that our living spaces are not only physical structures but also an atmosphere of energy. Modern living sometimes calls for changes to these old rules, but understanding and following even the basic ideas can make this important space in the home work better.

The real value of Vastu comes not from following rules mechanically but from understanding the idea behind them and applying them with awareness. A kitchen is at its best when food is prepared in it with proper technique, a positive mind and care.

## FAQs

### Which is the best direction for the kitchen?

The south-east corner is considered best, because it is the direction of Agni, the god of fire. If that is not available, the north-west corner is the second choice, with good ventilation.

### In which direction should the kitchen not be built?

Not in the north-east, because it is the zone of the water element and clashes with fire. The south-west is also not considered right, because tradition links it with health problems and disharmony.

### Which direction should the cook face?

East, because this direction is linked with positive energy and good health. If east is not possible, face north.

### Where should the sink and water storage go?

In the north-east of the kitchen. Keep enough distance between the sink and the stove, so that the water and fire elements do not clash.

### What can I do if I cannot change the position of the kitchen in a flat?

Inside the kitchen, place the stove in the south-east part and the sink in the north-east part, and face east while cooking. Add light colours, good ventilation and tidy counters.

### What are the ideal size and colours for a kitchen?

A rectangular kitchen of about 10×12 or 8×10 feet. Warm colours such as yellow and orange are considered suitable for a south-east kitchen, while black and dark grey are best avoided.
