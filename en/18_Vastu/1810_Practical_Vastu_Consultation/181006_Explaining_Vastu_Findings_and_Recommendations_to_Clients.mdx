# Explaining Vastu Findings and Advice to Clients

In a Vastu consultation the inspection matters, and so does explaining its findings and suggestions properly to the client. This article looks at how a consultant can present an analysis clearly, which tools help, and which mistakes to avoid.

## What explaining the findings to a client involves

This is the process by which a Vastu consultant passes the analysis and the suggested solutions on to the client. It joins old Vedic principles with modern ways of communicating, so that the client fully understands the guidance and can act on it.

- **The astrological side:** Some consultants relate planetary positions and the flow of energy to the design of the building, according to the client's situation.
- **The Vastu side:** Presenting practical suggestions, fitted to the client, for improving a home or workplace.
- **The spiritual side:** According to tradition, giving the client a sense of connection with cosmic energies.

### Historical context

The roots of this practice are believed to lie in classical texts such as the Brihat Samhita and in early manuscripts of Vastu Shastra. Teachers of old gave importance not only to analysis but also to the art of communicating Vastu knowledge, so that consultation was both a science and a service.

## Why it matters

### The astrological side

Explaining the findings to the client is needed for the following.

- **Matching with the birth chart:** Fitting the design of the space to the person's chart.
- **Advice on timing (muhurta):** Giving an auspicious time for structural changes or a ritual.
- **Understanding the remedies:** Describing the measures that can be taken for doshas or imbalances identified through astrology and Vastu.

### The Vastu side

A good explanation makes sure of the following.

- **Clarity of direction:** The client understands why changes are suggested in particular zones, such as the north-west or the north-east.
- **Balance of energy:** Suggestions are made to improve the flow and to reduce what is thought of as negativity.
- **Choice of materials and colours:** The client learns the reason for each change, which builds trust and cooperation.

## Main principles and how they are applied

### Principle 1: Clarity and fitting the advice to the client

Every client and every property is different. The explanation and the suggestions should follow the client's own needs and hopes.

In practice it goes like this.

- Take the client's own plans and concerns as the base of your advice.
- Present the findings in clear, simple language and with visual aids such as annotated maps.
- Suggest practical measures that need no demolition first, such as moving furniture or changing colours.

### Principle 2: Transparency and visual communication

The client gains most from an explanation in which spoken or written words come with visual aids.

The steps are as follows.

1. Annotate the property maps or floor plans to show the Vastu zones and the suggested changes.
2. In a video call, use an online whiteboard or share your screen, so that things can be shown at the moment.
3. Send by email a Vastu report made for the client, with diagrams, reasons and a summary of the main suggestions.

### Principle 3: Engagement and digital tools

In consultation today, digital platforms make the process easy to reach and interactive.

The suggestions for consultants are these.

- Record the video consultation, for the client to refer to afterwards.
- During the online meeting, encourage the client to ask questions and clear up doubts.
- Use survey and feedback tools for contact and follow-up after the consultation.

## Information needed and the method of analysis

### Information needed

- The client's plan (drawn by an architect or sketched by hand)
- A compass reading or the orientation of the building
- Photographs or a video walk-through of the property
- The client's goals and concerns
- (Optional) Birth details, for an analysis that includes astrology

### Steps of the analysis

1. Mark the main directions and the entry points of the property.
2. Record each room and main area of use against the Vastu zones. For example, tradition links the north with wealth and the east with health.
3. Use modern aids for accurate readings, such as digital maps and compass apps.

## Practical uses in Vastu

- Moving furniture to improve the flow of energy in key zones.
- Suggesting particular colours for walls or decor, in line with the orientation of the property.
- Suggesting where natural elements such as plants or mirrors should be placed.
- Advising on structural changes only when they are really necessary, and explaining both the cost and the benefit clearly.

Annotated maps and visual aids make these changes easy to understand and carry out, and give the client a plan that can be acted upon.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Using hard words or very complex language that leaves the client confused.
- Suggesting costly or impractical renovations when small changes would do.
- Not providing supporting visuals such as an annotated map or diagram.
- Promising too much about results, or making false promises about the outcome of Vastu.
- Not inviting questions or feedback during the consultation.

## Examples and practical tips

### Example 1: A change in a home

A family was struggling with repeated health and money troubles. The inspection found major obstructions to the flow of energy in the south-west and the north-east. Simple colour changes and decluttering were suggested, and the changes were shown on annotated maps and shared in a video call. Tradition links such zones with health and finances, but this is not a diagnosis, and health problems need a doctor.

### Example 2: Use in a business

A team had little cooperation and was losing customers. The inspection found a poor seating order at the workstations and a blocked north zone. A virtual session with a live walk-through of the office was held, a new seating arrangement was introduced and green plants were marked on the annotated office map.

### Practical tips for the consultant

- Check the accuracy of the property plans and the birth details before the consultation.
- Use several methods of analysis together for complete advice, such as directional analysis, energy mapping and astrology.
- Keep a record of the meetings and give the client a summary of the steps to be taken.
- Set expectations honestly and encourage follow-up meetings for continued support.

## Frequently asked questions

### What information should I give for a Vastu consultation?

A floor plan (a hand-drawn one will do), the compass direction, photographs of the property and your own goals or concerns allow a complete assessment.

### Can Vastu remedies be carried out without demolition?

Yes. Many Vastu corrections are small changes in layout, colour or decor and do not need costly renovation.

### How are the findings shared in an online consultation?

You receive a detailed report with annotated maps by email, often together with a video or virtual walk-through. A meeting held live over a video call allows a discussion.

### Is Vastu advice the same for every property?

No. Good Vastu consultants fit their advice to the layout and orientation of each property and to the client's own needs.

### What should I look for in a trustworthy Vastu consultant?

Look for experience, clear communication, practical solutions and openness about suggestions and fees.

## Conclusion

Explaining Vastu findings and suggestions to the client is an art that every consultant needs. By communicating clearly, using annotated maps and making good use of online tools, a consultant can help the client both understand and apply Vastu principles. Openness, advice fitted to the client and ethical practice make each consultation a more useful one.
