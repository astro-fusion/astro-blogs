# Using Modern Technology in Vastu Consultations

The traditional Indian science of Vastu Shastra is today joining with new technology. From virtual consultations to analysis based on artificial intelligence (AI) and augmented reality, technology is making Vastu easier to reach, more precise and more convenient for home owners, businesses and consultants. This digital shift links traditional knowledge with present-day living and offers new ways of arranging a harmonious space.

## Part 1: Remote consultations, expertise without borders

The biggest effect of technology has been online, or remote, consultation. Today consultants serve clients in many countries with the help of several digital tools.

### How it works

- **Virtual communication:** Video call platforms have become the usual means of live conversation between consultant and client.
- **Digital plans:** The plan of a house, drawings of the building and photographs can be sent easily by email or a messaging app.
- **Geospatial technology:** Online maps and other satellite imaging services are used to find the exact geographical orientation of a building, which is an important part of a Vastu analysis. Many people regard this as more accurate than a traditional magnetic compass.

This approach saves time and the cost of travel, and a person can reach different Vastu experts from where they live.

## Part 2: Vastu software and mobile apps, a personal guide

A growing number of software programs and mobile apps give people the chance to work with Vastu themselves.

### Main features of Vastu software and apps

- **Vastu compliance score:** Many apps give a building a Vastu score from an uploaded plan, which shows roughly how close the building is to the principles of Vastu.
- **Digital Vastu compass:** Special compass apps help to find directions correctly. They can also lay an outline of the Vastu Purusha Mandala over a plan, to show the proper place for different rooms and activities.
- **AI-based analysis:** Some advanced apps use artificial intelligence to examine a plan against Vastu principles and give suggestions for improvement.
- **Remedies and suggestions:** These digital tools often suggest remedies that need no demolition, such as placing a particular object, using colours or making a small change in the arrangement of furniture.

## Part 3: The next step, AR, VR and 3D modelling

It is believed that the future of Vastu consultation lies in technologies such as augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR) and 3D modelling.

- **Augmented reality (AR):** AR apps show digital information over the real world. With them you can see proposed changes in your own space at the moment.
- **Virtual reality (VR):** VR creates a fully digital setting. In it one can walk through a virtual form of a building before it is built.
- **3D visualisation and simulation of the flow of energy:** Vastu consultants use 3D modelling software to make detailed views of buildings and to simulate the flow of energy within a space.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an online Vastu consultation as useful as one held on the spot?

With a video call, an online map and clear photographs, an expert can form a good understanding of your building and make suggestions. An online consultation is therefore useful in many cases.

### What are the benefits of Vastu software?

Vastu software gives you a quick and easy way to check the Vastu position of your home. It also suggests remedies and helps you see proposed changes. For a deeper analysis, however, it is best to consult a qualified Vastu expert.

### What does a digital compass app help with?

Such apps help to identify directions and can lay an outline of the Vastu Purusha Mandala over a plan, to show which place suits which room or activity.

### What do I need to send for an online consultation?

Usually the plan of the house, drawings of the building and photographs can be sent by email or a messaging app. The geographical orientation of the building is checked with satellite map services.

### How is technology changing the future of Vastu?

Technology is making Vastu more accessible and convenient. With the help of AI, AR and VR, more new ways of arranging balanced and supportive homes and workplaces are expected.

## Conclusion

These technical aids are making the practice of Vastu easier to reach today. Whether it is a simple video call with a consultant or a VR experience, technology is helping people arrange spaces that are pleasing to the eye and also keep to the principles of harmony.
