# Vastu for Choosing a Plot and Building a New Home

Vastu Shastra, the traditional Indian system of architecture, offers a framework for shaping living spaces around the five elements and the directions. Vastu tradition holds that a home planned in harmony with these is more comfortable for the people who live in it. This article explains how a consultant can help a client choose a new plot or building, check a ready-built property against Vastu, and plan a new construction.

## Part 1: Selecting a New Plot

The choice of plot is the first and most important step in building a home along Vastu lines. These are the main points to consider.

- **Shape and size**: The ideal plot is square or rectangular, because these shapes symbolise stability and balance. Irregular shapes such as triangular, oval or L-shaped plots are believed to disturb the natural flow of energy and are best avoided.
- **Slope and elevation**: A plot that slopes gently towards the north or east is considered auspicious, with the north-east lowest. Plots sloping towards the south or west should be avoided.
- **Direction**: North-facing and east-facing plots are generally preferred, as they are believed to bring a positive and pleasant setting. South-facing and west-facing plots are not bad in themselves, but they need careful planning to reduce possible unfavourable effects.
- **Surroundings**: Vastu gives the surroundings a significant role. A plot with open space, greenery and a peaceful atmosphere is ideal. Avoid plots near high-tension wires, cemeteries, hospitals or other places that people regard as having negative associations.

## Part 2: Evaluating a Ready-Built Property Before Purchase

For a client who is considering an existing property, a careful Vastu evaluation is essential. This checklist guides the process.

- **Main entrance**: The main entrance is the gateway through which energy enters the home. Ideally it is in the north or east. It should be well lit, clean and free of obstructions.
- **Room placement**: Each room has a preferred position:
  - Living room: north-east, east or north.
  - Kitchen: south-east corner.
  - Master bedroom: south-west.
  - Bathroom: north-west.
- **Windows and ventilation**: Ample windows in the north and east allow natural light and ventilation, which are considered important for the flow of energy.
- **Staircase**: A staircase in the south or south-west promotes stability. It should ascend in a clockwise direction.
- **Water features**: Water features such as fountains are best placed in the north-east.

## Part 3: Recommendations for New Construction

When a new home is being built, Vastu principles can be included from the very beginning.

- **Start with a suitable plot**: The foundation of a Vastu-based home is a well-chosen plot. Follow the plot guidelines above.
- **Plan the layout carefully**: Design the house so that each room is in its preferred location.
- **Use natural materials**: Vastu tradition prefers natural materials such as wood and stone. Avoid using large amounts of synthetic material.
- **Include natural elements**: Bring the outdoors in with plants, water features and natural light.
- **Pay attention to colours**: Light and bright colours are associated with a positive atmosphere. Use them to create a sense of harmony in the home.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most important factor when selecting a plot?

The shape of the plot is among the most important factors. A square or rectangular plot is considered ideal because it stands for stability and balance.

### Can a home follow Vastu if the plot is not ideal?

Yes, it can. A qualified Vastu consultant can suggest remedies and adjustments that reduce the effect of an irregular shape or a poorly placed plot.

### Which way should the plot slope?

A gentle slope towards the north or east is considered auspicious, with the north-east as the lowest part. Plots sloping towards the south or west should be avoided.

### What are common Vastu mistakes in new construction?

Placing the kitchen in the north-east, the master bedroom in the south-east and the main entrance in the south or west are the common ones. A staircase in the centre of the house should also be avoided.

### What should I check before buying a ready-built house?

Check the main entrance, the position of each room, windows in the north and east, the staircase and any water feature, using the checklist in Part 2.

## Conclusion

Following these guidelines helps a client end up with a home that is attractive, functional and planned in line with Vastu tradition. Whether the task is choosing a plot or building a new house, a Vastu-based approach is a way of aiming for a space that suits the daily life of the people who live there.
