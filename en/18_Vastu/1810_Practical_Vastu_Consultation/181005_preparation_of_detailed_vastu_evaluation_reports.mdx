# How to Prepare a Detailed Vastu Evaluation Report

A carefully prepared Vastu evaluation report is the foundation of a good consultation. It gives the client a clear road map to a more harmonious space and it also shows the depth of the consultant's work. This article gives a framework for a detailed, professional Vastu report that brings together Astro-Vastu calculations, zone-by-zone analysis, practical remedies and clear supporting diagrams.

## Part 1: The foundation, information gathered before the consultation

Before the analysis begins, it is important to collect full information. This makes the report fit the client and keeps it accurate.

### A. Client and property details

- **Client information:** Full name, contact details and the names of all occupants.
- **Property address:** The complete address and a map location, for accurate directional analysis.
- **Type of property:** Residential (apartment, house), commercial (office, shop, factory) or other.
- **Scope of the consultation:** State clearly which areas are to be evaluated.

### B. Essential documents and data

- **Floor plan:** A floor plan drawn to scale is the most important document. If it is not available, a hand-drawn sketch with accurate measurements is the next best choice.
- **Birth details:** For Astro-Vastu analysis, take the date, time and place of birth of all the main occupants. This allows birth charts to be made, to identify the planetary influences.
- **The client's concerns:** A detailed understanding of the client's problems, whether financial, health-related or in relationships, helps to focus the analysis and the recommendations.

## Part 2: The blueprint, structuring the Vastu report

A well-organised report is easy for the client to understand and follow. The following structure can be used.

1. **Title page:**
    - "Vastu Evaluation Report for the client's name"
    - Property address
    - Date of the report
    - The consultant's or the firm's name and contact details
2. **Introduction and scope of work:**
    - A short introduction to the principles of Vastu Shastra.
    - A clear statement of the scope of the consultation, setting out which parts of the property were assessed.
3. **Property and occupant details:**
    - A summary of the client and property information gathered in the first phase.
4. **Astro-Vastu analysis:**
    - This section combines astrological understanding with Vastu principles for a more personal analysis.
    - **Birth chart analysis:** Include the birth charts (horoscopes) of the main occupants.
    - **Planetary influences:** Explain how the planetary positions in the occupants' charts are thought to relate to the energies of the different Vastu zones of the property.
    - **Personal directions:** On the basis of the charts, identify the directions that are favourable and unfavourable for each person for activities such as sleeping, working and studying.
5. **Zone-by-zone analysis:**
    - This is the main part of the report, in which the findings for each of the 16 Vastu zones are set out in detail.
    - **The Vastu Purusha Mandala:** Begin with a diagram of the Vastu Purusha Mandala laid over the floor plan of the property.
    - For each of the 16 zones, give the following:
        - **Governing element and attributes:** A short mention of the element of the zone and of the aspects of life it is believed to govern.
        - **Current state:** What presently exists in that zone of the property.
        - **Evaluation of Vastu compliance:** Whether the present arrangement follows Vastu principles or not.
        - **Observed effects (doshas):** Where it does not comply, explain the possible negative effects (doshas) as tradition describes them.
6. **Supporting diagrams and visuals:**
    - **Gridded floor plan:** A floor plan of the property with a grid showing the 16 Vastu zones.
    - **Zone-wise bar chart:** A chart showing the level of Vastu compliance of each zone.
    - **Diagrams for the remedies:** Simple diagrams showing where the recommended remedies are to be placed.
7. **Recommended remedies:**
    - **Priority list:** Sort the remedies into "high priority", "medium priority" and "low priority".
    - **Description of each remedy:** For each dosha identified, give a clear and short description of the remedy.
8. **General Vastu guidelines:**
    - A list of general Vastu suggestions the client can follow.
9. **Conclusion and summary:**
    - A short summary of the key findings and the most important recommendations.
10. **Disclaimer:**
    - State that Vastu Shastra is a traditional science and that results may vary.

## Part 3: Delivery and follow-up

- **Form of the report:** Deliver the report in a professional form, such as a PDF or a printed copy.
- **Presenting it to the client:** Walk the client through the report, explain the findings and answer any questions.
- **Support after the consultation:** Offer follow-up support, to learn how the client is progressing and to answer further questions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Astro-Vastu analysis?

It combines astrological understanding with Vastu principles for a more personal analysis. The planetary positions in the occupants' birth charts are considered, to see how they are thought to relate to the energies of the different Vastu zones of the property.

### Why are supporting diagrams important in a Vastu report?

Diagrams such as the gridded floor plan and the zone-wise bar chart make the report clear. They help the client see how closely the property follows Vastu and understand where the recommended remedies are to be placed.

### How should Vastu remedies be put in order of priority?

Remedies are put in order by the severity of the dosha and its impact on the well-being of the occupants. Doshas that are linked with trouble in areas such as health, relationships and finances come first.

### What documents does a consultant need before writing the report?

A floor plan drawn to scale is the most important. If there is none, take a hand-drawn sketch with accurate measurements. Details of the client's concerns are also needed and, if Astro-Vastu analysis is included, the birth details of the main occupants.

## Conclusion

By following these guidelines a consultant can prepare a full, professional and useful Vastu evaluation report that helps the client arrange a space that suits their way of living. A well-structured report is a practical guide for the client and also shows the consultant's care for the client.
