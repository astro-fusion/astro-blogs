# Remote and Online Vastu Consultation Best Practices

Vastu advice can now reach people far away through video calls, digital floor plans and photographs. This article explains what remote Vastu consultation is, how it works in practice, what to collect from the client, which steps to follow and which mistakes to avoid. The methods apply wherever the client lives.

## What Is Remote and Online Vastu Consultation?

Remote and online Vastu consultation is a way for a consultant to advise a client anywhere in the world with the help of technology. It has three parts.

- **Astrological side**: Some consultants also take the residents' birth details into account. This is optional.
- **Digital assessment**: Virtual walkthroughs, digital maps and clear photographs or videos are used to study the space.
- **Easy access**: Traditional Vastu knowledge becomes available to anyone, anywhere, quickly and at lower cost.

### Background

Detailed site visits were once considered essential. Today, video calls, online floor plans and satellite maps make it possible to assess a property from a distance while still following Vastu principles.

## Why Remote Consultation Works

### Birth details and timing

Some consultants combine the residents' birth charts with the analysis of the property to personalise recommendations. Choosing a Muhurta, an auspicious time, for renovation or purchase is also part of the tradition, and remedies can be planned through virtual guidance.

### What a remote consultation can do in Vastu

- **Directional alignment**: Digital maps and compass tools are used to check the orientation of the property.
- **Assessment of zones**: Virtual walkthroughs and photographs allow each zone to be reviewed and Vastu doshas, meaning imbalances in the layout, to be identified.
- **Advice on materials and space**: Suggestions for layout, decor and remedies are made to suit the client's surroundings.

## Key Principles and Applications

### Principle 1: Combining Digital Tools with Vastu

A remote consultation joins digital tools and Vastu knowledge for a complete analysis.

- Collect accurate floor plans, videos and a map location pin from the client.
- Study the layout using satellite images and CAD software for precision.
- Use video calls, email and messages to observe and understand the concerns on site.

### Principle 2: Virtual Walkthrough and Digital Map Sharing

Virtual walkthroughs and shared digital maps are central to accurate spatial analysis from afar. The steps are:

1. Obtain high-quality scans or digital copies of the floor plans.
2. Ask for a panoramic video or a live virtual tour of the property.
3. Use a map or satellite tool to check the orientation and the surroundings.
4. Overlay the Vastu grid, the Vastu Purusha Mandala, on the digital layout to identify the different zones.

### Principle 3: Remote Assessment of the Zones

Photographs, client feedback and, if needed, birth details are used to assess the zones and plan remedies.

- Take the residents' birth details when personal advice is wanted.
- Digital charts, diagrams of the zones or calculators can be used to judge the strength of each zone.
- Keep written notes of your observations and suggest simple remedies that need no demolition, such as changing colours, moving mirrors or balancing the elements.

## The Remote Assessment Process

### Information required

- Accurate scaled floor plans or digital blueprints
- A map pin or satellite location
- Photographs or video walkthroughs of all rooms and the main entrances
- Birth details of the residents (optional, for personal advice)

### Step by step

1. Overlay the property plan with the Vastu grid digitally, aligning the cardinal directions.
2. Review the purpose, position and current use of each zone against Vastu principles.
3. Identify imbalances using the photographs and digital survey tools.
4. Score or chart the zones with remote tools, for example bar charts.
5. Prepare the Vastu report with findings, remedial actions and supporting visuals.

## Practical Applications

Remote and online consultation lets you do the following.

- Choose a better property by reviewing digital layouts before purchase or lease.
- Assess a home or commercial space against Vastu from a distance.
- Receive a detailed report with colour-coded floor plans, remedy charts and personal recommendations.
- Carry out corrections with regular virtual follow-ups.

### Examples of digital deliverables

- A Vastu report with marked-up layouts
- An analysis of the zones using satellite and CAD mapping
- A video consultation summary with step-by-step instructions for corrections

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Relying on incomplete or inaccurate floor plans or site images.
- Ignoring exact directional alignment. Always verify it with a compass or satellite tool.
- Skipping a detailed discussion of the client's lifestyle, concerns or type of business.
- Overlooking the birth details when the advice is based on them.
- Not confirming through follow-up calls or photographs that the remedies were carried out.

## Examples and Practical Tips

### Illustration 1: A Residential Case

A family living abroad asked for a remote consultation about their home. The virtual inspection pointed to doshas in the north-east and south-west zones. Colour changes, careful placement of mirrors and changes in the kitchen were suggested on detailed digital maps, and virtual follow-up conversations helped the family put them into practice.

### Illustration 2: An Office Case

A start-up with team members in several countries had a video walkthrough of its office. The consultant noted that the layout did not suit the founder's astrological profile. The workspace was reconfigured, and digital corrections such as colour coding of the zones and placing plants were suggested.

### Practical tips for consultants

- Ask for clear, current floor plans and images.
- Use a live video tour wherever possible to settle any doubts.
- Give the client a clear, practical Vastu guide with visuals.
- Schedule virtual follow-ups to confirm that the remedies were adopted and to answer questions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can Vastu be analysed accurately without a site visit?

With clear digital maps, videos and a location pin, a remote consultation can give an analysis comparable to an on-site one in most cases. The quality depends on the accuracy of the plans and photographs.

### What types of property can be assessed online?

Homes, offices, shops and factories, whether owned or rented, can be assessed remotely.

### What remedies are suggested when structural changes are not possible?

Remedies that need no demolition are often suggested, such as choosing colours, moving objects, adding elements and rearranging rooms.

### What information should I have ready for an online consultation?

A scaled floor plan, a map pin, and photographs or videos of all rooms and the main entrances. The residents' birth details are optional.

### Why is follow-up important?

Follow-up calls or photographs show whether the remedies were actually carried out, and they give you a chance to ask questions.

## Conclusion

Virtual walkthroughs, digital maps and remote assessment have made traditional Vastu knowledge accessible to many more people. Guidance that was once tied to distance is now a video call away. These practices offer a clear, organised way to review a home or workplace against Vastu tradition, wherever the client is.
