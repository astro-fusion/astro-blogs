# Checking and Maintaining Your Home's Vastu Yourself

Checking and maintaining the Vastu of your own home or workplace is a simple, self-guided practice. This article explains what a do-it-yourself audit is, why it is considered worthwhile, how to carry it out with a few checklists and what mistakes to avoid. It also notes when to call a consultant.

## What a DIY Vastu Audit and Maintenance Is

It is a self-guided way of reviewing and maintaining the Vastu of your home or workspace. It has three parts.

- **Astrological side**: Using knowledge of planetary influences to make the home environment favourable.
- **Vastu application**: Simple routines, checklists and cleaning habits for harmony.
- **Spiritual side**: Keeping a positive atmosphere through mindful upkeep.

### Background

The idea of regular audits and upkeep is said to go back to classical texts such as the Brihat Samhita and the architectural tradition of Vastu Shastra, which stress keeping spaces orderly and pure.

## Why the Practice Matters

### The astrological side

- **Personal wellbeing**: Tradition holds that keeping the physical surroundings in step with favourable planetary energies is helpful.
- **Seasonal rituals**: There is a tradition of timing deep cleaning, rearranging rooms or updating decor to auspicious planetary periods.
- **Remedies**: Simple, mindful actions are believed to guard against unfavourable influences.

### The Vastu side

- **Directional harmony**: Entrances, rooms and furniture should follow the directions that Vastu recommends.
- **Flow of energy**: Cleanliness and less clutter keep prana, the life energy, from stagnating.
- **Overall upkeep**: Periodic self-checks and maintenance routines keep the household environment pleasant.

## Key Principles

### Principle 1: Making the Self-Audit a Routine

Making self-audit and maintenance an ongoing practice keeps the space in order.

- Set a regular time for cleaning the entrance, twice a week if possible.
- Make a checklist for the key areas: entrance, living room, bedrooms, kitchen and bathrooms.
- Use natural cleaners such as salt, lemon or vinegar for purification.
- Check the directional alignment and the placement of furniture against Vastu.

### Principle 2: Clutter Management

Vastu tradition holds that clutter disturbs both the flow of energy and the auspiciousness of a home.

1. Remove unnecessary items from entrances, hallways and living areas.
2. Set aside one day each month for decluttering.
3. Use storage that keeps surfaces tidy and orderly.
4. After decluttering, clean the space and refresh it.

### Principle 3: Periodic Review of Room Use and Decor

Room use and decor should change with the household's needs and with Vastu guidelines.

- Every quarter, check that each room is used in line with Vastu, for example bedrooms in the south-west and the pooja room in the north-east.
- Keep the decor calm. Choose peaceful landscapes and avoid heavy or dark artwork in the north and east.
- Change colour schemes as needed. Light colours are preferred for peace.
- Place electronics and heavy furniture so that they do not block pathways.

## How to Carry Out the Audit

### Information required

- The direction the home faces (check with a compass app)
- A list of rooms and their main uses
- A list of decor items and electronics

### Steps

1. **Entrance audit**: Is the main door in the north, east or north-east? Is it free of clutter, dirt and obstacles? Is there a threshold and a clean doormat?
2. **Clutter count**: In each room, count the visible items that have no practical or decorative purpose. The fewer, the better.
3. **Flow check**: Walk through the home and note any blocked pathways, dark corners or poorly ventilated areas.
4. **Decor audit**: List the wall art and decorative objects. Are they in the recommended directions? Are the colours light and harmonious?

Score each area regularly and set an improvement goal for the next audit.

## Practical Checklists

### Entrance cleaning checklist

- Sweep and mop the area near the main door every other day.
- Wipe the door and the threshold with salt water or lemon water every week.
- Keep footwear, rubbish bins and unused items away from the entrance.
- Keep the doormat clean and replace it from time to time.
- Add auspicious symbols or plants according to Vastu.

### Decluttering routine

- Hold one decluttering session each month for every major room.
- Remove broken objects, expired items and anything unused for six months.
- Use baskets or cabinets to keep surfaces clear.
- Donate or repurpose items that have sentimental value but no daily use.

### Review of room use and decor

- Every quarter, check whether room use matches the Vastu zones. For example, avoid placing a bedroom or kitchen in the north-east.
- Refresh wall art and decor with soft nature scenes and calm colours.
- Let in natural light and fresh air by cleaning window panes and opening windows.
- Make sure electronics and heavy items do not block the main pathways.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Neglecting the entrance and letting shoes or clutter pile up there.
- Using harsh chemicals or skipping regular cleaning. Tradition holds that this brings unfavourable energy.
- Ignoring regular audits. Vastu is not something to do once and forget.
- Filling the space with heavy or dark decor, especially in the north and east.
- Placing a mirror directly opposite the entrance or leaving items on the threshold.

## Examples and Practical Tips

### Illustration 1: A Home Entrance

A family's entrance was cluttered with shoes, old newspapers and a worn doormat. The audit led to a cleared entryway, a weekly salt-water cleaning routine and a bamboo plant.

### Illustration 2: Decluttering a Home Office

A person working from home felt stuck and stressed. The audit found the north-east overloaded with files and electronics. The files were moved to the south-west, gadgets were removed from the centre and a nature painting was hung in the north-east.

### Practical tips

- Audit with fresh eyes and involve household members for better results.
- Use natural cleaning materials to strengthen the purifying effect.
- Record each session and track improvements or new problems.
- Adjust room use and decor as the family's or business's needs change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I carry out a DIY Vastu audit?

Monthly is a good rhythm for decluttering, while entrance cleaning and general tidiness should happen at least twice a week.

### Which natural cleaners are used in Vastu?

Salt water, lemon juice or diluted vinegar are used to wipe surfaces. They are believed to purify both physically and energetically.

### How should I keep decor in line with Vastu?

Choose calm landscapes for wall art, keep colours light and harmonious, and avoid loading the north and east walls with heavy or dark items.

### Can I audit my own home, or do I need an expert?

An audit with the simple checklists above is enough for regular upkeep. For complex problems or structural changes, consult a Vastu expert.

### Why is the entrance cleaned so carefully?

Tradition regards the entrance as the way energy enters the home, so it is kept clean and free of obstruction.

## Conclusion

By checking and maintaining your own space, you take an active part in keeping it balanced and lively. Regular entrance cleaning, less clutter and periodic reviews of decor and room use follow the long-standing principles of Vastu tradition, and each of them is a routine you can carry out yourself.
