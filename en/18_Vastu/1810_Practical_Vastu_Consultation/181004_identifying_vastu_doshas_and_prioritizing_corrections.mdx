# Identifying Vastu Doshas and Ordering the Corrections

Vastu Shastra is the traditional Indian science of building. It aims at harmony between people and their surroundings by balancing the five elements: earth, water, fire, air and space. When the design of a building is said to disturb the natural flow of these energies, the result, according to tradition, is a Vastu dosha, or defect, believed to affect the health, relationships and prosperity of the occupants. Detecting these doshas and setting their order is an important part of a consultation. This article gives an approach to identifying Vastu defects and putting them in order for correction.

## Part 1: Detecting Vastu defects

Vastu defects can be identified in three ways: visual cues, talks with the occupants, and energy mapping.

### Visual cues

A visual inspection of a property can show several common Vastu doshas.

- **Irregular shapes and layouts:** A property of irregular shape, such as an L-shaped plot or one with cut-off corners, is believed to have an uneven distribution of energy. Square or rectangular shapes are considered ideal for the flow of energy.
- **Rooms in the wrong place:** The position of key areas such as the kitchen, bedroom and bathroom matters greatly in Vastu. For example, the north-east is the zone associated with water, and a kitchen there is thought to clash with the fire element of cooking. Tradition links this to trouble in health and finances.
- **Blocked entrances and windows:** Obstructions at the main entrance or blocked windows are thought to hold back fresh air and the flow of positive energy, so that energy is regarded as stagnant.
- **Clutter and dirt:** A cluttered and unclean space, especially in the north-east corner, is considered to block positive energy.
- **Structural problems:** Cracks in walls, leaking taps or sagging floors can point to deeper problems in the structure. Tradition also links them with negative energy.

### Talks with the occupants

Talking with the residents can give useful clues about possible Vastu doshas. Tradition takes the following as common signs of imbalance. They are not a diagnosis.

- **Recurring health problems:** Frequent or long illnesses among family members. Tradition links certain health problems with imbalance in particular directions. Illness needs a doctor, whatever the Vastu of the house.
- **Financial instability:** Persistent money trouble, such as debt, loss of money or an unsteady income.
- **Conflicts in relationships:** Frequent quarrels, misunderstandings and a lack of harmony in the family.
- **Career stagnation:** Difficulty in advancing in a career, loss of a job or repeated setbacks in business.
- **A general feeling of negativity:** Restlessness, negativity or a lack of peace in the home.

### Energy mapping

Energy mapping is a more advanced method used by Vastu experts to identify and analyse the flow of energy in a space. It may involve the following.

- **Directional analysis:** Using a compass to find the exact orientation of the property and the position of the rooms in relation to the main directions.
- **Vastu Shakti Chakra:** This diagram is used to divide a site plan into different energy zones. It helps to identify the attributes of the 45 deities of the Vastu Purusha Mandala and to balance the five elements.

## Part 2: Setting the order of Vastu issues

Once the Vastu doshas have been identified, they should be put in order according to their severity and their impact on the well-being of the occupants.

### Severity of the dosha

Tradition considers some Vastu defects more serious than others. For example:

- **North-east defects:** The north-east is regarded as a highly sacred and energetic zone. A toilet or heavy storage in this area is considered a major Vastu dosha.
- **South-west defects:** The south-west is regarded as the zone of stability and relationships. A defect here is linked with insecurity and instability in life.
- **The main entrance:** The main entrance is the principal point of entry of energy. A door facing a direction considered inauspicious is regarded in tradition as a major defect.

### Impact on the occupants

The effect of a Vastu dosha depends on the area of life it is linked with.

- **Health:** Defects linked with health should be given high priority, especially when there is a long or serious illness in the family. Medical care comes first in such a case.
- **Relationships:** If a dosha is thought to be behind serious tension and disharmony in relationships, it should be looked at early, for peace and emotional balance.
- **Prosperity:** Defects linked with financial instability or an obstacle in work are also given priority.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the most severe Vastu dosha?

In Vastu tradition a toilet or heavy storage in the north-east is counted among the most severe doshas. It is believed to be linked with a wide range of problems, including trouble in health, finances and relationships.

### How can I tell if my home has Vastu doshas?

You can look for visual cues such as irregular shapes, rooms in the wrong place and clutter. You can also notice problems that keep recurring in your life, such as health trouble, money struggles or conflicts in relationships. Tradition treats these only as pointers, not as a diagnosis.

### Should I call a Vastu consultant to identify doshas?

You can identify some basic doshas yourself. A professional Vastu consultant can still carry out a deeper analysis using methods such as energy mapping, and can suggest remedies suited to your situation.

### Which doshas should be corrected first?

Look first at the defects regarded as more severe, such as those of the north-east, the south-west or the main entrance, and at those that appear to affect health, relationships or financial stability.

## Conclusion

By identifying Vastu doshas in an orderly way and setting their order, remedies can be applied, which tradition regards as the way to restore balance and create a harmonious living environment. You can carry out a basic audit yourself or hire a professional. In either case, attention to the defects is the first step towards a home that suits the way you live.
