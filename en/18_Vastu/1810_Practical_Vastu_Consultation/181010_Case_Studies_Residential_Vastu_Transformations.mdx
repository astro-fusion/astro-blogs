# Residential Vastu Examples and Practical Lessons

This article explains how a Vastu consultation actually works in a home. It shows how a consultation begins, how the doshas of a house are found, how they are corrected, and what can be learned from three examples.

## What residential Vastu corrections are

Residential Vastu correction means putting the principles of Vastu into practice in a home. First the present difficulties of the home are understood, then corrective measures are carried out, and afterwards the household records what it has noticed.

- **The importance of directions:** According to Vastu tradition, directions influence the atmosphere of a home.
- **Practical Vastu measures:** Easy solutions for bringing the layout of a house in line with the principles of Vastu.
- **The family's own experience:** Recording changes in health, finances and relationships after the measures.

### Historical background

The roots of this subject are believed to lie in ancient texts, in which teachers such as Varahamihira discussed harmony between a building and its surroundings. Over the centuries these principles have been used in the Indian tradition of building.

### Main points in the use of Vastu

According to tradition, the following are looked at in residential Vastu.

- **Agreement of direction:** Having rooms in the right direction, for example the bedroom in the south-west and the kitchen in the south-east, is considered to bring stability to the home.
- **Flow of energy:** Thoughtful placing and correction of rooms, colours and the entrance is considered to balance the atmosphere of the home.
- **Choice of materials and colours:** The colours given for each direction are used in line with Vastu advice.

## Basic principles of the corrections

### Principle 1: First assess the present state of the house

Every correction starts with a close inspection of the present state of the house. It is seen how far the house follows Vastu.

In practice:

- The facing of the house, the position of the rooms and the flow of energy are examined.
- The occupants' first difficulties are recorded, such as health, relationships or shortage of money.
- The zones linked with these difficulties are marked on the plan of the house.

### Principle 2: Identifying and correcting the Vastu dosha

Identifying and correcting a Vastu dosha, that is an imbalance, is regarded as necessary to restore balance. The steps are these:

1. Fix the main directions and the different zones with the help of a compass and the plan.
2. Identify the doshas, such as a kitchen in the wrong direction, an obstructed entrance or a room put to the wrong use.
3. Carry out targeted measures, such as swapping rooms or giving them a new use, moving furniture, changing colours or placing Vastu symbols.

### Principle 3: Keeping a record of the changes

To see how useful the changes were, it is good to keep a record of the position before and after.

- Note things such as health, income and spending, and harmony in the home, before and after the measures.
- On the basis of the experience gained, keep improving the measures, so that the atmosphere of the home stays better over the long term.

## How the analysis is done

First the necessary information is collected:

- The plan of the house, showing the directions and the main rooms.
- A description of the difficulties the family is facing.

After that the following steps are taken:

1. Fix magnetic north and align the plan of the house with it.
2. Identify the zones of north, south, east and west and of their corners.
3. Examine each main part (bedroom, kitchen, bathroom and so on) for how far it follows Vastu.
4. Fix the doshas on the basis of direction and the use of the room.

For example, if a family has a continuing health problem, the consultant examines the south-east zone and the position of the master bedroom. Tradition links the south-east with fire, but this is not a diagnosis, and health problems need a doctor.

## Practical suggestions

A few main points come out of these principles.

- **Direction of the bedroom:** For stability, it is considered better to keep the bedroom in the south-west.
- **Position of the kitchen:** The kitchen should be in the south-east. If it is not, measures such as colours can be used.
- **The entrance:** The main entrance should be clear, well lit and in keeping with an auspicious direction.
- **Choice of colours:** The colours Vastu gives for each zone are adopted, such as green in the east and yellow in the fire zone.

## Common mistakes

- Making structural changes and not thinking of measures that need no demolition.
- Overlooking small corrections such as the position of windows, mirrors and unnecessary things.
- Putting up many artificial remedies, such as yantras, without solving the real problem of the layout.
- Paying attention only to decoration and forgetting the flow of direction.

## Examples

The three examples below are given as plain illustrations. They state no results and make no promise.

### Example 1: Health and relationships

**Situation:** A family, despite a comfortable life, was worried about health and unstable finances.

**Analysis:** The Vastu inspection found some doshas, such as the master bedroom being in the wrong direction and things piling up in the northern part of the house.

**Measures taken:**

- The bedroom was moved to the south-west.
- The northern part of the house was cleared and put in order.
- A suitable colour scheme was adopted.

### Example 2: A long-standing physical complaint

**Situation:** A person had long-standing pain in the back and knees that medical treatment had not settled.

**Analysis:** The master bedroom was found in a direction regarded as inauspicious, and the direction of the bed was also not right.

**Measures taken:**

- The direction of sleeping was changed and the bedroom was moved to another place.
- The affected zone was corrected according to Vastu.

Vastu measures are not a treatment. Medical advice should continue alongside them.

### Example 3: Family harmony and growth

**Situation:** A couple had wished for a child for several years.

**Analysis:** Imbalance was found in the south-west and north-west parts of the house, which was regarded as an obstacle to the growth of the family.

**Measures taken:**

- The use of rooms was changed to suit Vastu.
- The flow of energy was improved in the south-west and north-west.

A couple with such a concern should also seek medical advice.

### Practical tips for consultants

- Always work with an accurate plan of the house.
- Where possible, first use measures that need no demolition.
- Record the first position and the later changes, so that the effect can be judged.
- Change the approach when a new challenge or feedback comes in.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Vastu be useful in a rented home?

Yes. Many Vastu measures can be carried out without a change to the structure, so they can be used in a rented house too.

### How long after Vastu corrections can a change be seen?

Vastu tradition gives no fixed time. It differs from home to home, and the examples here state no results.

### Do I have to change the structure of my house to see any benefit?

Not always. In many corrections it is thought enough to give rooms a new use, change furniture, or add colours and symbols.

### In which direction is the bedroom better kept?

According to tradition, it is good to keep the bedroom in the south-west, for stability.

### What can be done if the kitchen is in the wrong direction?

The right place for the kitchen is regarded as the south-east. If it is not there, easy measures such as colours can be used.

## Conclusion

These examples show how much emphasis tradition places on bringing a home in line with Vastu. Where there is concern about health, finances or relationships, thoughtful corrections chosen to suit the needs of the home are the way tradition follows. They remain practices of tradition, and for illness a doctor should be consulted.
