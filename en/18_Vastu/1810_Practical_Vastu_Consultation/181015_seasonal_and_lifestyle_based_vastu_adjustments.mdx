# Seasonal and Lifestyle-Based Vastu Adjustments

Adapting a space to the changing seasons and to the natural changes in a family's circumstances is considered important for keeping it comfortable and balanced. Vastu is not a fixed list of rules. Vastu tradition holds that, when it is applied thoughtfully, it can support a home through every stage of life.

## Part 1: Adapting to the Seasons

Each season has its own character, and small changes to the home let you move with the rhythm of nature.

### Spring: Renewal and Growth

- **Declutter**: Spring is a good time for a thorough clear-out, removing stagnant items and making room for a fresh start.
- **Fresh air and light**: Open the windows so that fresh air circulates and the space feels clean.
- **Introduce greenery**: Bring in new plants, which symbolise growth and new beginnings.
- **Lighter colours**: Swap heavy fabrics for lighter ones in pastel shades to give a sense of openness.

### Summer: Vibrancy and Activity

- **Cooling colours**: Shades of blue, green and white create a calm, cool atmosphere.
- **Water elements**: A small fountain or an aquarium in the north-east is said, in Vastu tradition, to bring tranquillity and to balance the fire element of summer.
- **Natural light**: Allow as much natural light as possible, but soften harsh sunlight with sheer curtains.

### Autumn: Harvest and Consolidation

- **Warm and earthy tones**: Deep browns, oranges and yellows reflect the colours of the season and give a grounded feeling.
- **Cosy textures**: Wool blankets, velvet cushions and rustic wood accents make the room warm and inviting.
- **Harvest symbols**: A bowl of fruit or grain is a traditional symbol of abundance.

### Winter: Rest and Introspection

- **Warm lighting**: Candles, string lights and soft lamps give a cosy atmosphere.
- **Layered textures**: Throws, plush rugs and knitted cushions add warmth and comfort.
- **Preserve warmth**: Heavier curtains keep the warmth in and give a sense of shelter.

## Part 2: Adjusting for Changes in the Family

As the family changes, the home should adapt to the new situation.

- **Newly married couple**: The master bedroom is ideally in the south-west corner, which Vastu tradition associates with stability and a strong bond.
- **Arrival of a child**: Use soothing, calm colours in the child's room and keep the centre of the room open and free of clutter.
- **Growing children**: A study or work area in the west or south-west part of the house is considered suitable.
- **Children leaving home**: Repurpose rooms to suit the current lifestyle and refresh the home.

## Part 3: Vastu for a Business Through Its Stages

Vastu principles can be applied to a workplace as well, to keep it steady and well arranged.

- **Starting a new business**: The entrance to the office or shop is ideally in the north, north-east or east, which tradition regards as auspicious.
- **Business growth and expansion**: The sales and marketing department is traditionally placed in the north-west, to support contact with customers.
- **Facing business challenges**: Declutter the workspace regularly and keep the north zone tidy. Tradition links the north with career and financial opportunity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why make seasonal Vastu adjustments?

Each season has its own character. Small changes let you keep the home in step with the rhythm of nature and keep the space comfortable through the year.

### How can I adapt my home as my family changes?

As the family changes, the home should be adjusted to its new needs. This can mean repurposing rooms, changing colour schemes and moving furniture.

### Where should a newly married couple's bedroom be?

Vastu tradition places the master bedroom in the south-west corner, associated with stability and a strong bond.

### What are some simple Vastu tips for a home office?

Place the desk so that you face east or north, and keep it clean and organised. Keep a clear separation between living space and working space so that work and personal life stay balanced.

### Where should the entrance of a new shop or office be?

In the north, north-east or east, which tradition regards as auspicious.

## Conclusion

By reviewing your space from time to time and making these adjustments, you keep your home and workplace comfortable and well ordered through every season and every change in life, in line with Vastu tradition.
