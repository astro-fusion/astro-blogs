# Commercial and Office Vastu: Three Case Illustrations

Vastu Shastra, the traditional Indian system of architecture, is not limited to homes. Its principles are also applied to shops, offices and other workplaces, so that the layout of the space follows the five elements and the directions. This article walks through three illustrations of a business or office consultation. Each one describes the starting situation, what a Vastu audit noted and the changes that were agreed. They are plain illustrations of how a consultation proceeds, not records of results, and nothing here promises growth or profit.

## Case Study 1: A Struggling IT Firm

A mid-sized IT consultancy in Mumbai was facing several difficulties. Revenue was declining, employees were leaving often, staff motivation was low and decisions were delayed.

### What the Vastu audit noted

A Vastu consultant identified several "doshas", meaning imbalances in the layout:

- The main entrance was in the south-west, a direction that Vastu tradition associates with instability.
- The owner's cabin was in the north-east, which the tradition regards as weak for leadership.
- The finance department was in the north-west, a placement that the tradition links with unsteady finances.
- The workspace was cluttered and poorly lit.

### Changes agreed

- The main entrance was decorated with Vastu-friendly symbols and better lighting.
- The owner's cabin was moved to the south-west corner, with the desk facing north, because the tradition associates that corner with authority and firm decisions.
- The finance team was relocated to the north.
- The office was decluttered and brighter lighting was installed.
- Employees' seating was rearranged so that they face east or north, the directions traditionally linked with focus.

## Case Study 2: A Software Development Company

A software development company had problems with collaboration between teams and with productivity. These were affecting project schedules and the generation of new ideas.

### Changes agreed

The office layout was rearranged according to Vastu principles. Workstations were repositioned so that communication between team members flowed more easily, and furniture was placed in more favourable positions.

## Case Study 3: A Stagnant Tech Startup

A young tech entrepreneur in Mumbai felt that his business had stalled despite good ideas and a modern office. The team was demotivated and the office felt heavy and uninspiring.

### What the Vastu audit noted

- A cluttered entrance was blocking the flow of energy into the office.
- The seating arrangement did not support clear communication.
- The placement of electrical equipment was seen as creating an imbalance.

### Changes agreed

- The entrance was cleared of clutter and plants were placed there.
- Workstations were rearranged to support collaboration.
- The conference room was moved to a more auspicious location.
- The wall colours were changed to suggest calm and creativity.

## What These Illustrations Show

In all three cases the consultation followed the same path: understand the situation, audit the layout against the directions, and agree on practical changes that the client can carry out. Decluttering, good lighting, a sensible seating plan and a clear entrance are the changes that appear in every illustration. A consultant should present the reasoning as Vastu tradition and should not promise any particular business result.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the most common Vastu doshas in an office?

The common ones are a poorly placed entrance, an unsuitable seating arrangement for the owner and the employees, and a cluttered or poorly lit workspace.

### Can Vastu improve business growth?

Vastu tradition holds that a balanced, harmonious workspace supports the people who work in it, but there is no scientific proof of a business result. A consultation should be treated as guidance on layout, and growth depends on how the business is run.

### What simple changes does an office consultation usually suggest?

Decluttering the space, ensuring proper lighting and rearranging furniture so that movement and communication flow better. Placing plants and choosing calm wall colours also appear in the illustrations above.

### Where does Vastu place the owner's cabin?

The illustrations place it in the south-west corner, with the desk facing north, since the tradition associates that corner with authority. The north-east is regarded as unsuitable for it.

### Which way should employees face while working?

In the first illustration, staff were seated facing east or north, the directions traditionally linked with focus.

## Conclusion

Commercial Vastu applies the same principles of elements and directions that are used for homes, adapted to entrances, owner's cabins, finance desks, seating and meeting rooms. The illustrations show how a consultant moves from findings to agreed changes. The value of a consultation lies in a clear, practical plan, presented honestly as tradition and without promises about results.
