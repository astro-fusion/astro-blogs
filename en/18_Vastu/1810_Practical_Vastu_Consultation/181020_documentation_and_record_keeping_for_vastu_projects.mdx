# Documentation and Record-Keeping for Vastu Projects

A systematic approach to record-keeping helps a Vastu consultant keep projects continuous, deliver good service and build lasting relationships with clients. By keeping organised records of consultations, reports, follow-up notes and all communication with the client, a practitioner works more smoothly, is protected against possible disputes and builds a professional reputation.

## Core Principles of Effective Record Management

A sound record-keeping system rests on a few basic principles. All records should be kept in one secure place, digital or paper, so that they are easy to find and information is not lost. Security is equally important. A consultant must keep client data confidential so that trust is maintained. Records should also be easy for authorised people to reach, so that work runs smoothly and follow-ups happen on time.

## A Step-by-Step Guide to Organising Your Vastu Practice

### 1. Initial Client Intake

From the first contact, a structured way of collecting client information is essential. Use a standard client intake form that records the client's contact details, the property details and the appointment details.

### 2. The Consultation

Careful note-taking matters during an on-site or virtual consultation. A standard "site analysis" template is very useful. It should have sections for the layout of the property, the directional analysis, Vastu observations, and the client's concerns and questions.

### 3. The Vastu Report

After the consultation, prepare a comprehensive Vastu report. The report is the formal record of your findings and recommendations. A professional report includes the client and property details, a Vastu evaluation, the recommended remedies and a floor plan with markings.

### 4. Communication Log

Keep a record, in date order, of all communication with the client. The log should show the date and time of each contact, the mode of communication, a summary of the discussion and any follow-up action.

### 5. Follow-Up and Project Tracking

For projects in progress, a system for tracking progress is essential. It can be a simple project timeline document or a more advanced project management tool. It makes it easier to monitor implementation, schedule follow-up visits and record results.

## Digital Tools for the Modern Consultant

Technology can make record-keeping much easier. Some options are:

- **Simple and free**: For those starting out, a word processor, a spreadsheet and a calendar used together can be effective.
- **Project management tools**: A shared project board gives more advanced features for managing tasks and projects.
- **Client database software**: For an established practice, a client database can serve as one central place for all client information, interactions and project details.

## Legal and Professional Considerations

Keeping accurate, current records is a professional responsibility. In the event of a dispute or complaint, the records can be very valuable. Client files should be kept safely for a considerable time after a project is closed. How long depends on the rules where you practise, so check local rules.
