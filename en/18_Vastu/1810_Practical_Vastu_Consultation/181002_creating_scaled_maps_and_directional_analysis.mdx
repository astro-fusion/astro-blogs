# Scaled Maps and Directional Analysis for Vastu Work

In the practice of Vastu Shastra, precision matters most. A careful Vastu analysis relies on a floor plan drawn to scale, directions marked correctly, and the right identification of the entrance segments. These are the base on which the recommendations of a consultation rest. This article gives a step-by-step approach to creating scaled maps and carrying out directional analysis, so that a Vastu audit can be thorough.

## Part 1: Drawing an accurate scaled floor plan

A scaled floor plan is a drawing of the property in proportion. For an accurate Vastu analysis, all the dimensions in the drawing must be in the correct relation to one another.

### Essential tools

- **Measuring tape:** For accurate measurements of walls and spaces.
- **Graph paper and a ruler:** For drawing the floor plan by hand.
- **Drafting software:** For a digital and more precise floor plan.

### Step-by-step method

1. **Measure and sketch:** Begin by measuring the outer walls of the property. It helps to make a rough sketch first and write down the length of each wall. For an irregular plot, measure each segment of the boundary. Measure each side of the plot correctly, and also take diagonal or corner measurements for greater precision.
2. **Choose a scale:** Decide the scale of the drawing. For example, one square of the graph paper can stand for one foot of real space. This keeps the drawing a true picture of the property.
3. **Draw the outer walls:** Using the chosen scale, draw the outer boundary of the property on graph paper or in the software. The shape must be accurate, whether it is a perfect square, a rectangle or an irregular shape.
4. **Add the interior details:** Measure the interior walls to fix all the rooms, including the kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms and living areas. Mark the exact positions and sizes of doors, windows and any large fixed furniture.
5. **Find the centre (Brahmasthan):** To find the centre of the house, draw diagonal lines between opposite corners of the floor plan. The point where the lines cross is the centre of the property. This central point, called the Brahmasthan, is the reference for marking the Vastu zones.

## Part 2: Marking the 16 Vastu directions

Once the scaled floor plan is ready, the next important step is to determine the Vastu directions correctly and mark them.

### Using a compass or a compass app

- **Physical compass:** Stand in the centre of the property and hold the compass flat. Keep away from magnetic or electromagnetic objects that could disturb the reading. The needle points to magnetic north, which you can use as your reference.
- **Smartphone apps:** Many smartphones have a built-in compass app. Compass apps made for Vastu, which help in laying a grid over floor plans, are also available. When using a phone, make sure the compass is properly calibrated. It is advisable to take at least three readings from different parts of the house to be sure of accuracy.

### Marking the directions

1. **Mark north:** Line up the north shown by the compass with the floor plan and mark it clearly.
2. **Mark the cardinal directions:** From the centre point of the plan, draw a line to the north mark. This is the 0 degree or 360 degree line. From it you can mark the other cardinal directions: east at 90 degrees, south at 180 degrees and west at 270 degrees.
3. **Mark the 16 zones:** Vastu divides the 360 degrees around the centre into 16 zones or directions. Each zone covers an angle of 22.5 degrees. Starting from north, mark these zones by dividing the space between each cardinal and inter-cardinal direction. For example, the zone between north and north-east is north of north-east (NNE).

## Part 3: Identifying the 32 entrance segments (padas)

The main entrance of a property is a key element in Vastu. There are 32 possible entrance locations, called padas, and identifying them precisely is important.

### Understanding the 32 padas

- The 360 degrees of the property are divided into 32 segments, and each pada spans 11.25 degrees (360 divided by 32).
- Each of the four cardinal directions (north, east, south and west) contains 8 of these padas. For instance, the north direction has the entrances N1 to N8.

### How to mark the 32 padas

1. **Start from north:** Taking the centre of the floor plan as the pivot, mark lines at intervals of 11.25 degrees, moving clockwise.
2. **Mark each pada:**
    - Draw a line from the centre to the north point (0 degrees).
    - Draw the next line 11.25 degrees clockwise from the north line. This marks the boundary of the first pada.
    - Keep drawing lines at intervals of 11.25 degrees all the way round the circle until all 32 segments are marked.
3. **Label the padas:** Label each segment in order (for example N1, N2, N3 and so on to E1, E2 and so on) to identify the exact location of the main entrance.

## The role of digital tools

Several digital tools and apps can make this process much simpler.

- **Vastu apps:** Some apps let you upload a digital image of your floor plan.
- **Automatic gridding:** These apps can then lay the Vastu chakra over the plan, marking the 16 zones and even the 32 entrance padas directly on it. This can save time and reduce the chance of human error in calculation and drawing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Brahmasthan and why is it important?

The Brahmasthan is the central point of a property. Vastu tradition regards it as the source of all energy. According to tradition it should be kept open and free of clutter, so that energy is distributed in balance through the space.

### How accurate does the directional marking need to be?

Directional marking needs to be as accurate as possible. Even a small error can lead to a wrong Vastu analysis and wrong recommendations. Using a reliable compass and taking several readings is important.

### Can a Vastu analysis be done without a scaled floor plan?

A basic analysis can be done, but a scaled floor plan is essential for a detailed and accurate Vastu audit. It allows the zones, the entrances and the placement of objects to be identified precisely, which matters for any correction that follows.

### How many directions and entrance segments are marked?

Sixteen zones are marked around the centre, each covering 22.5 degrees, and 32 entrance padas, each spanning 11.25 degrees. Each of the four cardinal directions holds 8 padas.

### How do I get a dependable compass reading?

Hold the compass flat in the centre of the property, away from magnetic or electromagnetic objects. If you use a phone, calibrate its compass and take at least three readings from different parts of the house.

## Conclusion

Creating an accurate scaled map and carrying out a careful directional analysis are the foundation of a Vastu consultation. By following these steps you can base your analysis on precise measurements and markings. This attention to detail lets a consultant give recommendations that are targeted and clear for any space.
