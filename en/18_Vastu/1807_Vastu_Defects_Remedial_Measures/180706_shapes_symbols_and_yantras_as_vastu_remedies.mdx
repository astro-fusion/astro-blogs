# Shapes, Symbols and Yantras as Vastu Remedies

In Vastu Shastra, geometric shapes, sacred symbols and yantras are traditionally used to ease defects and to lift the feel of a space. This article describes what they are, how tradition places them, and what mistakes it warns against.

In this article you will learn:

- The ideas behind shapes, symbols and yantras in Vastu.
- The main kinds and where each is placed.
- How to think about choosing among them.
- Practical points about placing and keeping them in a home or an office.

## What Are Shapes, Symbols and Yantras?

Shapes, symbols and yantras are traditional means of correcting and improving the energy of a space. Tradition holds that they work by resonating with particular energies and by redirecting the flow of prana, the life energy.

- **Range of use:** They are used for defects ranging from a missing corner to a toilet in the north-east.
- **Spiritual side:** Using them is a way of making a place sacred and uplifting, which is meant to support spiritual practice.

### Historical Background

Sacred geometry and symbols have a long history in many old cultures. In India, yantras and mandalas have been part of spiritual practice for thousands of years.

## Yantras and the Vastu Tradition

### Yantras in Astrological Tradition

In the astrological tradition each planet has its own yantra, used to propitiate it. This is given as tradition only.

### Use in Vastu Shastra

The placing of yantras and other remedies can be guided by the Vastu Purusha Mandala, so that they sit in harmony with the energies of the space. They are also used to balance the five elements in a room; in Vastu tradition earth belongs to the south-west, water to the north-east, fire to the south-east, air to the north-west and space to the centre. Careful placing is said to improve the flow of prana in a room.

## Kinds of Shapes, Symbols and Yantras

### Pyramids

Pyramids are sold today as devices that generate energy and correct large defects, and some sellers describe placing them in the centre of a home or in a toilet in the north-east. These are sellers' claims, not classical Vastu, and they are not recommended here.

### Sacred Symbols

- **Belief:** Sacred symbols such as Om and the Swastika are regarded as carriers of positive energy.
- **Practice:** Tradition places them at the entrance of a home, to draw positive energy and to guard against negative energy.

### Yantras

- **Belief:** Yantras are geometric diagrams used to draw particular energies.
- **Practice:** Yantras are described for many purposes, including wealth, health and relationships. The appropriate yantra is placed in the matching direction of the home.

## Use in Homes and Workplaces

- **Homes:** Shapes, symbols and yantras are used to make a home a place of peace.
- **Shops and offices:** The same ideas are applied in a commercial space.
- **Specific defects:** Particular shapes, symbols and yantras are described for particular defects. A missing corner is a defect of the plan, and a symbol does not change it.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Using a fake or improperly made yantra. Be careful of sellers who claim special powers for an object.
- Placing a yantra in the wrong direction.
- Not keeping yantras clean and well cared for.

## An Illustration and Traditional Pointers

### Illustration: A Business with a Missing Corner

**Situation:** A business has an office with several Vastu defects, including a missing corner in the north.

**Traditional suggestion:** A Sri Yantra placed in the north, the direction linked with wealth. No result is claimed, and a business matter such as customers or profit is dealt with through the business itself.

### Pointers from the Tradition

- Yantras are treated with reverence and respect.
- They are obtained from a source you trust, and kept clean.
- They are placed with reverence in the matching direction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which yantra is regarded as the most powerful?

Tradition regards the Sri Yantra as the foremost Vastu yantra, a symbol of wealth, prosperity and abundance. This is a traditional belief.

### Where are pyramids placed?

Pyramids are a modern commercial item, not a classical Vastu practice. Claims made for them are sellers' claims, so this article does not recommend them.

### Where are Om and the Swastika placed?

Tradition places them at the entrance of the home.

### Can I make my own yantra?

It is not recommended unless you have been properly trained in making yantras.

### What mistakes are warned against when placing a yantra?

Using a fake or improperly made yantra, placing it in the wrong direction, and not keeping it clean.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, shapes, symbols and yantras are a way of lifting a space and easing defects. They are traditional practices handled with reverence, not promises of any result, and a defect of the plan remains a matter for an architect or an engineer.
