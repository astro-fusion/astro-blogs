# Vastu Shanti Puja and Other Traditional Rituals

In Vastu Shastra, rituals and ceremonies are performed to honour the Vastu Purusha, the deity linked with a property, and to ease defects. This article describes what these ceremonies are in tradition, how they are performed, and what care is advised before holding one.

In this article you will learn:

- The ideas behind Vastu rituals.
- How Vastu Shanti Puja and other ceremonies are described.
- Why timing is considered important.
- What to keep in mind when holding a ritual.

## What Are Vastu Rituals and Ceremonies?

Vastu rituals are sacred ceremonies performed to correct Vastu defects and to make a living space harmonious. According to tradition they work by invoking the blessings of the deities and purifying the energy of a space.

- **Range of use:** Rituals are used for defects ranging from a toilet in the north-east to a main entrance in the south.
- **Spiritual side:** Vastu rituals are a way of connecting with the divine and of making a sacred space that supports spiritual growth.

### Historical Background

Rituals and ceremonies have been part of Indian culture for thousands of years. In Vastu Shastra they are said to rest on the old texts, the Vedas and the Puranas.

## Rituals and the Vastu Tradition

### Rituals in Astrological Tradition

Vastu rituals are often performed together with planetary remedies. Tradition has the timing chosen at an auspicious moment (muhurta), and holds that an astrologer can suggest suitable rituals. This is given as tradition only.

### Use in Vastu Shastra

Vastu rituals are performed to honour the Vastu Purusha and the deities that reside in the Vastu Purusha Mandala. They are said to help balance the five elements, which in Vastu tradition are earth in the south-west, water in the north-east, fire in the south-east, air in the north-west and space in the centre. They are also said to purify the energy of a space and to improve the flow of prana, the life energy.

## Kinds of Vastu Rituals and Ceremonies

### Vastu Shanti Puja

- **Belief:** Vastu Shanti Puja is a ceremony performed to honour the Vastu Purusha and to remove Vastu defects from a home.
- **Procedure:** It is usually performed by a qualified priest. It involves the chanting of mantras, offerings of flowers and fruits, and a fire ceremony (havan).

### Griha Pravesh Puja

- **Belief:** Griha Pravesh Puja is performed on moving into a new home, to bless the space and to protect it from negative energies.
- **Procedure:** It is similar to the Vastu Shanti Puja and is held before the family moves into the new home.

### Navagraha Puja

- **Belief:** Navagraha Puja is performed to honour the nine planets and to ease their negative effects.
- **Procedure:** It involves the chanting of mantras and offerings of particular items to each of the nine planets.

## Use in Homes and Workplaces

- **Homes:** Rituals are performed to make a home a place of peace and happiness.
- **Shops and offices:** The same rituals are held for shops and offices.
- **Specific defects:** Particular rituals are described for particular defects. A ritual does not change the plan of a building; a structural defect remains a matter for an architect or an engineer.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Performing a ritual without faith and devotion.
- Not following the proper procedure.
- Not choosing a qualified priest to perform it.

## An Illustration and Traditional Pointers

### Illustration: A Family Home with Constant Quarrels

**Situation:** A family has constant arguments and conflict, and the home has several major Vastu defects.

**Traditional suggestion:** A Vastu Shanti Puja, as tradition describes it for a home with such defects. No outcome is claimed. Quarrels in a family are also dealt with through talking, and where needed through counselling.

### Pointers from the Tradition

- Consult a qualified priest or Vastu practitioner before holding any ritual.
- Understand the meaning of the ritual you are performing.
- Perform it with a pure heart and a positive intention.
- Agree the cost with the priest beforehand, and do not take on a ceremony you cannot afford.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I perform Vastu rituals myself?

It is best to have a ritual performed by a qualified priest. Simple acts such as chanting mantras and offering flowers can be done by the family.

### What happens in a Vastu Shanti Puja?

A qualified priest chants mantras, offers flowers and fruits, and performs a fire ceremony (havan).

### When is Griha Pravesh Puja held?

On moving into a new home, before the family moves in.

### Why is timing regarded as important?

Tradition holds that a ritual should be held at an auspicious moment (muhurta), chosen with the help of an astrologer.

### What should I keep in mind when holding a ritual?

Hold it with faith, follow the proper procedure, choose a qualified priest and understand its meaning.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, rituals are a way of honouring the Vastu Purusha and blessing a home. They are matters of faith and devotion, they carry no promise of any result, and they are held with the care and understanding described above.
