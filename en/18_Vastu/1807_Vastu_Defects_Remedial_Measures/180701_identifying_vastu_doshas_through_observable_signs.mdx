# How to Recognise Vastu Doshas in Your Home

Vastu Shastra is the traditional Indian science of building. It holds that the spaces we live in carry energy, and that when this energy is out of balance the effect is felt in daily life. This article describes the signs that Vastu tradition reads as a defect in the house, the causes it looks for behind them, and the remedies it describes.

In this article you will learn:

- What the basic idea of a Vastu dosha is.
- How subtle and obvious signs of a defect are recognised in a home.
- Which problem is linked with which imbalance of direction.
- Which simple traditional remedies are described.

Whether you are new to Vastu or already know it, this material is a guide to thinking about a balanced home. These are traditional beliefs, not a promise of any result.

## What Are Vastu Doshas?

Vastu doshas are flaws in the construction and layout of a building that, according to Vastu tradition, disturb the flow of positive energy. They can come from a wrongly oriented house, rooms placed in the wrong part of the plan, or other breaches of Vastu rules.

- **Spiritual side:** A home built in line with Vastu is believed to be a sacred place that supports peace of mind and spiritual practice.
- **Tradition of correction:** According to Vastu tradition, identifying a defect and correcting it brings balance to the atmosphere of the home.

### Historical Background

The principles of Vastu Shastra are held to come from ancient Hindu texts, including the Vedas and the Puranas. These texts give guidance on building in harmony with the laws of nature and the cosmos.

## The Basis of Vastu

### Directions and the Flow of Energy

Vastu tradition links each direction with a deity, and each corner and the centre with an element. An imbalance in one direction is therefore said to bring particular problems. The aim of Vastu is that prana, the life energy, moves freely through the whole house.

### The Five Elements

A balance of five elements is considered necessary for a harmonious home. They are earth, water, fire, air and space. In Vastu tradition earth belongs to the south-west, water to the north-east, fire to the south-east, air to the north-west and space to the centre.

## Signs of Vastu Doshas

### Sign 1: Repeated Illness

**Description:** If family members fall ill again and again despite a healthy routine, tradition reads this as a sign of a Vastu dosha. Persistent illness always needs a doctor first; Vastu is not a substitute for medical care.

- **Possible causes:** A toilet in the north-east, a kitchen in the north, or a bedroom in the south-east.
- **Traditional remedy:** Moving the toilet or the kitchen, where that is possible. Where it is not, tradition mentions remedies such as yantras and sea salt, which are said to calm negative energy. Treat these as information about the tradition, not as necessary advice.

### Sign 2: Financial Instability

**Description:** Lasting money trouble, such as sudden expenses, a fall in income or an inability to save, is linked in Vastu tradition with a dosha.

- **Possible causes:** A cut or an extension in the north or north-east, a toilet in the north, or a cluttered and dark entrance. These are believed to block the flow of wealth.
- **Traditional remedy:** Keeping the north and north-east clean and free of clutter. Tradition also speaks of a water fountain or a picture of a waterfall in the north-east.

### Sign 3: Tension in Relationships

**Description:** Frequent arguments, misunderstandings and a lack of understanding among family members are also taken as a sign of a Vastu dosha.

- **Possible causes:** A bedroom in the south-east, the direction of fire, a kitchen in the north-east, or a cut in the south-west.
- **Traditional remedy:** Keeping the bedroom in the south-west or the west, and using calm colours such as light blue or green in the bedroom.

## Practical Use

- **Homes:** Look for the signs above from time to time. Keep the house clean and orderly, especially the north-east.
- **Shops and offices:** Vastu principles are also applied to shops and offices.
- **Simple remedies:** Mirrors, colours, plants and yantras are described in Vastu tradition as simple means of correcting a defect.

## Common Mistakes

- Overlooking the early signs of a dosha.
- Having structural changes made without advice from a qualified architect or engineer.
- Adopting remedies without understanding the principle behind them.
- Forgetting the importance of cleanliness and order.

## An Illustration

**Situation:** After moving into a new apartment, a family had illness over a long period and frequent arguments.

**Examination:** A Vastu practitioner noticed that the kitchen was in the north-east and the master bedroom in the south-east.

**Traditional suggestion:** Repainting the bedroom walls in light blue, as tradition describes for calming a bedroom.

This is only an illustration of how a Vastu practitioner thinks about finding a defect and a remedy. No result can be promised from it.

### Some Practical Pointers

- Always use a compass to check the directions of the house.
- For a detailed review, consult a qualified Vastu practitioner.
- Take up remedies with patience, and see a doctor for any illness that continues.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a Vastu dosha really affect life?

Vastu tradition holds that the energy of a living space has a deep effect on physical, mental and emotional well-being. This is a traditional belief.

### Which signs point to a Vastu dosha?

According to tradition, the main signs are repeated illness, financial instability and constant tension in the family.

### Are Vastu remedies expensive?

Not necessarily. Many traditional remedies, such as keeping a room clean, using calm colours or growing plants, are simple and low in cost.

### How do I choose a Vastu practitioner?

Choose someone with good experience and a sound grasp of Vastu Shastra. Be careful of anyone who pushes you towards an expensive remedy.

### How do I check the directions of my house?

Use a compass to find the directions of the house.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, paying attention to the signs of a defect and taking suitable remedies is one way to make a home calm and balanced. By recognising the signs and keeping the house clean and orderly, you can make your home a place of peace.
