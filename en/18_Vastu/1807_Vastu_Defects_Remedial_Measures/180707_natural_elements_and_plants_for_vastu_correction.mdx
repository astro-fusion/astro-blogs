# Plants and Natural Materials in Vastu Correction

Nature offers many resources for balancing a living space. In Vastu Shastra, plants and other natural materials are used as traditional remedies to ease defects and to lift the feel of a home. This article sets out what tradition describes and what mistakes it warns against.

In this article you will learn:

- The ideas behind natural elements in Vastu.
- Which plants and natural materials are described.
- How they are used to balance the elements in a home.
- Practical points for bringing them into your home decoration.

## What Are Natural Vastu Remedies?

Natural Vastu remedies are corrections that use plants and other natural materials to balance the energy of a space. According to tradition they work by bringing the balancing energies of nature into the home.

- **Practical side:** They are a simple and low-cost way of dealing with defects. They are traditional practices, and no result can be promised from them.
- **Spiritual side:** Using them is a way of connecting with the natural world and of making a sacred space that supports well-being.

### Historical Background

The use of plants and natural materials for balance has a long history in many old cultures. In India, Tulsi (holy basil) and other sacred plants have been part of spiritual and domestic life for thousands of years.

## Plants and the Vastu Tradition

### Plants in Astrological Tradition

In the astrological tradition each planet is linked with particular plants and herbs. Keeping these plants at home is said to strengthen the influence of that planet. This is given as tradition only.

### Use in Vastu Shastra

The placing of plants and other natural materials can be guided by the Vastu Purusha Mandala, so that they sit in harmony with the energies of the space. They are also used to balance the five elements in a room. In Vastu tradition earth belongs to the south-west, water to the north-east, fire to the south-east, air to the north-west and space to the centre. Careful placing is said to improve the flow of prana, the life energy, in a room.

## Kinds of Natural Remedies

### Plants

- **Belief:** Plants are living things that bring the energy of nature into a home. They are also seen as giving a calm and relaxing atmosphere.
- **Practice:** A Tulsi plant is placed in the north-east, and a money plant in the south-east. Tradition links Tulsi with spiritual growth and the money plant with wealth.

### Natural Materials

- **Belief:** Natural materials such as wood, stone and metal are said to balance the elements and to give a more grounded and steady setting.
- **Practice:** Wooden furniture is used to give a warm and welcoming feel. A bowl of sea salt in the corners of a room is a traditional practice said to absorb negative energy.

### Crystals

Crystals and gemstones are sold today as remedies for Vastu defects. They are not part of classical Vastu, the claims made for them are sellers' claims, and this article does not recommend them.

## Use in Homes and Workplaces

- **Homes:** Natural materials and plants are used to make a home a place of peace and calm.
- **Shops and offices:** Plants and natural materials can make a commercial space more welcoming.
- **Specific defects:** Particular natural remedies are described for particular defects. A defect of the plan, such as a cut or an extension, is a matter for an architect or an engineer.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Keeping thorny plants, such as cacti, indoors. Tradition says they create a harsh and negative energy.
- Keeping dead or dying plants in the home.
- Buying objects sold with large claims about their energy.

## An Illustration and Practical Pointers

### Illustration: A Home Without Greenery

**Situation:** A person has allergies and breathing trouble, and the home has no plants.

**Traditional suggestion:** Bringing in plants such as the snake plant and the spider plant, which are described as plants that freshen a room. No outcome is claimed. Allergies and breathing trouble need a doctor, and plants are no substitute for medical care.

### Practical Pointers

- Choose plants that suit the climate and the light in your home.
- Use natural materials in your home decoration to give a balanced and harmonious feel.
- Keep plants healthy; a plant you cannot look after does not help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which plants are regarded as good for Vastu?

Plants with a positive and uplifting character are preferred, such as the Tulsi plant, the money plant and the bamboo plant.

### In which direction is Tulsi placed?

In the north-east, where tradition links it with spiritual growth and well-being.

### Where is the money plant placed?

In the south-east, where tradition links it with wealth.

### Can artificial plants be used for Vastu?

Real plants are preferred. If you are unable to care for real plants, good-quality artificial plants can be used.

### Which plants are not kept indoors?

Thorny plants such as cacti are not kept indoors, and dead or dying plants are not kept at all.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, plants and natural materials are a gentle way of lifting a home and balancing its elements. Used with care, they make a space more pleasant. These are traditional beliefs, and no result can be promised.
