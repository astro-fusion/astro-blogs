# Directional Vastu Doshas and Their Traditional Effects

In Vastu Shastra every direction has its own character and is linked with particular parts of life. When a defect falls in one direction, tradition says the matching part of life is affected. This article sets out what Vastu tradition holds for the north, south, east and west, and the remedies it describes.

In this article you will learn:

- What each direction means in Vastu Shastra.
- What defects in the north, south, east and west are said to bring.
- How to find directional defects.
- What simple remedies are described.

## What Are Directional Vastu Doshas?

Directional Vastu doshas are defects that belong to one particular direction. They can arise from a toilet, a kitchen or a main entrance placed in a direction that tradition does not favour.

- **Targeted correction:** By knowing what is traditionally linked with each direction, a home owner can see which part of the house a remedy should address.
- **Spiritual side:** A home free of directional doshas is regarded as a sacred place that supports spiritual growth and well-being.

### Historical Background

The idea of directional energies is central to Vastu Shastra. The old sages are said to have understood the subtle energies of the earth and the cosmos, and Vastu Shastra is described as their way of bringing human dwellings into harmony with these energies.

## The Basis of the Directions

### Planets and Directions

By tradition the directions are linked with the planets. For example, the east is linked with the Sun, the north with Mercury and the south with Mars. This is given only as tradition.

### The Vastu Purusha Mandala

The Vastu Purusha Mandala is a symbolic diagram that maps the energies of a building. Each part of the mandala corresponds to a direction and is linked with a deity and an aspect of life.

### The Five Elements

The five elements belong to the four corners and the centre. In Vastu tradition earth belongs to the south-west, water to the north-east, fire to the south-east, air to the north-west and space to the centre.

### Flow of Energy

Directional doshas are said to disturb the flow of prana, the life energy, and so to cause a variety of problems.

## Defects in Each Direction and Their Traditional Effects

### North Direction

- **Linked with:** Wealth, career and opportunities.
- **Doshas:** A toilet, a kitchen or clutter in the north is said to bring financial problems, a stalled career and missed opportunities.
- **Traditional remedy:** Keeping the north clean and free of clutter. Tradition also speaks of a water fountain or a picture of a green landscape in the north.

### South Direction

- **Linked with:** Health, fame and recognition.
- **Doshas:** A main entrance, a cut or an extension in the south is said to bring health problems, a poor reputation and a lack of recognition.
- **Traditional remedy:** Tradition speaks of heavy furniture or a large plant in the south to steady its energy, and of warm colours such as red, orange and yellow.

### East Direction

- **Linked with:** Health, new beginnings and growth.
- **Doshas:** A toilet, a storeroom or a staircase in the east is said to bring health problems, obstacles in new ventures and a lack of growth.
- **Traditional remedy:** Keeping the east open and well lit. Tradition also speaks of a picture of the rising sun in the east.

### West Direction

- **Linked with:** Creativity, relationships and children.
- **Doshas:** A kitchen, a toilet or a cut in the west is said to bring creative blocks, trouble in relationships and difficulty over children.
- **Traditional remedy:** Tradition speaks of calming colours such as blue and white in the west, and of a picture of a happy family.

## Practical Use

- **Homes:** Use a compass to find the directions of your house and look for directional defects.
- **Shops and offices:** Directions are considered in the layout of a shop or office in the same way.
- **Range of remedies:** Traditional remedies range from simple changes of colour and objects to the use of yantras. Anything structural, such as a cut or extension, is a matter for an architect or an engineer.

## Common Mistakes

- Ignoring the importance of directions in Vastu.
- Using remedies without understanding what is traditionally linked with each direction.
- Making structural changes to the home without advice from a qualified architect or engineer.

## An Illustration

**Situation:** A family finds money tight in spite of hard work.

**Examination:** A Vastu practitioner notices that there is a toilet in the north of the home, which tradition links with money matters.

**Traditional suggestion:** Tradition mentions a bowl of sea salt, described as a way of calming negative energy. This is a description of the practice, not advice, and no result can be promised.

### Some Practical Pointers

- Always use a compass to find the directions accurately.
- Take account of the needs and circumstances of the people living in the house.
- Where money or health trouble is real, deal with it directly as well, with a financial adviser or a doctor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which direction is most important in Vastu?

All directions matter, but the north-east is regarded as the most sacred and auspicious. It is the direction of spiritual growth.

### What is a north-direction defect linked with?

Tradition links a toilet, a kitchen or clutter in the north with financial problems, a stalled career and missed opportunities.

### Which colours are described for the south?

Warm colours such as red, orange and yellow are described for the south.

### Can Vastu remedies be used in a rented home?

Simple changes such as colours, plants and keeping rooms clear can be made in a rented home. Structural changes cannot, and need the owner's consent and professional advice.

### How do I check the directions of my home?

Use a compass to find the directions of the house.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, directional defects are linked with particular parts of life. Knowing what each direction stands for helps a home owner see where to look, and simple, practical remedies are described for each. No result can be promised.
