# Modern Vastu Devices: Market Claims and Cautions

Vastu Shastra is an old tradition, and today a range of gadgets is sold as Vastu remedies. This article describes what such devices claim to do and where they stand in relation to classical Vastu, so that a home owner can read the claims with care.

In this article you will learn:

- What modern Vastu devices are said to be.
- The kinds of device that are sold.
- How these devices relate to traditional remedies.
- What to watch for before buying one.

## What Are Modern Vastu Devices?

Modern Vastu devices are products sold as tools to correct Vastu defects and to improve the energy of a space. Sellers describe them as working through principles of physics, electronics and metaphysics.

- **Sellers' claims:** Some products are said to give a measurable check on energy improvements. This is a claim made by the sellers. It is not part of classical Vastu, and this article does not vouch for it.
- **Range of claims:** Sellers describe uses ranging from geopathic stress to electromagnetic radiation.
- **Stated aim:** The stated aim is to bridge science and spirituality and to bring a home into harmony with both the natural and the technological worlds.

### Historical Background

Modern Vastu devices are a recent development. Sellers describe their principles as drawn from Vastu Shastra and other esoteric traditions.

## Where They Stand in Vastu

### A New Approach

These devices are presented as a new, scientific approach to Vastu with measurable results. This appeals to people looking for a more rational approach. The claims, however, are sellers' claims, and they are not classical Vastu.

### Not a Replacement for Traditional Remedies

Sellers say the devices are meant to complement traditional remedies, not to replace them. A device cannot replace the traditional ways of looking at a building, such as the directions of its rooms and the cleanliness and order of the home.

## Kinds of Modern Devices

### Energy Measurement Devices

- **Claim:** These devices are said to measure the energy levels of a space and to find areas of negative energy.
- **Example:** The H3 Antenna is a popular device said to detect geopathic stress and other subtle disturbances. What it detects is a seller's claim.

### Energetic Shifters

- **Claim:** These are said to shift the energy of a space and to correct Vastu defects without any physical alteration.
- **Example:** Sellers say an energetic shifter can virtually shift the location of a toilet, a kitchen or a main entrance. A virtual shift changes nothing in the building. A real change of position is a large structural alteration for an architect or an engineer.

### Other Modern Devices

- **Claim:** Sellers also offer geopathic stress rods, energy plates and Vastu harmonizers.
- **Example:** They are said to neutralise negative energy and to make a more positive and harmonious setting. These too are sellers' claims, not classical Vastu.

## Use in Homes and Workplaces

- **Homes:** Devices are marketed to make a home free of negative energies.
- **Shops and offices:** They are marketed to commercial spaces as well.
- **Specific defects:** Products are advertised for a wide range of defects. Think about the cost before buying any of them.

## Mistakes to Avoid

- Relying only on modern devices and neglecting traditional remedies.
- Using devices without understanding the claims made for them.
- Not consulting a qualified Vastu practitioner before buying one.

## An Illustration and Cautions

### Illustration: Tiredness in a Bedroom

**Situation:** A person is often tired, and a seller says a bedroom has a zone of geopathic stress that a device has detected.

**What to bear in mind:** A seller may suggest a rod under the bed. This is a seller's claim, and no outcome is claimed here. Lasting tiredness or any other health problem needs a doctor.

### Cautions

- Be careful about the source of any device, and about the price asked.
- Follow the instructions given with a device if you decide to use one.
- Do not give up traditional remedies or proper medical care for a gadget.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are modern Vastu devices part of classical Vastu?

No. They are modern products, and the claims made for them are sellers' claims.

### Are these devices safe?

Check the maker's instructions for any electrical device, and consult a qualified Vastu practitioner and an engineer before using a new device.

### How do I know a device is working?

Sellers say some devices give a measurable check on energy. This is the sellers' claim, and it is not part of classical Vastu. Be careful of any promise of better health, finances or relationships.

### Are these devices expensive?

The cost varies with the kind of device. Weigh the cost carefully, since simple traditional measures such as cleanliness and order cost little.

### Can they replace traditional remedies?

Sellers say they are meant only to complement them. They cannot replace the traditional approach.

## Conclusion

Modern Vastu devices are products with sellers' claims attached, and they are not part of classical Vastu. A home owner is better served by the traditional basics of direction, cleanliness and order, by an architect or engineer for anything structural, and by a doctor for any health problem.
