# Colours in Vastu: Which Colour for Which Direction

Colours affect how we feel and how much energy we have. In Vastu Shastra, colour is used to balance the elements of a space and to ease Vastu defects. This article sets out the colours that tradition links with each direction and how they are used in a home.

In this article you will learn:

- The idea behind colour in Vastu.
- Which colours are described for which direction.
- How colour is used to offset an imbalance of elements.
- Practical ways of using colour: paint, fabrics and decorative items.

## What Is Colour Therapy in Vastu?

Colour therapy in Vastu is the use of particular colours to balance the energy of a space. Tradition holds that each colour has its own vibration and is linked with an element and a direction.

- **Range of use:** Colour is used for defects ranging from a toilet in the north-east to a kitchen in the north.
- **Spiritual side:** Using colour is a way of making a place calm and uplifting, which is meant to support spiritual practice.

### Historical Background

The use of colour for healing and balance has a long history in several old cultures, including India, Egypt and China. In Vastu Shastra the use of colour rests on a traditional view of the subtle energies of the natural world.

## The Basis of Colour in Vastu

### The Vastu Purusha Mandala

The colours used in a room should suit the colours of the matching direction in the Vastu Purusha Mandala.

### The Five Elements

Each corner and the centre is linked with one of the five elements, and each element with certain colours. In Vastu tradition earth belongs to the south-west, water to the north-east, fire to the south-east, air to the north-west and space to the centre. The north-east, for example, is linked with water and with light blue.

### Planets and Colours

By tradition each planet is linked with a colour. The Sun is linked with orange, the Moon with white and Mars with red. This is given only as tradition.

### Flow of Energy

Colour is said to help the flow of prana, the life energy, in a room.

## Colours for Different Directions

### North-East

- **Element:** Water
- **Colour:** Light blue
- **Traditional effect:** Said to bring peace, tranquillity and spiritual growth.

### South-East

- **Element:** Fire
- **Colour:** Orange, pink and silver
- **Traditional effect:** Said to bring passion, creativity and vitality.

### South-West

- **Element:** Earth
- **Colour:** Peach, earth colour and light brown
- **Traditional effect:** Said to bring stability, security and good relationships.

### North-West

- **Element:** Air
- **Colour:** White, grey and cream
- **Traditional effect:** Said to help communication, social contact and opportunities.

## Practical Use

- **Homes:** Colour can be used to make a home that reflects your own character and feels comfortable.
- **Shops and offices:** In a commercial space, colour can be used to give a place its own identity.
- **Specific defects:** Colour is used to ease many defects. For example, painting the wall of a toilet in the north-east light blue is described as a way of calming its negative energy. A toilet in the north-east is a defect that belongs to the plan of the house, and colour does not change that.

## Common Mistakes

- Using too many colours in one room, which can feel chaotic and overwhelming.
- Using dark and heavy colours in small rooms, which can make them feel smaller and more cramped.
- Ignoring the psychological effect of colours.

## An Illustration

**Situation:** A student finds it hard to concentrate on studies.

**Examination:** A Vastu practitioner notices that the study room is painted in a dark and distracting colour.

**Traditional suggestion:** Painting the study room light green, a colour associated with focus in the tradition.

This is only an illustration of how colour is chosen. No result is claimed.

### Some Practical Pointers

- Use a colour wheel to choose colours that go well together.
- Think about the lighting of a room when choosing colours.
- Use colour to create a focal point in a room.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best colour for a bedroom?

A light and calming colour such as light blue, green or pink. These are said to promote relaxation and restful sleep.

### Which colour is considered unsuitable for a kitchen?

Black. It is regarded as a heavy colour that suppresses the energy of the kitchen.

### Can I use wallpaper instead of paint?

Yes. Choose a wallpaper with a positive and uplifting design.

### Which colour is described for the north-east?

Light blue, linked with the water element.

### Which colours suit a small room?

Avoid dark and heavy colours in small rooms, because they make a room feel smaller. Lighter, calming colours are the better choice.

## Conclusion

According to Vastu tradition, colour is a simple way to balance the elements and give each direction its own character. Used with care and in moderation, it can make a home more pleasant. These are traditional beliefs, and no result can be promised.
