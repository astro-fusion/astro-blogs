# Three Vastu Defect Illustrations and Traditional Remedies

The best way to understand how a Vastu practitioner thinks is to see the reasoning applied to a case. This article gives three plain illustrations of defects that Vastu tradition discusses and the remedies it describes for them. They are illustrations only, with no outcomes, and none of them is a record of a result.

In this article you will learn:

- How Vastu tradition links defects with problems in different areas of life.
- Which traditional remedies are described for each defect.
- Key points to take from the illustrations.

## How Vastu Is Looked At in Practice

Vastu Shastra is a traditional way of reading a building. The illustrations below show how a practitioner moves from a defect in the plan to the remedies tradition describes. They do not show that any remedy works, and no result can be promised.

## Illustration 1: Money Trouble and a Toilet in the North-East

### The Situation

A family is under financial strain. The husband has lost his job and the household is struggling to make ends meet. The home has a toilet in the north-east, which Vastu tradition counts as a major defect and links with financial problems.

### Traditional Remedies

Tradition describes keeping the north-east corner clean and free of clutter, and placing a bowl of sea salt in the toilet to calm negative energy. These are traditional practices, not advice.

### Takeaway

A real financial crisis needs practical steps: looking for work, talking to creditors and getting sound financial advice. The remedies above are only a traditional addition to such steps.

## Illustration 2: A Kitchen in the North and the Atmosphere of the Home

### The Situation

A couple has frequent ill health and arguments. The home has a kitchen in the north, which tradition counts as a defect linked with health and relationship problems.

### Traditional Remedies

Tradition describes painting the kitchen light green and having meals in the dining room instead of the kitchen. Persistent ill health needs a doctor.

### Takeaway

Where there is tension at home, talking things over is as important as any change of room. The remedies described are habits and colours, and cost little.

## Illustration 3: A South-West Entrance and an Office

### The Situation

A business is finding it hard to draw customers. The office has its main entrance in the south-west, which tradition counts as a major defect.

### Traditional Remedies

Tradition describes an entrance in the north and a Sri Yantra in the office, and the owner sitting in a more favourable position. Moving a main entrance is a large structural change. It is a matter for an architect or an engineer, not a casual decision.

### Takeaway

How a business does depends on its own management and market. A yantra and a seating position are traditional practices only.

## What to Take from the Illustrations

- Vastu tradition links defects with real parts of life, and a defect is treated as something to look at calmly.
- Traditional remedies can be simple and low in cost.
- It is important to consult a qualified practitioner, and an architect or an engineer for any structural change.
- Vastu is a tradition. It does not take the place of a doctor, a financial adviser or good management.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these illustrations real events?

They are plain illustrations of how Vastu tradition reasons about defects. They are not records of real outcomes.

### Can Vastu solve all my problems?

No. It is not a way of solving every problem overnight. Tradition treats it as a way of making a home orderly and supportive, and the problems of life still need practical steps.

### What does tradition say about a toilet in the north-east?

It counts it as a major defect linked with financial problems. Tradition describes keeping the north-east clean and clear of clutter and a bowl of sea salt in the toilet.

### What remedies are described for a kitchen in the north?

Painting the kitchen light green and having meals in the dining room, as tradition describes.

### How do I choose a qualified Vastu practitioner?

Look for someone who is well regarded and who explains their reasoning clearly. Be careful of anyone who pushes you towards an expensive remedy or promises a result.

## Conclusion

These three illustrations show how Vastu tradition reads a defect and what remedies it describes. They claim no outcome. Used with good sense, alongside practical help where it is needed, they are a way of thinking calmly about the home.
