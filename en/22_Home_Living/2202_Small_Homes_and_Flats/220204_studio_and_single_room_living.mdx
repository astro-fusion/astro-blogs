# Living Well in a Single Room: A Layout That Works

A single room has to hold a bed, a place to sit, a place to eat, a small kitchen and all your clothes. After reading this you will be able to split the room into four zones, check each with a tape measure, and avoid the common problem of a room that is crowded and still has nowhere to sit.

## Think in zones, not furniture

Every piece you add should belong to one zone. Four zones are enough.

- **Sleep:** the bed and a place for the day's clothes.
- **Cook and wash up:** the counter, the sink, the stove and the fridge.
- **Sit and eat:** a small table with two chairs, or a bench along a wall.
- **Store:** the wardrobe and shelves.

Order the zones from the door inwards. Cooking and storing shoes sit near the door, where water and smell are easier to deal with. Sleeping goes at the far end, away from the stove and the traffic.

## Key sizes for a single room

| Item | Common size | Space to leave |
|---|---|---|
| Single bed | 90 x 190 cm (2 ft 11 in x 6 ft 3 in) | 60 cm (2 ft) beside it |
| Double bed | 150 x 190 cm (4 ft 11 in x 6 ft 3 in) | 60 cm (2 ft) on one side |
| Wardrobe | 60 cm (2 ft) deep | 60 cm (2 ft) in front for hinged doors |
| Kitchen counter | 60 cm (2 ft) deep, 85 to 90 cm (2 ft 9 in to 2 ft 11 in) high | 90 cm (2 ft 11 in) to stand and turn |
| Fold-away table | 80 x 60 cm (2 ft 7 in x 2 ft) | 60 cm (2 ft) behind for the chair |
| Fridge | about 65 x 65 cm (2 ft 2 in x 2 ft 2 in) | a few cm for air at the sides, as the maker says |

A walking path should be 75 cm (2 ft 6 in) wide at the least, and 90 cm (2 ft 11 in) wherever two people may pass.

## Separate the zones without building walls

You can mark zones with things that move or fold.

- **Open shelf unit:** 30 to 35 cm (1 ft to 1 ft 2 in) deep and 150 to 180 cm (4 ft 11 in to 5 ft 11 in) high. It divides sleeping from sitting and holds books and baskets. Keep it away from the window so that daylight reaches the whole room.
- **A rug:** it tells the eye where the sitting zone ends.

## Cooking in the same room

A kitchen in a room you also sleep in needs fresh air and a clear head about safety.

- Fit an exhaust fan or a chimney, and cook with the window or fan on. Steam and smell settle in clothes and bedding.
- Keep the stove at least 90 cm (2 ft 11 in) from the bed if the plan allows, and never hang a curtain near a flame.
- Gas connections, a gas cylinder position and electric points for an induction hob or a fridge are for a licensed gas fitter and a licensed electrician. Ask them where each can go before the carpenter builds.
- In the monsoon, wet cloth dries slowly in a room with a stove. Put the clothes line on the balcony or near the window and keep a vent open.

## Worked example: a 3.6 x 4.5 m room

Take a rented single room of 3.6 x 4.5 m (11 ft 10 in x 14 ft 9 in), with the door on one 3.6 m wall and the window on the other. The floor area is 3.6 x 4.5 equals 16.2 square metres (about 174 sq ft). One person lives here, with a double bed of 150 x 190 cm.

**Along the left long wall (450 cm).** The kitchen counter takes 210 cm (6 ft 11 in) for the sink and the stove, and the fridge takes 65 cm (2 ft 2 in). Together 210 plus 65 equals 275 cm. The fold-away table of 80 cm (2 ft 7 in) goes next. 275 plus 80 equals 355 cm, which leaves 450 minus 355 equals 95 cm (3 ft 1 in) of wall free.

**Along the right long wall (450 cm).** A wardrobe of 180 cm (5 ft 11 in) wide sits near the door, and the bed of 190 cm (6 ft 3 in) goes beside it at the far end. 180 plus 190 equals 370 cm, which leaves 450 minus 370 equals 80 cm (2 ft 7 in) of wall free.

**Across the width (360 cm).**
- At the wardrobe: 360 minus 60 (counter) minus 60 (wardrobe) equals 240 cm (7 ft 11 in) of floor. The wardrobe doors open into it without trouble.
- At the bed and table: 360 minus 60 (table) minus 150 (bed) equals 150 cm (4 ft 11 in). The chair pulled out takes 60 cm, so 150 minus 60 equals 90 cm (2 ft 11 in) of path is left. That passes the 75 cm (2 ft 6 in) minimum.

**Floor budget.** Bed 1.5 x 1.9 equals 2.85 sq m. Wardrobe 1.8 x 0.6 equals 1.08 sq m. Counter 2.1 x 0.6 equals 1.26 sq m. Fridge about 0.42 sq m. Table 0.8 x 0.6 equals 0.48 sq m. Total: 2.85 plus 1.08 plus 1.26 plus 0.42 plus 0.48 equals 6.09 sq m (about 66 sq ft). Open floor: 16.2 minus 6.09 equals 10.11 sq m (about 109 sq ft).

This works on paper. For seating, the bed doubles as the sofa, backed with bolsters, and two folding chairs live at the table. If the room felt tight, a single bed of 90 x 190 cm would free 150 minus 90 equals 60 cm of width, and the path at the bed would grow from 150 to 210 cm (6 ft 11 in) with the chair out of the way.

## Checklist for your tape measure

- [ ] Draw the room with the door and window. Mark pipes, drain and sockets, because you cannot easily move them.
- [ ] Place the four zones door to window: cook, store, sit and sleep.
- [ ] Add up each wall: do the furniture widths fit with a few cm spare?
- [ ] Check a 75 cm (2 ft 6 in) path between all zones.
- [ ] Check 60 cm (2 ft) in front of every cupboard door.
- [ ] Check the window can open, and is not blocked by a shelf or a bed head.
- [ ] Find a place for shoes, bags and a clothes line.

## Common mistakes

1. **Buying a sofa.** A sofa of 200 x 90 cm (6 ft 7 in x 2 ft 11 in) takes 1.8 square metres and a bed covers the same job. Use the bed as a seat, or choose a bench of 40 cm (1 ft 4 in) depth.
2. **Putting the bed next to the stove.** Heat, smell and grease reach the bed. Keep the stove at the other end of the room.
3. **Tall furniture in front of the window.** It cuts daylight and air for the whole room.
4. **One light for everything.** Use a lamp for the bed and a brighter light for the counter.

## FAQ

### How big is a single room?
Rented single rooms vary a lot in size. The example room of 16.2 sq m (174 sq ft) fits all four zones. If yours is smaller, change the double bed to a single first, then drop the table in favour of a wall-fold shelf.

### Can I keep a fridge in the room?
Yes, if the electrician can supply a point for it and it has air space around it as the maker advises. A fridge hums, so many people put it near the door and away from the bed.

### How do I stop the room smelling of cooking?
Run the exhaust fan or chimney while you cook and for a short time afterwards, keep the window open, and keep cooked food covered.

### Where do I sit if there is no sofa?
Use the bed as the seat, backed with bolsters against the wall, and keep two folding chairs at the table. A bench 40 cm (1 ft 4 in) deep is a second option.

### What is the smallest walking path I should leave?
A comfortable minimum is 75 cm (2 ft 6 in), and 90 cm (2 ft 11 in) where two people may pass. In the example room the tightest point is 90 cm.
