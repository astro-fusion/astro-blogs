# Furniture sizes that fit small rooms

Most furniture mistakes happen in the shop, not at home. A sofa looks small under a showroom ceiling and fills the whole living room once it arrives. After reading this you will know the usual sizes of the main pieces, the gaps to leave around them, and how to test a purchase on your own floor before you pay for it.

## The sizes you will meet

These are common sizes sold in Nepal and India. Makers vary, so always measure the actual piece.

### Beds

| Bed | Mattress size |
|---|---|
| Single | 90 x 190 cm (3 ft x 6 ft 3 in) |
| Double | 150 x 190 cm (4 ft 11 in x 6 ft 3 in) |
| Queen | 152 x 198 cm (5 ft x 6 ft 6 in) |
| King | 180 x 198 cm (5 ft 11 in x 6 ft 6 in) |

A bed frame is wider and longer than the mattress. A headboard or a box storage base can add 10 to 20 cm (4 to 8 in) on each side. Ask the maker for the frame size, not the mattress size.

### Seating and tables

| Piece | Usual size |
|---|---|
| Two-seat sofa | 140 to 160 cm long, 80 to 90 cm deep (4 ft 7 in to 5 ft 3 in, 2 ft 7 in to 2 ft 11 in) |
| Three-seat sofa | 190 to 210 cm long, 85 to 95 cm deep (6 ft 3 in to 6 ft 11 in, 2 ft 9 in to 3 ft 1 in) |
| Armchair | 75 to 85 cm square (2 ft 6 in to 2 ft 9 in) |
| Coffee table | 90 x 50 cm, 40 to 45 cm high (2 ft 11 in x 1 ft 8 in, 1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in) |
| Dining table, four seats | 120 x 75 cm or 100 x 100 cm (3 ft 11 in x 2 ft 6 in or 3 ft 3 in square) |
| Dining table, six seats | 150 x 90 cm (4 ft 11 in x 2 ft 11 in) |
| Dining chair | about 45 cm seat height, 45 cm square (1 ft 6 in) |

### Storage, desks and the rest

| Piece | Usual size |
|---|---|
| Hinged wardrobe | 55 to 60 cm deep, each door about 45 to 50 cm wide (1 ft 10 in to 2 ft deep) |
| Sliding wardrobe | 60 to 70 cm deep (2 ft to 2 ft 4 in) |
| TV unit | 35 to 45 cm deep (1 ft 2 in to 1 ft 6 in), 40 to 50 cm high (1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 8 in) |
| Study desk | 120 x 60 cm, 72 to 75 cm high (3 ft 11 in x 2 ft, about 2 ft 5 in) |
| Bedside table | 40 x 40 cm (1 ft 4 in) |

A hinged wardrobe also needs room for the door to swing, about the width of one door. A sliding wardrobe needs no swing space but is a little deeper because of the tracks.

## The gaps around furniture

A comfortable minimum is not the same as the size of the furniture. Leave these gaps when you can.

- Beside a bed where someone gets in or out: 60 cm (2 ft). Aim for 75 cm (2 ft 6 in) on the main side.
- Main walking route through a room: 90 cm (3 ft). A side route can be 75 cm (2 ft 6 in).
- Between a sofa and a coffee table: 40 to 45 cm (1 ft 4 in to 1 ft 6 in).
- Behind a dining chair to walk past: 75 cm (2 ft 6 in). Aim for 90 cm (3 ft) if people pass often.
- In front of a wardrobe: 90 cm (3 ft) so you can open a door and stand in front of it.
- Between a bed foot and a wall with a door: enough for the door to open fully.

## A worked example

Take a living room measuring 3.0 x 4.0 m (9 ft 10 in x 13 ft 1 in). The sofa is a three-seater of 2.0 m (6 ft 7 in) long and 0.90 m (2 ft 11 in) deep. Opposite it is a TV unit 0.40 m (1 ft 4 in) deep. There is a coffee table of 0.90 x 0.50 m.

Step 1, along the 4.0 m length. Put the sofa against one short wall and the TV unit on the other short wall.

- Space left between the sofa front and the TV unit: 4.0 minus 0.90 minus 0.40 is 2.70 m (8 ft 10 in).
- Coffee table: 0.45 m (1 ft 6 in) gap from the sofa plus 0.50 m (1 ft 8 in) for the table is 0.95 m (3 ft 1 in) used.
- Open floor left in front of the table: 2.70 minus 0.95 is 1.75 m (5 ft 9 in). That is plenty for walking and for children to sit on the floor.

Step 2, along the 3.0 m width. The sofa stands against the short wall, so its 2.0 m length lies across the 3.0 m width.

- Sofa 2.0 m plus a side table 0.40 m is 2.40 m (7 ft 10 in).
- Left over: 3.0 minus 2.40 is 0.60 m (2 ft). That is the minimum for one person to pass beside the sofa.

Step 3, improve it. Swap to a 1.8 m (5 ft 11 in) sofa. Now 3.0 minus 1.8 minus 0.40 is 0.80 m (2 ft 7 in). The side path is comfortable and the viewing distance is unchanged.

For viewing distance, 2.5 m (8 ft 2 in) or a little more is a workable seat-to-screen gap for a medium television. Here you have 2.70 m, so it fits.

## Test before you buy

- Mark each piece on the floor with newspaper or masking tape at its real size.
- Walk the routes. Open the door. Sit where the sofa will be.
- Measure the door, the staircase and the lift. A sofa that fits the room may not pass the entrance.
- Measure at the skirting and again at 90 cm (3 ft) height, because walls and switch boards are not always straight.

## Checklist

1. Measure the room at floor level, wall to wall.
2. Write down the door and window positions, and the swing of each door.
3. List the pieces you must have, with real sizes from the maker.
4. Draw them on paper at one scale.
5. Add the gaps from the list above.
6. Tape the plan on the floor and walk it.
7. Check that every piece can get through the entrance.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying from the shop floor size.** Always measure the depth and the arms of a sofa, not only the length.
- **Forgetting door swings.** A wardrobe door and a room door can hit each other.
- **A bed against the window wall with no side space.** You will climb in at the foot.
- **A large dining table in a living room.** Choose a table that folds or has a leaf.
- **Ignoring the monsoon.** Leave about 5 cm (2 in) behind furniture against an outer wall so air can move and damp does not build up.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the smallest bed that is comfortable for two?
A double of 150 x 190 cm or a queen of 152 x 198 cm. Leave 60 cm on each open side.

### How much space does a three-seat sofa really need?
About 2.1 m (6 ft 11 in) long by 0.9 m (2 ft 11 in) deep, plus 0.45 m (1 ft 6 in) in front before the coffee table.

### Should I buy a sliding wardrobe?
It is a good choice where the walking space in front is under 90 cm (3 ft), because no door has to swing.

### How big should a dining table be in a small flat?
A four-seat table of 120 x 75 cm works in a corner of the living room. Add 75 cm behind each chair.

### How do I check a piece will go through the door?
Measure the clear opening of the door and the stair turns. Compare the smallest face of the piece, not its length.
