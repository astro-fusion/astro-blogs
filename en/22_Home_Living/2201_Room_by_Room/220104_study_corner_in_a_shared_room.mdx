# Making a study corner in a shared room

After reading this, you will be able to fit a study desk into a room that other people sleep or sit in. You will know the desk and chair sizes, how much floor to keep behind the chair, where light should come from, and how to mark off the corner without a wall.

## What a study corner needs

A study corner needs a flat surface, a chair that fits the person, light that does not shine into the eyes, and a place for books. It works best in the same spot every day.

The sizes below are common practice for a school child or an adult. For a young child, see the article on a children's room that grows with the child.

| Part | Aim for |
|---|---|
| Desk top, one person | 120 x 60 cm (4 ft x 2 ft) |
| Desk top, two people side by side | 180 to 200 cm wide, 60 cm deep (5 ft 11 in to 6 ft 7 in) |
| Desk height, adult or teenager | 73 to 76 cm (2 ft 5 in to 2 ft 6 in) |
| Chair seat height | 42 to 46 cm (1 ft 5 in to 1 ft 6 in) |
| Knee space under the desk | at least 60 cm wide, 45 cm deep (2 ft, 1 ft 6 in) |
| Floor behind the chair, to pull it back | 90 cm (3 ft) |
| Shelf above the desk, depth | 25 to 30 cm (10 in to 1 ft) |

Test the height with a simple rule. Sit with your feet flat on the floor and your thighs level. Your elbows should rest near the desk top, with your shoulders loose. If your feet hang, add a footrest. If the desk is too high, raise the chair and add the footrest.

## Where to put it in the room

- **Light from the side.** Put the desk so that window light comes from the side, from the left if you write with your right hand and from the right if you write with your left. This keeps your hand from casting a shadow on the page.
- **Not facing the window.** Daylight behind a screen causes glare. Daylight behind your back also shades the page.
- **Away from the bed.** The study corner should not be in the path to the bed or the door.
- **Near a socket.** Put the desk where a socket already is. If you need a new point, ask a licensed electrician. Do not use long chains of extension cords across the floor.

Vastu tradition prefers that a student faces east or north while studying. If your room allows it, you can choose that wall, but the light and the socket matter more for the actual work.

## Marking the corner without a wall

In a shared room, you can separate the corner with furniture instead of a wall.

- A bookshelf of 30 cm depth and about 120 cm height, placed at right angles to the wall, makes a side screen and holds books. Ask a carpenter to fix a tall shelf to the wall so it cannot tip.
- A curtain on a ceiling rail or a light rod can close the corner while someone sleeps. Keep it clear of fans and lamps.
- A rug under the desk tells the eye where the corner is.

## Worked example: a 3.0 x 3.6 m (10 x 12 ft) room shared by two children

The room is 3.0 x 3.6 m, so the area is 3.0 x 3.6 = 10.8 square metres (about 116 sq ft). It has a bunk bed for the two children, a wardrobe, and one study desk.

**Step 1. Place the bunk bed.** A bunk bed is 90 x 190 cm. Put it along one long wall. It takes 0.9 x 1.9 = 1.71 square metres, and 190 cm of the 360 cm wall length.

**Step 2. Choose the desk wall.** Use the short wall at the far end, which is 300 cm wide. A desk of 120 x 60 cm sits against it, between 150 and 270 cm along that wall. If the window is on a long wall, it gives side light.

**Step 3. Add the chair space.** The chair needs 90 cm behind the desk. The desk and chair space together are 120 cm wide and 60 + 90 = 150 cm deep. That is 1.2 x 1.5 = 1.8 square metres.

**Step 4. Check the side gap to the bunk.** The bunk reaches 90 cm out from its wall, and the desk starts 150 cm from that wall. The gap is 150 - 90 = 60 cm (2 ft), the minimum for a path. Keep it empty.

**Step 5. Check along the length.** The desk and chair space take 150 cm of the 360 cm length, so they start 360 - 150 = 210 cm from the bunk's end of the room. The bunk is 190 cm long, so 210 - 190 = 20 cm separates them. Pulling the chair back does not hit the bunk.

**Step 6. Count the floor left.** 10.8 - 1.71 - 1.8 = 7.29 square metres. A wardrobe of 1.2 x 0.6 = 0.72 square metres leaves 7.29 - 0.72 = 6.57 square metres for walking, a play mat and the door arc.

**Step 7. If two children study at once.** A desk of 180 cm would run from 120 to 300 cm along the wall, leaving only 120 - 90 = 30 cm beside the bunk. That is too tight. In this room, share one desk in turns, or add a shelf-top for the second child.

## Light for evenings and power cuts

Use two kinds of light: one general light in the ceiling, and a task lamp on the desk. Place the lamp on the side opposite the writing hand. Choose a warm or neutral white bulb that you find comfortable, and keep a bare bulb out of your line of sight. Keep a rechargeable lamp for power cuts.

## Heat and cold

In the hot plains, a desk in the afternoon sun is uncomfortable. Choose a shaded wall, or use a light curtain. In hill winters, avoid a desk against a cold outside wall, and keep a mat on the floor for your feet.

## Checklist

- Measure the wall where the desk will stand, and the floor behind it.
- Check 120 x 60 cm desk top, 90 cm behind the chair.
- Check the window light comes from the side.
- Check there is a socket within reach, without long extension cords on the floor.
- Check the chair height: feet flat, thighs level.
- Check the corner is not in the path to the door or the bed.
- Decide how you will close the corner: shelf, curtain or rug.

## Common mistakes

1. **A desk with no chair space.** If you cannot pull the chair back 90 cm, you will sit sideways.
2. **A lamp on the writing-hand side.** It throws a shadow on the page.
3. **Studying on the bed.** The desk keeps the bed free for sleeping.
4. **Facing the window.** The glare and the view both pull the eyes away.
5. **A tall shelf that is not fixed to the wall.** It can tip. Ask the carpenter to fix it.

## FAQ

### What size study table fits a small room?
A desk of 120 x 60 cm with 90 cm behind it needs 120 x 150 cm of floor. A wall-fixed folding desk of 90 x 45 cm needs less.

### Can I use a dining table as a study desk?
Yes, if the height is 73 to 76 cm and the chair can slide under it. Clear it after use so the family can eat.

### Where should the study table face?
Choose light and quiet first. Tradition prefers east or north, but the light from the side matters more.

### How do I stop noise from disturbing study?
Put the desk far from the TV wall and the door. Use a rug and a curtain, which soften sound a little. Headphones help.
