# A puja space in a modern flat: size, height and safety

After reading this you will be able to choose a place in a flat for daily puja, size the shelf and the sitting area, and handle lamps and incense safely. You will get a worked example for a corner of 1.2 x 1.1 m (4 x 3 ft 7 in).

For many households, daily puja is a quiet, respected part of the day. A flat may not have a separate room for it, but a small, well-planned corner can serve just as well.

## Choosing the place

Practical points come first.

- **Quiet.** Choose a place away from the television and the busiest walking route, so the person praying is not disturbed.
- **Not behind a door.** A door that swings into the space makes it cramped. Keep the swing clear.
- **Air.** A window or a ventilation point lets smoke from incense and lamps clear. In the monsoon, check that a wall is not damp.
- **Away from a shared bathroom wall.** A wall shared with a bathroom can pick up damp. Check it before you fix a shelf.
- **Light.** Daylight or a gentle lamp helps the person see the images and the book.
- **Tradition.** Vastu tradition places the puja space in the north-east of the home.

## Four common setups

- **Wall-mounted shelf.** The lightest and cheapest option. It suits a rented flat if the wall can take it.
- **A floor unit or a cabinet.** A small cabinet with doors keeps the images covered when not in use and hides the storage.
- **A built-in niche.** A niche in a wall of the living room or passage looks neat. Cutting into a wall is structural work, so ask a licensed engineer first.
- **A compact portable unit.** A small table or a stand can move with you if you move house.

## Sizes to start with

These are common-practice figures. Adjust them to the images and the way your family prays.

- **Shelf width.** 60 to 90 cm (2 ft to 3 ft) holds a few images, a lamp and a bell. 120 cm (4 ft) is generous.
- **Shelf depth.** 30 to 35 cm (1 ft to 1 ft 2 in) holds images and a lamp, without leaving the lamp at the edge.
- **Height.** Many households place the main images at about chest height when standing, between 120 and 130 cm (4 ft to 4 ft 3 in) from the floor. If the family prays seated, a lower shelf at about 90 to 100 cm (3 ft to 3 ft 3 in) is easier to see.
- **Sitting area.** A person sitting cross-legged needs about 60 x 90 cm (2 x 3 ft) of floor, mat included. Two people side by side need 120 cm (4 ft) of width.
- **Standing room.** If you stand, leave 60 cm (2 ft) in front of the shelf.
- **Heavy images.** Stone or marble images are heavy. Put them on a floor unit or tell your carpenter and engineer their weight before fixing a shelf to the wall.

## Lamps, incense and fire safety

An open flame needs care in a small home.

- Keep a lamp on a tray of stone, tile, steel or brass, never directly on wood or plastic.
- Leave at least 60 cm (2 ft) clear above the lamp and 60 cm (2 ft) to the sides from curtains, wooden shelves and paper.
- Keep incense in a holder with an ash catcher, and never leave a flame or burning incense unattended.
- Keep a small bowl of water or a sand bucket nearby, and keep children and pets away from the flame.
- Ventilate when you light incense. If the smoke bothers someone, use less of it or open a window.
- A lamp with a battery or an electric light can replace an open flame when no one is there. Any new wiring is for a licensed electrician.

## Storage and cleaning

- Keep a drawer or a lidded box for incense, matches, wicks, oil and small items.
- A cover or a curtain keeps dust off the images. It should be flame-safe and far from the lamp.
- Use a washable tray or mat below the shelf for wiped-off oil and ash.

## Worked example: a corner of 1.2 x 1.1 m (4 x 3 ft 7 in)

A family of four has a corner beside the window in the living room. Two adults pray together each morning.

1. **Floor needed.** Two people seated side by side need 2 x 60 = 120 cm (4 ft) of width.
2. **Depth needed.** A mat is 90 cm (3 ft) deep. Add a gap of 20 cm (8 in) between the mat and the wall unit. The shelf is fixed high on the wall with nothing under it, so the floor depth needed is 90 + 20 = 110 cm (3 ft 7 in).
3. **Shelf.** Mount a shelf 90 cm (3 ft) wide and 30 cm (1 ft) deep, with its top at 125 cm (4 ft 1 in) from the floor.
4. **Lamp clearance.** The lamp sits on a stone tray on the shelf. Leave 60 cm (2 ft) above it. Total height needed from the floor: 125 + 60 = 185 cm (6 ft 1 in), which is below the ceiling of 270 cm (8 ft 10 in).
5. **Side clearance.** The shelf is 90 cm wide, and the corner is 120 cm wide. This leaves 120 - 90 = 30 cm (1 ft), or 15 cm (6 in) on each side if the shelf is centred. Keep the window curtain well away from it.
6. **Check.** The corner is 120 x 110 cm. The mat zone needs 120 x 90 cm, plus 20 cm of gap. It fits.

## Checklist for the tape measure

- A clear floor of 60 x 90 cm (2 x 3 ft) for each person sitting.
- Shelf height decided for standing or seated prayer.
- 60 cm (2 ft) clear above and beside the flame.
- A wall that is dry and solid.
- A heat-proof tray for the lamp.
- Storage for incense and matches.
- Ventilation or a window.

## Common mistakes

- **A shelf directly above a lamp.** Soot and heat build up.
- **Curtains near the flame.** This is a fire risk.
- **A shelf on a damp wall.** Check the wall first.
- **A heavy idol on a thin shelf.** Use a floor unit or have the fixing checked.
- **No ventilation.** Smoke lingers and settles on walls.

## FAQ

### Do I need a separate room?
No. A shelf, a cabinet or a corner can serve well. What matters is that the space is clean, quiet and safe.

### Which direction should it face?
Vastu tradition places the puja space in the north-east. In a flat the choice is often limited, so start with a quiet, dry, ventilated spot where a flame can be kept safe.

### Can I place it in the kitchen or bedroom?
Many families do, for lack of space. A cover or a closed cabinet helps, and keep the flame away from bedding.

### How high should the images be?
Many families set them at about chest height when standing, 120 to 130 cm (4 ft to 4 ft 3 in), or lower if prayer is seated.

### Is incense safe indoors?
Use it sparingly and with air movement. If anyone coughs or has breathing trouble, ask a doctor and consider using less or none.
