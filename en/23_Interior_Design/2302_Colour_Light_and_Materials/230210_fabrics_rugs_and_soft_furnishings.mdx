# Fabrics, Rugs and Soft Furnishings That Last

Fabric is what you touch every day, and it takes the dust, the sun and the monsoon damp. After reading this you will know which fabric suits each job, how to size a rug and how to work out the fabric a pair of curtains needs.

## Know the fibres

| Fibre | Good for | Weak points |
|---|---|---|
| Cotton | Curtains, covers, bedspreads, washable cushions | Creases, shrinks if not pre-washed, can fade in strong sun |
| Linen and linen blends | Light curtains, covers | Creases heavily, costs more |
| Polyester | Curtains, sofa fabric, dust-resistant pieces | Can feel warm in hot weather, holds static |
| Blends of cotton and polyester | Everyday sofa covers and curtains | Quality varies, so check the label |
| Wool | Rugs and winter throws in hill homes | Needs airing, moths can attack in storage |
| Jute and other plant fibres | Rugs on dry floors | Can go mouldy in damp, hard to clean when wet |
| Acrylic, nylon, polypropylene | Rugs for busy areas | Can look shiny, can melt from a hot ember or iron |

Where the label gives a fibre content, read it. If it does not, ask the shop.

## Sofa and upholstery fabric

- A tight weave resists snags and dust better than a loose one.
- A **rub test** figure, such as the Martindale rubs, tells you how long the fabric survives wear. Ask for it. A figure of 20,000 or more is usually chosen for a sofa in daily use, and lower figures suit a bedroom chair that is rarely sat on. Treat the figure as a rough guide.
- Avoid very pale fabrics if there are children, pets or oily food in the house, unless the covers come off and wash.
- Leather and similar sheets crack in dry sun and feel sticky in humid summers. Keep them away from a hot window.
- A removable cover, or a throw you can wash, is the cheapest way to extend the life of a sofa.

## Curtains

- Use a lined curtain on a west or south window to cut glare and heat. Use a light, unlined or sheer curtain on a shaded window, or add a sheer layer behind a heavier one for privacy and light.
- Dark colours fade faster in strong sun than pale ones.
- Wash cotton and cotton blends before you stitch, so they do not shrink afterwards.
- In the monsoon, do not leave a damp curtain bunched against a wet wall.

## Rugs

- Under a living-room sofa set, aim for a rug large enough that at least the front legs of the seats stand on it. Leave 30 to 45 cm (1 ft to 1 ft 6 in) of bare floor around the edge.
- Under a bed, a rug should extend 50 to 60 cm (1 ft 8 in to 2 ft) past the sides and foot, where your feet land.
- At a dining table, a rug should extend 60 to 75 cm (2 ft to 2 ft 6 in) past the table edge, so a pulled-back chair stays on the rug.
- Use a non-slip pad under every rug on a smooth tile floor. Check the pad does not stain the floor.
- In damp or coastal-humid homes, pick a rug in synthetic or wool and lift it now and then to let the floor dry. Do not lay jute on a damp floor.
- Shoes off at the door helps all rugs last longer.

## Worked example 1: the rug for a 3.0 x 3.6 m living room

**Step 1: the space.** The room is 3.0 m wide and 3.6 m long.

**Step 2: leave a border.** Take 40 cm of bare floor on each side. 3.0 minus 2 x 0.4 = 2.2 m is the widest rug that leaves this. 3.6 minus 2 x 0.4 = 2.8 m is the longest.

**Step 3: pick a stock size.** Common sizes are 1.6 x 2.3 m (5 ft 3 in x 7 ft 6 in) and 2.0 x 3.0 m (6 ft 7 in x 9 ft 10 in). The 2.0 x 3.0 m rug is 0.2 m longer than the 2.8 m limit, so it leaves a little less border at the ends: 3.6 minus 3.0 = 0.6 m, which is 30 cm at each end. Its width gives 3.0 minus 2.0 = 1.0 m, so 50 cm at each side.

**Step 4: decide.** The 2.0 x 3.0 m rug leaves 30 cm at each end and 50 cm at each side. That is close to the guide, so it works. The 1.6 x 2.3 m rug leaves 70 cm at each side and 65 cm at each end, which looks small but fits easily. Choose the larger if the sofa legs should sit on it.

## Worked example 2: fabric for a pair of curtains

A window is 1.5 m wide. The rail is 1.9 m wide (20 cm past each side), set at 2.30 m, and the curtains hang to 1 cm above the floor. The fabric is 1.4 m wide.

**Step 1: width.** For soft folds the curtain needs about twice the rail width: 1.9 x 2 = 3.8 m. 3.8 / 1.4 = 2.71, so 3 widths of fabric. A pair needs matching curtains, so round up to 4 widths, 2 for each curtain.

**Step 2: drop.** Finished length 2.30 minus 0.01 = 2.29 m. Add 10 cm for the hem and 10 cm for the heading: 2.29 + 0.20 = 2.49 m per cut length.

**Step 3: total.** 4 x 2.49 = 9.96 m, so buy 10 m, and ask the shop for a little extra if the fabric has a pattern to match.

## Checklist

- Check the label for fibre content and care.
- Ask for a rub figure on sofa fabric.
- Test the rug size with newspaper on the floor.
- Buy a pad for each rug on tile.
- Pre-wash cotton before cutting.
- Check the colour in daylight, since shop lights change it.
- Plan where you will dry washed covers in the monsoon.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying a rug that is too small.** It floats in the room. Furniture should sit at least partly on it.
- **Skipping a pad.** The rug slides, and the corner turns up.
- **Pale sofa fabric with no removable cover.** A stain stays.
- **Putting jute or cotton rugs on a damp floor.** They go mouldy.
- **Narrow curtains.** Curtains that do not have enough fabric look flat and thin.

## FAQ

### Which fabric is best for a sofa with children?
A tight weave in a mid-tone with a high rub figure, with covers you can remove and wash.

### How do I keep rugs from smelling in the monsoon?
Lift them, air them in dry hours, and keep the floor beneath dry. Avoid natural fibres on damp floors.

### What size rug for a living room?
Large enough for at least the front legs of the sofa and chairs to sit on it, with 30 to 45 cm of bare floor around.

### Are lined curtains worth it?
On sun-facing windows, yes. They cut glare and help the fabric last longer.

### How often should I wash curtains and covers?
When they look or smell dirty, and at least once in a season in dusty cities. Follow the care label.
