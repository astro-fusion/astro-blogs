# Hanging heights for mirrors, shelves, switches and pictures

Things fixed to a wall are hard to move once the holes are drilled. After reading this you will be able to choose a height for each item, mark the wall with a tape measure, and avoid the common errors of hanging pictures too high and shelves out of reach.

## One rule for most things: eye level

Most people hang pictures and mirrors too high. The usual rule of thumb is to place the centre of the picture or mirror at about 145 to 150 cm (4 ft 9 in to 4 ft 11 in) from the floor. That is roughly the eye level of an average standing adult. If your household is mostly short or tall, move it down or up by 5 to 10 cm (2 to 4 in).

## Common heights

These are habits of good practice, not standards. Adjust for the people who use the room.

| Item | Height from floor | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Centre of a picture or mirror | 145 to 150 cm (4 ft 9 in to 4 ft 11 in) | Eye level |
| Picture above a sofa | Bottom 20 to 25 cm (8 to 10 in) above the sofa back | Keep it tied to the furniture |
| Full-length mirror | Bottom 20 to 30 cm (8 to 12 in), top about 170 to 180 cm (5 ft 7 in to 5 ft 11 in) | Lean it or fix it with proper fixings |
| Bathroom mirror above basin | Bottom 20 cm (8 in) above the basin rim; basin rim 80 to 85 cm (2 ft 7 in to 2 ft 9 in) | Centre at about 150 cm (4 ft 11 in) |
| Towel rail | 110 to 120 cm (3 ft 7 in to 3 ft 11 in) | Lower for children, 90 to 100 cm (2 ft 11 in to 3 ft 3 in) |
| Shelf above a desk | 45 to 50 cm (1 ft 6 in to 1 ft 8 in) above the desktop | Clears a monitor |
| Shelf above a kitchen counter | 45 to 60 cm (1 ft 6 in to 2 ft) above the counter | See the kitchen article |
| Everyday shelf in a hall | 100 to 150 cm (3 ft 3 in to 4 ft 11 in) | Within easy reach |
| Wardrobe handle | 100 to 110 cm (3 ft 3 in to 3 ft 7 in) | Hip to waist |
| TV centre | 100 to 110 cm (3 ft 3 in to 3 ft 7 in) | Seated eye level |
| Light switches | About 120 cm (3 ft 11 in) | Common practice in homes |
| Wall sockets | About 30 cm (1 ft) for general use | Higher above kitchen counters and basins |

On switches and sockets: the heights above are common marking heights. Show your electrician where your furniture will stand and let them decide the final positions and wiring. Never drill or chase a wall without checking where cables and pipes run.

## Shelves

- **Everyday items** sit between hip and shoulder, about 80 to 150 cm (2 ft 7 in to 4 ft 11 in). Heavy items go lower, light and decorative ones higher.
- **A shelf above head height** is for things used a few times a year. Use a stool, not a chair, to reach it.
- **Spacing:** for books, 25 to 30 cm (10 to 12 in) between shelves suits most paperbacks and files. Tall albums need 35 cm (1 ft 2 in).
- **Wall type matters.** A brick or concrete wall takes plugs and screws. A hollow block or a thin partition needs different fixings, and a plasterboard partition needs fixings made for it. Tell the carpenter what the wall is made of.
- **Weight:** a shelf with books is heavy. Ask your carpenter what the shelf and its fixings can carry, and keep the load to that.

## Worked example: a pair of pictures above a 200 cm sofa

The sofa is 200 cm (6 ft 7 in) wide and its back is 85 cm (2 ft 9 in) high. You have two frames, each 60 cm (2 ft) wide and 80 cm (2 ft 7 in) high.

**Step 1: choose the vertical position.** Put the centre of the frames at 150 cm (4 ft 11 in). Each frame is 80 cm high, so half is 40 cm. The bottom edge is 150 - 40 = 110 cm (3 ft 7 in). The top edge is 150 + 40 = 190 cm (6 ft 3 in).

**Step 2: check the gap to the sofa.** The sofa back is 85 cm, so the gap is 110 - 85 = 25 cm (10 in). That is at the top of the 20 to 25 cm range, so the pictures look connected to the sofa.

**Step 3: set the width.** Leave 10 cm (4 in) between the frames: 60 + 10 + 60 = 130 cm (4 ft 3 in). Two thirds of the sofa width is 200 x 2 / 3 = about 133 cm, so 130 cm is close to the rule of thumb.

**Step 4: centre the pair.** The unused wall beside the group is 200 - 130 = 70 cm. Half of that is 35 cm (1 ft 2 in) at each end. The first frame's centre is 35 + 30 = 65 cm (2 ft 2 in) from the sofa's left end. The second frame's centre is 35 + 60 + 10 + 30 = 135 cm (4 ft 5 in) from the same end.

**Step 5: find the hook height.** Pull the wire tight on the back of a frame and measure from the top edge down to the highest point of the wire. Suppose it is 10 cm (4 in). The hook goes at 190 - 10 = 180 cm (5 ft 11 in) from the floor. Do this for each frame.

**Step 6: mark and check.** Stick tape on the wall at 65 cm and 135 cm along the sofa, and mark the hooks at 180 cm. Check the marks with a spirit level before drilling.

## Mirrors

- **Opposite a window** a mirror carries daylight deeper into the room. Opposite a bright window it can also throw glare, so test with a board first.
- **In a hall,** check your whole face and shoulders without stepping back. A mirror about 100 cm (3 ft 3 in) tall centred at 150 cm (4 ft 11 in) does this for most adults.
- **In a bathroom,** keep the mirror out of the spray zone and fix it with moisture-resistant fixings. Steam shortens the life of a plain backing.
- **Heavy mirrors** need proper fixings into a solid wall. If you are unsure, ask the carpenter.

## Checklist with a tape measure

1. Ask the household who is tallest and who is shortest.
2. Mark the eye-level line at 145 to 150 cm (4 ft 9 in to 4 ft 11 in) with a pencil dot.
3. Mark each item on paper first and tape the paper to the wall.
4. Check the gap to furniture.
5. Check the wall for cables and pipes.
6. Use a level before drilling.
7. Keep the tape in place for a day and look from the chair or the door.

## Common mistakes

- **Pictures hung too high,** so they float above the furniture.
- **Pictures too small for the wall** or too small for the sofa.
- **Shelves at the same height everywhere,** with heavy things up high.
- **Drilling above or beside a switch** without checking for cables.
- **A mirror hung where it reflects glare** into the room or the stairs.

## FAQ

### How high should I hang a picture?

Aim for the centre at 145 to 150 cm (4 ft 9 in to 4 ft 11 in) from the floor.

### How far above a sofa should a picture go?

Roughly 20 to 25 cm (8 to 10 in) above the sofa back.

### How high should a switch board be?

About 120 cm (3 ft 11 in) is common. Your electrician should confirm positions for your rooms.

### How high is a bathroom mirror?

Keep the bottom about 20 cm (8 in) above the basin rim and the centre near 150 cm (4 ft 11 in).

### Can I drill anywhere in the wall?

No. Cables and pipes are often hidden. Ask your electrician or plumber where they run.
