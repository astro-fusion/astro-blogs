(a) The planet Venus, the son of Bhrigu, the preceptor of the Demons, is situated at a distance of 67 millions of miles away from the Sun. It takes about 7 1/2 months to complete one revolution round the Sun. The Earth, during this period, makes about 225 rotations on its axis. The planet Venus in Indian Astrology is known by several names, each standing for one or the other characteristics indicated by her. The names are:

1. Bhrigu
2. Shukra
3. Sita
4. Daityaguru
5. Usha
6. Bhargava

(b) The planet Venus represents among other things, beauty, love, romance, marriage, sex, sensuality, pleasure, luxury, comfort, art, music, dance, drama, fashion, jewellery, cosmetics, perfumes, flowers, vehicles, conveyances, wealth, prosperity, happiness, good fortune, fame, success, social life, friends, relationships, diplomacy, tact, charm, refinement, creativity, imagination, etc. The native born under its influence is generally beautiful, charming, and attractive. He or she is also likely to be artistic, creative, and talented.

(c) Venus in Signs

**[Aries](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is handsome, wealthy, and famous. He is fond of music and fine arts. He is also lustful and passionate. Venus in Aries brings passion and spontaneity to relationships with enthusiastic and assertive approaches to love.

**[Taurus](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is very wealthy and prosperous. He is also very beautiful and charming. He is fond of music and fine arts. Venus in its own sign enhances sensuality, stability, and deep appreciation for beauty and comfort.

**[Gemini](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is intelligent, learned, and wealthy. He is a good speaker and writer. He is also fond of traveling and the occult sciences. Venus in Gemini brings intellectual charm and versatility in relationships.

**[Cancer](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is endowed with happiness, vehicles, and landed property. He is loved by his mother, and has a charming personality. Venus in Cancer emphasizes emotional nurturing and home-centered love.

**[Leo](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is endowed with wealth, happiness, and fame. He is strong, victorious, and has many brothers. Venus in Leo brings dramatic flair and royal treatment in relationships.

**[Virgo](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is bereft of wealth and sons. He is unhappy, and has few friends. Venus in Virgo brings practical approach to love with attention to details and service.

**[Libra](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is very beautiful and charming. He is also very wealthy and prosperous. He is fond of music and fine arts. Venus in its exaltation sign brings perfect harmony, beauty, and diplomatic skills.

**[Scorpio](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native has a sickly wife. He is bereft of happiness, and is troubled by diseases. Venus in Scorpio brings intense, transformative love with deep emotional connections.

**[Sagittarius](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is endowed with wealth and happiness. He is religious, and has a happy father. Venus in Sagittarius brings philosophical love and adventure in relationships.

**[Capricorn](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is endowed with wealth, happiness, and fame. He is engaged in virtuous deeds, and is respected by the king. Venus in Capricorn brings structured, long-term approach to love and relationships.

**[Aquarius](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is endowed with wealth, happiness, and fame. He has many friends, and is engaged in virtuous deeds. Venus in Aquarius brings unconventional love and humanitarian approach to relationships.

**[Pisces](/blogs/06_Planet_in_Houses/0606_Venus_in_Houses/0606002_Venus_in_sign)**: The native is endowed with wealth, happiness, and fame. He has many friends, and is engaged in virtuous deeds. Venus in Pisces brings spiritual, compassionate love with deep emotional sensitivity.
